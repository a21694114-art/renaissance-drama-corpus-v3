COme away bleſt ſoules no more
Feede your eyes with what is poore.
'Tis enough that you haue bleſt
What was rude; what was undreſt,
And created in a trice
Out of Chaos paradiſe.
Come away and caſt your eyes
On this humble ſacrifice.
We no golden apples giue,
Here's no Adam, here's no Eve:
Not a Serpent dares appeare,
Whilest your Majeſties ſtay here,
Oh then ſit, and take your due,
Thoſe the firſt fruits are that grewe
In this Eden, and are throwne
On this Altar as your owne
Set a chaire for earth's Jove.
Bring another for his love.
Come away, vouchſafe to taſte
What was gathered up in haſte,
If we live another yeare
By your grace and favour here,
Italy, and France, and Spaine
Of their fruits ſhall boaſt in vaine.
