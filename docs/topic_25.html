Reflection on crowns, fear, laws and the art of rule: Greville's choruses, Sejanus, Byron, Misfortunes of Arthur, Cymbeline's cave speech — a real political theme across authors. Two things are not separated here and go into the limitations: Greville's half-share, and his unregularised spelling (feare, lawes, loue in the keywords).
