Title pageMonuments of Honor. Deriued from remarkable Antiquity, ... Directed in their most affectionate Loue, at the Confirmation of their right Worthy Brother IOHN GORE in the High Office of His Maiesties Liuetenant ouer this His Royall Chamber. Expressing in a Magnificent Tryumph, all the Pageants, Chariots of Glory, Temples of Honor, besides a specious and goodly Sea Tryumph, as well particularly to the Honor of the City, as generally to the Glory of this our Kingdome.
