Friars, priors and nuns as agents of the action: friars who marry lovers in secret, conjure, carry messages or plot murder; Shakespeare, Peele, Greene, Marlowe (Jew of Malta, Massacre), the Robin Hood plays, Davenport. The role holds it together, not one story; comparable across works.
