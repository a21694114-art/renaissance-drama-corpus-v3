So then, there the Horse complains of the Apes rank-riding: the Jockey makes mouths, but is fined for it: the Stag is not jeered by the Monkey for his horns: the Ass by the hare for his burden: The Ox by the Leopard for his yoke, nor the Goat by the Ram, for his beard, only the Fox wraps himself warm in Bever, bids the Cat mouse, the Elephant toil, the Boar gather acorns, whiles he grins, feeds fat, tells tales, laughs at all, and sleeps safe at the Lions feet.— Save ye people. Why save thee too, if thou be'st of heavens making: What art?— Fear nothing Don, we have our blades, Are metal men ourselves, try us who dare. Our brother speaks our mind, think what you please on't. A match: observe well this switch; with this only switch have I pashed out the brains of thirteen Turks to the dozen for a breakfast. What man? thirteen? is't possible thou lyest not?
