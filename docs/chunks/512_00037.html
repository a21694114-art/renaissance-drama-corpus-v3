Is it no other? No other, on my trust. For your more surety, Here is that letter too. (How easily, Do wretched men believe, what they would have! Looks this like plot? Noble Arruntius, stay.) He names him here without his titles. (Note. Yes, and come of your notable fool. I will.) No other, than Sejanus. That's but haste In him that writes. Here he gives large amends. And with his own hand written? Yes. Indeed? Believe it, gentlemen, Sejanus breast Never received more full contentments in, Then at this present. Takes he well th'escape Of young Caligula, with MACRO? Faith, At the first air, it somewhat troubled him. (Observe you? Nothing. Riddles. TIll I see Sejanus struck, no sound thereof strikes me.) I like it not. I muse h' would not attempt Somewhat against him in the Consulship, Seeing the people 'gin to favour him. He doth repent it, now; but h' has employed PAGONIANUS after him: and he holds That correspondence, there, with all that are near about CAESAR, as no thought can pass Without his knowledge, thence, in act to front him. I gratulate the news. But, how comes MACRO So in trust, and favour, with Caligula? O sir, he has a wife; and the young Prince An appetite: he can look up, and spy Flies in the roof, when there are fleas i' bed; And hath a learned nose t'o'assure his sleeps. Who, to be favoured of the rising sun, Would not lend little of his waning moon? 'T is the safest ambition. Noble Terentius. The night grows fast upon vs. At your service. SWell, swell, my joys: and faint not to declare Yourselves, as ample, as your causes are. I did not live, till now; this my first hour: Wherein I see my thoughts reached by my power. But this, and gripe my wishes. Great, and high, The world knows only two, that's Rome, and I. My roof receives me not; 'tis air I tread: And, at each step, I feel my advanced head Knock out a star in heaven! Reared to this height, All my desires seem modest, poor, and sleight, That did before sound impudent: 'Tis place, Not blood, discerns the noble, and the base. Is there not something more, then to be CAESAR? Must we rest there? It irks, t' have come so far, To be so near a stay. Caligula, Would thou stoodst stiff, and many, in our way. Winds lose their strength, when they do empty fly, Unmet of woods or buildings; great fires die That want their matter to withstand them: so, It is our grief, and will be our loss, to know Our power shall want opposites; unless The gods, by mixing in the cause, would bless Our fortune, with their conquest. That were worth Sejanus strife: durst fates but bring it forth. SAfety, to great Sejanus. Now, Terentius? Hears not my lord the wonder? Speak it, no. I meet it violent in the people's mouths, Who run, in routs, to POMPEY'S theatre, To view your statue: which, they say, sends forth A smoke, as from a furnace, black, and dreadful.
