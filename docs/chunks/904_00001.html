You have your wish: Behold there's the full number of the Game, Kings and their Pawns, Queens, Bishops, Knights and Dukes. Dukes they're called Rooks by some. Corruptively: Le roch, the word custody de La Rock, The keeper of the forts in whom both Kings Repose much confidence, and for their trust's sake, Courage and worth do well deserve those titles. Thy answer's high, see my son and daughter. Those are two pawns the black Queen, & black Bishops. Pawns argue but poor spirits, and light performents: Nor worthy of the name of my Disciples: If I had stood so nigh I would have cut That bishop's throat, but I'd have had his place; And told the Queen a love tale in her ear, Would have made her best pulse dance, there's elixir Of brain, and spirit amongst 'em. Why? would you have 'em play against themselves. That's quite against the Game (Ignatius.) Pish, I would rule myself, not observe rule. Why you you'd play a game all by yourself. I would do any thing to rule alone, 'tis rare to have the world ruled in by one. See'em anon, and view 'em in their play, Observe as in a dance they glide away. Oh with what longing will this breast be tossed, Until I see this great Game won and lost. I Never see that face, but my pity rises, When I behold so clear a Masterpiece Of heaven's art, wrought out of dust and ashes, In that not ours, but the Daughter of Heresy: My soul bleeds at mine eyes. Where should truth speak If not in such a sorrow? they're tears plainly: Beshrew me if she weep not heartily? What is my peace to her to take such pains in't? If I wander to loss, and with broad eyes Yet miss the path she ran blindfold in. (Through often exercise) why should my oversight, Though in the game that ere Christian lost, Raise the least spring of pity in her eye, 'tis doubtless a great charity, and no virtue, Could win me surer. Blessed things prevail with't, If ever goodness made a gracious promise, It is in yonder look, what little pains Would build a Fort for virtue to all memory In that sweet creature, were the groundwork firmer. It has been all my glory to be firm, In what I have professed. That is the enemy That steals your strength away, and fights against you, Disarms your soul, even in that heat of battle: Your firmness that way, makes you more infirm, For the right Christian conflict; there I spied A zealous primitive sparkle, but now slew, From your devoted eyes: Able to blow up all the Heresies That ever sat in Council with your spirit; And here comes he whose Sanctimonious breath, Can make that spark a flame: list to him (virgin) At whose first entrance Princes will prostrate: Women are weaker Vessels. By my penitence A comely presentation, and the habit To admiration reverend.
