O my Lord the house is full of halberts, and a great many Gentlemen ask for the room where Prince Iohn is? Lend me thy Apron, run and fetch a pot from the next room. Betrayed, 'swounds betrayed, by gout, by palsy, by dropsy; O brave boy, excellent blood: up, take my cloak And my hat to thy share, when I come from Kent, i'll pay Thee like a King. I thank you my Lord. Now fortune help or never: they come, and ye were a Prince as ye say ye are, ye would be ashamed to abuse a poor servant thus, but and if you were not of the blood Royal, I'd break the neck of ye down the stairs, so would I, I'd teach you to hurt prentices. Who hurt thee fellow? Prince devil or his dam, Prince Iohn they call him. Gloster I hope. I doubt not but its Skinke. Where is he? Up them stairs, take heed of him. He's in the Crown. Alas poor fellow, he hath crowned thee shrewdly. In recompense, if it be him I seek, I'll give thee his whole head to tread upon. Fellow me brother, come old Faukenbridge, Keep the stairs sheriffs, you see it waxeth dark, Take heed he slip not by you. Hang yourselves, this darkness shall convey me out, of doors I'll swim the Thames, but I'll attain Blackheath, London farewell, curse Iohn, rave Faukenbridge, Skinke 'scapes you all by twy lights privyledge. Where is he? lights, bring lights, drag out that boy. This is my cloak, my hat, my rapier, And either it was Skinke or Gloster. I know not who 'twas sir, he said he was Prince Iohn, he took away my apron and a pottle pot with him, and all to bloodied his head and face. We met him, by S. Anthony, we met him. The fire of S. Anthony confound This changing counterfeit whatsoever he be. It makes me laugh at envious greediness, Who feeds upon her own heart's bitterness. Sirrah you that were borne to cry anon, What other copesmates have you in the house? Sir, my Masters guess be none of my copesmates, Well your guess, can ye guess who they be? Marry here's a pursuivant, that this Gentleman sir Richard Faukenbridge left sick even now. Marry of God did I, thou lying knave? I am a poor boy sir, your worship may say your pleasure, our maids have had a foul hand with him, you said he would be sick: so he is with a witness. Look about Faukenbridge, here's work for you, You have some evil Angel in your shape, Go sirrah, bring us forth that Pursuivant? Gloster, thou wilt be too too venturous, Thou dost delight in those odd humours so, That much I fear they'll be thy overthrow. O O O not too fast; O I am sick, O very sick.
