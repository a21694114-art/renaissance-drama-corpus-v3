Tender eyed modesty, how it grieves at this, I'm as far off for all this, strange imposture, As at first interview, where lies our game now? You know I cannot marry by my order. I know you cannot (sir) yet you May venture on a contract. Hah! Sure you may (sir,) Without all question so far, without danger Or any stain to your vow, and that make take her: Nay do't with speed, she'll think you Mean the better too. Be not so lavish of that blessed spring, You've wasted that upon a cold occasion now, Would wash a sinful soul white by our love joys, That motion shall ne'er light upon my tongue more, Till we're contracted, than I hope your mine. In all just duty ever. Then do you question it? Pish, then you're man and wife, All but Church Ceremonies: Pray let's see it done first, She shall do reason then: Now i'll enjoy the sport, and cousen you both, My blood's game is the wages I have Worked for. Pawn, I have spoke to the Fat bishop for thee, I'll get thy absolution from his own mouth: Reach me my Chair of ease, my Chair of cozenage seven thousand pounds in women, reach me that I love a life to sit upon a bank Of Heretic gold: Oh soft and gentle (sirrah) there's a foul flaw i'th' bottom of my bum (pawn) I ne'er shall make sound soldier, but sound treacher With any he in Europe, how now? quality Thou hast the paltrest fool that ere I met with, It cannot bear one suckling villain: Mine can digest a monster without credit, A fin as weighty as an Elephant, And never wamble for't. ay, you have been used to't (sir) That's a great help, the swallow of my conscience Has but a narrow passage, you must think yet It lies i'th' penitent pipe, and will not down, If I had got seven thousand pounds by Offices And gulled down you, the boar would have been bigger. Nay, an thou provest factious, I shall hug thee, Can a poore-pocht-soft rear iniquity So rude upon thy conscience, I'm ashamed of thee, Hadst thou betrayed the witness to the black, beggared a Kingdom by dissimulation, Unjointed the fair frame of peace, and traffic Poisoned allegiance, set faith back and wrought Women's soft souls even up to the masculine malice, To pursue truth to death if the cause roused 'em, That stairs and parets as first taught to curse thee: All these and ten times troubled has this brain been parent too, they are my offsprings all. A goodly brood. Yet I can jest as lightly, Laugh and tell strange stories to Court Madames, (Daughters of my seducements) with alacrity, As high and heartily, as youth's time of innocence That never knew a sin, to shape a sorrow by; I feel no tempest, not a leaf wind stirring, To shake a fault, my conscience is be calmed rather,
