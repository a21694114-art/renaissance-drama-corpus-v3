Why so you may say, if a man send his servant As Factor into another Country, And he by any means miscarry, You may say the business of the master, Was the author of his servants misfortune. Or if a son be employed by his father, And he fall into any lewd action, you may say the father Was the author of his sons damnation. But the master is not to answer for his servants, The father for his son, nor the king for his subjects: For they purpose not their deaths, when they crave their services: Some there are that have the gift of premeditated Murder on them: Others the broken seal of Forgery, in beguiling maidens. Now if these outstrip the law, Yet they cannot escape God's punishment. War is God's Beadel. War is God's vengeance: Every man's service is the kings: But every man's soul is his own. Therefore I would have every soldier examine himself, And wash every moth out of his conscience: That in so doing, he may be the readier for death: Or not dying, why the time was well spent, Wherein such preparation was made.
