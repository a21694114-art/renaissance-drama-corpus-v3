I am as you are, mortal; 'tis my skill In Physic, and experience in the rare Virtue of herbs, that wrought this miracle; No Divinity, or power in me. Amyntas, when shall we requite this kindness? Yes, I had almost Forgot it, I would fain have seen her too. You cannot now, because to night she lodged With one Laurinda. O my Urania, welcome, Amyntas bids thee so, I that till now Was not Amyntas: come my joy, and meet me Full of our happiness!
