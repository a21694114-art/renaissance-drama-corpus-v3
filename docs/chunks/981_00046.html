I pray you, Forsooth, or sweet madam, or what you please; Where is my Niece? Will you believe me sir? you may: For 'tis Truth, as I have any; And before this worthy Gentleman; I never saw your Niece in my life; only I have heard she is a pretty Gentlewoman: likely to make a good Match, for which I told my brother of her, and would have treated with you for her, could I have spoken with you as I wished by two or three Messages. But whither my brother has got her, or where he, or she is of my own knowledge, I cannot say directly. she mocks me to my face all this while. Well goodwife, Mistress, madam— Well my Lord Innkeepers second Son: does your Provender prick you? Prick madam: I tell thee thou Thing, made up of Chippings, broken Bear, Candle-ends, and sisting of Sea coal. out you Currycomb. Forbear sweet Lady, let him be mad by himself. I will be so revenged— How pray? He had been better to have killed a Man, ravished ● Virgin; nay, done the most dangerous contempt that law could devise to punish, than if I take him to suffer under my revenge. Ha, ha, ha. I'll muster up my Constables, and send out a privy search immediately.— What think you of your Brother's success, now madam? Much the better, that it vexes him so: Scurvy foul unouthed fellow. Look you now madam. See who here comes. Sister fall down, and adore me for my great achievement. Humphrey kneel down to her that she may dub thee for thy service. Never did the best nosed Dogs, that ever were coached for their goodness, hunt more truly, take more bravely, and carry away more cleanly, than we have done this dainty piece of flesh here. Sister kiss her, and be better acquainted: she is mine own flesh, I'll uphold it. she is a Holdup herself, if I mistake not her name. Being your flesh brother, her nearest affinity of blood runs in my veins. Therefore with a sister's love I embrace you, and bid you welcome. Mine Uncle will by right wood I fear me. But I'll ne'er greet for that sir, while I have your love. I know it is she by her tongue, though I never heard her before. Nor ever fear sweet sister, we shall be all friends shortly. I would be glad and 'twere so. Sister come hither. Now hear and admire my wit; as well as my Fortune. Humphrey come and take thy share of my sister's wonder. I hope I performed my duty. Which we must not see unrewarded sister. No: I mean to give him my Maid, and a hundred Marks with her, besides all she has about her. I am made for ever. I thank your languishing Ladyship. Well said Howdee: for my Ladyship is e'en at the last gasp. I am to be Divorced within this half hour. But your proceedings brother? How did she receive you at first?
