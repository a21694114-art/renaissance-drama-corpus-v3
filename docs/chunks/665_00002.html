Her body lighter than the feathered crest. Another powtes and scoules, and hangs the lip, Even as the bankrupt credit of her husband, Cannot equal her with honours livery, What doth she care, if for to deck her brave, he's carried from a Gatehouse to his grave. Another in a railing pulppet key, Draws through her nose the accent of her voice, And in the presence of her good man Goat, Cries fie, now fie upon these wicked men. That use such beastly and inhuman talk: When being in private, all her studies warn, To make him enter into Capricorn. Another as she goes, treads a Canary pace, jets it so fine, and minces so demure, As mistress Bride upon her marriage day: Her heels are Cork, her body Atlas, Her Beauty bought, her soul an Atomus. Another with a spleen devoured face, Her eyes as hollow as Anatomy: Her tongue more venom than a Serpent's sting, Which when it wags within her chap-faln jaws, Is noise more horrid than a cry of hounds With open mouths, pursuing of their game, Wants she but rich attire or costly diet, With her the Devil can near live in quiet. Yet these are weaker vessels, heaven doth know, Lay on them ought but ease, you do them wrong: They are as weak as water, and indeed as strong, And then like mighty ships, when pellets sink, To them lay more men, she'll never shrink. Mistress, that face wants a fresh Gloss. Prithee dib it in well Bos. Pigmaleon, Pigmaleon, I conjure thee appear, To work, to work, make more Marble Ingles, Nature, thou art a fool, Art is above thee: Belzebub, paint thy face, there's some will love thee. Rare, Mistress, here's a cheek like a Chameleon or a blazing Star: you shall here me blaze it, here's two saucers sanguine in a sable field, pomegranate, a pure pendant, Ready to drop out of the stable, a pin and web argent in hair de Roy. And a fools head in the Crest. In the Crest? oh sweet Vermilion mistress! 'tis pity the Vermilion Worms should eat thee, i'll set it with precious stones and ye will. Enough sweet Boss, throw a little water to spurt's face and let's away. Hold up, so sir now away: oh Mistress your scantling, most sweet mistress most derydent star. Then most rydent stars fair fall ye. Nay sure 'tis the Moon herself, for there's her man and her Dog before. I sir, but the man is not in the moon, & my bushis before me, ergo not at my back, et ergo, not moon sir. What's your will sir? That you would leave us. Leave you, zounds sir, we scorn their companies, come, they are still, do not open to them, we have no Coneys to catch.
