Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

She is asleep, master shall I wake her? O no Gunophilus there let her sleep, And let us pray that she may be recured. Stesias thou pitiest her that loves thee not The words we told thee Stesias were too true. Never did Iphicles dissemble yet, Believe me Stesias she hath been untrue. Yet will you slay me with your slanderous words Did you not all swear for her chastity. It was her subtle wit that made us swear, For Stesias know she showed love to us all, And severally sent for us by this swain, And unto me he brought such honey lines, As overcomed, I flew unto her bower. Who when I came, swore she loud me alone. willing me to deny the words I spoke, And she at night would meet me in the grove. Thus meaning simply, lo I was betrayed, Gunophilus brought me a bloody cloth, Saying for my love she was almost slain, And when I came she used me as this swain, Protesting love, and pointing me this place, And by this bearer I received a ring, And many a loving word that drew me forth, O that a woman should dissemble so. She then for swore Learchus and this swain, Saying that Iphicles was only hers, Whereat I promised to deny my words. And she to meet me at Enepius' banks, Wert thou the messenger unto them all. I was, and all that they have said is true, She love not you, not them, but me alone, How oft hath she run up and down the lawns. Calling aloud where is Gunophilus. Ah how my heart swells at these miscreants words. Come let us leave him in this pensive mood. Fret, Stesias fret, while we dance on the plain. Such fortune happen to incredulus swains. Sweet is a single life, Stesias farewell. Go life, fly soul, go wretched Stesias, Cursed be Utopia for Pandora's sake, Let wild boars with their tusks blow up my lawns, Devouring wolves come shake my tender lambs, Drive up my goats unto some steepy rock, And let them fall down headlong in the sea, She shall not live, nor thou Gunophilus, To triumph in poor Stesias overthrow. Stay shepherd, stay. Hurt not Pandora lovely Stesias. What means my love to look so pale and wan. For thee base strumpet am I pale and wan. Speak mildly, or I'll make thee crabbed swain. Take her again, and love her Stesias. Not for Utopia, no, not for the world. Ah canst thou frown on her that looks so sweet? Have I offended thee? I'll make amends. And what canst thou demand more at her hand? To slay herself that I may live alone. Flint hearted shepherd thou deservest her not. If thou be Ioue convey her from the earth, And punish this Gunophilus her man. O Ioue, let this be my punishment, to live still with Pandora.
