an't please your Majesty, a Rascal that swagger'd with me last night: who if alive, and ever dare to challenge this Glove, I have sworn to take him a box o' th' ere: or if I can see my Glove in his cap, which he swore as he was a Soldier he would wear (if alive) I will strike it out sound. What think you Captain Fluellen, is it fit this soldier keep his oath. He is a Craven and a Villain else, and't please your Majesty in my conscience. It may be, his enemy is a Gentleman of great sort quite from the answer of his degree. Fellow, and see there be no harm between them. Go you with me, Uncle of Exeter. I warrant it is to Knight you, Captain.
