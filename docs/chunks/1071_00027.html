Help, help, and if you be a gentleman. Forbear this rudeness, he that touches her, Draws against me. Against you sir, apply thou, that shall be tried. Help, help, part them help. With patience hear her speak. Now Trot, now Granam, what canst thou say for thyself: what Luce hear be patient and put up them, she must not see the end. Than trince of all sides, if we come for counsel, Let us with patience hear it: Then first to me. You would prevent young Chartley's marriage, you shall: hark in your ear. It pleaseth me. You-forestall Gratiana's wedding, 'tis but thus. I'll do't, You would enjoy Luce as your wife, and lie with her tomorrow night. Hark in your care. Fiat. Away, you shall enjoy him, you are married, Luce away, you shall see Chartly discarded from Gratiana, Sencer be gone, and if I fail in any of these or the rest, I lay myself open to all your displeasures.
