Time was. Well friar Bacon, you spent your seven years' study well that can make your Head speak but two words at once, Time was: yea marry, time was when my master was a wise man, but that was before he began to make the Brazenhead, you shall lie while your arme ache and your Head speak no better: well I will watch and walk up and down, and be a Perepatetian and a Philosopher of Aristotle's stamp, what a fresh noise, take thy pistols in hand Miles. Time is past.
