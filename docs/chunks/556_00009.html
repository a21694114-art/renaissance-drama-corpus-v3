Your will sir. You have left many Countries behind you in seeking your friend Lisander, and yet you cannot find him. True sir. I have seen much gold lying upon Lombard's stalls, and could never finger penny of it. Very well. Nay, 'twas not well sir. What conclude you then? That you were best sit down, and see what you have got by your journey. I have seen a face as beautiful as heaven. That's nothing, a prisoner sees the face of heaven itself, when he looks but out at the prison-gate, i'll stand to't, a man were as good be hanged, so a meet a handsome hangman, & a strong rope, as be in love. Your reason for that. Marry this sir, hanging is end of all troubles, and love the beginning: Nay further, I think a Lover cannot be saved, for he is of all religions. Your proof for that. This; he thinks with the Atheist there's no God but his Mistress, with the Infidel no heaven but her smiles, with the Papist no Purgatory but her frowns, and with the family of love, holds it lawful to lie with her, though she be another man's wife. So sir, what follows? Servingmen sir, the Master goes in before his wife, and the Servingman follows his master. Sirrah forbear, I must meditate. As the Usurer before he parts with money, meditates upon the assurance. If Violetta's presence ha'not quenched The memory of all things but herself, I should be more familiar with that face. I have left my country to seek my friend. And I my country and my friend for love. And in the search of him have lost myself In the strange Region of a woman's eye. In love, and in Arcadia. As much as heaven transcends the humble earth, So towers her praise her face differs as far From others, as a glow-worm from a star. She is a Princess that my soul affects. And rich. Half heir unto this Dukedom. And she were whole heir to the four moral virtues, 'twere nothing: when shall I see the time that men will love for virtue, or a rich heir marry a poor wench without a portion? never I think. Had not my friend Lisander. What of me? Left me in Thrace. We had never met in love, His syllables betray him. I arrest you. At whose suit? Not at his Tailors in any case, for there's no greater stitch to a younger brothers conscience, then to pay for a suit of apparel when 'tis worn out. Lisander or his ghost? Demetrius, Or some illusive tenant in his shape? Unkind, why didst thou lean my company? For that which made the amorous Gods leave heaven, ●or love: but why is Demetrius thus disguised? For that which would make a jackanapes a Monkey, and he could get it: a tail. Peace rogue. Why wag, is thy master in love?
