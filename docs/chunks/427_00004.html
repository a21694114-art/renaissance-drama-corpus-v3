I love the sport well, but I shall as soon quarrel at it, as any man in England: you are afraid if you see the Bear lose, are you not? I indeed Sir. That's meat and drink to me now: I have seen Sackerson lose, twenty times, and have taken him by the Chain: but (I warrant you) the women have so cried and shrekt at it, that it past: But women indeed, cannot abide'em, they are very ill-favoured rough things. Well Sir.
