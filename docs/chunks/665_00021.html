Neither i'faith, kneel not, rise, rise, I pray You both confess you have offended me. We do, we have. Then for this offence, be this your doom, Tully must die, but not till fates decree To cut your vital thread, or Terentia Find in her heart to be your Deathes-man? Faith the Fates may do as they may, but Terentia will never find in her heart to kill him, she'll first bury him quick. The like is doomed to fair Terentai, How say you both, are ye content? My thoughts are plunged in admiration. But can your honour bury such a wrong? I can, I can, here Tully, take Terentia, Live many happy years in faithful love, This is no more than friendship's laws allow, Think me thyself another Cicero. 'tTwere better my Lord, you did persuade her to think you another Cicero, so you might claim some interest in her now and then. That I would claim with you, fair Lady, hark in your ear, nay, I must conclude with you. you'll not bite my Lord? No, of my faith my Lady. Thus far my love, our hopes have good success, One storm more past, my griefs were much the less. Friendship itself hath been more prodigal, Then a bold face could beg upon a friend. Why then, there's a bargain. Strike hands upon the same, I am yours to command. I'll love with ye, i'll lie with ye, i'll love with all my heart, With all my strength, with all my power and virtue: Sealed and delivered in the presence of us: Marcus, Tullius, and Cicero. Then you deliver this as your act and deed? I do, and seal it with this— Why well said, 'tis done, see, we begin but now, And are as ready to go to Church as you: What needs further ceremony? Yes, a little matrimony. I Lady, come Tully and Terentia, One day shall shine on both our Nuptials, Fear not, i'll quench the fire of your Father's heat With my consent. I prithee appoint the time. About a week hence love. Oh, 'tis too intolerable long. Then four days. Four days is four times four & twenty hours that's too long too. We cannot sooner be ready: Yes and unready too, in a day and a half. Well then two days. Till than we'll feed on conceit, Tully thank me but for your company, I would not tarry so long: come Tully since we shall be married all at one time, we'll go to bed so, and he shall be master of the Cockpit, that bids his Gossips first. Nay quick Graccus, lest our hour forestall us, i'll in and deal for your disguise, tarry thou, & give mine host a share of our intent, marry charge him to keep it as secret as his Garbage. He undoes our drift and clothes the fool in sackcloth during his life.
