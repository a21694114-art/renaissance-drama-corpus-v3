Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

See where he comes, occasion favours thee. God bless my sons, rise rise, & speak to me: Have you not some Venetian friends unkind. Their friendship, with thy fortunes took an end. I thought on less, but why is Lelio so discontent: Tut give over man, the stream will run with us at last. I come to thank my father for his love, And pray him by those arms he honours most, To deign my dutiful and kind adieu. Why whether goes my son so suddenly? To Venice father, to redeem your banishment. I am not banished, you wrong my fames? Living for him, I live at liberty. But Lelio must not live, unless he be resolved To high to Venice, that thou mayest return. These are thy children Brishio, these exceed In kindness towards thee, and towards me. Oh honours to your father and to me: Let me embrace you for your courtesy. Brishio far well, accept a thousand thanks. Why, who wills thee hence? Father, they will him hence, that will be actors In his Tragedy, unless he to Venice go speedily, And quit thee from this exile with his head, These are not Brishios' sons, bids Lelio hence: These are not Brishios' sons that draw their swords: Thou art my son, these two are fortunes slaves; Avaunt vain boys, come not in my sight, By heaven and heavens adorning sun, These are no sons of mine that sin so much. Ashamed, we beg a pardon at thy hands, Ashamed, I beg a pardon at thy hands, In rendering nature, that hath lost thy power, To breed such manslayers in an honest stock. O bend not those knees, to which nature bends. O Lelio sue our pardons, plead for us, Our judgements were disgraced by our loves. Rise father of kind sons, sons kind to father, Brother's unkind, in kindness to your brother: I kind to churlish of my kind, Do yield kind death, to save your lives Swear puytence, lewd boys, Submit to Lelio humbly on your knee, Else will I never bless you graceless youths. We humbly crave submission of our brother. And thou my son, nay more than son my friend Here plight thy faith to bide and stay with me, Or I shall think my love but spent in vain. I vow to be as sequest to my Father, And with my heart I do forgive my brothers. Then will I now incorporate these Lads, And hug them in my arm, and hold them dear. Now will I hold a festival to day, For Lelio, Zepherius and Orphinio: And I with favour of my noble Lord, Will first give order to my soldiers, And then return to Brishio in his tent. Be careful of thy charge my noble son, I will not hinder thee in virtuous things, But let us meet again before we march, For I have many things for to impart to thee.
