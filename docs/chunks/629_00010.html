This my discourse intends, Were husband's Saints, some wives would have close friends. Some bade perhaps, but— Husbands but for't, true, wilt? prithee wilt thou? What? ud's Hartlings do? Do what? Love-tricks quickly, wilt thou prithee. No, I prize my credit. Credit? life a man, What talks of credit? art not known a strumpet? You do me wrong. Damnation, what a gloss, This gilded copper, Diamond of glass, What strange show it makes? i'll give you a color Your face was near worse painted, then i'll paint Your wainscot credit, you are a private bit, Kept for some great made dives. Red hot scandal Suck the native colour of your souls As it hath scorched thy credit, shriveled bawd, Sponge, Lemon-pill, and more irrelishable Than o'erdried Stockfish, fie. Upon thy shame. Thy husband, O thy husband's bow-legged fame Death I shall hate thee. O for honour do. Swear thou art my strumpet. I'll endure that to. Shalt not deny't, heart, canst not. Yes, I can. Do't for thy life, thy soul, base courtesan. Farewell, if friendship be at such a rate, Before i'll purchased, i'll live still in hate. Heart, a new fashion, A Lady poor, beautiful and chaste? clean From the bias of Custom: to be poor, painted, And proud is as common in Genoa, as felt and feather In the fortunate Island: but chaste and poor, As singular and rare, as Conscience with the Anabaptist. Come hither Boy, didst never owe me service Yes Madam, more than ever I paid you honestly, and were a not my Master. Thy Master, who? Horatio, he that puts chastity to the quick, & honour to the stab, but i'll show him the nature of a right Frenchman, deny him homage, for swear allegiance, and come behind hand in cash keeping, & with his own purse maintain you against him. Thy purpose briefly. In bald sentences, your noted wrongs are pitied. Pitied, by whom? By me, lodgings prepared and diet, which I humbly tender your honour, as first payment of the ancient debt, my service owes you. But is the lodging private? At mine own mothers, and though I say't, she keeps an honest house, though my father be a Citizen. Of what profession? Neither fool nor Physician, but an ingenious Pothecary. And what resort? Very civil and most quiet resort, patients, the house is set round with patients twice or thrice a day, and because they'll be sure not to want drink, every one brings his own water in an Urinal with him. Doth a use physic too? that's beyond his warrant. O Lord Madam, better men than he strain courtesy with their warrants in this age, besides, you being musical, shall meet much content, for my mother lodges three or four Knights & ladies' Daughters that practise music and needlework, and will no doubt be exceeding proud of your company, wilt please you walk?
