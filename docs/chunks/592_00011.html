Good child, now thou art passed Lapides Stones, proceed to the cases Nous, say you next Nous, where's your lesson Nous. I am in a verb forsooth. Say on for sooth say say. A verb is a part of speech declined with mood and tense and betokeneth doing as Amo I love, How many kind of verbs are there? 2. Personal and impersonal, Of verbs personals, how many kinds. Five, Active Passive Neuter Deponent and Common. A Verb Active endeth in O and betokeneth to do as Amo I love and by putting to R it may be a passive as Amor I am loved. Very good child, now learn to know the Deponent and common: Say you slip. Cedant arma togae, concedant lauria linguae. What part of speech is lingua, inflect, inflect. Singulariter, nominativo Hec lingua. Why is lingua the Feminine gender? Forsooth because it is the Feminine gender.
