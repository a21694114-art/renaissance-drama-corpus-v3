See now whether pure fear and entire cowardice, doth not make thee wrong this virtuous gentlewoman to close with us: is she of the wicked, is thine hostess here of the wicked, or is thy boy of the wicked or honest Bardolfe whose zeal burns in his nose of the wicked? Answer thou dead elm, answer. The fiend hath pricked down Bardolfe irrecoverable, and his face is Lucifer's privy kitchen, where he doth nothing but roast mault-worms, for the boy there is a good angel about him, but the devil blinds him too. For the women. For one of them she's in hell already, and burns poor souls: for th'other I own her money, and whether she be damned for that I know not. No I warrant you. No I think thou art not, I think thou art quit for that, mary there is another indictment upon thee, for suffering flesh to be eaten in thy house contrary to the law, for the which I think thou wilt howl.
