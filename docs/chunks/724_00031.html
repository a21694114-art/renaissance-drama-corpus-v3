Yes truly, would I had near seen his eyes, ha has undone me and himself and his children, & there he lives at home & sings, & hoights, & Revels among his drunken companions, but, I warrant you, where to get a penny to put bread in his mouth, he knows not: and therefore if it like your Worship, I would entreat your letter, to the honest Host of the bell in Waltham, that I may place my child under the protection of his Tapster, in some settled course of life. Iasper. God's hand is great in this, I do for give him, Yet I am glad he's quiet, where I hope He will not bite again: boy bring the body And let him have his will, if that be all.
