Oh unhappy day, False, credulous, inconstant Abigal! But let 'em go: And Ithimore, from hence ne'er shall she grieve me more with her disgrace; ne'er shall she live to inherit aught of mine, Be blessed of me, nor come within my gates, But perish underneath my bitter curse Like Cain by Adam, for his brother's death. Oh master. Ithimore, entreat not for her, I am moved, And she is hateful to my soul and me: And lest thou yield to this that I entreat, I cannot think but that thou hat'st my life. Who I, master?
