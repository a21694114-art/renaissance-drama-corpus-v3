But now and she speak she spoils all, or if he call her by my Mistress his name, hast thou no: tricks to enjoin them both to silence till they come sure. Phaw, that's a stale one, she shall speak to him in her own accent, he shall call her by her own name leaving out the Bawd, yet she shall violently believe he loves her, and he shall confidently believe the same which he requires, and she but presents. Fall off, she comes. Iaruis. Here I have discovered him, 'tis he by his coughs, remember your instructions, & use few words, for though till tonight you knew it not, and will he married early in the morning, to prevent a vintner's Widow, that lays claim to him. Iaruis. Good old man, I know him by his tongue. Is she come? is she come Iaruis? Ask her if she wood live sir, she walks a loose yonder. We shall cozen all her wooers. Nay, amongst all of you, we'll cozen one great one, that had laid a pernicious plot this night, with a cluster of his roaring friends to surprise her, carry her down to the water side, pop her in at Puddle-dock, and carry her to graves's end in a pair of Oars. What, what is his name I prithee. he's a Knight abounding in deeds of charity, his name Sir Nicholas Name. And wood he pop her in at Puddle-dock? And he could but get her down there. By my troth we shall pop him fairly, where is she? where is she? Ha, do you not perceive a fellow walk up and down muffled yonder? There is something walks. That fellow has dogged us all the way, and I fear all is frustrate. Not I hope man. This 'tis to be in love, if I do not dwindle. I know him now. 'tis none of Sir Nicholas spies, is't? He serves him. He wonot murder me, will he? He shall not touch you, only I remember this afternoon, this Fellow by what he had gathered by caves dropping, or by frequent observation, asked me privately if there were no meeting betwixt you and my Mistress tonight in this place, for a Widow he said he knew you were to meet. Good. Now I handsomely threw dust in's eyes, and yet kept the plot swift a foot too, I told him you were here to meet a Widow too, whom you long loved, but would not let her know't till this afternoon, naming to him one of my Ants, a Widow by Fleet-ditch, her name is Mistress Grey, and she keeps diverse Gentlewomen lodgers. Good again. To turn the scent then, and to cheat Inquisition the more ingeniously. And to bob Sir Nicholas most neatly. be sure all this night studably in the hearing of any that ye shall but suspect to be within hearing to call her nothing but Mistress Coote. Or Widow Coote.
