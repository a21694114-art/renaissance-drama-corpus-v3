For the man himself Is such a poor and miserable thing— But such another word, and I take off My blessing: how now Iane? , I feare He is in earnest, marry me to my grave, o that you shall haue my consent, oh do not Enforce me to be guilty of a false Vow, both to Heaven and Angels; on my knees— Humble your heart, rise and correct your sullenness, I am resolved, would you be sacrificed To an unthrift, that will dice away his skin, Rather than want to stake at Ordinaries? Consume what I have gathered at a breakfast, Or morning's draught? and when you ha' teemed for him Turn Semptress to find milk and clouts for babies: Foot stockings, to maintain him in the Compter? Or if this fail, erect a bandy Citadel, Well manned, which fortified with demi-canon Tobacco pipes, may raise you to a fortune, Together with the trail— Oh my cruel stars! Star me no stars, i'll have my will— One minute hath ruined all my hope, Milisent Was cruel thus to mock me. unde— You hear, now he talks. But I ha' found the way, 'tis but procuring Acquaintance with the foreman of the jury, The session's bell-wether, he leads the rest Like sheep when he makes a gap, they follow In huddle to his sentence. Speak to him sir. God save you sir Iohn Belfare. I am a little serious— do not trouble me. D''ee not know me? I neither know, nor care for you, unless You can be silent. I me your neighbour— Master Doctor— Away fool. No sir, a Physician. A Physician? can you cure my daughter? ay sir, where is she? Cannot you find her out by art? a good Physician, should be acquainted with the Stars: Prithee erect a figure, grave Astronomer, shalt ha' the minute she departed; turn Thy Ephemerides a little, i'll lend Thee Ptolemy, and a nest of learned rabbis To judge by: tell me whither she be a live, Or dead, and thou shalt be my Doctor, i'll Give thee a round per Annum pension, And thou shalt kill me for it. He has a strange De lyrium. ay sir. A Vertigo in's head. In his head. What says the Raven? He says, you have two hard words in your head sir. Have you forgot me sir, I was but late Familiar to your knowledge. Ha'your pardon gentle sir I know you now, Impute it to my grief, 'thath almost made me Forget myself. I come to visit you. And cannot but be sorry, to behold You thus afflicted. Doctor I am sick, I'm very sick at heart loss of my daughter I fear, will make me mad, how long d''ee think Man's nature able to resist it, can Your love or art prescribe your friend a Cordial? No, no, you cannot. Sir, be comforted. we have our manly virtue given us, To exercise in such extremes as these.
