I should rather Doubt that you will not marry. Madam by heaven, as fully I am resolved to marry now, And will to, if you do not hinder it, As ever lover was, only because The World has taken notice of some passage Twixt you and me, and then to satisfy My sweet heart here, who poor soul is a feared, To have some public disgrace put upon her, I do require some small thing at your hands. But see what thought the Bridegroom takes, my conscience knows now, this is a most preposterous match, yet for the commodity, we wink at all inconveniency. My Lord, my Lord. ump, ump, ump, I beshrew you for waking of me, now shall I have such a fit of coughing, hum, hum— Oh hapless wife, that shall have thee, that either must let thee sleep continually, or be kept waking herself by the cough. Marry, e'en God be with her, grief will not help it, ump, ump, ump. A most excellent spouse.
