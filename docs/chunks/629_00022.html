O would a were, then should not Genoa's Crown, For want of strong supportance be priest down. I not enough, that like a harmeles Prince Spending my hours in Contemplation, I let you hold the rains of government. Unfit for me, my father hath a deep And searching judgement that can brush and sweep such idle brained and antic Parasites, Forth of his presence, pray your absence, I can but smile to sue how Protheus like, They turn the shape of their discourse & project, Thinking to lead me in an idle maze, after their folly, well, I'll temporize, and note the issue, come my Lords let's in, His humour's grounded, and like subtle fire: The more suppressed, it mounts so much the hire. Why so, this jest came smoothly of and was not soiled in the working That boy is worth his weight in pearl, dist mark what a tale of a Cock and a Bull, he told my father whilst I made thee and the rest away, by a bill of Conveyance at his back? And I did simple Knights service in persuading the Duke the boy was lunatic. 'twas admirable, doth not this jest deserve to be chronicled? No by my troth, yet I must needs say, some as bad have been, for howsoever our practice pass currant with your father for the present, our villainies must needs break forth, they are so notorious and public. No matter let 'em, have not we brains? brains and they be well minced are sauce for any meat, let mine uncle turn Turxe and break forth, let the whole town turn cuckold and blow their horns in our disgrace, I have brains, let the Sun and the seven Stars be opposed, I have brains for that too my present wit shall give 'em all the lie in their throats, and may maintain it at pocket, dagger, and pistol when I have done. A mourner boy? what solemn funeral, Hath hung that sable livery on thy back? Her death my Lord that hath cut out the like, For both your honours, the Countess is deceased. Then will my uncle be a heavy mourner. But how the manner of her death? I'll tell your honour that hereafter and give you the ground of an admirable jest. And we play not true moles and work it out of the ground, let me die of the green-sickness. The ground then comes? No I'll tell you that in private, the life of a jest thrives in the first revealing, it concerns the manner of your aunt's death and a law-trick of your uncle. Come to the solemn rites, and weep at least those being ended we'll receive i' jest. Solemn Music to a funeral song the Hearse borne over the stage, Duke Lurdo, Polymetes, Angelo, Iulio, Horatio and mourners etc.
