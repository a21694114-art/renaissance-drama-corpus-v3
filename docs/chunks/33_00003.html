Why dost thou not know that every pedled In every tryfull must be a meddler specially in women's tryflynges Those use we chief above all things Which things to see if ye be disposed Behold what ware here is disclosed This gear showeth itself in such beauty That each man thinketh it saith come buy me Look were yourself can like to be chooser Yourself shall make price though I be loser Is here nothing for my father Palmer Have ye not a want on in a corner For your walking to holy places By cryste I have herd of as strange cases Who liveth in love or love would win Even at this pack he must begin Where is right many a proper token Of which by name part shall be spoken Gloves, pins, combs, glasses unspotted Pomanders, hooks, and lasses knotted broaches, rings, and all manner beads Lace round and flat for women's heads Nedyls, thread thymbell, shers, and all such knacks Where lovers be no such things lacks Sypers swathbondes rybandes and sleeve lace's Gyrdyls, knives, purses, and pyncases. Do women buy their pyncases of you. Ye that they do I make god a vow So mote I thrive then for my part I be shrew thy knaves naked heart For making my wife is pyncase so wide The pins fall out they can not abide Great pins must she have one or other If she lose one she will find an other Wherein I find cause to complain New pins to her pleasure and my pain Sir ye seem well seen in women's causes I pray you tell me what causeth this That women after their arising Be so long in their appareling Forsooth women have many lets And they be masked in many nets As frontlettes, fyllettes, parlettes, and barcelettes And then their bonnets and their poynettes By these lets and nets the let is such That speed is small when haste is much. An other cause why they come not forward Which maketh them daily to draw backward And yet is a thing they can not forbear The trimming and pynning up their gear specially their fydling with the tail pin And when they would have it prick in If it chance to double in the cloth Then be they wode and sweareth an oath Till it stand right they will not forsake it Thus though it may not yet would they make it But be ye sure they do but defer it For when they would make it oft times mar it But prick them and pin them as nyche, as ye will And yet will they look for pynning still So that I durst hold you a joint Ye shall never have them at a fall point Let women's matters pass and mark mine What ever their points be, these points be fine Wherefore if ye be willing to buy Ley down money, come of quickly. Nay by my troth we be like friars We are but beggars we be no buyers
