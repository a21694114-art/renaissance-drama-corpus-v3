Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Yet had he lived, when the old Sibyl, Presented her divine Manuscripts, to The dull Roman; he would have scolded with her, Unless his Pamphlets had attained the first Acceptance. True, for every Poet thinks himself The best Poet in the world. And that satire not the worst; wherein He chides Women, for wearing their half-ruffs, Which pinned behind trans situates the face, Or makes'em look, like Ianus with two faces. A just exception: for going hastily To kiss his whore; he could not find her Mouth. Why sure her breath was strong enough To direct him to it. Yet I have heard nothing, but what deserves More pity, than anger. Now when he hath provided some high toy For the Press; he thinks on dedication, straight chooseth one of the faction; who must Not Patronize, but buy what he makes vendable, With praise in the Epistle. Can you deny this Cousen Satirist? And nothing makes Learning so cheap; but that Every writer sells his works. Nay let's follow; and worry him to pieces. Our ruin is contrived above. If our Master prove unkind, the Planets Govern ill: For our gratitude, and care, Deserves more constancy. Looks he so strangely on ye? As if the object were but new to him. And his own heart unsettled in his breast. Is his violence so soon tired? survey The Register of your own deeds. Speak Sir, Have you so engrossed his ears, as if their Organ, were yours, not his. Confined 'em to Your own tongue: and so deprived the sorrowful, The grieved in heart, of an easy audience? never. Since you have shared the Duke's prerogative, And by his love, held opposition. At such great advantage. did you e'er slight With cheap regard, those of high, and Noble birth? My soul abhors such tyranny. Have those who wear th'eternals Livery Bought their wages of ye? Or have they found Bold, and skilful flattery, more helps Advancement; then deep and modest Learning? Never, since my distinction was of power, To help its choice. In nice trial, or evidence of Law, Hath Custom (which only gives us hope Of certainty in justice) been traduced By your obscure help? Never. Hath the desolate Widow scared mercy From your eyes, with her old ruined beauty, (For grief was never amorous) or hath The torn Beggar too soon dismissed your charity Because not giddy enough to delight Wantonness. Never. Then if our great Master withdraw his love; The weight of sufferance cannot bruise ye; For the whole world will share i'th' burden. From whom is this sir? From my Lord Marquise de Loretta. I humbly kiss his hand. Now luck flatter me but once, and I am made— 'tis short, pray heaven it be sweet, or I'll ne'er love The Proverb. Sir, have you ever been in service, Under any eminent Commander? Never yet.
