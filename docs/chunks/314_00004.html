But Ida thou art coy. And why dread King? In that you will dispraise so sweet A thing, as love, had I my wish. What then? Then would I place his arrow here, His beauty in that face. Yea they shall part us, but we first must meet, And woe, and win, and yet the world not seeet. Yea there's the wound, & wounded with that thought So let me die: for all my drift is nought.
