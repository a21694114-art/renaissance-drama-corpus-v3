Cupid if a God thou art, Transfix this Monsters stubborn heart. But if all thy shafts be flown, And thy quiver empty grown, Here be Ladies that have eyes Can furnish thee with new supplies. Yet winged Archer do not shoot at all, 'Tis pity that he should so nobly fall. Heigh hoe— 'Tis a fine morning this as I have seen, And a most early Spring— but daughter Merda, Why Merda I say, why daughter Merda, what, Have not the Fleas yet made a breakfast of you?
