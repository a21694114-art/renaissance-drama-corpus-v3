I was nigh no feathers since I came from my bed. No sir, it was a hair that was fall from your head. My men come when it please them. By your leave.. What is that? Your gown was foul spotted with the foot of a gnat. Their master to offend they are nothing afeard. What now? A lousy hair from your mastership's beard. And sir for Nurse's sake pardon this one offence. We shall not after this show the like negligence. I pardon you this once, and come sing near the worse. How like you the goodness of this gentleman nurse? God save his mastership that so can his men forgive, And I will hear them sing ere I go, by his leave. Mary and thou shalt wench, come we two will dance. Nay I will by mine own self foot the song perchance. Go to it sirs lustily. Pipe up a merry note, Let me hear it played, I will foot it for a groat. Now nurse take this same letter here to thy mistress. And as my trust is in thee ply my business. It shallbe done. Who made it? I wrote it each whit Then needs it no mending. No, no. No I know your wit. I warrant it well. It shall be delivered. But if ye speed, shall I be considered? Whough, dost thou doubt of that? What shall I have? An hundred times more than thou canst devise to crave. Shall I have some new gear? for my old is all spent. The worst kitchen wench shall go in Lady's raiment. Yea? And the worst drudge in the house shall go better Than your mistress doth now. The I trudge with your letter. Now may I repose me: Custance is mine own. Let us sing and play 〈◊〉 toward it may be known. But are you sure, that your 〈◊〉 is well enough? I wrote it myself. Then sing we to dinner. WHo took thee this letter Margerie Mumblecrust? A lusty gay bachelor took it me of trust, And if ye seek to him he will low your doing. Yea, but where learned he that manner of 〈◊〉? If to sue to him, you will any pains take, He will have you to his wife (he sayth) for my sake. Some wise gentleman belike. I am bespoken: And I thought verily this had been some token From my dear spouse Gavin Goodluck, whom when him please God luckily send home to both our hearts ease. A jolly man it is I wot well by report, And would haue you to him for marriage ret: Best open the writing, and see what it doth speak. At this time nurse I will neither read ne break. He promised to give you a whole peck of gold. Perchance lack of a pyrite when it shall be all told. I would take a gay rich husband, and I were you.
