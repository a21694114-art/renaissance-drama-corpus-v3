Sweet beauty, I could sigh as fast as you, But that I know that, which I weep to know, His fortunes should be such he dare not show His open presence. I know he loved me dearly, dearly, I: And since I cannot live with him, I die. Fore heaven, her speech falters, look she swoons. Convey her up into her private bed. I hope she'll live. If not Antonio's dead, the fool will follow too, he, he, he, Now works the scene; quick observation scud To coat the plot, or else the path is lost: My very self am gone, my way is fled: ay, all is lost, if Mellida is dead. Alberto, I am kind, Alberto, kind. I am sorry for thy couz, i'faith I am. Go, take him down, and bear him to his father: Let him be buried, look ye, I'll pay the priest. Please you to admit his father to the Court? No. Please you to restore his lands & goods again? No. Please you vouchsafe him lodging in the city? God's fut, no, thou odd uncivil fellow: I think you do forget sir, where you are. I know you do forget sir, where you must be. You are too malapert, i'faith you are. Your honour might do well to Peace Parasite, thou burr, that only sticks Unto the nap of greatness. Away with that same yelping cur, away. ay, I am gone: but mark, Piero, this. There is a thing called scourging Nemesis. God's neakes he has wrong, that he has: and 'Sfoot, and I were as he, I would bear no coals, law I, I begin to swell, puff. How now fool, fop, fool? Fool, fop, fool? Marry muff. I pray you, how many fools have you seen go in a suit of Satin? I hope yet, I do not look a fool i'faith: a fool? God's bones, I scorn't with my heel. S'neaks, and I were worth but three hundred pound a year more, I could swear richly: nay, but as poor as I am, I will swear the fellow hath wrong. Young Galeatzo? ay, a proper man. Florence, a goodly city: it shall be so. I'll marry her to him instantly. Then Genoa mine, by my Maria's match, Which I'll solemnize ere next setting Sun. Thus Venice, Florence, Genoa, strongly leagued. Excellent, excellent. I'll conquer Rome, Pop out the light of bright religion: And then, helter-skelter, all cock sure. Go to, 'tis just, the man hath wrong: go to. Go to, thou shalt have right. Go to Castilio, Clap him into the Palace dungeon: Lap him in rags, and let him feed on slime That smears the dungeon cheek. Let Poets roar, And all deplore: for now I bid you god night.
