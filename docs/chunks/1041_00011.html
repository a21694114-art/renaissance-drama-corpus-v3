Sooth badly, she has been, Planet-struck e'er since you went? she fell into a Lethargy since noon, a kind of qualm came o'er her stomach like a Cramp or a Convulsion? The meaning of this Music? We had a Wedding today, and the young fry tickle trench-more. A Wedding, and here this day. Bless me what prodigious Object Is yond, that blasts mine eyes, and like a thief, Steals my understanding! certes 'tis she. Is it not speak Ursula? I know not, for had she as many bodies as hearts, she might be here, and yonder too. Now by my life. Nay swear not; if you have any ill language to spare I'll send my cousin to you presently. Strange fears assail my senses, and begins Conflicts of despairs, doubts, and fears, And but I have a resolution fixed On her fidelity; this frontispiece And other entertainments might confirm Former presages. Who is't would speak with me? One that may be jealous though he wears no yellow. Her sight like to a cordial has expelled All former gross suggestions, methinks I taste my happiness ere I touch it. Beshrew thy heart for this. Beshrew your own false, if their be ill 'tis of your own begetting i'll provide Cock-broths, and caudles for your old cock-sparrow. she's dumb with joy, and I like to a man Entranced with joys unutterable, cannot speak? But I have lost myself, I am awake, And see a substance more than dreamers do, Thus in the arms of love I do enfold thee. I do not know you— touch me not? I wonder then how I dare know myself, When thou forgettest me? I had thought Had I been sullied with the sooty Moore, Or tanned with heat like some Egyptian slave, Or spotted like the Persian Leopards, Or in the worst form can be termed, Or imagined, yet thou couldst have known me, I am thy Bateman Nan! If you be Bateman; 'twere best you traveled from my father's ground lest he indite you? If he should, yet if thou stand the judge I know thou wilt acquit me of the crime? But thou art pleasant, and like to a tender nurse Heightens my infant joys before it comes, Be not so strange, this nicety in you, Has not been usual. It must be now for I am married. I know thou art, to me my fairest Nan. Our vows were made to Heaven, and on Earth They must be ratified, in part they are By giving of a pledge, a piece of Gold. Which when we broke, jointly then we swore Alive or dead for to enjoy each other, And so we will spite of thy father's frowns. You talk idly sir; these sparks of love That were 'twixt you, and I, are quite extinct Pacify yourself, you may speed better, You'll show much wit, and judgement if you do? She flouts me.
