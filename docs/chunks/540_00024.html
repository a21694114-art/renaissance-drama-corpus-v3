In Arms well met, ambitious Peridure, Vigenius thou salutes me with a title, Most proper to thyself, Art thou not proud. War is the soldiers harvest it cuts down. The lives of such as hinder our renown. Such as are apt for tumult. Such as you, That to our lawful Sovereign are untrue. Blushes not Peridure to brave us so. Blushes Vigenius at thy overthrow, Who was't that told me he would submit. 'twas I my Lord. Peace fool thou dost forget, 'tis not an hour since, to our princely ear, Thou saidst thou did desire us to forbear.
