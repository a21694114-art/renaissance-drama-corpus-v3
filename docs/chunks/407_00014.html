Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Nay good Thewte heart, good kind Iack, stay, if you would love me, as I love you, we would live & die together: and please God, would I were dead, and you are gone. And here's M. Iohn so de king, a very honest man too. ay, ay, he's a very good honest man: for there's not a hair betwixt him and heaven. he'll live with us now & teach us French. I by my trot, and you help me to a Vench now, me teach you French. 5. thousand, thousand year, o your Secke is hot, and make me brule, and brule, and burn, for a (he) by gour your Seck is hot. Welcome Basilisco, thou wilt carry level, and knock one's brains out with thy pricking wit. Kiss me sweet wench, kiss me. He my Vinifride, by gour you are come, in te very nick to pleasure me, pray you kiss me, clip me, love me, or by gour me ang die certain. Out you French Dog, touch my Love, and I'll— Touch her, by gour me touch her, and touch her, and touch her. I'll touch you, I'll slash you, I'll vench ye. Put up, put up, for the passion of God put up, or if you'll needs too it, sheath both your weapons in me first. Heart touch my love, touch my Winifrid? Hark you Jack, come to my chamber an hour hence, and you shall have what you will ask, and I can grant. Why then my choler's down. Iohn fo de King. Foutra for you. Foutra for me, futtra, futtra, futtra, five thousand futtras for you. Stay friend Jack, I'll reel along with you, if you'll not swagger. Sweet, sweet monsieur, hang you slaves, I love you infinitely. By gour me teach you French four thousand year dan. Well monsieur, I'll give you pleasure. But will you presently? quickly, for by gour me am a hot shot. I so they say, I heard you were under the torrid zone last day. Pish 'tis no matter, me am like a Tobacco Pipe, de more me am burn, de cleaner me am. Well then, two hours hence come to my chamber, and Timothy Twedle shall give you me in a sack. In a sack? Ha very well. And you shall carry me to my masters house at Holloway, for in the house we cannot be private without suspect. Till then, farewell. By my trot unreasonably good, I carry de vench on my back, and devench carry me on her (he) fine back, fine vench, fine monsieur, fine, fine, fine Knight, all fine, unreasonably fine, me sing vor joy; by gour me sing la, liro, liro la, lilo. Gentlemen, as e'er you loved wench, observe M. Puff and me. What shall we observe you for? Oh for our complement. Complement, what's that? Complement, is as much as (what call you it) 'tis derived of the Greek word, a pox on't.

Nay good Thewte hart, good kind Iack, stay, if you would loue mee, as I loue you, we would liue & die together: and please God, would I were dead, and you are gone. And heeres M. Iohn so de king, a verie honest man too. I, I, hee's a verie good honest man: for theres not a haire betwixt him and heauen. Heele liue with vs now & teach vs French. I by my trot, ang you helpe mee to a Vench now, mee teach you French. 5. towsand, towsand yere, ô your Secke is hote, and make mee brule, and brule, and burne, for a (hee) by gor your Seck is hote. Welcome Basilisco, thou wilt carry leuell, and knock ones braines out with thy pricking wit. Kisse me sweet wench, kisse mee. Hee my Vinifride, by gor you are come, in te very nick to pleasure mee, pree you kisse mee, clip mee, loue mee, or by gor mee ang die certaine. Out you French Dogge, touch my Loue, and Ile— Touch her, by gor mee touch her, and touch her, and touch her. Ile touch you, Ile slash you, Ile vench ye. Put vp, put vp, for the passion of God put vp, or if youle needs too it, sheath both your weapons in mee first. Hart touch my loue, touch my Winifride? Hark you Iacke, come to my chamber an houre hence, and you shall haue what you will aske, and I can graunt. Why then my chollers down. Iohn fo de King. Fontra for you. Fontra for me, futtra, futtra, futtra, fiue towsand futtra's for you. Stay friend Iacke, Ile reele along with you, if youle not swagger. Sweete, sweete Mounsieur, hang you slaues, I loue you infinitely. By gor me teach you French foure towsand yeare dan. Well Mounsieur, I'le giue you pleasure. But will you presently? quickly, for by gor me am a hot shot. I so they say, I heard you were vnder the Torred zone last day. Pish tis no matter, me am like a Tabacco Pipe, de more me am burne, de cleaner me am. Well then, two houres hence come to my chamber, and Timothy Twedle shall giue you mee in a sacke. In a sacke? Ha very well. And you shall carrie me to my Maisters house at Holloway, for in the house we cannot be priuate without suspect. Till then, farewell. By my trot vnreasonablie good, I carrie de vench on my backe, and devench carie me on her (hee) fine backe, fine vench, fine Mounsieur, fine, fine, fine Knight, all fine, vnreasonablie fine, me sing vor ioy; by gor me sing la, liro, liro la, lilo. Gentlemen, as e're you lou'd wench, obserue M. Puffe and me. What shall we obserue you for? Oh for our complement. Complement, whats that? Complement, is as much as (what call you it) tis deriued of the Greeke word, a pox ont.
