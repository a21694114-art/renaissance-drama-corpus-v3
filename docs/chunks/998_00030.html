I'll pledge thee, love, and therefore drink it off. sayst thou me so? have at it; and do you hear? Go to, it shall be so. Of that condition I will drink it up; here's to thee. Nay, I'll have all or none. There, if thou lov'st me do not leave a drop. Love thee, fill me three glasses. Three and fifty dozen, I'll pledge thee, knavily spoke, and like a Knight at Arms. hay Rivo Castiliano, a man's a man. Now to the Iew. Ha to the Iew, and send me money you were best. What wouldst thou do if he should send thee none? Do nothing; but I know what I know, He's a murderer. I had not thought he had been so brave a man. You knew Mathias and the governor's son, he and I killed 'em both, and yet never touched 'em. Oh bravely done. I carried the broth that poisoned the Nuns, and he And I snickle hand too fast, strangled a Friar. You two alone. We two, and 'twas never known, nor never shall Be for me. This shall with me unto the Governor. And fit it should: but first let's ha' more gold Come gentle Ithimore, lie in my lap. Love me little, love me long, let music rumble, Whilst I in thy incony lap do tumble. A French Musician, come let's hear your skill? Must tuna my Lute for sound, twang twang first. Wilt drink Frenchman, here's to thee with a— Pox on this drunken hiccup. Gramercy monsieur. prithee, Pilia-borza, bid the Fiddler give me The posy in his hat there. Sirrah, you must give my mistress your posy. A voustre commandement Madam. How sweet, my Ithimore, the flowers smell. Like thy breath, sweetheart, no violet like 'em. Foh, methinks they stink like a hollyhock. So, now I am revenged upon 'em all. The scent thereof was death, I poisoned it. Play, Fiddler, or I'll cut your cat's guts into chitterlings Pardona moy, be no in tune yet; so now, now all be in. Give him a crown, and fill me out more wine. There's two crowns for thee, play. How liberally the villain gives me mine own gold. methinks he fingers very well. So did you when you stole my gold. How swift he runs. You run swifter when yond threw my gold out of My Window. Musician, hast been in Malta long? Two, three, four month Madam. Dost not know a Iew, one Barabas? Very much, monsieur, you no be his man. His man? I scorn the Peasant, tell him so. He knows it already. 'Tis a strange thing of that Iew, he lives upon Pickled Grasshoppers, and sauced mushrooms. What a slave's this? The Governor feeds not as I do. He never put on clean shirt since he was circumcised Oh rascal! I change myself twice a day. The Hat he wears, Iudas left under the Elder When he hanged himself. 'Twas sent me for a present from the great Cham.
