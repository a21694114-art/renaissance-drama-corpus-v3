I have only a Cock-loft, please you gentle Friars, To make best use of that and some fresh straw, Best harbour that my husband's absence yields Y'are welcome to. We entertain it gladly, And thank you my good Dame, I am for sleep, That's this my night's ambition. Please you enter, but yet no further than I'll lock you in I'll keep the Key myself. At your best pleasure. My Watch is set, charge given, and all at peace, But by the burning of the Candle blue, Which I by chance espied through the lantern, And by the dropping of the Beadle's Nose, I smell a Frost; now to prevent this cold, To which I am subject, I have made my Deputy, Given up my staff and power into his hands; myself intending to spend all this night Here, at an honest Neighbours; some provision I have sent in, and some I have brought along, A cast of Manchets, and a Bottle to Of the best Wine in Turnball, which, they say All London cannot better: Silent all, None stirring near, I'll knock but softly for fear Of waking Neighbours. whose's there? 'Tis I. Not Master Constable? The same fair love. Have you disposed your Watch? Yes, at you further Corner. Not too loud, mean time I softly will sneak down The Stairs, and softly let you in. Gramercy wench; the kindest loving Neighbour the Town yields, the Hamlet cannot match her; a smug Lass, And one that knows it too; I would not lose her For half my state in the Parish, while I'm in Office, She's safe as Mouse in Mill: Oh, are you come? I was in fear you would have broke with me, But now I see y'are currant. Sweet a kiss, and hark in thine ear. friar Bernard's fast, he snores and sleeps as Snug as any Pig in Peas-straw; but myself Cannot once close an eye, which makes me wonder That I am grow so wakeful. Here's to thy good fat Pullet that's within, Two fine Cheat Loaves were sent me from the Court, A Cup of Nipsitate brisk and neat; The Drawers call it Tickle-brain, 'twill do't, But is the Roast-meat ready? Piping hot, I'll go fetch Salt and Trenchers. Quick, good Wench. Dream I? or is't substantial that I see? Was this your cunning Dame, to lock us up Like Prisoners, and afford us nought but straw To feather our nests withal? not allow us drink, Food, nor a Candle, lest we should fire the House, For that is her excuse; fire on you both, Have you these tricks? yet God a mercy cranny That I can spy all this. Now let's be merry, but in any case Speak not too loud, lest we should wake the Friars. The Friars? what Friars? Two Abbey Lubbers that are locked up fast, Of whom I could not rid me; but I think I have fitted them, they have neither light nor bed, Nor any other Comfort.
