Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Come, let him come I pray, I'll whip ye boy, I'll teach you to out face. Come, come, come, but one at once, ye dasterds come Keep the King's peace, I see you are both deceived, He that was last hear, was not Faukenbridge. They slander me, who says that I was hear? We do believe ye sir; nor do you think My brother Iohn deceived you of a chain. He did, I did deliver it with this hand. I'll die upon the slanderer, Let the boy come. I, let him come, let him come. Fellow, thou spak'st even now, as if prince Iohn Had been at some old Tavern in the town. I sir, I came up now, but from the Salutation, And a drawer that doth not use to lie, told me Prince Iohn hath been there all this after noon. The Devil in my likeness then is there. The Devil in thy likeness or thyself, Had my gold chain. Thou art the Devil, for thou Hadst my good sword, all these can witness it. God's Mother thou bely'st me. Give me the lie? Nay calm this fury, lets down to the Tavern, Or one, or both, these counterfeits are there. I know him well enough that had my chain, And there be two Iohns, if I find one there, be'r Lady, I will lay him fast. It is this Skinke that mocks us I believe. Alas poor Skink it is the Devil Gloster; Who if I be so happy once to find, I'll give contentment, to his troubled mind. I hope he's far enough, and free enough: Yet these conseytes I know delight his soul. Follow me Block, follow me honest Block. Much follow you, I have another piece of work in hand; I hear say Redcaps father shall be hanged this after noon, I'll see him slip a string though I give my service the slip; beside my Lady bade me hear his examination at his death: I'll get a good place, and pen it word for word, and as I like it, set out a mournful Ditty to the tune of Labandalashot, or row well ye Mariners, or somewhat as my muse shall me invoke. A charitable deed, God bless the King, He shall be then repreeved. I sir, some day or two, till the young King and Prince Iohn change it, especially if the good Earl be not found which God forbidden. What house is this that we are stepped into to read this warrant in? A Tavern sir, the Salutation, A Tavern? then I will turn prodigal, Call for a pint of Sack good fellow. Drawer? Anan sir. A pint of thy best Sack my pretty youth. God bless your worship sir, ye shall have the best in London sir. What know'st thou me? know'st thou old Faukenbridge? I am no Tavern hunter I can tell thee.
