Nay then Ferando he must needs sit out, For he may call I think till he be weary, Before his wife will come before she list. 'tis well for you that have such gentle wives, Yet in this trial will I not sit out, It may be Kate will come as soon as I do send. My wife comes soon for a hundred pound. I take it. I'll lay as much to yours, That my wife comes as soon as I do send. How now Ferando, you dare not lay belike. Why true, I dare not lay indeed: But how, so little money on so sure a thing, A hundred pound: why I have laid as much Upon my Dog, in running at a Deer, She shall not come so far for such a trifle, But will you lay five hundred marks with me, And whose wife soon comes when he doth call, And shows herself most loving unto him, Let him enjoy the wager I have laid, Now what say you? dare you adventure thus? I, were it a thousand pounds I durst presume On my wives love: and I will lay with thee. How now sons, what in conference so hard, May I without offence, know where about? Faith father a weighty cause, about our wives, Five hundred marks already we have laid, And he whose wife doth show most love to him, He must enjoy the wager to himself. Why then Ferando he is sure to lose it, I promise thee son, thy wife will hardly come, And therefore I would not wish thee lay so much. Tush father, were it ten times more I durst adventure on my lovely Kate, But if I lose i'll pay, and so shall you. Upon mine honour, if I lose I'll pay. And so will I upon my faith I vow. Then sit we down and let us send for them: I promise thee Ferando I am afraid thou wilt lose. I'll send for my wife first, Valeria, Go bid your mistress come to me. I will my Lord. Now for my hundred pound, Would any lay ten hundred more with me I know I should obtain it by her love. I pray God you have not laid too much already. Trust me Ferando I am sure you have, For you I dare presume have lost it al. Now sir ha, what says your mistress? She is something busy but she'll come anon. Why so, did not I tell you this before, She was busy and cannot come. I pray God your wife send you so good an answer She may be busy, yet she says she'll come. Well, well: Polidor, send you for your wife. Agreed, Boy desire your mistress to come hither. I will sir. I, so, so, he desires her to come. Polidor, I dare presume for thee, I think thy wife will not deny to come, And I do marvel much Aurelius, That your wife came not when you sent for her.
