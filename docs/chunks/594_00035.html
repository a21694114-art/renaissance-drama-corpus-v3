Content, I'm for any wether? heart step close, here he comes? My honoured Lord? Oh me; you both present. E'en newly my Lord, just as your Lordship entered now? about this place we had notice given he should be, but in some Came your honour private? Private enough for this: only a few Attend my coming out. Death rot those few. Stay yonder's the slave. Mass there's the slave indeed my Lord; 'tis a good child, he calls his Father slave. ay, that's the villain, the damned villain: softly, Tread easy. Puh, I warrant you my Lord, we'll stifle in our breaths. That will do well: Base rogue, thou sleepest thy last, 'tis policy, To have him killed in's sleep, for if he waked He would betray all to them. But my Lord. Ha, what sayst? Shall we kill him now he's drunk? I best of all. Why then he will near live to be sober? No matter, let him reel to hell. But being so full of liquour, I fear he will put out all the fire, Thou art a mad breast. And leave none to warm your Lordship's golls withal; For he that dies drunk, falls into hell fire like a Bucket a water, qush qush. Come be ready, nake your swords, think of your wrongs This slave has injured you. Troth so he has, and he has paid well for't. Meet with him now. You'll bear us out my Lord? Puh, am I a Lord for nothing think you, quickly, now. Sa, sa, sa: thump, there he lies. Nimbly done, ha? oh, villains, murderers, 'tis the old Duke my father. That's a jest. What stiff and cold already? O pardon me to call you from your names: 'tis none of your deed,— that villain Piato Whom you thought now to kill, has murdered him, And left him thus disguised. And no unlikely. O rascal was he not ashamed, To put the Duke into a greasy doublet. He has been cold and stiff who knows, how long? Marry that do I. No words I pray, off any thing intended: Oh my Lord. I would same have your Lordship think that we have small reason to prate. Faith thou sayst true? i'll forthwith send to Court, For all the Nobles, Bastard, duchess, all? How here by miracle we found him dead, And in his raiment that foul villain fled. That will be the best way my Lord, to clear us all: let's cast about to be clear. Ho, Nencio, Sordido, and the rest. My Lord. My Lord. Be witnesses of a strange spectacle: Choosing for private conference that sad room We found the Duke my father gealed in blood. My Lord the Duke— run hie thee Nencio, Startle the Court by signifying so much. Thus much by wit a deep Revenger can: When murders known to be the clearest man We're furthest off, and with as bold an eye, Survey his body as the standers by.
