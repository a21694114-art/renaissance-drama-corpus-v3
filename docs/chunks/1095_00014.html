You may do much, and yet I could desire You were less honourable, for though you have Blood of a deeper crimson, the good Lady Out of her love could wish you were a thing Beneath a foot man, and that you had no kindred But Knights o'th' post. Good colonel. Nay pardon me, In the humour I am in, I wish, and heartily, You were a son o'th' people. colonel, How the devil came he by this? Under the Rose there was a gentleman Came in at the Wicker, these are tales of which The Greeks have store, fair hopes Gentlemen. How came you by this intelligence. Nay I'll no whispering, what I say to one Will concern every man, she has made You coxcombs. It does appear. And more than does appears yet I had my share. That's some comfort, I was afraid. But you shall pardon me, I'll conceal The particulars of her bountiful abuses To me, let it suffice I know we are all jeered most abominably, I stood behind The hangings when she signed your several passes, And had my own at last worse than the Constables, That this is true, you shall have more than oath, I'll join we in revenge, and if you won't, I will do't alone. She is a devil. Damn her then, till we think on something else, let's all go back, and rail upon her. Agreed, a pox upon her. We cannot be to bitter, she's a hell Cat. D'ee hear, listen to me our shames are equal, Yet if we all discharge at once upon her, We shall but make confusion, and perhaps Give her more cause to laugh, let us choose one, To curse her for us all. 'tis the best way, and if you love me gentlemen, Engage me, I deserve this favour for my Discovery, I'll swear her into hell. Troth I ha' no good vein, I me content. Gentlemen, noble colonel as you respect A wounded branch of the Nobility, Make it my office, she abused me most, and if The devil do not furnish me with language, I'll say he has no malice. If they consent. With all our hearts. I thank you gentlemen. But let's us all together, I'll not be barred, Now and then to interpose an oath, As I shall find occasion. You'll relieve me When I take breath, than you may help, or you, Or any to confound her. Let away. Never was witch so tortured. Noble Mr. Freshwater welcome from travel.
