Why Friends, you go to do you know not what: Wherein hath Caesar thus deserved your loves? Alas you know not, I must tell you then: You have forgot the Will I told you of. Most true, the Will, let's stay and hear the Wil. here is the Will, and under Caesar's Seal: To every Roman Citizen he gives, To every several man, seventy five Drachmas. Most Noble Caesar, we'll revenge his death. O Royal Caesar. Hear me with patience.
