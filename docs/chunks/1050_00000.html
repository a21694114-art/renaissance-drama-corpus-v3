I cannot tell; she may have a conceit, Some Dolphin has preserved him in the storm, Or that he may be tenant to some Whale; Within whose belly he may practise lent, And feed on fish, till he be vomited Upon some coast, or having scaped the seas, And bills of Exchange failing, he might purpose To foot it o'er the Alps in his return, And by mischance is fallen among the mice, With whom perhaps he battens upon sleep, Beneath the Snow.
