Yes wondrously,— a kind of Megrim Sir. I think to bind Your Temples with the Crown of Spain would ease you. The Crown of Spain! my Temples! Nay, I but jest, A Kingdom would make any Sick man well, And Iohn I would thou hadst one. It shall go hard else. The King I thank him says that you and I— What? Cut you out sir in steaks: I'll not be silent, And that I am an Ass, and a Fox you; Have I any dealings with you? When I am to deal sir, A wise man then shall hold the Cards. Now I'm called fool too. Sir if you remember Before he came, you buzzed into mine ear, Tunes that did found but scurvily. I buz! What buz! That he should sell me to the Portugal. Were't thou as big as all the Kings i'th' world, 'Tis false and I defy thee. Nay Sir, and more,— Out with't; no whispering, I shall blush to speak it, Hark you, a Pox upon't, cannot you soothe His sullen Lordship up, you see I do Platter him, confess any thing. A good jest! I should confess to him I know not what, And have my throat cut, but I know not why. would your Grace Would licence me a while to leave the Court To attend my health. Do. I take my leave— as for you Sir- My Lord do you see this Change i'th' Moon, sharp horns Do threaten windy weather, shall I rule you Send to him dead words, write to him your mind And if your hearts be unsound purge both, all humours That are corrupt within you. I'll never write, but to him in person. Pray Madam rise. Do you know this old fury? No: what is she? She's the king's nut-hook (if report has not a blister on her tongue) that when any Filbert-tree is ripe; pulls down the bravest bows to his hand: a Lady panderess, and (as this years' Almanac says) has a private hothouse for his Grace only to sweat in: her name the Lady Dildoman: the poor Knight her Husband is troubled with the City Gout, lies i'th' Counter. I'll hang him that stirs in't, the proudest Falcon that's perched up nearest the Eagle, if he dare, make this his prey, how many years! fifteen and upwards if it please your Grace. Some two footed Devil in our Court, Would thrust you out of all, Enclosed! or Common! 'Tis yet enclosed if it like your Grace. Entailed! Newly Entailed, as there 'tis to be seen in black and white. This case myself will handle; fee no Lawyer I'll stand for you, ha! Servants of mine turned grinders! To oppress the weak! What slave is't! from my sight, lest my heaved hand swerve awry, and Innocence smite.
