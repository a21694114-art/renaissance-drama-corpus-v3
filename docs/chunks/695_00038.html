By the meskin methought they were so indeed. S'life, the hungry knave her squire, could not hold in the open streets. What should she be? The Doxy was muffled in her cloak, I had but a glimpse of her; but 'slight I will know her, she passes not so, come we'll follow. I'll beat the Rogue and take away's whore from him. COme courage coz, we have sailed the man of War out of sight, and here we must put into harbour. Hist, hawe Amila? O welcome good Angelo. Here take in, go, get up lightly, away, take heed you slip not coz, remember you're short heeled. Hold fast for God's sake. Nay hold you fast, you'll shame us all else; so Ioue receive thy soul: I take away the ladder: Now till you have deceived the Provost, farewell, remember your lesson coz. I warrant you. How unhappily did we miss 'em? they slipped into some vaulting house, I hold my life. Faith it's good we missed'em, she was some stale punk I warrant her. Twenty to one she is some honest man's wife of the Parish that steals abroad for a trimming, while he sits secure at home, little knowing, God knows, what hangs over his head; the poor Cuckold esteeming her the most virtuous wife in the world. And should one tell him, he had seen her dressed like a Page following a knave thus, I'll lay my life he would not believe it. Why no Captain, wives take all the faith from their husbands. And that makes 'em do so many good works as they do. Mercy for that i'faith Lieutenant, stand close. My Mistress in man's apparel sayst thou? Thy Mistress in man's apparel I assure thee, and attended by Angelo. Would to heaven I had seen her, canst tell whither she went. Full butt into Lorenzo's house, and if thou knewst him, thou know'st wherefore, an ill-favoured trimming is her errand. 'Tis very well, she trims my Captain prettily, in the mean time his head pays for all, and yet alas poor hornstock, he thinks her to have no fault, but her too much dotage upon him, well my conscience will not let me keep her counsel, he shall know on't. Why man if both of us should tell him her fault he will not believe us. No, nor if he had seen it with his own own eyes I think, I shall never forget how the profound Cockatrice hung on his sleeve today, and he should not from her sight, she'd follow him into the wars, one day should make an end of both their loves and lives, and then to see him the wittal, my Captain began to strut, and battle the pride of his merits that so heightened her affection. True, and how the foppasty his Lieutenant, stepped in to persuade with her, to take it patiently, for friends must part, we came not all together, and we must not go all together.
