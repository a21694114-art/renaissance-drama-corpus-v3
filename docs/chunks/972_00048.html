You would dye a calf's death feign: but 'tis an Ox's, Is meant you. To be fairly knocked o'the head. With a good jeer or two. And from your jawbone, Don Assinigo? Shunfield, a jeer, you have it. I do confess a washing blow? but Snarle, You that might play the third dog, for your teeth, You ha' no money now? Not, nor no Mortgage. Nor Band. Nor Statute. Not, nor blushet Wax. Nor you no Office, as I take it. Cymbal, A mighty jeer. Pox o'these true jests, I say. He will turn the better jeerer. Let's upon him, And if we cannot jeer him down in wit, Let's do't in noise. Content. Charge, man o' war. Lay him, aboard. We'll give him a broad side, first. where's your venison, now? Your red-Deer-pyes? Wi' your baked Turkeys? and your Partridges? Your Pheasants, & fat Swans? Like you, turned Geese. But such as will not keep your Capitol? You were want to ha' your Bream— And Trout sent in? Fat Carp, and Salmon? I, and now, and than, An Emblem, o'your self, an o'ergrown Pike? You are a Iack, Sir. You ha' made a shift To swallow twenty such poor jacks ere now. If he should come to feed upon poor- Iohn? Or turn pure jack-a-lent after all this? Tut, he'll live like a Grasshopper— On dew. Or like a Bear, with licking his own claws. I, If his dogs were away. He'll eat them, first, While they are fat. Faith, and when they are gone, Here's nothing to be seen beyond. Except His kindred, Spiders, natives o' the soil. Dust, he will ha' enough here, to breed fleas. But, by that time, he'll ha' no blood to rear 'hem. He will be as thin as a lantern, we shall see thorough him, And his gut colon, tell his Intestina— Rogues, Rascals (baw waw) He calls his dogs to his aid. O! they but rise at mention of his tripes. Let them alone, they do it not for him. They bark, se defendendo. Or for custom, As commonly currres do, one for another. Arm, arm you, gentlemans jeerers, th'old Canter Is coming in upon you, with his forces, The Gentleman, that was the Canter. Hence. Away. What is he? stay not to ask questions. he's a flame. A furnace. A consumption, Kills where he goes. See! the whole Covey is scattered, Ware,' ware the Hawks. I love to see him fly. YOu see by this amazement, and distraction, What your companions were, a poor, affrighted, And guilty race of men, that dare to stand No breath of truth: but conscious to themselves Of their no-wit, or honesty, ran routed At every Panic terror themselves bred. Where else, as confident as sounding brass, Their tinkling Captain, Cymbal, and the rest, Dare put on any visor, to deride The wretched: or with buffoon licence, jest At whatsoe'er is serious, if not sacred. Who's this? my brother! and restored to life!
