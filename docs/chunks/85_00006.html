Undoubtedly my sovereign lord, he seemeth unto me, Not to sequest his father's steps, in feats of chivalry: But rather for to imitate, the flower of great 〈◊〉 land, I mean Achilles that same knight, by whose one only hand The Graecians have obtained at length the conquest of old Troy For which they did whole x. years space, their labour great employ sith he is gone for to purvey, such things as shall in deed, Suffice to serve his turn in wares, whereof he shall have need Let us depart and when he shall, return hither a gain, To see the must or of his men, we will sure take the pain. Far well adieu, that courtlike life, To war we tend to gowe: It is good sport to see the strife, Of soldiers on a row. How mereley they forward march, These enemies to slay: With heigh trim and tryxey to, Their banners they display. Now shall we have the Golden cheats, When others want the same: And soldiers have foul many feats, Their enemies to tame.
