Therefore fair maid, bridle these brutish thoughts, And learn to follow what the fates assign, When Saturn heard that Jupiter his son Should drive him headlong from his heavenly seat, Down to the bottom of the dark Avern, He did command his mother presently, To do to death, the young and guiltless child: But what of that, the mother loathed in heart, For to commit so vile a massacre. Yea Ioue did live, and as the fates did say, From heavenly seat, drove Saturn clean away. What did avail the Castle all of Steel, The which Acrisius caused to be made, To keep his daughter Danae clogged in? But Marble stones needs no colouring, And that which every one doth know for truth, Needs no examples to confirm the same. That which the fates appoint must happen so, Though heavenly Ioue, and all the Gods say no.
