My wives intemperance hath got that name, And mistress Barns, I doubt and shrewdly doubt, And some great cause begets this doubt in me, Your husband and my wife doth wrong us both. How? think ye so, nay master Goursey then You run indebt to my opinion, Because you pay not such advised wisdom, As I think due unto my good conceit. Then still I fear I shall your debtor prove. Then I arrest you in the name of love, Not bale, but present answer to my plea, And in the Court of reason we will try, If that good thoughts should believe jealousy,
