By this light, had I but scented out your train, ye had slept with a bare pillow in your arms, & kissed that, or else the bedpost, for any wife ye had got this twelvemonth yet: I would have vexed you more than a tired post-horse: & been longer bearing, then ever aftergame at Irish was.
