There is always good remedy for that That is to compel them to do some what So that each man having inheritance Have some auctoryce and governance wherein he should take pain and business To coustrayn him to eschew Idelnes Then this great mischief should follow of hit Oft times they should rule that have little wit Or disposed to be proud & covetous Or to life after their lusts voluptuous which if such men had authority Many things no doubt misordered should be where justice should be/ there would be tyranny where peace should be war debat & envy So there is no good reason that I can see To prove that any inheritance should be yes that shall I prove by good authority For read in the bible and thou shalt therein see God said to abraham tibi dabo Terram hanc et semine tuo which is as much to say to expound it true I shall gift this land to the and thine issue Here is a good prove that it was god's will.
