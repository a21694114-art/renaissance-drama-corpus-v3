Aller. With all my heart Sr. Marmaduke let's help To exercise the Ladies. A good motion. And begar noting in de world mor profet Your body den de motion all a more de France. I am for any frisk. Ha de frisk you jump upon my name, and Begar you have my nature to de right, heigh, and All de world is but frisk. A Country dance then. Hah, monsieur Madam aller, forboon, tres excellent begar, so I crave your patience Madam, gentlemen, you be at de Ball, mofoy you See dat was never in dis world. What monsieur? What do you think dat is, me tell you, begar You see me play de part of de Cupid. A French Cupid. Begar French Cupid, why? dear is no love like De French love, dat is Cupid, love is hot, and de French is hot How comes it to pass that you are to play Cupid Moun. My Lord give me command me have device, & de mask For de Ladies, and me no trust little jacknape to play Young Cupid but myself. Cupid is a child, you have a beard monsieur. Me care not de hair for dat, begar de little god may have De little beard, Venus his mother have de mole, and Cupid Her shield may have the black mussel. But monsieur, we read Cupid was fair, and You are black, how will that agree? Cupid is fair, and monsieur is black, why monsieur Is black den, and Cupid is fair, what is dat? a fair Lady love de servant of the black Complexion de ban eur, the colour is not de much, Vulcan was de black Smith, and Cupid may be de Black gentleman his son legitimate. 'tis de way to make Cupid the boy no bastard. But do you no publish this invention, me meet you At de Ball armed with quiver, and de bow. You won't shoot us, I hope you'll spare our hearts. Begar me shit you if me can, and your arts shall Bleed one, two, tree, gallows adieu madam Serviteur gentlemen tresemble. Adieu monsieur, now Madam with your favour, I must renew my suit. Yad better buy a new one, Nay then we shall be troubled. You'll withdraw, I'll follow you. Come, come I know you love me. You may enlarge your folly my dear knight, But I have pardoned you for love already. This sha'not serve your turn, I came hither Not to be jeered, and one of you shall love me. Oh impudence dares he return. It seems so. Most gracious Madam, my cosin your Lord Loveall Commends himself in black and white. To me? D'ee think 'tis from myself. You might ha' doubt in black and blue. Scutilla how does thy poor soul, thou Hast no husband nor children to commend me to. The poor soul's well, I hope your body is Recovered, does not your left cheek burn still, We ha' so talked on you?
