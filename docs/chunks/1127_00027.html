Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

I'll mend it next peal. To entreat a knowledge of you, whether it be By the Ear you ring thus cunningly or by the Eye; For to be plain, he has laid ten pounds upon't. But which way has he laid? That your Ear guides you, And not your Eye. Has won, has won, the Ear's our only instrument: But how shall we be sure on't. Put all the lights out, to what end serve our Eyes then, A plain Case. You say true, 'tis a fine cunning thing to ring by th'ear sure: And can you ring i'th' dark so? All night long boy. 'Tis wonderful, let this be certain gentlemen, And half his wager he allows among ye; Is't possible you should ring so? Possible, thou art a child, I'll ring when I am dead drunk; Out with the lights, no twinkling of a candle, I know my rope to, as I know my nose, And can bang it soundly i'th' dark, I warrant you. Come let's confirm him straight and win the wager. Let me hear to strengthen me; And when you've rung I'll bring the money to you. So so, follow 'em; They shall have a cool reward, one hath gold of mine, Good store in's pocket, But this will be revenged in a short warning. They are at it lustily; hay, how wantonly They ring away their clothes, how it delights me; Here, here, sir. Hast Wildbrain's? His whole case sir; I felt it out, and by the guards This should be the coachman's, another suit too. Away Boy, quickly now to the Usurer, His hour to wake approaches. That once finished, You'll give me leave to play sir: here they come. I am monstrous weary, Fie, how I sweat? Reach me my cloak to cover me, I run to oil like a Porpoise; 'twas a brave peal. Let me light my candle first, then I'll wait on you: A very brave peal. Carman, you came in close now. Sure 'tis past midnight. No stirring in the streets I hear, Walk further, was that a pillar? 'tis harder than my nose. Where's the Boy promised us five pound? Room, I sweat still; come, come, my cloak, I shall take cold Where lies it? Here, here, and all our clothes. Where, where? Ith' corner. Is thy candle blind too, give me the bottle, I can drink like a Fish now, like an Elephant. Here are the corners, but here are no clothes; Yes, here is a cuff. A cuff? give me the candle. Cuffs won't cover me— I smell a knavery. Is't come to a cuff? my whole suit turned to a button? Now am I as cold again as though 'twere Christmas; Cold with my fear, I'll never ring by the ear more. My new clothes vanished? My all clothes Toby. Here's none. Not one of my dragon's wings left to adorn me Have I muted all my feathers? Cheated by the ear; a plot to put out the candle; I could be mad; my chain, my rings, the gold, the gold.
