Bless thee, most excellent Duke; I here present thee as worthy and learned a Gentleman, as ever I (and yet I have lived threescore years) conversed with; take it from me, I have tried him, and is worthy to be privy-counselor to the greatest Turk in Christendom: of a most apparent and deep understanding, slow of speech, but speaks to the purpose; Come forward, Sir, and appear before his Highness in your own proper Elements. Then let me cast myself beneath thy foot, True virtuous Lord: Know then, Sir, her proud heart Is only fixed on you in such extremes Of violence and passion, that I fear, Or she'll enjoy you, or she'll ruin you.
