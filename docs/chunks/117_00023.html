Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Alas sir, he'll be here anon, ha' be handled to bad. Master bailie, sir ye be not such a fool well I know, But ye perceive by this lingering, there is a pad in the straw. Thinking that Hodg, his head was broke, and that gammer Would not let him come before them. Chil show you his face, ich warrant thee, lo now where he is. Come on fellow it is told me thou art a shrew iwis, Thy neighbour's hens thou takest, and plays the two legged fore Their chickens & their capons to, & now and then their Cocks. Ich defy them all that dare it say, cham as true as the best. Wart not thou take within this hour, in dame chats hen's nest? Take there? no master chold not dot, for a house full of gold. Thou or the devil in thy cote, swear this I dare be bold. ¶ wre me no swearing quean, the deuill h the sorow, All is not worth a gnat, thou canst swear till to morrow, Where is the harm he hath? show it by God's bread, Ye beat him with a witness, but the stripes light on my head. Bet me? gog's blessed body, chold first ich trow have burst the Ich thinke and chad my hands loose callet chould haue crust th. Thou shitten knave I trow thou knowest the full weight of my fist I am foully deceived, unless thy head & my door bar kissed Hold thy chat whore thou criest so loud, can no man else be hard Well knave, & I had the alone, I would surely rap the costard. Sir answer me to this, is thy head whole or broken? Yea master bailie, blessed be every good token. Is my head whole? ich warrant you, 'tis neither scurvy nor sealed What you foul beast, does think 'tis either pilled or bald. Nay ich thank god: chil not for all that thou mayst spend That chad one scab on my narse, as broad as thy finger's end. Come nearer hear. Yes That iche dare. By our Lady here is no harm, Hodges head is hole enough, for all dame Chats charm. By gog's blessed, however the thing he clocks or smolders, I know the blows he bore away, either with head or shoulders, Camest thou not knave within this hour, creeping into my pens And there was caught within my house, groping among my hens. A plague both on thy hens & thee, a cart whore, a cart, Chould I were hanged as high as a tree, & chware as false as thou art Geue my gāmer again her washial, yu stole away in thy lap. Yea master baily there is a thing, you know not on may hay This drab she keeps away my good, the devil he might her snare Ich pray you that ich might have, a right action on her.
