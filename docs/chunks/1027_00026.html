Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

The Duke my Lord. How the Duke? Signior Depazzi. He has been earnest with me, and please your highness To be his humble suitor, he may have Freedom to leave the Court. He shall be banished How? What time will your grace allow to provide? Two hours. I had rather lose my head at home, and save charges Of travail, I beseech your grace. Well 'tis granted, let him not trouble us. Enjoy the country, and return when the Duke sends for you. I humbly thank his highness, and will pray for your increase of grace. Lorenzo, are we private? Yes my Lord. I am very melancholy. I know the cause, 'tis Amidea. Right. I do not wish her dead. It were a sin. Not in heaven sir, yet There be Ladies, that would think it a promotion. It were pity she should leave The world, till she hath taught by her example The nearest way I am very confident she's yet honest. Yet Lorenzo? ay sir, but I'm not of opinion it is Impossible to know a change. Take heed. I must confess she has been very valiant, In making you remove your siege, and showed Pretty dexterity at the poniard, See herself bleed: but this a mortal Virgin Might do, and not be adored for't: other women Have gone as far, or else false Legends have Been thrust upon the easy world: some say There have been creatures that have killed themselves, To save their sullen chastities; but I Have no strong faith that way, yet you were startled To see her strike her arm, and grew compassionate. I was not Marble, we break Adamant With blood, and could I be a man, and not Be moved to see that hasty ebb be of life For my sake? I have read some aged Stories: What think you of Lucrece? she is remembered. Chastities great example. How the world Was cozened in her? she knew of Tarquin first, And then suspecting she should never meet Again the active Gentleman, having Determined of his death, with well dissembled Sorrow did stab herself, in hope to meet The gamester in Elysium, Amidea, You will allow beneath this Roman Dame. Lorenzo had the burning ravisher, Made this attempt on Amidea, she Would have compelled his penitence, to quench His fire with holy tears. I had a body Refined to air, or I was borne up by A thousand wings: methought I could have flown And kissed the cheek of Cynthia, thence with ease Have leapt to Venus' Star, but I was wounded, And the gay feathers, in whose pride I had My confidence, served now but with their weight To hasten me to earth. Ascend again, And fix in your loved orb, he brings this comfort That can assure it, if you have not lost A heart to entertain with love and pleasure The beauteous Amidea. Ha? You shall enjoy her. Enjoy fair Amidea? do not tempt, Or rather mock my frailty with such a promise.
