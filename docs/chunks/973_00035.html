You are not urged with 'hem. I can accuse, Sir, none but mine own judgement, For though it were his crime, so to betray me: I'm sure, 'twas more mine own, at all to trust him. But Master Either-side tells me, that he likes Your other Business better. Which? O' the Toothpicks. I never heard on't. Ask Mr. Mere-craft. Madame? he's one, in a word, I'll trust his malice, With any man's credit, I would have abused! Sir, if you think you do please me, in this, You are deceived! Not, but because my Lady, named him my kinsman; I would satisfy you, What I think of him: and pray you, upon it To judge me! So I do: that ill men's friendship, Is as unfaithful, as themselves. Do you hear? Ha' you a Business about Toothpicks?
