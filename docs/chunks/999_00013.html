Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Possible thus cheated of a hundred pieces, A handsome halter, and the hangman's wages popped in the place, what an acute witness we have in wickedness. 'tis done, and handsomely you. Here's a pottle of rich Canary, and a quart of neat claret Gentlemen, and there's a Gentleman below, he says he is your Brother, Master Bloodhound, he appointed to meet you here. The expected thing that bought the Bristol stone. Send him up prithee, remember how it must be carried? I am her Grandmother, forget not that by any means. And pray remember that you do not mumble as if you were chewing Bacon, and spoil all. I warrant ye. And hark. Are these the company sir? Yes, but those I like not, these are not they, I'll stay i'th' next room till my company come. Where you please Sir, pray follow me. I hear him coming up gingerly. Oh he tramples upon the bosom of a Tavern with that dexterity, as your Lawyers Clerks do to Westminster Hall upon a dirty day with a pair of white silk stockings, Brother Tim, why now y'are a man of your word I see. Nay I love to be as good as my say, see brother, look, there's the rest or your money upon the Ring, I cannot spend a penny, for I have ne'er a penny left, what are these, what are these? Gallants of note and quality, he that fits taking Tobacco is a Captain, Captain Carvegut. He will not make a Capon of me, will he. Are you not my brother? He that powers out that sparkling sprightly Claret, is a Lieutenant vnder him, Lieutenant B. He was a Sergeant first. Of the Poultry, or of Woodstreet. Of the Poultry, of a Woodcock: A Sergeant in the Field, a man of blood. I'le take my leaue brother, I am in g ha. That delicate sweet young Gentlewoman. Fough this Tobacco. That bears the blush of morning upon her cheeks, whose eyes are like a pair of talking Twins. Sh lookes ust vpon e. I think you are in haste. No, no, no pray: Whose lips are beds of Roses, betwixt which There steals a breath sweeter then Indian Spices. Sweeter than Ginger. But then to touch those lips— you stay too long sure. Pish I tell you, I do not, I know my time, pray what's her name? But 'tis descended from the Grecian steam, the great tribute Lindabride's her name, that ancient marron is her reverent Granham. Niggers I have read of her in the Mirror of Knighthood. Co they shall know you. Nay brother. I say they shall. Let me go down and wash my face first. ce is a ne face my brother Gentle. Sir, you are victoriously welcome. That word has e'en conquered me. I desire to kiss your hand Sir. Indeed but you shall not Sir, I went out early, and forgot to wash 'em.
