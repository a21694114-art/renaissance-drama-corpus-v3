Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

I am come to survey the Tower this day; Since Henry's death, I fear there is Conveyance: Where be these Warders, that they wait not here? Open the Gates, 'tis Gloster that calls. Who's there, that knocks so imperiously? It is the Noble Duke of Gloster. Who ere he be, you may not be let in. Villains, answer you so the Lord Protector? The Lord protect him, so we answer him, We do no otherwise then we are willed. Who willed you? or whose will stands but mine? There's none Protector of the Realm, but I: Break up the Gates, I'll be your warrantise; Shall I be flouted thus by dunghill Groomes? What noise is this? what Traitors have we here? Lieutenant, is it you whose voice I hear? Open the Gates, here's Gloster that would enter. Have patience Noble Duke, I may not open, The Cardinal of Winchester forbids: From him I have express commandment, That thou nor none of thine shall be let in. Faint-hearted Wooduile, prizest him 'fore me? Arrogant Winchester, that haughty Prelate, Whom Henry our late Sovereign ne'er could brook? Thou art no friend to God, or to the King: Open the Gates, or I'll shut thee out shortly. Open the Gates unto the Lord Protector, Or we'll burst them open, if that you come not quickly. How now ambitious Vmpheir, what means this? Pieled Priest, dost thou command me to be shut out? I do, thou most usurping Proditor, And not Protector of the King or Realm. Stand back thou manifest Conspirator, Thou that contriued'st to murder our dead Lord, Thou that giv'st Whores Indulgences to sin, I'll canvas thee in thy broad Cardinal's Hat, If thou proceed in this thy insolence. Nay, stand thou back, I will not budge a foot: This be Damascus, be thou cursed Cain, To slay thy Brother Abel, if thou wilt. I will not slay thee, but I'll drive thee back: Thy Scarlet Robes, as a Child's bearing Cloth, I'll use, to carry thee out of this place. Do what thou darest, I beard thee to thy face. What? am I dared, and bearded to my face? Draw men, for all this privileged place, Blue Coats to Tawny Coats, Priest, beware your Beard, I mean to tug it, and to cuff you sound. Under my feet I stamp thy Cardinal's Hat: In spite of Pope, or dignities of Church, Here by the Cheeks I'll drag thee up and down. Gloster, thou wilt answer this before the Pope. Winchester Goose, I cry, a Rope, a Rope. Now beat them hence, why do you let them stay? Thee I'll chase hence, thou Wolf in Sheep's array. Out Tawney-Coates, out Scarlet Hypocrite. Fie Lords, that you being supreme. Magistrates, Thus contumeliously should break the Peace. Peace Maior, thou knowst little of my wrongs: Here's Beauford, that regards nor God nor King, Hath here distrayned the Tower to his use.
