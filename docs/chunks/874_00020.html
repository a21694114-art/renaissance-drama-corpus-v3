If word, nor oath Prevail not, go and see: if you can bring Tincture, or lustre in her lip, her eye Heat outwardly, or breath within, I'll serve you As I would do the Gods. Thou didst speak but well, When most the truth: which I receive much better, Then to be pitied of thee. Prithee bring me To the dead bodies of my Queen, and Son, One grave shall be for both: Upon them shall The causes of their death appear (unto Our shame perpetual) once a day, I'll visit The Chapel where they lie, and tears shed there Shall be my recreation. Their sacred will's be done: go get aboard, Look to thy bark, I'll not be long before I call upon thee.
