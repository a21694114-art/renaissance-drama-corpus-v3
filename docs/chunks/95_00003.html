I bless thee here with all such gifts as nature can bestow And for thy sake I would they were as many hundred more Take therewith all this child, to wait upon the stil. A bird of mine some kin to thee, his name is Will. Welcome to me my will, what service canst thou do, All things forsooth, sir when me list and more to.
