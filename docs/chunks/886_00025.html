If you have tears, prepare to shed them now. You all do know this Mantle, I remember The first time ever Caesar put it on, 'Twas on a Summer's Evening in his Tent, That day he overcame the Neruij. Look, in this place ran Cassius Dagger through: See what a rent the envious Caska made: Through this, the well-beloved Brutus stabbed, And as he plucked his cursed Steel away: Mark how the blood of Caesar followed it, As rushing out of doors, to be resolved If Brutus so unkindly knocked, or no: For Brutus, as you know, was Caesar's Angel. judge, O you Gods, how dearly Caesar loved him: This was the most unkindest cut of all. For when the Noble Caesar saw him stab, Ingratitude, more strong than Traitors arms, Quite vanquished him: then burst his Mighty heart, And in his Mantle, muffling up his face, Even at the Base of Pompey's Statue (Which all the while ran blood) great Caesar fell. O what a fall was there, my Countrymen? Then I, and you, and all of us fell down, Whilst bloody Treason flourished over us.
