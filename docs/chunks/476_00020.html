Ah my sweet friend, I feel thy words To comfort my distressed soul, Leave me a while to ponder on my sins. I go sweet Faustus, but with heavy cheer, fearing the ruin of thy hopeless soul. Accursed Faustus, where is mercy now? I do repent, and yet I do despair: Hell strives with grace for conquest in my breast, What shall I do to shun the snares of death? Thou traitor Faustus, I arrest thy soul For disobedience to my sovereign Lord, Revolt, or I'll in piece-meal tear thy flesh. Sweet Mephastophilis, entreat thy Lord To pardon my unjust presumption, And with my blood again I will confirm My former vow I made to Lucifer. Do it then quickly, with unfeigned heart, Lest greater danger do attend thy drift. Faustus, this, or what else thou shalt desire, shallbe performed in twinkling of an eye. Was this the face that launched a thousand ships? And burned the topless Towers of Ilium? Sweet Helen, make me immortal with a kiss: Her lips sucks forth my soul, see where it flies: Come Helen, come give me my soul again.
