SI quisquam est, qui placere se studeat bonis Quam plurimis, et minime multos laedere, In his poeta hic nomen profitetur suum. What? shall we have Latin again? Master Prologue youngster. I pRay you go to the University, and set up your Stage there. What, Mistress Lala Spinster? content yourself, I now repeat that which is past, I do not represent that which is present. I pray you then tell us so much in honest English. I will, for your sake. For mine? nay, for every she, Whom here you see: And for our honest neighbours, many a good man that never Was infected with this raving latin ever. Keep silence, thou particoloured chattering Mappy. en speake sence, thou iabbeing al▪ blacke Iackdaw, with a greene coxcome. Good men, good wives, good friends, good maids, good boys. I marry, this tastes for my stomach, I'm in the number of the fourth rank. Who meet, to see, and hear our childish toys. Well said, such easy drops sink well into our brains. Of late ye know, if ye have not mistook us. I do not mistake you now, you take us very well with your plain dealing English Poetry. we acted Comic Terence his Eunuchus. Eunuchus! Out upon him, and thee too, and all of his kin. I have heard my brother say, that this same Eunuchus is a man and no man, The very scorn and hate of women. I'll be sure, I'll hang, draw and quarter him; and for my part, sir Prologue, I had rather ye should beat us, Then thus defeat us, vs, Then v For my part dame Lall, I do but act my part, you must not interrupt me. I will not corrupt you, unless you name that hateful bilding Jade. O what a queam he hath cast over my stomach with that filthy word. But now we condescend with mild audacity, In vulgar language unto low capacity. See you hold on your vulgar tongue, and bring not hither your latin vulgars, and lessons which you have chanced in the school. We come not with old worn common Thais, trade the sister of Corinthian Lais. Lais? Thais? Walk where your way is, And tell us what this Play is. Nor Parmeno, nor scolding Pythias. Thou pithless ass, dost thou tell us of scolding? This wasp had almost put me out. Out with her. But in a nearer circle now we walk, Where with ourselves, and of ourselves we talk. Our School imagine here new built and dight, This Scene our forms, our day this candle light. Sir Prologue, I fear thou talk'st English extrumpery besides thy part, only to beguile me, I doubt there is Latin in his budget. I avow to thee, jealous Lala, that this same scholar's feast is dressed in English. I dare not trust you, for you say you are here in the school. And you scholars must not speak English in the school. We are not now at our task, but we have leave to play, and we play at our best game.
