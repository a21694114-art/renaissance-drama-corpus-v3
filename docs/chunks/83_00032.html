Be not at one with her upon any amends. No though she make to me never so many friends. Now saint George to borrow, Drum dub a dub afore. What mean you to do sir, commit manslaughter. To kill forty such, is a matter of laughter. And who is it sir, whom ye intend thus to spill? Foolish Custance here forceth me against my will. And is there no mean your extreme wrath to slake. She shall some amends unto your good maship make. I will none amends. Is her offence so sore?

If I were as ye be, by gogs deare mother, I woulde not leaue one stone vpon an other. Though she woulde redéeme it with twentie thousand poundes. It shall be euen so, by his lily woundes. Bee not at one with hir vpon any amendes. No though she make to me neuer so many frendes. Not if all the worlde for hir woulde vndertake, No not God hymselfe neither, shal not hir peace make, On therfore, marche forwarde, soft, stay a whyle yet. On. Tary. Forth. Back. On. Soft. Now forwardset What businesse haue we here? out alas, alas. Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha. Dydst thou sée that Merygréeke? howe afrayde she was? Dydst thou sée how she fledde apace out of my sight? Ah good swéete Custance I pitie hir by this light. That tender heart of yours wyll marre altogether, Thus will ye be turned with waggyng of a fether. On sirs, keepe your ray. On forth, while this geare is hot Soft, the Armes of Caleys, I haue one thing forgot. What lacke we now? Retire, or else we be all slain. Backe for the pashe of God, backe sirs, backe againe. It is a thing must be had. Let vs haue it then. But I wote not where nor how. Then wote not I when. But what is it? Of a chiefe thing I am to séeke. Tut so will ye be, when ye haue studied a weke. But tell me what it is? I lacke yet an hedpiece.
