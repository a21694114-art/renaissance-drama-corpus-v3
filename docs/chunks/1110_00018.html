Would we might see them. All vostro commando, Asterothe, ill pane in hac camera presto. Oh, now 'tis done; mine Host, put but your hand Into that Corner and pull forth two Loaves. Are you in earnest? But see what Art can do. Pox on the Friar, have you these tricks? Two fine and delicate Manchets. The best in Spain, or friar Iohn much mistakes: Place them good mine Host, behind the Salt. I shall. There's more behind. Not possible in Art. Peace Father, more will be done anon; What Climate yields the best French Grape? My Spirit whispers Orleans grabe's the best, What says mine Host to a pure Cup of Orleans? Faith I say, would I might see't. je vou pre Monsieur Asterothe, once more A Cup of Divine Claret; no, a Bottle of some two quarts, Gramercy, thou hast done't, mine Host, but cast your eye Upon that place, and you shall find it there. Most admirable, see, here's a Bottle full. But taste mine Host, and try if it be right. Your Devils take you; you know where's the best liquour. Excellent stuff, I ne'er drank better claret. How comes this? No hurt to you still, Father. Bread and Drink? how shall we come by Meat? Let me see, there is a Midnight supper now served in At Prague in Germany, where the emperor's Court Lies for the most part, and seldom is he absent; Among all all other dainties, speak, what dish Desire you from the Table? Any thing. What say you to a Pullet piping hot, Now standing on the Dresser? Nothing better. The Devil take all such smell-feasts. But once more I'll employ thee, Asterothe, For this night no more trouble thee. Veni, assist, in hac re succurre; I smell the Pullet Smoking, and Sauce unto't. ay, but where? Somewhere about this Room, who hath the Key Of that same Cupboard? Marry Nan, my Wife. Call for it, good mine Host, You see I come near nothing, use fair play, Saw neither fire nor candle to provide this, Touched neither Lock nor Key within your house, But was asleep i'th' straw; unlock mine Host, See what the Cupboard yields. A poison on thee, would it might prove so to thee, It was never provided for those chops. What find you there? A hot fat Pullet, newly dressed and sauced. I never heard the like, 'Tis above wonder. You see what Art can do. Tell me, Friar Iohn? I'll talk with you anon, in the mean time Eat while 'tis hot, 't hath come a pretty journey, And marvel 'tis not cold. Good wine, good bread, good victuals, stomach good, And all to meet together? nay fall to, And he be thanked that sent it. This to me appears beyond imagination, Nay, gentle Hostess sit. Many provide good Cates that taste them not, Now blessing on their hearts. But curse on thine, and on thy stomach too.
