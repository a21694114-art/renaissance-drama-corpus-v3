They will assuredly come: may, CARLO (as thou lov'st me) run over 'em all freely to night, and especially the knight; spare no sulphurous jest that may come out of that sweaty forge of thine: but ply 'em with all manner of shot, minion, saker, culvertine, or any thing what thou willt. I warrant thee, my dear case of petronels, so I stand not in dread of thee, but that thou'lt second me. Why, my good german tapster, I will. What, GEORGE. Lomtero, Lomtero, etc. Did you call, master CARLO? More nectar, GEORGE: Lomtero, etc. Your meat's ready, sir, and your company were come. Is the loin of pork enough? I, sir, it is enough. Pork? heart, what dost thou with such a greasy dish? I think thou dost varnish thy face with the fat on't, it looks so like a gluepot. True, my raw-bon'd-rogue, and if thou wouldst farce thy lean ribs with it too, they would not (like ragged lathes) rub out so many doublets as they do: but thou know'st not a good dish, thou. O, it's the only nourishing meat in the world. No marvel though that saucy, stubborn generation, the Iewes, were forbidden it: for what would they ha' done, well pampered with fat pork, that durst murmur at their maker out of garlic, and onions. 'Slight, fed with it, the whoreson strummell, patched, goggle-eyed Grumbledories, would ha' Gigantomachized. Well said, my sweet GEORGE, fill, fill. This savours too much of profanation. O, servetur ad imum, qualis ab incepto processerit, & sibi conflet. The necessity of his vain compels a toleration: for, bar this, and dash him out of humour, before his time. 'Tis an Axiom in natural philosophy, What comes nearest the nature of that it feeds, converts quicker to nourisment, and doth sooner essentiate. Now nothing in flesh, and entrails, assimulates or resembles man more, than a hog, or swine— True; and he (to requite their courtesy) oftentimes doffeth his own nature, and puts on theirs; as when he becomes as churlish as a hog, or as drunk as a sow: but to your conclusion. Marry, I say, nothing resembling man more than a swine, it follows, nothing can be more nourishing: for indeed (but that it abhors from our nice nature) if we fed one upon another, we should shoot up a great deal faster, and thrive much better: I refer me to your usurous Cannibals, or such like: but since it is so contrary, pork, pork, is your only feed. I take it, your devil be of the same diet; he would ne'er ha' desired to been incorporated into swine else. O, here comes the melancholy mess: upon 'em CARLO, charge, charge. 'Fore god, sir PUNTARVOLO, I am sorry for your heaviness: body a me, a shrewd mischance! why, had you no unicorns horn, nor bezoars stone about you? ha? SIr, I would request you, be silent. Nay, to him again.
