To move wild laughter in the throat of death? It cannot be, it is impossible. Mirth cannot move a soul in agony. Why that's the way to choke a gibing spirit, Whose influence is begot of that lose grace, Which shallow laughing hearers give to fools: A jests prosperity, lies in the ear Of him that hears it, never in the tongue Of him that makes it: then, if sickly ears, Deafed with the clamours of their own dear groans, Will hear your idle scorns; continue then, And I will have you, and that fault withal. But if they will not, throw away that spirit, And I shall find you empty of that fault, Right joyful of your reformation. A twelvemonth?
