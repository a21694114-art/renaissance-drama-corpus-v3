And had I been the mistress of her judgement And constant temper, skilful in the knowledge Of man's malicious falsehood, I had never Upon his hell-deep oaths to marry me, Given up my fair name, and my maiden honour To his foul lust, nor lived now being branded In the forehead for his whore the scorn & Shame Of all good women.
