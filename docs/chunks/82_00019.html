WHat sturdy champion stout doth joy with glee Our chieftains royal bower safe to see, Then to his court I counsel him to wend, 'gainst which the populus rout their force doth bend. The rulers run amazed to fetch the guard, And armed troops of men, their town to ward. Nor woodness rashly caught through fear doth cease, But more and more, their power doth increase. What sudden rage doth beat their brawling brain? The garrisons great with fury astoned again, And stirred up for Queen Octauias sake With monstrous mischief vile, their rage to slake, They rumbling rush into the Palace far. What dare they do, their counsellors who are? Advance their Empress old, subvert the new: And grant her, brothers beds as is her due. Which Poppy now, with whole consent doth hold? Yea that unbridled rage in breast uprolled, Sets them agog, and makes them wondrous wood. whatever image graven in marble stood, If Poppies badge it bore, or if in sight, It tended for to show her beauty bright, Though it on heavenly altars brave did stand, They break, or pull it down, with sword or hand. Some parts with ropes sure tide, they trail them forth Which spurned with dirty feet, as though nought worth With filthy stinking mire, they it all bewray. And with their deeds their talk doth jump agree, Which mine amazed mind, thinks true to be For fiery flames they threat for to prepare, Wherewith to waste, the PRINCE's Palace fair, Unless, unto their furious mood he give His second wife, and with Octavia live, But he by me shall know in what hard stay The City stands: the rulers I'll obey. A lack, what made you cruel wars, in vain To move, sith prisoner love you can not gain You can not him overcome, your fiery flame He recketh not: his sire overcomes the same. He darkened hath those thundering thumps that shake Heaven, Earth, Hell, sea, all things that makes to quake. Yea mighty Ioue, in heaven that wears chief crown His flames from welkin high hath brought adown. And you, not victors now, but vanquished, Shall ransom pay, the price of heart's blood red. Love, patient can not be, but hight in rage, No easy thing it is, his wrath t'assuage, Achilles worthy sight, that was so stout, To twang the Harp he made in Lady's rout, Prince Agamemnon stern that boy benumbed, And rabble rude of Greeks with love brands burned. King Priam's reign he topsy-turvy tossed, Aud goodly Cities great he chiefly lost. And now my mind sore frighted stands aghast, What Cupid's furious force brings us at last.
