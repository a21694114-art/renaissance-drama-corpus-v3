Forsooth sir my mind is this at few words All my pleasure is in catsing of vyrdes And making of snow bails and throwing the same For the which purpose to have set in frame With my god father god I would fain have spoken desiring him to have sent me by some token Where I might have had great frost for my pytfalles And plenty of suowe to make my snow balls This once had boys lives be such as no man leddes O to se my snow balls light on my fellows hedes And to here the birds how they slyckere there wings In the pytfale I say it passeth all things Sir if ye be god's servant or his kinsman I pray you help me in this it ye can. Alas poor boy who sent the hither. A hundred boys that stood together Where they heard one say in a cry That my god father god almighty Was come from heaven by his own accord This night to sup here with my lord And farther he said come whose will They shall sure have their bills full I fall wethers who list to crave Each sort such wether as they list to have And when my fellows thought this would be had And saw me so pretty a prateling lad Upon agreement with a great noys Send little Dick cried all the boys By whose assent I am purveyed To sew for the wether a foresaid Wherein I pray you to be good as thus To help that god may give it us. give boys weather quoth a nonny nonny. If god of his wether will give nonny I pray you will ye sell any Or send us a bushel of snow or twain. And point us a day to pay him again. I can not tell for by this light. I chepte nor barowed none of him this night But by such shift as I will make Thou shalt see soon what way he will take. Sir I thank you then I may depart. The farewell good son with all my heart Now such an other sort as here hath been In all the days of my life I have not seen No suitors now but women knaves and boys And all their suits are in fancies and toys If that there come no wiser after this cry I will to the god and make an end quickly Oyes, if that any knave here Be willing to appear For whether foul or clear Come in before this flock And be he hole or syckly Come show his mind quickly And if his tail be not lyckly Ye shall lick my tail in the neck All his time I perceive is spent in waist To wait for more suitors I see none make hast Wherefore I will show the god all this process
