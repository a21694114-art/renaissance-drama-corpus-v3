Then Mistress Miniver S. Adam's Prickshaft must not hit you; Peter take up all the clothes at the table and the Plums. Save thee my little worshipful Harper; how do ye my little cracknels? how do ye? Welcome M. Tucca, sit and shoot into your belly some Sugar pellets. No, Godamercy Cadwallader, how do you Horace? Thanks good Captain. where's the Sering thou carriest about thee? O have I found thee my scouring-stick; what's my name Bubo? Would I were hanged if I can call you any names but Captain and Tucca. No Fyeest, my name's Hamlet revenge: thou hast been at Paris garden hast not? Yes Captain, I ha' played Zulziman there. Then M. Horace you played the part of an honest man. Death of Hercules, he could never play that part well in's life, no Fulkes you could not: thou call'st Demetrius jorneyman Poet, but thou putst up a Supplication to be a poor jorneyman Player, and hadst been still so, but that thou couldst not set a good face upon't: thou hast forgot how thou amblest (in leather pilch) by a play-wagon, in the high way, and took'st mad Ieronimo's part, to get service among the Mimics: and when the Stagirites banished thee into the I'll of Dogs, thou turnedst Bandog (villainous Guy) & ever since bitest, therefore I ask if thoust been at paris-garden, because thou hast such a good mouth; thou bait'st well, read, lege, save thyself and read. Why Captain these are Epigrams composed on you. Go not out farthing Candle, go not out, for trusty Damboys now the deed is done, I'll pledge this Epigram in wine, I'll swallow it, ay, yes. God bless us, will be drunk with nittigrams now. So, now arise sprite ath Buttery; no Herring-bone I'll not pull thee out, but arise dear Echo rise, rise devil or I'll conjure thee up. Good Master Tucca let's ha' no conjuring here. Uds blood you scald gouty Captain, why come you to set encumbrances here between the Ladies. Be not so tart my precious Metheglin, be not (my old whore a Babylon, sit fast.) O Iesu it I know whereabouts in London Babylon stands. Feed and be fat my fair Calipolis, stir not my beauteous wriggle-tails, I'll disease none of you, I'll take none of you up, but only this table-man, I must enter him into some filthy sink point, I must. Captain, you do me wrong thus to disgrace me. Thou thinkst thou mayst be as saucy with me as my Buff jerkin to sit upon me, dost? Damn me, if ever I traduced your name, What imputation can you charge me with? sblood, ay, what computations can you lay to his charge? answer, or by Sesu I'll canvas your coxcomb Tucky. If they draw sweet hearts, let us shift for ourselves. My noble swaggerer, I will not fall out with thee, I cannot my mad comrade, find in my heart to shed thy blood.
