Will you? by all the leeks that are worn on Saint Dauies day I will buy not only a Coach, with four wheels, but also a white Mare and a stone horse too, because they shall traw you, very lustily, as if the devil were in their arses. How now, more Tailors— How sir; Tailors. O good Captain, 'tis my Cousin. Is he, I will Cozen you then sir too, one day. I hope sir then to Cozen you too. By gad I hobe so, farewell Sidanien. Her's both money, and sugar. O sweet villain, set it up. 'sfoot, what tame swaggerer was this I met Doll. A Captain, a Captain: but hast 'scaped the Dunkirks honest Philip? Philip rials are not more welcome: did thy father pay the shot? He paid that shot, and then shot pistolets into my pockets: hark wench: chinck chink, makes the punk wanton and the bawd to wink. O rare music. Heavenly consort, better than old Moons. But why? why Dol, go these two like beadles in blue? ha? There's a moral in that: flay off your skins, you precious Cannibals: O that the Welsh Captain were here again, and a drum with him, I could march now, ran, tan, tan, tara, ran, tan, tan, sirrah Philip has thy father any plate in's house. Enough to set up a goldsmith's shop. Canst not borrow some of it? we shall have guests tomorrow or next day and I would serve the hungry ragamuffins in plate, though 'twere none of mine own. I shall hardly borrow it of him but I could get one of mine Ants, to beat the bush for me, and she might get the bird. Why pree, let me be one of thine Ants, and do it for me then. As I'm virtuous and a Gentlewoman i'll restore. Say no more 'tis done. What manner of man is thy father? 'sfoot i'd fain see the witty Monkey because thou sayst he's a Poet: i'll tell thee, what i'll do: Leverpoole or Chartly, shall like my Gentleman usher go to him, and say such a Lady sends for him, about a sonnet or an epitaph for her child that died at nurse, or for some device about a mask or so; if he comes you shall stand in a corner, and see in what State i'll bear myself: he does not know me, nor my lodging. No, no. Is't a match Sirs? shall's be merry with him and his muse. Agreed, any scaffold to execute knavery upon. I'll send then my vaunt-courier presently: in the mean time, march after the Captain, scoundrels, come hold me up: Look how Sabrina sunk i'th' river Severn, So will we four be drunk i'th' shipwreck Tavern. Come Wife, our two gallants will be here presently: I have promised them the best of entertainment, with protestation never to reveal to thee their slander: I will have thee bear thyself, as if thou madest a feast upon Simon and Iudes day, to country Gentlewomen, that came to see the Pageant, bid them extremely welcome, though thou wish their throats cut; 'tis in fashion.
