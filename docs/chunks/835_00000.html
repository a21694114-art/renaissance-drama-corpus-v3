FLora, Flora! We call thee heere, We call thee heere, From forth thy fragrant Bower, Thou Queene of euery laughing Flower, Appeare! Appeare to vs, To vs appeare: Thou Banquet of the Yeare, Or if a Name may be more sweet, more deere. Harke, Summer harke, Marke, Autumne, marke, How coughing Winter mournes to see This smiling Houre, Would it were nipt for me, But soft I feele no such decay But I may liue to kisse faire May, And in the Morne and Euening howers, Leaue my cold sweats vpon the Flowers. Alasse poore Mumps, at thy weake power We laugh, —Oh—Oh—O— He's strucke cold At Floraes first appearing, Looke, in a Sound, Will drop to'th ground. Helpe, helpe, helpe, he wants your cheering. Oh I confesse Feild Emperesse, The Beauty of thy power amazes, I am content to ioyne With those three Friends of thine, And helpe to chant thy prayses; Now all the Seasons of the Yeare agree To giue, (Faire Flora) the prime place to Thee. Where's Hyacinth! the Boy Appollo loude, And turnde into a Flower? Here, Queene of sweetnes. Adonis! thou that for thy beauteous chastity, Wert turnde into the chastest of all Flowers, (The closse-infolded Rose) blowen into Blushes It is so mayden-modest, What's thy pleasure Faire Empresse of sweete Odours, Willing Seruants! I haue Employment for you both, and speedy, We waite with much Ioy to receiue the charge on't; Hast, to the two Assisting Magistrates, Those worthy Citty Consulls, Beare our sweete wishes to e'm, and speake Ioy From vs, to both their Feasts, And to that part of their Graue-worthy Guesse Which here we misse to day, though here be those Whom we ought more especially to Honor, Say though we cannot there our selfe appeare, Because we owe our greater seruice here, Yet that they shal not faile of all their due, We send the wishes of our Heart by you. Which shall be faithfully tendred, Tis presum'd; But to this faire Assembly present now I, and these yeelding Sweets all their heads bow In honour of this Feast, of the Day, chiefe, Made solemne by the workes of your Reliefe, Your Cares, your Charities, the holy Vse Of pious exercise; all which infuse Blessings into your Fortunes, you abound In temporall things, cause blessed fruits are found Vpon the Stocks you graft on, marke the Encrease;
