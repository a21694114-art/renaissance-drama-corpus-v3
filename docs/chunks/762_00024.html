What sir; why Leadenhall, could you not see the four Spouts as you came along? Certenemento Leden hall, I hit my head by de way, dare may be de voer Spouts: I pray de gratia, wish be de weigh to Crochefriers? How, to Croched-friers? Marry you must go along till you come to the Pump, and then turn on your right hand. signior, adio. Farewell and be hanged signior: Now for your fellow, if the Ass would come. By my trot me do so much tincke of dit gentlewoman de fine Wenshe, dat me tincke esh hover ten day, and esh day ten year, till I come to her: Here be de huise of sin vader, sall all and knock. What a bot's ail you, are you mad? Will you run over me and break my Glasses? Glasses, what Glasses? Pray is monsieur Pisaro to de mayson? Hark Ned, there's thy substance Nay by the Mass, the substance's here, The shadow's but an Ass. What Master Pisaro? loggerhead, here's none of your Pisaro's? Yes but dit is the houis of mester Pisaro. Will not this monsieur Motley take his answer? I'll go and knock the ass about the pate. Nay by your leave sir, but I'll hold your worship. This stir we should have had, had you stood there. Why, would it not vex one to hear the ass, Stand prating here of dit and dan, and den and dog? One of thy mettle Ned, would surely do it: But peace, and hark to the rest. Do no de fine Gentlewoman matresse Mathea dwell in dit Plash? No sir, here dwells none of your fine Gentlewoman: 'twere a good deed sirrah, to see who you are; You come hither to steal my Glasses. And then counterfeit you are going to your Queans. I be deceu dis dark neight; here be no Wenshe, I be no in de right plash: I pray Monsieur, what be name dis Street, and wish be de way to Croshe-friers? Marry this is Fanchurch-streete, And the best way to Crotched-friers, is to follow your nose Vanshe, street, how shaunce me come to Vanshe street? vell monsieur, me must all to Croche-friers. Farewell fortypence, go seek your signior, I hope you'll find yourselves two Dolts anon: Hush Ferdinand, I hear the last come stamping hither. Ha sirrah, I have left my fat Dutchman, and run myself almost out of breath too: now to my young mistresses go I, somebody cast an old shoe after me: but soft, how shall I do to counterfeit the Dutchman, be cause I speak English so like a natural; Tush, take you no thought for that, let me alone for Squintum squantum: soft, her's my masters house, whose's there. whose's there, why sir here is: Nay that's too good English; Why here be de growtte Dutchman. Then there's not only a grout head, but an Ass also. What be you, you be an English Ox to call a gentle moan Ass. Hark Ned yonder's good greeting.
