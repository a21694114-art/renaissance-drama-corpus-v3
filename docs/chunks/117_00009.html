That cock is stolen. What was he fet out of the hens-rust? I can not tell where the devil he was kept, under key or lock. But Tib hath tickled in gammer's ear, that you should steal the cock Have I strong hoore? by bread and salt. What soft, I say be still. Say not one word for all this gear. By the mass that I will, I will have the young hoar by the head, & the old trot by the throat Not one word dame Chat I say, not one word for my coat. Shall such a beggar's brawl as the thinkest thou make me a thief The pocks light on her hores sides, a pestilence & a mischief Come out thou hungry needy bitch, o that my nails be short. gog's bred woman hold your peace, this gear will else pass sport I would not for an hundred pound, this matter should be known, That I am author of this tale, or have abroad it blown Did ye not swear ye would be ruled, before the tale I told I said ye must all secret keep, and ye said sure ye would. Would you suffer yourself diccon, such a sort, to revile you With slanderous words to blot your name, & so to defile you? No goodwife chat I would be loath such drabs should blot my name But yet ye must so order all, the Diccon hear no blame. Go to then, what is your read: say on your mind, (ye shall me rule herein. God a mercy to dame chat, in faith thou must the gear begin It is twenty pound to a goose turd, my gammer will not tarry But hitherward she comes as fast as her legs can her carry, To brawl with you about her cock, for well & hard Tib say The Cock was roasted in your house, to breakfast yesterday, And when ye had the carcase eaten, the feathers ye out flung And Doll your maid the legs she hid a foot deep in the dung. Do gracious god my heart is bursts. Well rule yourself a space And gammer gurton when she cometh anon into this place Then to the Quean let's see tell her your mind & spare not So shall Diccon blameless be, and then go to I care not. Then sure beware her throat, I can abide no longer In faith old witch it shallbe seen, which of us two be stronger And Diccon but at your request, I would not stay one hour, Well keep it in till she be here and then out let it power, In the meanwhile get you in, and make no words of this More of this matter with in this hour to hear you shall not miss Because I know you are my friend, hide it I could not doubtless Ye know your harm, see ye be wise about your own business So fare ye will.
