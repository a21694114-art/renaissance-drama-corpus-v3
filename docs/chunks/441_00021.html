The great swollen bellied Burghers get brown Bills, As to drive rascal beggars from their doors; The madding people so amazed with fear, That turning head with every little noise, Stopped up the entrance of the streets with throngs, That when Count Egmont, Hawry and the rest, Called to the Burghers for supplies of men, The uncertain murmur of the multitude, Increased but the confusion of the town: The villainous and dastard recreant Almains, Kneel to the Spaniards, casting down their arms.
