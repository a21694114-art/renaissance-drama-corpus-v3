Why then master you are jealous? jealous knave, how can I be but jealous, to see you ever so familiar together? Thou art not only content to drink away my goods, but to abuse my wife. Two good qualities, drunkenness and lechery, but master are you jealous? I knave and thou shalt know it ere I pass, for I will beswindge thee while this rope will hold. My good husband abuse him not, for he never proffered you any wrong, Nay whore, thy part shall not be behind. Why suppose master I have offended you, is it lawful for the master to beat the servant for all offences? I marry is it knave. Then master will I prove by logic, that seeing all sins are to receive correction, the master is to be corrected of the man, and sir I pray you, what greater sin is, than jealousy? 'tis like a mad dog that for anger bites himself. Therefore that I may do my duty to you good master, and to make a white son of you, I will so beswinge jealousy out of you, as you shall love me the better while you live. What beat thy master knave? What beat thy man knave? and I master, and double beat you, because you are a man of credit, and therefore have at you the fairest for forty pence. Alas wife, help, help, my man kills me. Nay, even as you have baked so brew, jealousy must be driven out by extremities. And that will I do, mistress. Hold thy hand Adam, and not only I forgive and forget all▪ but I will give thee a good Farm to live on. Begun Peasant, out of the compass of my further wrath, for I am a corrector of vice, and at night I will bring home my mistress. Even when you please good Adam. When I please, mark the words, 'tis a lease parol, to have and to hold, thou shalt be mine for ever, and so let's go to the Alehouse. Where servants 'gainst masters do rebel, The Commonweal may be accounted hell. For if the feet the head shall hold in scorn, The City's state will fall and be forlorn. This error London, waiteth on thy state, Servants amend, and masters leave to hate. Let love abound, and virtue reign in all, So God will hold his hand that threateneth thrall. What strange encounters met you on the sea? That thus your Bark is battered by the floods, And you return thus sea-wrecked as I see. Most mighty governor the chance is strange, The tidings full of wonder and amaze, Which better than we, our M. can report. M. discourse us all the accident.
