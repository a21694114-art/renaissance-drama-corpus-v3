And I will, I bid thee that thy money ne'er shall do, save thee. Beshrew you sir, you made me start, What is your will with me? I speak to thee As to the Drawer at the vintner's Bar: What is all paid? And like the Bar-boy I When you bring ready money, and keep touch All's paid sir, and y'are welcome. 'Tis confessed. What's he that looks so ghastly? 'Tis the Devil. My Pen and Inkhorn bless me. All those crosses that I have figured on the Princes Coin, stand still betwixt me and danger, These are discharged, what refuge hast thou now? Nay bring me unto all thy Creditors; I'll prove thy debts discharged. I pray how many Of our best London Gallants that hope Heaven, Can say the like? merit I Hell for that? But I must have my bargain. Two words to't. Hast thou not owed to these? Most true, I have. Resolve me friends, as you are Gentlemen, In what known sums did this man stand engaged to Each of you? I know not why you ask sir, But the time was he aught me some few pieces, I thought them desperate once, but nobly, since He to the full hath seen me satisfied. I never met with a more generous debtor, I only trusted him with some few sums, And he hath paid me use and principal, A thing I ne'er expected. That's all Gentlemen— morrow The like to you; I wonder what the Devil Slightall ails, he looks so strangely on us? Sparks no doubt, of his first madness. Do but name the man, to whom thou canst appeal. I must confess I'm gone by th'common Law, The Chancery too, because of thee, my conscience, If to any, I appeal to the Church. And to whom there? To any reverent Father. Then to these, to both, or either, freely take thy choice. Holy friar Bernard then, my parting breath Could not desire a better confessor. read there, and tell me what thou think'st of that. Give me but leave to take my second eyes, I will resolve you presently. In th'interim, what's the best news in the parish? Where I live? Yes. I'll tell thee all I know, We are better to the Suburbs than they planted Within the City; thereon holy days The tavern doors are ever shut till six, Else is the Vintner fined; but there where we Keep rendezvous may be let in at three, and never stir The Informers. Horrible; what tremor this begets? Is that the Devil? So this Indenture speaks. Ten thousand Creeds, as many Paternosters, Ave Maries stand between me and harm. In these conditions, I like a cunning Lawyer find a Clause to cheat the Devil, and to save a soul; Be'st thou the greatest fell with Lucifer, Nay he himself, I now am armed for thee: what claimest Thou of this man? I claim a soul. Which is not forfeit. Show me reasons why.
