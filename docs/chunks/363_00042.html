Why wherefore is sir Raphe so late abroad? Why he meant to kill a Buck, I'll say so to save his honesty, but my Nan was his mark, & when he sent me for his bow, and when I came, I holloa'd for him, but I never saw such luck to miss him, it hath almost made me mad. Well stay with us, perhaps sir Raphe and he, Will come anon, hark I do here one hollo.
