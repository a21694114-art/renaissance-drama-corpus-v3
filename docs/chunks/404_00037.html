Why are you so sure of your hand at all times? Tut, never mistrust upon my soul. Mass I would not stand in signor Giuliano state, then; And you meet him, for the wealth of Florence. Why signor, by Iesu if he were hear now: I would not draw my weapon on him, let this gentleman do his mind but I will bastinado him (by heaven) & ever I meet him. faith and i'll have a fling at him. Look yonder he goes I think. 'Sblood what luck have I, I cannot meet with these bragging rascals, It's not he: is it? Yes faith it is he? I'll be hanged then if that were he. Before God it was he: you make me swear. Vpon my saluation it was h. Well had I thought it had been he: he could not have gone so, but I cannot be induced to believe it was he yet. Oh gallant have I found you? draw to your tools, draw, or by God's will i'll thrash you. signor hear me? Draw your weapons then: Signior, I nuer thought it till now: body of S. George, I haue a warrant of the peace serued on me euen now, as I came along by a waterbearer, this gentleman saw it, signior Mat. The peace? 'Sblood, you will not draw? Hold signor hold, under thy favour forbear. Prate again as you like this you whoreson cowardly rascal, you'll control the point you? your consort he is gone? had he stayed he had shared with you infayth. Well gentlemen bear witness I was bound to the peace, by jesu. Why and though you were sir, the law allows you to defend yourself; that's but a poor excuse. I cannot tell; I never sustained the like disgrace (by heaven) sure I was struck with a Planet then, for I had no power to touch my weapon. I like enough I have heard of many that have been beaten under a planet; go get you to the Surgeons, 'sblood and these be your tricks, your passadoes, & your Mountauntoes ilenone of them: oh God that this age should bring forth such creatures? come cozen. Mass i'll have this cloak. God's will: it's Giullianos. Nay but 'tis mine now, another might have tane it up aswell as I, I'll wear it so I will. How and he see it, he'll challenge it assure yourself. I but he shall not have it; I'll say I bought it. Advise you cozen, take heed he give not you as much. Now trust me Prospero you were much to blame, T'incense your brother and disturb the peace, Of my poor house, for there be sentinels, That every minute watch to give alarames, Of civil war, without adjection, Of your assistance and occasion.
