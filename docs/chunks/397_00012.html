Porter what Porter where's this drowsy ass? Who calls? my Lord of Gloster all alone? Alone and have your wisdoms company, Pray where's the stammering chatterer your son? He's ever running but he makes small haste, I'll bring his lither legs in better frame, And if he serve me thus another time. Hark sir your clients knock, and't be your pie, Let him vouchsafe to chatter us some news. Tell him we dance attendance in our chamber.
