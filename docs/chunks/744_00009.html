Send but his head from Spain, to tread upon, And I should count it an valued gift. As for good Savoy, and his German Friends, Ere long I'll set them free, or make the soil That holds them prisoners a Marsh-ground for blood, Till I hear news from Spain of good success. Each day I live will be a year of grief. Pleaseth my Liege to lay aside your sorrow, And with your Royal person grace my house, Clynton will hold it an exceeding favour.
