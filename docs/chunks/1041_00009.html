My letters want but sealing, follow me To the general's Tent. Fellow Bateman farewell commend me to my old windmill at Rudington, oh the Mooter dish, the miller's thumb and the maid behind the Hopper? tell mistress Ursula I fight for her sake, and will live as long as I can die when I can no longer live, yet will love her in spite of her heart, in stead of nutmegs, and ginger, I send her the three bawbees I got at Dundee. Mary's, and tell'em my Chops water to chime all in? As it is in the painted cloth, in morning still when thou dost rise see that in mind thou have to spend the day that doth ensue as bed might be thy Grave; commend me to my learned brother Spritchall the Cobbler of Nottingham brig? and bid him look up, and give me a coal, wishing him good health, as my cat, and I was at the making hereof? I will be mindful of you all farewell. Now is the time make your appearance? Shoot shoot. An the bred an gad man spear the bonny lasses. Down with the bonny Bells? And what are you pretty morsel. An the deal an the crag i'll a Lards wife ganging to seek my Lourden; And you are ganging to your Lurden, that your Lurden may catch you by the crag, and claw you are the weame, till your guts garr haggergath, haggergath. Will you be contented to leave the wicked, and live among the familists, exercising your body in the brether-hood's cause? An the Lard nare thee with an my bare bones. Kiss again then— in sincerity she Kisses open mouthed like a zealous sister— And you can wash, and scour, and help to launder the camp, and dress the booties we steal, and at night be content to Kennel with me in straw. I by Saint Andrew? Let us congregate ourselves, and ponder on their miseries. Now is the time each man draw, and fight.
