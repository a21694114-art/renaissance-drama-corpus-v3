Yet where ye will I swear by saint quintine Ye passed them all both in your own conceit and mine If we had wether to walk at our pleasure Our lives would be merry out of measure One part of the day for our appareling Another part for eating and drinking And all the teste in streets to be walking Orin the house to passed time with talk ynge. When serve ye god. Who boasteth in virtue are but daws. Ye do the better namely sins there is no cause How spend ye this night In dancing and singing Till midnight and then fall to sleeping. Why sweet heart by your false faith can ye sing Nay nay but I love it all thing. Now by my troth for the love that I own you You shall here what pleasure I can show you One song I have for you such as it is And if it were better ye should have it by gys. Marry sir I thank you even heartily. Come on sirs but now let us sing lustily. Sir this is well done I heartily thank you Ye have done me pleasure I make god a vow Ones in a night I long for such a fit For longe time have I been brought up in it. Oft time is seen both in court and town. Long be women a bringing up & soon brought down So feet it is, so neat it is, so nice it is, So trick it is, so quick it is, so wise it is I fear myself except I may entreat her I am so far in love I shall forget her. Now good master I pray ye let me kiss ye Kys me quoth a why nay sir I iwis ye. What yes hardly kiss me once and no more I never desired to kiss you before. Why have ye always kiss her behind In faith good enough if it be your mind And if your appetite serve you so to do By lady I would you had kiss my arse to. To whom dost thou speak foul hore canst thou tell. Nay by my troth I sir not very well But by coniectour this ges I have That I do speak to an old bawdy knave I saw you dally with your symper de cocked I read you beware she pick not your pocket Such idle husyfes do now and than Think all well won that they pick from a man Yet such of some men shall have more favour Than we that for them daily tail and labour But I trust the god will be so indifferent That she shall fail some part of her intent No doubt he will deal so graciously That all folk shall be served indifferently How be it I tell the truth my office is such That I must report each suit little or much Wherefore with the god sins thou caused not speak Trust me with thy suit I will not fail it to break.
