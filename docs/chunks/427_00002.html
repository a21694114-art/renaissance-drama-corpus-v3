Is this true, Pistol? No, it is false, if it is a pickpurse. Ha', thou mountain Forreyner: Sir Iohn, and Master mine, I combat challenge of this Latin Bilbo: word of denial in thy labras here; word of denial; froth, and scum thou liest. By these gloves, than 'twas he. Be advised sir, and pass good humours: I will say marry trap with you, if you run the nut-hooks humour on me, that is the very note of it. By this hat, than he in the red face had it: for though I cannot remember what I did when you made me drunk, yet I am not altogether an ass. What say you Scarlet, and Iohn? Why sir, (for my part) I say the Gentleman had drunk himself out of his five sentences. It is his five senses: fie, what the ignorance is. And being fap, sir, was (as they say) cashiered: and so conclusions past the Careires. I, you spoke in Latin then to: but 'tis no matter; I'll ne'er be drunk whilst I live again, but in honest, civil, godly company for this trick: if I be drunk, I'll be drunk with those that have the fear of God, and not with drunken knaves.
