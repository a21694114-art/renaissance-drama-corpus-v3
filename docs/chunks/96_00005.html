Strike of his head at one blow: we be Soldiers, Gog's heart let him know. O the passion of God, I have done by mine honesty: I will take your part héerafter verily. Then content let us agreed: Shake hands with me, I shake hands with thee. You are full of courtesy, that is the best: And you take great pain, ye are a mannerly gest. Why masters do you not know me? the truth to me tell: Not trust us, not very well. Why I am Ambidexter who many soldiers do love Gog's heart to have thy company needs we must prove. We must play with both hands with our hosts & host Play with both hands and score on the post. Now and then with our Captain for many a delay? We will not stick with both hands to play. The honester man ye, ye may me trust. Enter Meretrix with a staff on her shoulder. What? is there no lads here that hath a lust: To have a passing Trul to help at their need? Gog's heart she is come indeed. What mistress Meretrix by his wounds welcome to me: What will ye give me I pray you let me see? By his heart she looks for gifts by and by. What master Ruf, I cry you mercy. The last time I was with you I got a broken head. And lay in the street all night for want of a bed. Gog's wounds kiss me my Trul so white: In thee I swear is all my delight. If thou shouldest have had a broken bed for my sake: I would have made his head to ache. What master Ambidexter, who looked for you? Mistress Meretrix I thought not to see you here now. There is no remedy at meeting I must have a kiss: What man? I will not stick for that by gisse. So now gramercy, I pray thee be gone: Nay soft my friend I mean to have one. Nay soft I swear, and if ye were my brother: Before I let go I will have an other. Gog's heart the whore would not kiss me yet: If I be a whore. thou art a knave than it is quit. But hearst thou Meretrix, with who this night wilt the lyee With him that giveth the most money. Gog's heart, I have no money in purse, ne yet in clout: Then get thee hence and pack like a Lout. Adieu like a Whore. Farewell like a knave. Gog's Nails, Mistress Meretrix now he is gone: A match ye shall make strait with me: I will give thee sixpences to lie one night with thee. Gog's heart slave dost thou think I am a six penny Iug:: Not wis ye Iack I look a little more smug. I will give her xviij. pnce to serve me first: Gramercy Snuff, thou art not the worst.
