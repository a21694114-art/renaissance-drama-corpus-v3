Then I'll prefer (sir) the French to your Dislike or praise: whom though a surly Don, Calls an impertinent people; giddy Trifles? yet in my esteem they merit Highly. They are active even in discourse Let us begin cheerly, No matter On what slight or trivial subject; Be it On that single melancholy hair upon Your chin. Rise and fall by my example. M'affaires call me to Court, serviteur tres humble. Is't possible? This Lord must rise when I am Duke: I'll prefer none but such as can speak French and dance; Crispino, prepare my Bath, I'll distil and grow amorous. Madam you encourage me To serve your goodness, my Lord you undo me, With too much honour. signior, if your eye take delight in prospect There's a room will feed it richly, Show him Carintha, some Affairs call me to Court.
