Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Master Bungler, Master Bungler, you're mightily mistook, I am content to do a gentleman a pleasure for once, so his pawn be neat and sufficient. Why what say you to my Grandfathers seal ring here I marry sir, this is somewhat like. Nay view it well, an ancient arms, I can tell you. What's this sir? The great codpiece with nothing in't. How? The word about it, parturiunt montes. What's that I pray sir? You promise to mount us. And belike he was not so good as his word. So it should seem by the Story, for so our names came to be Bunglers. A lamentable hearing that so great a house should shrink and fall to ruin. Two quatres and yet lose it heart, boy?▪ i'faith? what is't? Five pound sir. By my troth this boy goes forward well, ye shall see him come to his preferment i'th' end! Why how now, whose's that gentlemen, a bargeman? I never have any luck Gallants, till my doublets off, I'm not half nimble enough, at this old finquanter drivelbeard: Your worship must pay me all these sir? There, and feast the devil with 'em. Hell gnaw these dice. What do you give over Gallants. Is't not time. I protest, I have but one angel left to guide me home to my lodging. How much thinkst? Some fourscore angels, sir. Peace, we'll join powers anon, and see how strong we are in the whole number, mass, yond guilt Goblet stands so full in mine eye, the whoreson tempts me, it comes like cheese after a great feast, to digest the rest, he will hardly scape me i'faith I see that by him already; back for a parting blow now,--- boy? Anon anon sir: Fetch a pennyworth of soft wax to seal letters. I will sir? Nay had not I strange casting, thrice together, 2. quatres and a dews. Why was not I as often haunted with two treas, and a quater: there's wax for your worship— anon, anon, sir. Screen me a little you whoreson old cross biter. Why what's the business? fill chiton hob Goblet Goblet. And what has master Bowser lost? Faith not very deeply sir, enough for a scholar, some half a score royals. 'sfoot I have lost as many with spurs at their heels. Come Gallants shall we stumble: What's o'clock. Here's none on't Dick, the Goblets carried down. Nay 'tis upon the point of three, boy, Drawer, what's to be done sirs? All's paid, and your worships are welcome, Only there's a Goblet missing Gentlemen, and cannot be found about house. How a Goblet: What manner a one. A guilt Goblet sir, of an indifferent size. 'sfoot I saw such a one lately. It cannot be found now sir. Came there no strangers here? No sir. This a marvelous matter, that a Goblet should be gone, and none but we in the room, the loss is mere all, here as we are; keep the door Vintner. No I beseech your worship.
