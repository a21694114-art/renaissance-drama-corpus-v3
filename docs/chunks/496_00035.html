I am a weak Scholar my Lord, But on condition that your Highness, not these reverent Lords, Will take no exceptions at my woman's wit, I am content to hold them Argument: And first with reverence to his Majesty. Pray tell me, why would ye make the King believe. His Highness and the people under him, Are tied so strictly to obey the Pope? Because fair Queen he is God's Deputy. So are all Kings; and God himself commands The King to rule, and people to obey. And good my Liege who knows their proud intent, That thus rebel against your government.
