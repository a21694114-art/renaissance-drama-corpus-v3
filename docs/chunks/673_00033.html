Be gentle Madam. If thou be'st gentle leave me Mahomet Our loves like our religions are at wars And I disclaim all peace. And I a lovers smoothness: your uncle's dead His power is mine, and you must go. Soul of wrongs: whither? you're both to weak there's more than woman in me: villain, slave: You urge me unto violence come to my chamber. In hell or in my grave: a rape, treason: treason. A guard, a guard. Death of my hope the Court is up. From hence the voice was heard, be circumspect. Treason, treason. Who speaks that word? Iulia your Sovereign. Silence or thou diest. Error of darkness in what Labyrinth Our souls are plunged: raise the Court: Iulia? I. Iulia and Mulleasses? Iulia and Mulleasses fond Venetian Prevented at the point of happiness: Thus I redeem her. And like Cephalus kill thine own Procris. Save me. Thy death shall be her freedom infidel. Why stop you in your courses short breathed Christians? Nail us together. Now methinks I stand Lk a prod Lyon with a richer prize Than Nessus would have stolen from Hercules And dare your envies: my death unto your state shallbe as ominous as his poisoned shirt: Your false Protector's dead: he mocked your griefs And made you weep at Julia's funeral, Whose hope I underwrought, and now had worn The wreath of Florence: Love and ambition, Kindled my cold brain from their mutual heat Sprung my aspiring aim: nor shall it sink But in the death of Iulia: since I cannot Quench my hot thirst of Lust, and cool the heat That hotter than the coals of Parta Burn in my liver: like the snowy Dragon, Tangling the Elephant in his snarled orbs: I'll die in the pursuit of my desire, And mix our bloods in death to sate my fire, Hold monster. Damnation on thy soul. Thy death shall ransom her. Death double thy feared force, and it some form Affright pale Hecate darken the Moon, I like the Sun, backed on th'Arcadian beast, When in his burning progress he did singe Adonis' gardens: from my soul's fair light Chase cloudy fear: and like Thetis son, When he was ointed with Ambrosia Am more than fire-proof: lives Iulia yet? She lives damned villain and outlives thy hate. Death had been kind in her: with her I might Under the cool shades of Elysium Played before Pluto and made Proserpine As jealous as Iuno of my love— But since I must not Up from the dark earth's exhalations Thicker than Lernas foggy mists and hide me: I cannot lose their sight, hell of fear! It flies our eager steps: follow, follow. What means these clamours: Borgias? Hah, Borgias:
