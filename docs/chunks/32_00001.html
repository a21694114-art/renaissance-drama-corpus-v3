Ye whoreson trowest thou so Be aware least thou thither go Hence caitiff go the way Or with my dagger I shall the slay Hens knave out of this place Or I shall lay the on the face Sayest thou that I shall go to hell For ever more there to dwell I had liefer thou had evil far A yet sir do by my read And ask mercy for thy misdeed And thou shalt be an herytoure of bliss Where all joy and mirth is Where thou shall see a glorius sight Of angels singing with saints bright Before the face of God what sirs above the sky I had need of a ladder to climb so high But what and the ladder slip Than I am deceived yet And if I fall I catch a quecke I may fortune to break my neck And that joint is ill to set Nay nay not so O yet remember call to thy mind The mercy of God passeth all thing For it is written by noble clerks The mercy of God passeth all works That witnesseth holy scripture saying thus Miseratio domine super omnia opera ●iu● Therefore doubt not gods grace There of is plenty in every place What me think ye be clerkyshe For ye speak good gibbryshe Sir I pray you and you have any store Soil me a question or ye cast out any more Lest when your cunning is all done My question have no solution Sir and it please you this why do men eat mustered with salt fish Sir I pray you soil me this quistion That I have put to your discretion Thus question is but vanity It longeth not to me Such questions to assoil Sir by god that me dear bought I see your cunning is little or nought And I should follow your school Sun ye would make a fool Therefore crack no longer here Lest I take you on the ear And make your head to ache Sir it falleth not for me to fight Nether by day ne be night therefore do my counsel I say Than to heaven thou shalt have the wai No sir I think ye will not fight But to
