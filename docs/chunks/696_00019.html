If I could meet my enemies one by one thus, I might make pretty shift with 'em in time, And make 'em know, she that has wit, and spirit, May scorn to live beholding to her body for meat, Or for apparel like your common dame, That makes shame get her clothes, to cover shame. Base is that mind, that kneels unto her body, As if a husband stood in awe on's wife, My spirit shall be Mistress of this house, As long as I have time in't.—— oh Here comes my man that would be: 'tis his hour.
