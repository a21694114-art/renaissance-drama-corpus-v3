You will restore that box? Her will not restore her, 'twas Mary Bloodhounds gave her the box, Randalls have married Mary Bloodhounds and guled Ancients, mark her now. Mark him good Sir, methinks he says he has married Mary Bloodhound. Hang him, he's mad! 'zounds, make Tog of Randalls? come out here Maries. Look here was Mary Bloodhounds. Enter Maid & Hugh. now, I pray tumble down of her Marybones, and ask her father blessing? This, why this is your Maid Widow, This is Mary the Widow's Maid man. And here is Mary Ploodhound, my choleric shred of Cadwallader, married to this Gentleman, who has a hundred a year dangling at your girdle there. I pray Mistress are you married to this Gentleman? By six i'th' morning forsooth, he took me for Mary Ploodhound, having it seems, never seen any on's before, and I being something amorously affected, as they say to his Welsh ditties, answered to her name, lay with him all night, and married him this morning, so that as he took me for her, I took him as he was forsooth. she means for a fool, I am fain to answer for you. Ha, ha, ha, Cupid this twenty four hours has done nothing but cut cross capers. Do ye hear, sir Bartholomew Bayard, that leap before ye look, it will handsomely become you, to restore the Box to that Gentleman, and the Magnitude of your desire, upon this dainty, that is so amorously taken with your ditties. Her wail in woe, her plunge in pain. And yet begar her do not neither, Randalls will prove herself Prittaines borne, and because her understands Ancients was brave fellows, and great Travellers, there is her box for her. I thank ye. And because was no remedies, before her aule, here will Randals embrace Maries, and take a pusse— Save ye gallants, do ye want any guess? Call me thy Coz, and carry it handsomely. Who have we here trow? Dost thou know the Gentleman that whispered to thee? Oh wondrous well, he bid me call him Coz, and carry it handsomely. Widow, wood I were off again. Know all, this Gentleman has to obtain his Lust, and loose desires, served me this seven months, under the shape and name of Iarvis. Possible? Look well, do ye not know him? The very face of Iarvis. I truly Father, and he were any thing like him, I would swear 'twere he. I must cast my skin; and am catched— why Coz. Come y'are cozened, And with a Noble craft, he tempted me In mine own house, and I bid him keep's disguise But till this morning and he should perceive, I loved him truly, intending here before ye, To let him know't, especially i'th' presence Of you Sir, that intend me for your Wife. What should this mean? Some witty trick I warrant thee, prithee dispatch him presently, that we were at Church. First, then know ye for truth sir, I mean never to marry. How Woman?
