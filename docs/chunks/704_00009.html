I'll tell you, sir, the monstrous hazards you shall run with a wife. Good sir! have I ever cozened any friends of yours of their land? bought their possessions? taken forfeit of their mortgage? begged a reversion from 'em? bastarded their issue? what have I done, that may deserve this?
