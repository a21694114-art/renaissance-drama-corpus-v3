They wear those names, That come to murder me. Yet send for CATO, And QVINTUS CATULUS; those I dare trust: And FLACCUS, and POMTINIUS, the Praetors, By the back way. Take care, good brother MARCUS, Your fears be not formed greater, than they should; And make your friends grieve, while your enemies laugh. 'Tis brother's counsel, and worth thanks. But do As I entreat you. I provide, not fear. Was CAESAR there, say you? CURIUS says, he met him, Coming from thence. O, so. And, had you a counsel Of ladies too? Who was your speaker, madame? She that would be, had there been forty more; SEMPRONIA, who had both her greek, and figures; And ever and anon, would ask us, if The witty Consul could have mended that? Or Orator CICERO could have said it better? she's my gentle enemy. Would CETHEGUS Had no more danger in him. But, my guards Are you, great powers; and th'unbated strengths Of a firm conscience, which shall arm each step ta'en for the state: and teach me slack no pace For fear of malice. How now, brother? CATO, And QVINTUS CATULUS were coming to you, And CRASSUS with 'em. I have let 'em in, By th' garden. What would CRASSUS have? I hear Some whispering 'bout the gate; and making doubt, Whether it be not yet too early, or no? But I do think, they are your friends, and clients, Are fearful to disturb you. You will change To another thought anon. Ha' you given the porter The charge, I willed you? Yes. Withdraw, and hearken. THe door's not open, yet. Youwere best to knock. Let them stand close, then: And, when we are in, Rush after us. But where's CETHEGUS? He Has left it, since he might not do't his way. Who's there? A friend, or more. I may not let Any man in, till day. No? why? Thy reason? I am commanded so. By whom? I hope We are not discovered. Yes, by revelation. Pray thee, good slave, who has commanded thee? He that may best, the Consul. We are his friends. All's one. Best give your name. Dost thou hear, fellow? I have some instant business with the Consul. My name is VARGUNTEIUS. True, he knows it; And for what friendly office you are sent. CORNELIUS, too, is there? We are betrayed. And desperate CETHEGUS, is he not? Speak you, he knows my voice. What say you to't? You are deceived, sir. No, 'tis you are so; Poor, misled men. Your states are yet worth pity, If you would hear, and change your savage minds. Leave to be mad; forsake your purposes do; urge thine anger, still: good heaven, and just. Tell guilty men, what powers are above them. In such a confidence of wickedness, 'Twas time, they should know something fit to fear. I never saw a morn moreful of horror. To CATILINE, and his: But, to just men, Though heaven should speak, with all his wrath at once, That, with his breath, the hinges of the world Did crack, we should stand upright, and unfeared.
