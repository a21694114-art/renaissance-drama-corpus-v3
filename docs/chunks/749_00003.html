I wonder they should choose him: Why he does every thing on the sudden. Oh he plots shrewdly. If they had not looked too it, He had undermined the whole parlement-house. I but he's Nobody nowadays, he's blown up long ago. Well, if either you, or any body else should meet with Powder, yet it's a hundred to one, if he meet not with his match. Nay, you should let him alone Dagger, and you should see that Swords ' as notable a bragger as Powder. He thinks he shall near be matched too: but he shall, and overmatched too, by Rapier; I warrant him. Come Sword, after your long Parley, Dare you go into the Fields? Dare I? You shall soon see that: Go, and I'll follow you. Well said, desperate Dick. Sword, You may be ashamed to offer it. You know you are twohanded- Sword, and Rapier has but one hand (unless I help him) to do any good withal, and yet you'd have him go into the Fields with you. Come, there's neither of you shall go. Do not you know that Duels are put down? You'll be bound toth' Peace ever hereafter, if you strike but one stroke. Therefore you had best let me decide your controversy for you. Why, you are a backe-Friend to us both Dagger. Nay, you know I'm equally allied to you both, and therefore shall prove an impartial judge: How say you, will you be ruled by me? Nay let him choose, Rapiers at a good point, whether he will or no. Why are are you so long Sword before you speak? Are duels put down? Then I am forced to be Friends: Speak then. Then in brief it shall be thus. Sword, you shall bear Chief force i'th' Camp, and be made General of the Field, to bear sway every where. As for you Rapier, since Duels are put down, you shall live quietly and peaceably here i'th' Court, and go every day in Velvet: You shall be Friends with every one, and be on every one's side, that if occasion serve, and Sword be absent, so that matters are driven to a push, Rapier shall be the only man to perform a Combat: And I myself will back you both, as occasion shall serve. How say ye, are ye content? We are. Then go before to my House, to the Dagger in Cheap: and there we'll conclude all. A longsword. Our weapons drawn, and yet no hurt ye find, Did Dagger then defend unto your mind? He that defended others not long sense, At last he dares not stand in's own defence; But this he hopes, with you it will suffice, To crave a pardon for a scholars Prize.
