What a fair fist the pretty whoreson writes, as if he had had manners and bringing up, a farmer's Son: his Father damns himself to sell musty Corn, while he ventures the Money, 'twill prosper well at Sea no doubt he shall near see't again So, Captain you deliver this as your deed. As my deed, what else sir? The ugliest deed that ere mine eye did witness So my Lord, you have her, clip her, enjoy her, she's your own, and let me be proud to tell you now my Lord, she's as good a soul if a man had a mind to live honest, and keep a wench, the Kindest, sweetest, comfortablest Rogue.— Hark in thine ear, The Baser slave art thou: and so i'll tell her, I love the Pearl, thou sold'st, hate thee the Seller— Go, to Sea, the end of thee— is Lousy. This fine work: a very brave end, hum— Well thought upon, this Scrivener may furnish me Why should this fellow be a Lord by birth? Being by blood a Knave, one that would sell His Lordship if he liked her Ladyship. Yes my Lord. What's here Now? I have employment for a trusty fellow, bold, sure— What if he be a Knave my Lord? There thou comest to me, why he should be so, and men of your quill are not unacquainted. Indeed all our chief living my Lord, is by fools and knaves, we could not keep open shop else: Fools that enter into bonds, and Knaves that bind 'em. Why now we meet. And as my memory happily leads me: I know a fellow of a standing estate, never flowing: I durst convey treason into his bosom, And keep it safe nine years. A goodly Time. And if need were would press to an attempt, And cleave to Desperate action. That Last fits me. Thou hast the measure right, look I hear from thee. With duteous speed. Expect a large reward: I will find time of her to find regard. The end of me is lousy. Oh my Lord! I have strange words to tell you. Stranger yet? i'll choose some other hour to listen to thee, I am yet sick of this, discover quickly. Why, will you make yourself known my Lord I. Who scourgeth sin let him do't dreadfully. Pox of his dissemblance: I will to sn. Nay, you shall to sea, thou wouldst poison the whole land else— why how now Captain? In health. What, drooping? Or a shamed of the sale of thine own wife? You might count me an ass then i'faith. If not asham'd of that, what can you be a shamed of then? prithee ha' done, I am ashamed of nothing. I easily believe that. This Lord sticks in my stomach. How? take one of thy feathers down, and fetch him up. I'd make him come.
