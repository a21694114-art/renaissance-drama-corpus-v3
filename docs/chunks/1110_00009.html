Which from his pre-eminence Must by mere consequence redound to you. You talk like an old woman, not like one That should make her first choice, as I must now; When I am grieved, can honour cure my heart? If discontent, can my Nobility Give ease unto my Corrosives? when your Lordship Is with your Trulls and Concubines abroad, Where is my loving Husband then at home To keep me warm at midnight? I am he. Sir, that's the thing I doubt. Why, I entreat you? You are a man? I am so. A Lord too? It is confessed. Could you not lend your Lordship to a friend, And keep the man yourself? To whom I pray? To a poor Gentleman, one Master Slightall, Who, had he but that slight Addition, I gladly would embrace. You trifle with me. Therefore to show me serious, Noble man, I take my leave thus gently. That's no answer; Why Nan, why Minion, good your Lordship, take nought in ill part; a peevish thing, God wot, that wants a little tutoring. Slightall quoth 'a? if she slight all as she hath slighted me, she'll fright hence all her Suitors. This I told you. Still will you prate? will't please your honour, take Such cheer as this our suddenness affords? And there's no question but ere dinner's done This Tempest will blow over. I'll take your kindness, he's no good Soldier that at first repulse Will leave the Breach; belike she's fasting now, I'll take her in full stomach. Please you enter? Attendance for his Lordship. 'Tis signed, sealed, and delivered? As fast as wax and witness can make good. And to my use? Yes, and as great an use as e'er you lent out money on. Is there no hope he will redeem't at all? Redeem this did you say? tush, had he more, More he would soon send after; why he's all expense and riot. I shall love expense and riot while I live; Not in myself, I must confess, but in such prodigals By whom we Usurers profit. He spends all. So let him, what he hath; But this I'll look to safe as my life. He minds nor cares for nothing. For this he minds not, my care is took already. Troth he's sinking, he's up to the neck already. May he drown for him that holds him by the Chin. Alas poor sheep, each Bramble shares his wool. Till he be fleeced quite. What makes he then 'mongst Briers? this be his comfort, His flesh will show the better when he's shorn, he'll make sale for the Shambles. Oh these Dice, Drabs, and Drink? Excellent sokers, brave pills to purge the purse: But for my part, I will take no such Physic. What will you do, sir? Marry, first home, and safely lock up these, Then seek some other new come to his Lands, To make like prey on him.
