The very naming of him hath made me forget myself. O, O, O, O, that Rascal is so made-of every where. Who Oblivio? ay, for our Courtiers hug him continually in their ungrate-ful bosoms, & your smoth-belly fat back, barrel-paunched, tun-gutted drones are ever without him, as for Memory he's a false hearted fellow, he always deceives thē, they respect not him, except it be to play a game at Chests, Primero, Saunt, Maw, or such like. I cannot think such fellows have to do with Oblivio since they never got any thing to forget. Again, there prodig all swagerers that are so much bound to their Creditors, if they have but one Cross about them, they'll spend it in Wine upon Oblivio. To what purpose I prithee? Only in hope he'll wash them in the Lethe of their cares. Why then no man cares forthee. Yes a company of studious Paper-worms and lean Scholars, and niggardly scraping Usurers, & a troop of heart-eating envious persons, and those canker-stomached spiteful creatures, that furnish up common place-bookes with other men's faults●▪ The time hath been in those golden days, when Saturn reigned, that if a man received a benefit of another, I was presently sent for to put him in mind of it, but now in these Iron afternoon's, save your friend's life, and Oblivio Will be more familiar with him than you. Phantastes not at Court? be't possible 'tis the strangest accident that ever was heard oft, I had thought the Ladies and Gallants would never lie without him. Hist, hist, Mendaicio I prithee observe Heuresis, it seems he cannot find his Master, that's able to find out all things; and art thou now at a fault, canst not find out thine own master? nor? I'll try one more way. O yes. What a Proclamation for him? ay, I, his nimble head is always full of proclamations▪ O yes. But doth he cry him in the wood? O good sir, and good reason, for every beast hath Fantasy at his pleasure. O yeast if any man can tell any tidings, of a spruce, neat, apish, nimle, finbe, foolish, absurd, humorous, conceited, Fantastic Gallant, with hollow eyes, sharp look, swart complexion, meager face, wearing as many toys in his apparel, as fooleries in his looks and gesture, let him come forth & certify me thereof, and shall have for his reward.— I can tell you where he is, what shall he have? A box o'the ear sura, (snap) How now Invention, are you so quick fingered? i'faith, there's your principal sirrah, (snap) and here's the interest ready in my hand (snap) Yea? have you found out scratching? now I remember me. Do you bite you Rascal? Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, here's the lively picture of this axiom, A quick Invention and a good Memory can never agree. Fie fie, fie Heuresis, beat him when he's down?
