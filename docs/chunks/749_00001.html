Better for me? Alas he knew not how to use me. He used you too well indeed: for when you were with him he furnished you with Siler and every thing, but now you are worn out of all fashion. You are even like a Lapwing, you are no sooner Hatched Rapier, but you run abroad presently from him. Yet I scorn to run away from him. But it were more wisdom then to stand: For the Cutler is a man as well Armed as any man I know, and has as good skill in's weapons. Ha Sword, is the wind in that Door? Faith now I see which way you stand bend Sword, you had rather sleep in a whole skin, then go into the Fields to try yourself. Sir, Sword has been proved before now, and yet he'll prove himself again with you, if you dare follow him. Do you hear Sword? If you go, look for Dagger at your backs presently; For I am a justice of Peace, and am sworn to keep and defend the Peace. Sir, we will fight, and do you take it in Dudgeon Dagger if you please: if you once offer to hinder us, I'll so Pommel you Dagger, that you were never better Pummeld in your life; And howsoever, I hope there's Law against you as well as others, and Rapier can put up a Case against you. Alas I care not if you were both Plaintiffs against me, you shall find Dagger your Defendant I'll warrant you: I, and if need be, I could put up an Action of Battery against you. What talk you of Law? Sword scorns to have any other Law then Martial law, and that upon you Rapier. Away Sword, the Time was indeed when thou wast a notable Swashbuckler, but now thou art grown old Sword. I, you do well, to excuse his Cowardice. Why Sir, 'tis well known that Sword has flourished in his days. Flourished? i'faith Sir I, I have seen Sword hang with nothing but Scarves ere now. With Scarves? with a Halter, if he had been well served, For he's a notable Thief. A Thief? I, a Thief. Did you near hear of Cutting-Dicke, this is the very same man. Nay Rapier, near hit Sword i'th' teeth with that: for you know you were both Indicted for treason before now, and were in danger to be hanged, and drawn too, and had escaped well if you had not been quartered. I hope knows how to keep his quarter's You are a crafty Fox Sword: It were well if you knew how to keep your friendship too, for Gentlemen and Friends should not fall out. Rapier a Gentleman? A Gentleman? and has Arms.
