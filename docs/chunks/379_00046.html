And with his Spirits, sadly I survive, To mock the expectation of the World; To frustrate Prophecies, and to race out Rotten Opinion, who hath writ me down After my seeming. The Tide of Blood in me, Hath proudly flowed in Vanity, till now. Now doth it turn, and ebb back to the Sea, Where it shall mingle with the state of Floods, And flow henceforth in formal Majesty.
