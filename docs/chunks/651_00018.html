Mistress Harebrain, give my wit thanks hereafter, your wishes are in sight, your opportunity spacious. Will you but hear a word from me? Whooh— My husband himself brought me toth' door, walks below for my return; jealousy is prick-eared, and will hear the waging of a hair. Pish, you're a faint-liver, trust yourself with your pleasure, and me with your security, go. The fullness of my wish. Of my desire. Beyond this sphere I never will aspire. I'll listen, now the flesh draws nigh her end, At such a time women exchange their secrets, And ransack the close corners of their hearts; What many years hath whelmed, this hour imparts. Pray sit down, there's a low stool, good Mistress Harebrain, this was kindly done; huh give me your hand; huh, alas how cold you are: e'en so is your husband, that worthy wise Gentleman; as comfortable a man to woman in my case, as ever trod— huh— shoe leather, love him, honour him, stick by him, he lets you want nothing, that's fit for a woman; and to be sure on't, he will see himself that you want it not. And so I do i'faith, 'tis right my humour. You live a Lady's life with him, go where you will, ride when you will, and do what you will. Not so, not so neither, she's better looked to. I know you do, you need not tell me that; 'twere e'en pity of your life i'faith, if ever you should wrong such an innocent gentleman; fie Mistress Harebrain, what do you mean? come you to discomfort me? nothing but weeping with you? she's weeping, t'as made her weep, my wife shows her good nature already. Still, still weeping? huff, huff, huff, why how now woman? heigh, hy, hy, for shame leave; suh, suh, she cannot answer me for snobbing. All this does her good, beshrew my heart and I pity her, let her shed tears till morning; I'll stay for her, she shall have enough on't by my good will; I'll not be her hindrance. O no, lay your hand here Mistress Harebrain: I there, Oh there, there lies my pain good gentlewoman: sore? Oh ay, I can scarce endure your hand upon't. Poor soul, how she's tormented. Yes, yes, I eat a cullis an hour since. There's some comfort in that yet, she may scape it. Oh it lies about my heart much. I'm sorry for that i'faith, she'll hardly scape it. Bound, no, no, I'd a very comfortable stool this morning. I'm glad of that i'faith, that's a good sign, I smell, she'll scape it now. Will you be going then? Fall back, she's coming. Thanks good Mistress Harebrain, welcome sweet Mistress Harebrain, pray commend me to the good gentleman your husband. I could do that myself now.
