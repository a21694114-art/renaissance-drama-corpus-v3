And my good Lord, you're happily met. Hearty welcome: young Tully welcome to, ye come well to ease my charge, these Ladies find fault with their Guardian, I go too softly for them: old blood is stiff, & young Ladies will not bear with age: I resign, I resign to you that follow. If they admit us for their Guardian, we'll dare dangers ere we part from them. Why well said my Lords, Soldiers will not fly indeed, I have seen the day I could have cracked a tree of yew, made my bowstring, whisper in mine care if they twang: tossed my pike lustily: 'tis since the siege of Parthia, byth' mass a great while, I was lusty then, at the service was done there, yet I love the discourse: come my Lord, I choose your company, leave Tully to the Ladies, he can tell them tales of Venus and Adonis, and that best pleaseth them. Now I must here of raps and blows, and Bills and Guns, and swords and bucklers: I loved it once, come, our Cooks are backward, discourse will beget stomachs, you're like to tarry long for lean Cates. Now gentle Tully, advocate my suit, Her fore-amazing person makes me mute. I'll bear these Lady's company, If they shall deem acceptance. With interest of thanks to Cicero, Faith I like not this odds of female, an equality were better: yet of both 'ttwere fitter the woman should undergo the odds, I had rather a said three men to one woman, than two women to one man: here's Tully addressed to Terentia, Terentia drawing near to Tully: her's small comfot left for Flaevia, well gentles, i'll leave ye to the Goddess: so ho my Lords, take me with ye. Nay, stay good Flavia, You'll not lose the sight of Lentulus. Nor you of Tully, come if you tell, i'll blab. But sweet Lady, Tully is not here. But Cicero is, his near friend, that's as good. He was Lady, till he changed his habit, by putting on the office of an unskilful Servingman, intending to guard Terentia to her father's house. Then Flavia must guard herself: well, use good words, and good action, and stalk well before your Lady, she's kindey faith, and a litele thing will please her. Will it please Flavia to partake? Oh fie, 'ttwere an injury, I could brook myself therefore, i'll leave ye, but be brief, stand not on points, cut them all first, & if ye fall to kissing, kiss not to long for fear ye kiss the post. Go to, you'll still be a wag Flavia. But what says Tully to Terentia? Lady I must maintain my former argument, Tullie's not here, but here is Tully's friend, For ere I speak, I must entreat, you will Transform poor Tully into Lentulus. I have no power of Metamorphosing, If Tully be not here, you must conceal, I cannot make of Tully Lentulus.
