This is worth the observation, signor. Now, sir; here's to you; and I present you with so much of my love. I take it kindly from you, sir, and will return you the like proportion: but withal, sir, remembering the merry night we had at the countesses, you know where, sir. By heaven, you put me in mind now of a very necessary office, which I will propose in your pledge, sir: the health of that honourable countess, and the sweet lady that sat by her, sir.
