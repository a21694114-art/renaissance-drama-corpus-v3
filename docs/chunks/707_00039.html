Spir. Ha, ha, ha. Dam. Torments inutterable! oh! damned for nothing? Terrors incomprehensible. Back: you're blown up else. Whooh: hot, hot, hot,- drink,- I am heart-burnt. One drop, a bit. Now, now, now. I am parboiled, I am stewed, I am sod in a kettle of brimstone, pottage.— it scalds,— it scalds,— it scalds,— it scalds— whoop Ha ha ha: But one half crumb, a little little drop, a bit. Towers, towers, towers, towers, pinnacles & towers, battlements and pinnacles, steeples, abbeys, churches and old chimneys. Zounds drink, shall I choke in mine Inn? drink. Drink, drink, oh! one drop, one drop, to cool us. So many tapsters in hell, and none fill drink here: Ball no more, you shall be liquored. Why art thou damned toth' horrors of one hell, Yet feelst ten thousand. Wherefore is thy soul Made sensible of tortures which (each minute) Kill thee ten thousand times, yet canst not die? Some sack. Why for a few sins that are long hence past, Must I feel torments that shall ever last? Ever ever. Let the sack be mulled. Why is the devil, (If man be born good) suffered to make him evil? Man is an ass, if he sit broiling thus i'th' glass house Without drink: two links of my chain for a three half penny bottle of mother consciences Ale: drink. One drop of puddle water to cool us. Leave howling and be damned. Here's drink for thee royal villain. Oh! Is't not good! For blood thoust thirsted, and thy drink is blood. Strikes it so cold to thy heart? here's that shall warm thee (Again. Danation, suries, fire-brandes.— Ha, ha, ha. One drop of moisture, but one crumb. Art hungry, eat this adder: dry? Suck this Snake. Suck and be damned thyself: I'll starve first. Away Is not this all waters? Ruby water, some Ruby water, Or else a bottle of postern water to save charges, or else a Thimbleful of lemon water, to cool my stomach the ru-● By is swilled up all, here's lemon, down with't. Foh, the great devil or else some Aquavite woman has made water, It scalds me. Oooh. Ha ha ha▪— Hell grins to hear this roaring: where's this black child of fathomless perdition? rarest devil That ever hold in Barathrum? here, (dear pupil) Of a new damnation's stamp, Saucer-eyed Lucifer, has drunk to thee this deep infernal bowl off, wilt pledge his ugliness? Reach it me. Choke with it. Ha ha ha. Give fire, blow all the world up. Bounce: 'tis done. Ha ha ha.— I shallbe grinded into dust; It falls: I am mad. I am mad, I am mad.— 4. Ha ha ha. Ho ho ho.—
