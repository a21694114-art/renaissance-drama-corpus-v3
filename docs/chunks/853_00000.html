GIve ear, the worthy, hear what Fame proclaims. What? what? Is't worth our ears? Or eyes? Or noses? For we are curious, Fame: indeed, the Curious. We come to spy. And harken. And smell out. Moore than you understand, my hot Inquisitors, We cannot tell. It may be. However, go you on, let us alone. We may spy out, that, which you never meant. And nose the thing you sent not. First, whence come you? I came from Saturn. Saturn, what is he? Some Protestant I warrant you, a Time-server, As Fame herself is. You are near the right. Indeed, he is Time itself, and his name KRONOS, How! Saturn! Chronos! and the Time itself! You're found: enough. A notable old Pagan! One of their Gods, and eats up his own children. A Fencer, and does travel with a sigh Instead of a longsword. Hath been often called from it, To be their Lord of misrule. As Cincinnatus Was from the plough, to be Dictator. Yes. We need no interpreter, on, what of Time? The Time hath sent me with my Trump to summon All sorts of persons worthy, to the view Of some great spectacle he means to night, T'exhibite; and with all solemnity. O, we shall have his Saturnalia. His days of feast, and liberty again. Where men might do, and talk all that they list. Slaves of their lords. The servants of their masters! And subjects of their Sovereign. Not so lavish. It was a brave time that! This will be better: I spy it coming, peace. All the impostures, The prodigies, diseases, and distempers, The knaveries of the Time, we shall see all now. And hear the passages, and several humours Of men, as they are swayed by their affections: Some grumbling, and some mutining, some scoffing, Some pleased, some pining, at all these we laughing. I have it here, here, strong, the sweat of it, And the confusion (which I love) I nose it, It tickles me. My four eyes itch for it. And my ears tingle, would it would come forth: This room will not receive it. That's the fear. What? what? my friends, will not this room receive? That which the Time is presently to show us. The Time? Lo I the man, that hate the time That is, that love it not; and (though in rhyme, I here do speak it) with this whip you see, Do lash the Time, and am myself lash-free. Who's this? 'Tis Chronomastix, the brave Satire, The gentlemanlike Satire, cares for no body, His forehead tiped with bays, do you not know him? Yes Fame must know him, all the Town admires him. If you would see Time quake and shake, but name us, It is for that, we are both beloved, and famous. We know, Sir. But the Time's now come about. And promiseth all liberty. Nay licence. We shall do what we list. Talk what we list. And censure whom we list, and how we list.
