The charge is small charge sir, I thank God my father left me wherewithal, if it please you sir, I have a great mind to this gentlewoman here in the way of marriage. I thank you sir: please you come to Lewsome to my poor house, you shall be kindly welcome: I knew your father, he was a wary husband: to pay here Drawer. All is paid sir: this gentleman hath paid all. I'faith you do us wrong, But we shall live to make amends ere long: Master Flowerdale, is that your man? Yes faith, a good old knave. Nay then I think you will turn wise, Now you take such a servant: Come, you'll ride with us to Lewsome, let's away, 'tis scarce two hours to the end of day.
