What news could I hear since you left me last? Were you not here even now? lent me your chain, I think you dote. Sweet Prince, age, age forgets, my brother's chain? a pretty accident, I'll have't and be but in the spite of Iohn▪ there's more, and more, I'll geld it ear it go. This same shall keep me in some saverne merry, Till night's black hand curtain this to clear sky. My sweet Prince, I have some cause to use my chain, Another time (when ere your Lordship please) 'tis at your service, o mary God it is. Hear palsy, take your chain, stoop and be hanged, Yet the fish nibbled, when she might not swallow; Gout I have kirtled what I could not borrow. He's gone away in frets, would he might meet My brother Faukenbridge in this mad mood, There would be rare ado; Why this fits me, My brain flows with fresh wit and policy. But Gloster look about, who have we yonder? Another Iohn Prince, Richard and the Sheriff? Upon my life, the slave that had the chain, Was Skinke, escaped the Fleet by some mad sleight, Well, farewell he, better and better still, These seek form, yet I will have my will. Sheriff, in any case be diligent. Whos's yonder, Faukenbridge? How now sweet chuck, how fares my lovely Prince? What carest thou? or well, or ill, we crave no help of thee. God's mother do you scorn me? Gout, what then? Fie, leave these idle brawls, I prithee Iohn Let's follow that we are enjoined unto. I mary Prince, if now you slip the time, Gloster will slip away; tut though he hate me I have done service, I have found him out. A shame confound thee for thy treachery, Inconstant dotard, timorous old ass, That shakes with cowardice not with years. Go, I have found him, I have wound him. O let me hug thee gentle Faukenbridge, Forgive my oft ill using of thine age, I'll call thee Father, i'll be penitent, Bring me where Gloster is I'll be thy slave, All that is mine, thou in reward shalt have. Soft, not too hasty, I would not be seen in't, Mary a god my wife would chide me dead, If Gloster by my means should lose his head. Princely Richard at this corner make your stand: And for I know you love my sister well, Know I am Gloster and not Faukenbridge. Heaven prosper thee sweet Prince in thy escape. Sheriff, make this your quarter, make good guard. Iohn, stay you here, this way he means to turn, By Thomas I lack a sword, body a me. What wouldst thou with a sword old Faukenbridge? O sir to make show in his defence, For I have left him yonder at a house A friends of mine, an honest Citizen. we'll fetch him thence.
