Or if all fail, you may hire a good suit at a Iewes: or at a brokers, 'tis a common thing and specially among the common sort. To search through my house, I have no Varlets no knaves, no stewed prunes, no she fiery phagies, my Chambers are swept, my sinks are all scoured, the honest shall come in, the knaves shall go by, yet will I master Constable, go search through my house, I care not a sheeps skin. We are compelled to do it mine host, a Gentleman is robbed last night, & we are to search every privy corner. Mine host is true metal, a man of reputation, a true Holofernes, he loves juice of grapes, and welcome master Constable. Graccus, how lik'st thou this? Excellent, for now must he needs fall into the Constable's hands: and if he have any grace, 'twil appear in his face, when he shall be carried through the street in a white sheet 'twill be a good penance for his fault. Now fortune favour that my husband find him not he'll be horn mad, & never able to endure it: why woman if he have but as much man in him as a Marrowbone, he'll take the burden upon his own neck, and never discover you Alas here they come, let's away Gossip. Fortune my foe, why dost, etc. Oh fie, that's bitter, pray the go comfort him. Faith he should be innocent by his garment: signor, I grieve for this, but if I can help, look for it. I thank ye sir. We must contaminate our office, pray regard us as little as ye can. Me thinks this should put him quite out of tune: now so, let him go, now to mine Host, there's he, and he, and he, there's she, and she, i'll have about with all: & critics, honnies sweetest, mixed with gall. Go to, there's knaves in my house, I know of no Varlets, I have an eye has his sense, a brain that can reach, I have been called Politician, my wife is my wife, I am her top, i'm her head: if mine Host say the word, the Mouse shall be dun. Not so my sweet Host, mum, mum, no words against your wife, he that means to live quiet, to sleep in clean sheets, a Pillow under his head, his diet dressed cteanely, mum, mum, no words against his wife. thou'rt a fool, thou'rt a fool, be ruled by mine host, show thyself a brave man of the true seed of Troy; a gallant Agamemnon, thou'st a shrew to thy wife, if she cross thy brave humours, kick thy heel at her huckle bone. Gentles, most happily encountered, how good hap hath turned two labours into one, I was addressed to both, and at once have met both, sure I must entreat that you must not deny.
