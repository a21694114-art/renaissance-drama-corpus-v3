This is not the church, of disguised hypocrites Of apish shavelings, or papistical Sodomytes Nor yet as they call it, a temple of lime and stone But a lyvysh building, grounded in faith alone On the hard rock Christ, which is the sure fosidation And of this church, some do reign in every nation And in all countries, though their number be but small Their number is such, as hath run over all The same Danes are they, men prophecy of plain Which should over run this realm yet once again. What Danes speakest thou of? thy meaning show more clearly. Dane Iohan, Dane Robert, Dane Thomas, and Dane Harrye. The same are those Danes, that lay with other men's wives. And occupied their lands, to the detriment of their lives. These are accounted, a great part of the church For in God's service, they honourably wurche yelling and crying, till their throats are full sore. That Church was describe, of Esaye long afore This people (saith God) with their lips honour me In vain worship, they teaching men's fatuyte Apparent is that church, and open to the eyes Their worshippings are, in outward ceremonies That counterfetchurch standeth all by men's traditions Without the scriptures, & without the hearts affections My church is secret, and evermore will be Adoring the father, in spirit and in verity By the word of God, this church is ruled only And doth not consist, in outward ceremony This congregation, is the true Church militant Those counterfeit Desardes, are the very Church malignant. To whom Christ will say, I know none of your sort Moche are they to blame, that brethren so report Such are no brethren, but enemies to Christ's blood As put salvation, in shaven crown mitre, or hood. I pray ye how long, have your sweet spouse continued Sens the beginning, and now is in Christ renewed. Adam did promise, of Christ's incarnation So had Abraham, with his whole generation Which was unto them, a preaching of the Gospel Into salvation, and deliverance from hell. By this time I hope, ye have a fair increase? She is not barren, but beareth and never cease The Corinth's first epistle, hath this clear testimony In Christo Iesu, per Euangilium vos genui I have begot you, in Iesu Christ saith Powle By the Gospel preaching, to the comfort of your soul. Than are ye a ouckolde, by the blessed holy mass As I said afore, so cometh it now to pass For I am a prophet, by high inspiration led Now like I myself, much better than I did Ye say that saint Paul, begat your wife with child. By misunderstanding, thou art ungraciously beguiled An only minister, was paule in that same doing That he therein did, was by the gospel preaching His mind is the Gospel, to have done that operation And this must thou hold, for no carnal generation.
