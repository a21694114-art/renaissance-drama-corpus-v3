Thou art false, Falser than thy religion. Do but think me Something above a beast; nay more, a monster, Would fright the Sun to look on, and then tell me If this base usage, can invite affection? If to be mewed up, and excluded from Humane society; the use of pleasures; The necessary, not superfluous duties Of servants to discharge those offices, I blush to name. Of servants? can you think That I that dare not trust the eye of Heaven To look upon your beauties, that deny myself the happiness to touch your pureness Will ere consent an Eunuch, or bought handmaid Shall once approach you? there is something in you That can work Miracles, or I am cozened, Dispose and alter sexes. To my wrong In spite of nature. I will be your nurse, Your woman, your physician, and your fool, Till with your free consent, which I have vowed Never to force, you grace me with a name That shall supply all these. What is't? Your husband. My hangman when thou pleasest. Thus I guard me, Against your further angers. Which shall reach thee Though I were in the Centre. Such a spirit In such a small proportion I ne'er read of Which time must alter, ravish her I dare not The magic that she wears about her neck, I think defends her, this devotion paid To this sweet Saint, mistress of my sour pain 'tis fit I take mine own rough shape again. The train is vanished They have done him some good office he's so free And liberal of his gold. Ha, do I dream, Or is this mine own natural Master; 'tis he, But strangely metamorphosed. You have made sir. A prosperous voyage, heaven grant it be honest, I shall rejoice then too. You make him blush To talk of honesty, you were but now In the giving vain, and may think of Gazet Your worship's prentice. There's gold, be thou free too And Master of my shop, and all the wares we brought from Venice. Rivo then. Dear sir This place affords not privacy for discourse But I can tell you wonders, my rich habit Deserves least admiration; there's nothing That can fall in the compass of your wishes Though it were to redeem a thousand slaves From the Turkish galleys, or at home to erect Some pious work, to shame all Hospitals, But I am master of the means.
