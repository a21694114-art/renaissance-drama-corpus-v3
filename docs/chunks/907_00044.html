Let her foure weeles carry Apollos curse with her, that none of her kindred shall euer get aboue the petty forme of Apollos schoole. Passe o . Iohn Gingle, Gentleman Would-be. Apollo pronounceth that his lot must be, before he be forty years old, to spend all his five Manors upon the five Senses: Except the Mansion house of Foolingham, which is entailed to the heirs of his body woefully begotten. For my part I will not be of counsel to cut off the entail. Compliment, pretended Captain, alias Captain Comp. Fashionmonger. He is fled, as I hear. Fled with a witness. For he flew like a bird up in the air. Then has he saved us a labour. For Apollo had doomed him to be blown up with Vulcan's bellows into the middle Region of the air. The boys in the outer-court of Apollo's Palace, when they perceived by Mistress jingles discontent, that the Captain was like to be banished, they prevented execution, by tying squibs to his skirts; which being fired, this light fusball mounted up into the ay● as high as a lark. One boy said, that this was the captain's lofty capering trick. And whither did this whirl wind carry him? Who knows? But most do think to the Island of Coxcombria, a place thick impeopled with such riff-raff. Jack Implement, Page to the aforesaid Compliment. Vous havez Implement. Here sir, at hand. I pray you Master Musaeus be good to a poor Page. I had but a hard service under him My bed and board was scarce to the maintenance of a water-spaniel. I would sane go to school again.
