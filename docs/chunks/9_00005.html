Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Geamercy my friends & kinsmen kind Now shall I show you the grief of my mind I was commanded by a messengere That is an high kings chief officer He had me go a pilgrimage to my pain But I know well I shall never come again Also I must give reckoning straight For I have a great enemy/ that hath me in wait Which intendeth me for to hinder What a count is that/ which ye must render That would I know Of all my works I must show How I have lived/ and my days spent Also of ill deeds/ that I have used In my time/ sith life was me lente And of all virtues/ that I have refused Therefore I pray you/ go thither with me To help to make mine account/ for saint charity What to go thither/ is that the matter Nay everyman I had liefer fast breed & water All this five year and more Alas that ever I was borne For now shall I never be merry if that you forsake me A sir what ye be a merry man Take good heart to you/ and make no moan But one thing I warn you/ by saint Anne As for me/ ye shall go alone My cousin/ will you not with me go No by our lady/ I have the cramp in my to Trust not to me/ for so god me speed I will deceive you/ in your most need It availeth not us to tyse ye shall have my maid/ with all my heart She loveth to go to feasts/ there to be nice And to dance/ and a broad to start I will give her leave/ to help you in that iurnaye If that you and she may agree Nw show me the very effect of your mind Will you go with me/ or abide behind Abide behind/ ye that will I & I may Therefore farewell/ till another day How should I be merry or glad For fair promises/ men to me do make But when I have most need/ they me forsake I am deceived that maketh me sad Cousin everyman farewell now For verily I will not go with you Also of my own/ a unredy reckoning I have to account/ therefore I make tarrying Now god keep thee/ for now I go A Iesus is all come hereto Loo/ fair words maketh fools feign They promise/ & nothing will do certain My kinsmen promised me faithfully For to abide with me steadfastly And now fast away do they flee even so fellowship promised me What friend were best me of to provide I lose my time here longer to abide yet in my mind a thing there is All my life I have loved riches If that my good now help me/ might It would make my heart full light I will speak to him/ in this distress Where art thou my goods and riches.
