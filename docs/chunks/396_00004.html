By my troth, they have voices as clear as Crystal: this is a pretty thing, if it be for nothing but to go a-begging with. Believe me, Ver, but thou art pleasant bent, This humour should import a harmless mind: know'st thou the reason why I sent for thee? No faith, nor care not, whether I do or no. If you will dance a Galliard, so it is: if not, Falangtado, Falangtado, to wear the black and yellow: Falangtado, Falangtado, my mates are gone, I'll follow. Nay, stay a while, we must confer and talk. Ver, call to mind I am thy sovereign Lord, And what thou hast, of me thou hast, and hold'st. Unto no other end I sent for thee. But to demand a reckoning at thy hands, How well or ill thou hast employed my wealth. If that be all, we will not disagree. A clean trencher and a napkin you shall have presently. The truth is, this fellow hath been a tapster in his days. How now? is this the reckoning we shall have? My Lord, he doth abuse you: brook it not. Summa totalis I fear will prove him but a fool. About, about, lively, put your horse to it, rain him harder, jerk him with your wand, sit fast, sit fast, man; focle, hold up your ladle there. O brave hall! O, well said, butcher. Now for the credit of Wostershire. The finest set of Morris-dancers that is between this and Stretham: mary, methinks there is one of them danceth like a clothier's horse, with a woolpack on his back. You friend with the Hobby-horse, go not too fast, for fear of wearing out my Lords tile-stones with your hobnails. So, so, so, trot the ring twice over, and away. May it please my Lord, this is the grand capital sum, but there are certain parcels behind, as you shall see. Nay, nay, no more; for this is all too much. Content yourself, we'll have variety. Trip and go, heave and hoe, Up and down to and fro, From the town, to the grove, Two, and two, let us rove a-Maying, a playing: Love hath no gainsaying: So merrily trip and go. Beshrew my heart, of a number of ill legs, I never saw worse dancers: how blessed are you, that the wenches of the parish do not see you! Presumptuous Ver, uncivil nurtured boy, Thinkest I will be derided thus of thee? Is this th'account and reckoning that thou mak'st? Troth, my Lord, to tell you plain, I can give you no other account: nam quae habui, pordidi; what I had, I have spent on good fellows, in these sports you have seen, which are proper to the Spring, and others of like sort, (as giving wenches green gowns, making garlands for Fencers, and tricking up children gay) have I bestowed all my flowery treasure, and flower of my youth.
