If in thy face I look (oh lovely boy) Then is this love a paradise of joy. But if I turn and view my spirit well, Then 'tis a flame of deep infernal hell. Nymph, no more words, give me my dog and do. Nay give me first, the love you promised. Have I not given it? what a stir is here, Her to cnntent: take it, do what thou wilt, Who doth forbid thee? what wouldst thou have more? Thou sow'st thy seed in sand wretched Dorinda. What would you have? why do you linger thus? As soon as you have got what you desire, (Perfidious Silvio) you are gone from me. No trust me Nymph. Give me a pledge. What pledge? I dare not tell. And why? I am ashamed. Are you ashamed to speak, and not ashamed It to receive? If you will promise me To give it, I will tell. I promise you. (Silvio my dear) do you not understand me yet? I should have understood you but with half of this. Thou art more subtle much then I. I am more earnest, and less cruel much then thou. To say the troth, I am no Prophet I, You must speak if you'll have me understand. O wretch one of those which thy mother gave to thee. A blow on th'ear? A blow on th'ear to one that loves Sometime she maketh much of me with one of them. Doth she not kiss you then? Nor she nor any else Doth kiss me. But perhaps you'd have a kiss. You answer not, your blushing you accuseth, I am content, but give me first my dog. Youhave promised me? 'tis true, I have promised thee. And will you stay? Tush what a stir is here? I will. Come forth Lupino, Lupino dost not hear? Who calls? I come, I come, it was not I, It was the dog that slept. behold thy dog More courteous than thyself. O happy me. He in these arms that thou despisest so, Did put himself. O my most dear Melampo. Esteeming dear my kisses and my sighs. I'll kiss thee thousand times poor cur. Hast thou no harm in running poor Melampo? O happy dog might I change lots with thee: Am I not brought unto an excellent pass, That of a dog I must be jealous thus? Lupino go unto the hunting straight, I'll follow thee. Mistress I go. Is aught behind? Where is the Do you promised me? Will you her have alive or dead? I understand you not. How's she alive, hath not my dog her killed? But say the dog hath not. Is she alive? Alive. So much more welcome she's. Only she's wounded in the heart. Thou mockest: How can she live and wounded in the heart?
