Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

A tooth ache Lady, a paltry rheum. De diet is very goot for de rheum. How far off dwells the house surgeon Mary Fough. You are a profane fellow i'faith, I little thought to hear such ungodly terms come from your lips. Pray de now, 'tis but a toy, a very trifle. I care not for the value, Frank, but i'faith- I fait, me must needs have it (dis is Beatrice ring, oh could I get it,) feet pree now, as ever you have embraced me with a hearty arm, a warm thought, or a pleasing touch, as ever you will profess to love me, as ever you do wish me life, give me dis ring, dis little ring. pray be not uncivilly importunate, sha' not ha''t, faith I care not for thee, nor thy jealousy, sha' not ha''t i'faith. You do not love me, I hear of Sir Hubert Subboys daughter Mistress Beatrice, God's Sacrament, ick could scratch out her eyes, and suck the holes. Go you're grown a punk rampant. So get thee gone, near more behold mine eyes by thee made wretched. Mary Fough farewell, farewell Frank. Sall I not ha' de ring? No by the Lord. By te Lord? By the Lord. Go to your new Blouze, your unproud sluttery, your modest Mettre forsooth. Marry will I forsooth. Will you marry forsooth? Do not turn witch before thy time. With all my heart Sir, you will stay. I am no whit myself, Video meliora proboque, But raging lust my fate all strong doth move: " The Gods themselves cannot be wise and love. Your wishes to you. Beauty entirely choice: Pray ye prove a man of fashion, and neglect the neglected. Can such a rarity be neglected, can there be measure or sin in loving such a creature: O mine poor forsaken heart. I can not contain, he saw thee not that left thee, If there be wisdom, reason, honour, grace Of any foolishly esteemed virtue, In giving o'er possession of such beauty, Let me be vicious, so I may be loved, Passion I am thy slave, sweet it shall be my grace, That I account thy love, my only virtue: Shall I swear I am thy most vowed servant. Mine vowed, go, go, go, I can no more of love, no, no, no, you been all unconstant, O unfaithful men, tyrants, betrayers, de very enjoying us, loseth us, and when you only ha' made us hateful, you only hate us: O mine forsaken heart. I must not rave, Silence and modesty two customary virtues: will you be my mistress? Mettres? ha, ha, ha. Will you lie with me? Lie with you, O no, you men will outlie any woman, fait me no more can love. No matter, let me enjoy your bed. O vile man, vat do you tinck on me, do you take me to be a beast, a creature that for sense only will entertain love, and not only for love, love? O brutish abomination! Why then I pray thee love, and with thy love enjoy me. Give me reason to affect you, will you swear you love me.
