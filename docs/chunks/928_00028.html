Stay miracle of honour, and of love. If you proceed, as it concerns your happiness, I can secure all fear of me, I am Resolved a course wherein I will be dead To her, yet live to pray for her, and you, Although I never see you more, will you royal Lord? did ever lover plead against himself before? I love her still, And in that study her advancement Sir, In you. I cannot give her. Well, I will still love her, and solicit. And not open That I am living. Not a Syllable. I am confident, let me but kiss your hand. again, may blessings dwell with you for ever. He was always noble, but this passion Has outgone history, it makes for me, Hail to my courteous fate, Foscari thankes, Like the aged Phenix, thy old love expires, And from such Death, springs life to my desires. The Father is not come yet, nor my Lord yet when they do, I have no way T lpe my selfe, nor haue I power to goe From hence, sure this is the Religious man. Ha' 'tis the same. Father Valentio? Dear Leonora. Sir the same. Oh let My tears express my joys, what miracle Gave you this liberty? I was rescued, By th'happy valour of a Gentleman, To whom in gratitude, I pay this service, He bade me here expect a holy man, And is it you? The circumstance confirms it. Are you the goodman whom my Lord expects▪ 'tis so me refreshing in the midst of sorrow, To meet again. And heaven hath heard my prayer. But I am miserable still unless Your counsel do relieve me. Why my charge? This noble Gentleman, to whom I own My preservation, who appointed you To meet him here, having resolved to enter, Into Religion, hath been very urgent, For me to do so too, and overcome With many importunities, I gave Consent, not knowing what was best to do, Some cure or I am lost, you know I cannot Mix with religious men. Did you consent? I did, and he is now upon the point Of his return. Y'are in a strait▪ I must Confess, no matter, hold your purpose, and Leave all to me, he is returned. Good Father. Now I am ready, have you disposed him For such a life. He is constant to attend you, I have prepared him, and made way to the Abbot, For your reception, I am blessed, Dulcimo, Nay no distinction now, me thinks we move Upon the wings of Cherubins already, 'tis but a step to heaven, come my sweet boy We climb by a short ladder to our joy▪ This my Lord is her garden, into which you see My key hath given us private access, 'tis full of curiosity, You see that grove. I do. There is her house of pleasure, let your eye entertain Some delight here, while I give her happy Knowledge you are entered▪
