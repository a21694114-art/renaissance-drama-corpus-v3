Welcome Manasses: I have present employment for thee, in which I must borrow— Pardon me Madam, I learned of my Lord, to lend nothing without security and pawns. 'Tis not money (Manasses) but counsel and furtherance that we desire. Good counsel is worth good money Madam. Thou shalt be well considered; there's twenty Crowns in earnest. Nay Madam, this hand is like a fellow that takes every thing in jest; if you be in earnest, let me feel it here: So Lady, now betwixt earnest and jest, if your Will be ready drawn, before your friend delivered as your deed, and put me in trust to execute it.
