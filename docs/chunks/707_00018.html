It did. In very deed (for I not swear) It did not sir: to me you malice bear, As if that all such mischief done, were mine, But cause yourself shall see how I repine To see vice prosper, pardon me good Lord Prior▪ If I a tell-tale be of what mine eyes Beheld with water in them: sin will rise In holy circles I see sometimes. What sin? What haste thou seen? Would present I had not been, But till I utter it, my clogged conscience bears▪ A man upon a woman. Ha. I speak't in tears: Scumbroth our cook, and a female I beheld Kissing in our orchard: on her lips he dwelled I think some half hour. Shame to our reverend order! A woman in our covent! Sin black as murder. Our cook shallbe severely punished: a woman, A tempter here. Abominable! Rush, thou'lt rebuke sin. Though my Lord I'm bad, I'm not given that way. Let us some plagues invent to lay on this lecherous knave. Some light punishment (Good my Lord Prior) suppose 'twere your own fault, Whip as you would be whipped, the best's nought. He shallbe punished, and then lose his place. That sir shall be as we will: to our Vines: away. For shame give o'er, dare you profane this day That is to holy uses consecrate? Why? what day is this? Lambert the martyr. No matter, To vex thee deeper, this whole day we'll spend, Only about our Vines. You vex not me, But heaven: what warrants you to this? Our will. Thou hast thy will, thy wish thou ne'er shalt have, In sight of heaven who sees and punishes Men's black impieties; And in sight of these (Sharers in thy full sin:) And in his sight, T' express whose vileness, there's no epithet. No matter what he says Rush. I'm known what I am. To thee I prophecy, (vicious old man to thee, Who erst with lift-up-hands, and down-bowed knee, Seemest to have had work in heaven: now (full of spite, Only to eat a liquorish appetite; Digst our religious wales up, planting there Luxurious fruits to pamper belly-cheer▪ (For all thy pains to dress it,) of this Vine Thy lustful lips shall never taste the wine.
