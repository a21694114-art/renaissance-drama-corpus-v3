I had even as leaf ye bade me hang myself, Leave him: no no I would you all knew, You be but loiterers to him, my Will tells me true, I conld be content with a week, yea a month or twain, But 3. or 4. years, marry that were a pain, So long to keep me, and lie like a hog. A life withal my heart I would not wish a dog. Will a week serve. No. A month. Neyther● No. Not so. Not nor so many more. Then farewell all for as I hope to thrive, I will prove him or I sleep, if I be alive, And if ye be mine and good fellows all three, Go thither, out of hand and take your chance with me. For my part, I know I can do you no good. You are a proper man of your hands by the Rood, Yet welfare him that never his master forsaketh. What sayst thou Study. My head acheth.
