To prove that Beauty best, Which not the colour, but the feature Assures unto the creature. BE silent, now the Ceremonies done. And NIGER, say, how comes it, lovely Son, That thou, the AETHIOPES River, so far East, Art seen to fall into th' extremest West Of me, the King of floods, OCEANUS, And, in mine empire's heart, salute me, thus? My ceaseless current, now amazed stands!
