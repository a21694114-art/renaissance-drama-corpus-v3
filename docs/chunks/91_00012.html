Why then I perceive poor Ragau shall have none. Well, much good do it you with your pottage of Rice: I would fast and fare ill, ere I eat of that price. Would I sell my birthright being an eldest son? Forsooth then were it a fair thread that I had spun. And then to let it go for a mess of pottage, What is that, but both unthriftiness and dotage? Alack, alack, good blessed father Isaac, That ever son of thine, should play such a lewd knack. And yet I do not think but God this thing hath wrought, For Iacob is as good as Esau is nought. But forth cometh Mido, as fast as he can trot: For a cycle, whether to call me in or not? HA, ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, Now who saw ere such another as Esau? By my truth I will not lie to thee Ragau, Since I was borne, I never see any man So greedily eat rice out of a pot or pan. He would not have a dish, but take the pot and sup. Ye never saw hungry dog so slab pottage up. Why how did he sup it? I pray thee tell me how? marry even thus, as thou shalt see me do now. Oh I thank you Iacob: with all my heart Iacob. Gently done Iacob: A friendly part Iacob. I can sup so Iacob. Yea then will I sup too Iacob. Here is good meat Iacob. As ere was eat Iacob. As ere I saw Iacob. Esau a daw Iacob. Sweet rice pottage Iacob. By Esau's dotage Iacob. joily good cheer Iacob. But bought full dear Iacob. I was hungry Iacob. I was an unthrift Iacob. Ye will none now Iacob. I can not for you Iacob. I will eat all Iacob. The devil go withal Iacob. Thou art a good son Iacob. And would he never have done Iacob? No, but still cogeld in like Jack daw that cries ka kob. That to be killed I could not laughing forbear: And therefore I came out, I durst not abide there. Is there any pottage left for me that thou wot? No. I left Esau about to lick the pot. Lick quod thou? now a shame take him that can all lick. The pot shall need no washing, he will it so lick. And by this he is sitting down to bread and drink. And shall I have no part with him, dost thou think? No, for he prayed Iacob ere he did begin, To shut the tent fast that no more gests come in. And made he no mention of me his servant? He said thou were a knave, and had thee hence, avant. Go shift where thou couldst, thou gottest nothing there. God yield you Esau, with all my stomacher. I must in again, lest perhaps I be shent, For I asked nobody licence when I went.
