Oh (sir) put on the sparlingst trim of glory, Perfection will shine for most, and I know you By the Catholical mark you wear about you, The mark above your forehead. Are you grown So ambitious in your observance: well, your business, I have my Game to follow. I have a worm Follows me, so that I can follow no game, The most faint hearted pawn, if he could see His play, might snap me up at pleasure: I desire (sir) to be absolved, my conscience Being at ease, I could then with more courage Play my Game. 'Twas a base fact. 'Twas to a Schismatic pawn (sir.) What's that to the ability of revenge Suffices I have neither will nor power To give you absolution for that violence, Make your petition to the penance Chamber, If the Tax Register relieve you in't, By the black bishop's clemency you ha'wrought out, A single piece of favour with your money, It's all your refuge now. This sting shoots deeper. Yonder's my Game, which like a politic Chessner, I must not seem to see. Oh my heart. That 'tis. The very same that the Magical Presented lately unto me. Aud how like A most regardless stranger he walks by, Merely ignorant of that sat, you are not indeed The principal'st part of him, what strange mysteries Inscrutable love works by. The time you see, Is not yet come. But 'tis in our power now To bring time nearer, knowledge is a mastery, And make it observe us, and not we it I would force nothing From its proper virtue, Let time have his full course. I'd rather die The modest death of undiscovered love, You have heavens left and lowest servant suffer, Or in his motion receive check for me: How is my soul's growth altared that single life, The fittest garment that peace ere made for't Is grown too straight, too stubborn on the sudden. He comes this way again. Oh here's a Traitor Leapt from my heart into my cheek all ready. That will betray all to his powerful eye, If it but glance upon me. By my verity Look, he's passed by again, down in neglect Without the prosperous hint of so much happiness To look upon his forehead, how close fate Seals up the eye of humane understanding, Till like the sun's flower, time and love encloses it, 'Tis pity he should dwell in ignorance longer. What will you do? Yes, die a bashful death, do And let the remedy pass by unused skill, You're chained enough all ready, look into't, Absolute (Sir) with your most noble pardon, For this my rude intrusion I am bold To bring the knowledge of a secret nearer, By many days (sir) then would it arrive, In its own proper revelation with you, Pray turn and fix, do you know yond noble goodness,
