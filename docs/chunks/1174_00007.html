But at his fall there happed a chance. Worth mark? I! what was that sweet Marian You'll not hear? I love these interruptions in a Story; They make it sweeter. You do know, as soon As the Assay is taken. On my Marian. I did but take the Assay. You stop one's mouth, And yet you bid 'em speak— when the Arbours made. Pulled down, and paunch turned out. He that undoes him; Doth cleave the brisket-bone, upon the spoon Of which, a little gristle grows, you call it— the Ravens-bone. Now, o'er head sat a Raven! On a sear bough! a grown great Bird! and Hoarse! Who, all the while the Deer was breaking up, So crooked and cried for't, as all the huntsmen, (Especially old Scathlock) thought it ominous! Swore it was Mother Maudlin; whom he met, At the Day-dawne; just as he roused the Deer, Out of his Laire: but we made shifted to run him Of his four legs, and sunk him e'er we left. Is the Deer come? He lies within o the dresser! Will you go see him Mellifleur? I attend you. Come Amie, you'll go with us? I am not well. she's sick o' the young Shep'ard that be kissed her. Nay, you must give them all their rudenesses; They are not else themselves, without their language. And what do you think of her? As of a Witch. They call her a Wise-woman, but I think her An arrant Witch. And wherefore think you so? Because, I saw her since, broiling the bone Was cast her at the Quarry. Where saw you her? I' the Chimley nuik, within: she's there, now. Your Hunt holds in his tale, still; and tells more! My Hunt? what tale? How! cloudy, Marian! What look is this? A fit one, Sir, for you. Hand of rude Ranger! Sirrah, get you in And bear the Venison hence. It is too good For these course rustic mouths that cannot open, Or spend a thank for't. A starved Muttons carcase Would better fit their palates. See it carried To Mother Maudlin's, whom you call the Witch, Sir. Tell her I sent it to make merry with, she'll turn us thanks at lest! why standest thou, Groom? I wonder he can move! that he's not fixed! If that his feeling be the same with mine! I dare not trust the faith of mine own senses. I fear mine eyes, and ears! this is not Marian! Nor am I Robin-hood! I pray you ask her!
