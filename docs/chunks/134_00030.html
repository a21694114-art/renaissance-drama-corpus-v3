Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Where shall I wretched mother of Alcides wish to be? Where is my child? where is my son? If sight deceive not me With gasping mouth, and panting heart lo where he sprawling lies. Where as (alas) in raging heart of boiling fits he fries, He groves, all is dispatched, dear child let me Alcides mine Embrace thy pining limbs: with kiss enfolded my arms in thine Where are the limbs? where is the neck that bore the skies alone? What thus hath mangled thee that all thy corpse is waste and gone? I am your Hercles' mother dear, whom thus ye see here lost. Acknowledge me all though God knows I seem but as a ghost. Why do you turn your face away and mourning visage mild. Are ye ashamed that Hercules should counted be your child? What world hath bred this uncouth bug? what land engendered it? Or else what monstrous mischief may on thee triumphing sit? Who is't that conquers Hercules? By treason of his Wife Thou seest how wretched Hercules do lose his loathed Life. To overthrow my Hercules, what treason hath the might? That which a wrathful Dame doth seek to case her of her spite. How hath this pestilence gotten to thy Limbs and bleeding bones? acp-p a shirt the woman had conveyed it for the nonce. Where is the shirt for nothing but thy naked corpse I see? The vesture by the poison rank devoured is with me. And can such poison be contrived? I think within my guts, That hideous Hydra hissing Snake his sloughy body puts, A thousand plagues of Lerna Pool within my Bowels ramps: What raging dear is this that drives up all Sicilia damps? What Clime of Hell forbids the day to pass the boiling zone? O Maies amid the greedy gulfs and pools let me be thrown. What Ister can my Carcase cool? no not the Ocean main Of these my stewing vapours may the raging quench again? (All moisture of my limbs in these my fits are fried away) The juice will soon be soaked up, what precedent of hell Let me return from under ground again with Ioue to dwell He ought to have retained me still, receive me once again Into thy dungeon dark that hell may in this pickle plain Behold the man that conquered it, no booty bring I will Away with me: why dost thou quake for fear of Hercles' still. Set on me death courageously for now I may be killed Now stint thy tender tears that down thy checks so long have trilled, And master this thy malady compel thy sorrows sloupe. And show that in these plunging pangs Alcides did not droop, And as it hath been raised thy guise force death and hell to shrink.
