What on your trust's imposed is the redeeming Those two betrayed young men, whom Ward did sell, When to his barbarous cruelty they opposed, And lost themselves, & state: their ransom's here, As you prove just, from all mishaps rest clear, And if I fail to accomplish your desires, All my sins haunt me, when my breath expires.
