You flatter, you flatter sir Gallant? but whist, here she enters; I prithee question her? oh you're welcome. Its this you new scholar Master Primero? Marry its her sir. I'll commend your judgement in a wench while I live, that face will get money i'faith, 'twill be a get penny I warrant you? got to? your fortune was choice pretty Bliss to fall into the regard of so kind a Gentleman. I hope so sir. See, what his care has provided already for you, you'll be simply set o' to the world: if you'll have that care now to deserve his pains oh that will be acceptable: and these be the Rudiments you most chiefly point at: to counterfeit cunningly, to wind in gentlemen with pourefull attraction, to keep his house in name and custom, to dissemble with your own brother, never to betray your followed imperfections, nor lay open the state of their bodies to strangers, to believe those that give you, to gull those that believe you, to laugh at all under Taffeta, and these be your rudiments. there's e'en all i faith, we'll trouble you with no more, nay you shall live at ease enough: for nimming away jewels and favours from Gentlemen (which are your chief veils) hope that will come naturally enough to you, I need not instruct you, you'll have that with, I trussed to make the most of your pleasure. I hope one's mother-wit will serve for that sir. Oh properest of all wench: it most be a she wit that does those things,& life mother was quick enough at it in her days. give me leave sister to examine you upon 2. or 3. particulars: and you make you ready be no ashamed, hers noon but friends— are you a maid. Yes, in the last quarter sir. Very proper, that e'en going o', a maid in the last quarter, that's a whore in the first;— let me see, new moon on Thursday, she'll be chande by that time too; are you willing to pleasure gentlemen? We are all borne to pleasure our country forsooth. Excellent, can you carry yourself cunningly, and seem often holy. Oh fear not that sir, my friends were all puritanes. I'll near try her further. She's done well i'faith, I fear not now to turn her lose to any gentleman in Europe. You need not sir of her own accord, I think she'll be lose enough without turning— Arthur. Here sir. Go make haste, shift her into that suit presently. It shall be done. Arthur do't neatly Arthur? Feared not sir. Fellow him wench. With all my heart sir—. But master In what are we forgetful all this while? In what? The wooing business man. Heart that's true. The Gallants will prevent vs. Are you certain. I can avouch it there's a general meeting, at the deceased knights house this afternoon there's rivalship enough. N doubt in that? Would either thou or I might bear her from 'em.
