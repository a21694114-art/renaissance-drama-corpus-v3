Now Iesus gammer gurton, what driveth you to this sadness I fear me by my conscience, you will sure fall to madness. Who is that, what Diccon, cham lost man: fie fie. Marry fie on them that be worthy, but what should be your trouble, Alas the more ich think on it, my sorrow it waxeth double My goodly tossing sporyars needle, chaveave lost ich wot not where. Your needle, when? My needle (alas) ich might full ill it spare, As god himself he knoweth near one beside chaveave. If this be all good gammer, I warrant you all is save. Why know you any tidings which way my needle is gone? Yea that I do doubtless, as ye shall here anon, A see a thing this matter toucheth, within these .xx. hours, Even at this gate, before my face, by a neighbour of yours, She stooped me down, and up she took a needle or a pin: I durst be sworn it was even yours, by all my mother's kin. It was my needle diccon ich wot, for here even by this post Ich sat, what time as ich up start, and so my needle it lost: Who was it lieve son? speak ich pray thee, & quickly tell me that?
