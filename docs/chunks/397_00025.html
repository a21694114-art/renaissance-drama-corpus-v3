Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

What picture of the pestilence is this? A poor man sir, a poor man sir: down I pray ye, I pray let me sit down. A sir Richard, sir Richard, a good sir Richard: what have I deserved to be thus dealt with all at your worship's hands? a ha, ah, ah. At my hands knave? at my hands paltry knave? And I should be brought to my book oath sir: What Ieffrey? Anan, anon. A plague upon your jeffring, is your name Ieffrey? I and't please you sir. Why gentle Ieffrey then stay you awhile, What can you say, if you come to your book? If I be posed upon a book sir, though I be a poor prentice, I must speak the truth, & nothing but the truth sir. And what's your truth sir? O, O my heart. Mary sir this Knight, this man of worship. Well, what of me? what did my worship do? Mary ye came into the Bell, our room next the Bar, with this honest man as I take it. As thou tak'st it? O sir 'tis too true, too true, too true O Lord. And there he called for a pint of Sack, as good Sack (I'll be posed upon all the books that ever opened and shut) as any is in all Christendom. Body of me, I come and call for Sack? O ye did, ye did, ye did, O O. Well forward sirrah. Gloster hath done this jest. And you called then for Sugar sir, as good Sugar and as wholesome, as ever came in any cup of Sack: you drunk to this man, and you do well God be thanked, but he no sooner drunk: But I, but I, but I, O my head, O my heart. I cannot choose but smile at these conseites. I am mad, and yet I must laugh at Faukenbridge: Brother, look how sir Richard acts his rage? I came? I call? the man is like to die, Practise by th'emafse, practise by the marry God, Iohn loves me not, Prince Richard loves my wife, I shall be charged here, for a poisoned knave. Practise by th'Lord, practise I see it clear. And more Sir Richard, O Lord O Sir Richard, What more? what hast thou more? what practise more? O my box, my box, with the King's arms, O my box, O my box, it cost me, O Lord every penny O, my box, And what of your box sir. Mary sir it's lost, & 'tis well known my Master keeps no thieves in his house, O there was none but you and he. O then belike thou thinkest I had his box, O sir Richard I will not, O Lord I will not charge you for all the world, but, but, but for the warrant the old King signed to reprieve the Porter of the fleet, O God, O God
