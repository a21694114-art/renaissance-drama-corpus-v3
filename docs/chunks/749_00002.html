But me thinks Sword by the very sounding of his Name should be the better Gentleman, and has the better Arms too: for if the truth were well known, Rapier hath but one arm. Sword bear Arms? he's a base companion. Alas I have known you bear a Basket Sword. If you look i'th' book of armory, I'll warrant you, you shall find Sword of more antiquity than you Rapier: He derives his Pedigree from Morglay, Bevis of South-hamptons sword; and that from S. George his sword, that killed the Dragon. I, the dragon in Sussex, the other day: But I'll warrant you Rapiers of as good a blood as he for his heart. Byth' mass, I think indeed, you are both well sanguind. Your both of one blood, only there's this difference, that sword comes of the elder brother, & you Rapier of the younger. And oftentimes the younger brother proves the better Soldier. Nay, Dagger will defend you both for good Soldiers. Rapier a Soldier? When did you ere know Rapier fight a Battle? Yes Rapier is a Soldier, and a Man as well Armed at all points as any one. No, no, its Sword that's the notable Soldier; Why, there's none of all you Captains could do any thing in War without him. I hope Rapier hath not been at Fencing-school all this while for nothing. Alas there's none Master of Defence but Dagger: But yet if you speak of Soldiers, there's Bow, Bill, and Gun, worth twenty such as we are. Indeed they say that Bow has been an old Soldier. Yet he's not fit for a Soldier, any man may bend him as he list. And as for Bowstring, I dare undertake to whip him myself. Then let me alone to tickle Bows owns neck i'faith. But what say you to Bill? he's a notable sturdy villain. Bill, I'll pay him sound, if ere I reach him. It's more than you can do, I'm afraid; For Bill's a Tallfellow on's hands, and will quickly be Wood: and then there's Gun, such another Bouncing-fellow too. Gun, Alas he's Nobody: any little Boy will make him roar. I have made him go off o'th' Fields, a good many times myself. Well, is Gun Nobody? I'm sure he'll give some blows sometimes. Its ' near but when Powder overcharges him, than indeed he'll be somewhat hot o'th' matter. I think that Powder is a vile bragger, he doth nothing but crack. Faith I know not what Soldier he is, but they say, he's an excellent politician. He a Politician? Why, he has an excellent wit? Pish, It's nothing but a flash. I'm sure I can remember since he was a Parlement-man. He a Parlement-man: For what Shire? Nay ber-lady, it was for the whole Country.

Gunne, Alas hees No-body: any little Boy will make him roare. I haue made him goe off 'oth Fieldes, a good many times my ſelfe. Well, is Gunne No-bodie? Ime ſure heel giue ſome blowes ſometimes.
