And not a jot of Tranio in your mouth, Tranio is changed into Lucentio. The better for him, would I were so too. So could I i'faith boy, to have the next wish after, that Lucentio indeed had Baptista's youngest daughter. But sirrah, not for my sake, but your masters, I advise you use your manners discreetly in all kind of companies: When I am alone, why then I am Tranio: but in all places else, you master Lucentio.
