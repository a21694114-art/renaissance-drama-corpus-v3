Thou art like one of these fellows, that when he enters the confines of a Tavern, claps me his Sword upon the Table, and says, God send me no need of thee: and by the operation of the second cup, draws him on the Drawer, when indeed there is no need. Am I like such a Fellow? Come, come, thou art as hot a jack in thy mood, as any in Italy: and as soon moved to be moody, and as soon moody to be moved. And what too?
