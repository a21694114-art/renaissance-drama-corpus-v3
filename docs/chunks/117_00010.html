soft Diccon and drink, what Doll I say Bring here a cup of the best ale, let's see, come quickly a way. Ye see masters the one end tapped of this my short device Now must we broche tother to, hour the smoke arise And by the time they have a while run. (I trust ye need not crave it. But look what lieth in both their hearts ye are like sure to have it Yea gog's soul, art alive yet? what Diccon dare ich come? A man is well hied to trust to thee, I will say nothing but mum But and ye come any nearer I pray you see all be sweet. Tush man, is gammers needle found, that chould gladly weet She may thank thee it is not found, for if thou had kept thy standing The devil he would have fet it out, even hodg at thy commanding gog's heart, & cold he tell nothing where the needle might be found Ye foolish dolt, ye were to seek, ere we had got our ground, Therefore his tale so doubtful was, that I could not perceive it. Then ich see well something was said, chop one day yet to have it, But diccon, diccon, did not the devil cry ho, ho, ho, If thou hadst tarried where thou stoodst, thou wouldest have said so Durst swear of a book, chard him roar, straight after ich was gone But tell me diccon what said the knave: let me here it anon. The whoreson talked to me. I know not well of what One while his tongue it ran and paltered of a Cat, Another while he stammered still upon a Rat, Last of all there was nothing but every word Chat, Chat, But this I well perceived before I would him rid, Between Chat, and the Rat, and the Cat, the needle is hid, Now whether Gyb our cat have eat it in her maw, Or Doctor Rat our curate have found it in the straw, Or this dame chat your neighbour have stolen it, god he knoweth But by the morrow at this time, we shall learn how the matter goeth Canst not learn tonight man, seest not what is here, 'tis not possible to make it sooner appear, Alas Diccon then chaveave no shift, but lest ich tarry to long high me to Sym glovers shop, there to seek for a Thong, There with this breech to tatch and tie as ich may. To morrow hodg if we chance to meet, shalt see what I will say. NOw this gear must forward go, for here my gammer: cometh, Be still a while & say nothing, make here a little roomth. Good lord, shall never be my luck my needle again to spy: Alas the while 'tis past my help, where 'tis still it must lie.
