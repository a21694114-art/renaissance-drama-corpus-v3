She who most strove to hinder and dissuade him. What had he done? That which deserves all life and love. How fine the heaven's powers can sorrows frame! The fates will play, and make my woe their game. Where is he? Safe enough I warrant you, get's leave of the Priest, master, and we'll go fetch him. We caught him out of the water. O, he had supped a abundance of salt porridge! And brought him to the ship where the mariners keep him. Why stand I idle here! O to the shore i'll fly, And either with him live, or for him die. Master, master, master. I'll follow him: nature can do no less Than either help, or pity such distress. Nay if you go too, then farewell all, Farewell ye rocks, farewell to thee O love, You lovely rocks, you hard and rocky love. Nay I shall turn swain presently and sing my final song. I marvel what it is that stays Dicaus. Marry let him stay till I send for him, the Cyclops shall want their breakfast this month. Here I must stay for him. Faine would I know how my gin thrives and prospers. Olinda is fast, and by my disamour hath quenched her love with death: if now Glaucilla be taken in that snare, then am I cunning: well may I prove a fisher, who have took too maids so soon with oneself bait and hook. Is not that Nomicus? I shall learn of him. Nomicus? Who Cosma? Why are these fishers bound? For you. For me? I for you, had not you 'cused Glaucilla, she had not been condemned: if she had not been condemned, Perindus would not have died for her: if he would not have died for her, he had not fallen from the rock: had he not fallen from the rock, we had not saved him: if we had not saved him, we had not been bound: were we not bound, we would show a fair pair of heels, What talks this fool? Perindus fall'n from the rock! Hast thou not heard then of Perindus' faith and fall? No, not a word; but fain would hear. And shalt: my tongue is as ready as thy ear; meanwhile lead these away, soon as Dicaeus returns, I'll overtake you. I prithee Mr Priest, let me crave one favour; that I may have an Epitaph for me in Neptune's church porch, I'll never go farther. Here's no time for Epitaphs, away. Nay, 'tis soon done, I'll trouble never a poet of them all, I have it already. Cancrone valorous and kind, where art thou? Cancrone too kind and valorous to live, Engulfed in Cyclops guts. Readers, why start you? His life for his master he did freely give. Ungrateful Sicelie that want'st his bones, Instead of members keeping his memory in stones. Short and sweet, Mr Priest. Cancrone, this is a land voyage, you must lead the way. But when we sail down the Cyclops throat, I'll give you the pre-eminence.
