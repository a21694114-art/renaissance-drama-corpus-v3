Thou traitor to my father dear, what makest thee here in field, Repent the of thy wickedness, and to me straight do yield. Thou princocks boy & bastard slave, thinks thou me to subdue? It lieth not with in thy power, thou boy I tell the true. But if I take thy corpses, it shallbe a food the birds to feed. Strike up your drums & forward now, to wars let us proceed. Oh villain traitor now the gods, ne mortal man shall save Thy corpse from death for blood for blood my father's death doth crave Oh tyrant first couldst thou vouchsafe, my father so to slay? But now no force for thou hast wrought, at last thine one decay A lack a lack yet spare my life, Horestes I the pray. Thy life? nay traitor vile, that chief I do denay. For as thou hast deserved, so I shall thy fact requite. That once couldst seem to me & mine, for to work such despite Therefore come forth and for thy fact, receive due punishment Repent I say this former life, for this is my judgement. That for my father's death, the which we find the chief to be, The causer of thou shalt be hanged, where we thy death may see And as thou for my father's death, due punishment receive, So shall my mother in likewise, for that she gave the leave. Him for to slay, and eke to it, with good will condescend, Therefore come of and soon dispatch, that we had made an end. Ah heavy fate & chance most ill, woe worth this hap of mine, For give my fault you sacred gods, and to my words incline Your gracious ear for causer first, I was this is most plain, Of Agamemnon death, wherefore I must receive this pain. Pardon I crave, vouchsafe ye gods, the same to grant it me, Now soldier work thy will in haste, I pray the heartily. Ah heavy fate would god I had, in turmoil great been slain Sith nothing can Horestes hands, from shedding blood restrain How chance you did not then lament his father when you slew? Yet hope I that he will me grant, my life that I should have. even as much as thou vouchsafest, his father's life to save, Therefore come of we must not stay, all day to weight on thee. Lo mighty prince for whom ye sent, lo present here is she. Have mercy son & quite remit, this fault of mine I pray, Be merciful Horestes mine, and do not me denay. Consider that in me thou haddest, they human shape composed That thou shouldst slay thy mother son, let it not be disclosed, Spare to pierce her heart with sword, call eke unto thy mind, Oedipus' fate and as Nero, show not thyself unkind.
