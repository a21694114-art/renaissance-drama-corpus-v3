'Twere insolence, to think his powers Can work on us, and equal ours. Return, Return, Like lights to burn On earth, For others good: Your second birth Will fame old LETHE'S flood, And warn a world, That now are hoorld About in tempest, how they prove Shadows for love. Leap forth: your light it is the nobler made, By being struck out of a shade. WHy, now you take me! these are rites That grace loves days, and crown his nights! These are the motions, I would see, And praise, in them that follow me! Not sighs, nor tears, nor wounded hearts, Nor flames, nor ghosts: but airy parts Tried, and refined as yours have been, And such they are, I glory in. Look, look unto this snaky rod, And stop your ears against the charming god; His every word, falls from him, is a snare: Who have so lately known him, should beware. Come, do not call it CUPIDS' crime, You were thought dead, before your time. If thus you move to HERMES will Alone; you will be thought so still. Go, take the ladies forth, and talk, And touch, and taste too: Ghosts can walk. Betwixt eyes, tongues, hands, the mutual strife Is bred, that tries the truth of life. They do, indeed, like dead men move, That think they live, and not in love. Nay, you should never have left off: But stayed, and heard your general CUPID scoff, To find you in the line you were. HERMES, your too much wit, breeds too much fear. Good Fly, good night. But, will you go? Can you leave LOVE, and he entreat you so? Here, take my quiver, and my bow, My torches too; that you, by all, may know I mean no danger to your stay: This night, I will create my holiday, And be yours, naked, and entire. As if that LOVE, disarmed, were less a fire? Away, away. Yet lest that VENUS' wanton Son Should, with the world, be quite undone, For your fair sakes (you brighter stars, Who have beheld these civil wars) FATE is content, these Lovers here Remain still such: so LOVE will swear Never to force them act to do, But what he will call HERMES too. I swear: and with like cause thank MERCURY, As these have, to thank him, and destiny. All then take cause of joy: for who hath not? Old LETHE, that their follies are forgot; We, that their lives unto their fates they fit: They, that they still shall love, and love with wit.
