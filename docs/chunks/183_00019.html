Disturbed thoughts drives me from company, And dries my marrow with their watchfulness, Continual trouble of my moody brain, Feebles my body by excess of drink, And nips me, as the bitter north-east wind, Doth check the tender blossoms in the spring. My golden time was when I had no gold, Though then I wanted, yet I slept secure, My daily toil, begat me night's repose: My night's repose made daylight fresh to me.
