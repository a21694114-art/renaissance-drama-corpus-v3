Why, is it not true, that such a text there is? Yes, there is such a text, but ye wrest it all amiss. As the Psalm doth say, God hath commanded Angels, To preserve the just, from dangerous plagues and perils. Well, than I said true, and as it lieth in the text. Yea, but ye omitted, four words which followeth next, As (in all thy ways) which if ye put out of sight, Ye shall never take, that place of scripture a right. Their ways are such rules, as God hath them commanded, By his living wo●de, justly to be observed. If they pass those rules, the Angels are not bound, To be their safeguard, but rather them to confound. To fall down backward, of a wanton penyshnes, Is none of those ways, that God ever taught doubtless. Then if I did it, I should tempt God very sore, And deserve to have, his anger evermore. I will not so do, for their fathers in the desert, Deed so tempt him once, and had the hate of his heart▪ The clause that ye had, maketh for non outward working, If ye mark the Psalm, thoroughly from his beginning. But what is the cause, ye went not fourth with the next verser It made not for me, if ye will, ye may it rehearse. Thu shalt (saith the Psalm) subdue the cruel serpent, And tread under foot, the lion and dragon pestilent. No nyghar (I say) for there ye touch free hold. Some love in no wise, to have their rudeness told. To walk in God's ways, it becometh a mortal man, And therefore I will, obey them if I can. For it is written, in the sixth of deuteronomy, Thu shalt in no wise, tempt God presumptuousely. What is it to tempt God? after your judgement. To take of his wo●de, an outward experiment, Of an idle brain, which God neither thought not meant. What persons do so? Make that more evident. All such as forsake, any grace or remedy, Appointed of God, for their own policy. As they that do think, that God should fill their belly, Without their labours, when his laws are contrary. And they that will say, the scripture of God doth slay, They never searching, thereof the verity. Those also tempt God, that vow presumptuously, Not having his gift, to keep their continency. With so many else, as follow their good intentes. Not grounded on God, nor yet on his commandments. These throw themselves down, into most deep damnation. little good get I, by this communication. Will ye walk farther, and let this prattling be? A mountain here is, which I would you to see, Trust me and ye will, it is a commodyouse thing. If it be so good, let us by thither going.

Some loue in no wyſe, to haue their rudeneſſe tolde. To walke in Gods wayes, it becometh a mortall man, And therfor I wyll, obeye them if I can. For it is written, in the ſext of Deutronomy, Thu ſhalt in no wyſe, tempt God preſumptuouſely. What is it to tempt God? after your iudgement. To take of hys wode, an outwarde experyment, Of an ydle brayne, whych God neyther thought not ment. What perſones do ſo? Make that more euydent. All ſoch as forſake, anye grace or remedye, Appoynted of God, for their owne polycye. As they that do thynke, that God ſhuld fyll their bellye, Without their labours, whan hys lawes are contrarye.
