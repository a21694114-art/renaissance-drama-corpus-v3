Yes sir, now you may choose, whether you'll stay till I let him down, or whether I shall cut him down? Cut him down master Mouse, cut him down And let's see, how he'll tumble. Why sauce, who asked your counsel? Let him down. What, with a Cushion too? why you provided To lead your life as did Diogenes; And for a Tub, to creep into a Basket. Ick sall seg v Vader, Ick quame here to your Huise and spreake tol de Dochterken. M. Mendall, you are welcome out of the Basket: I smell a Rat it was not for nothing, that you lost me. Oh skellum, you run away from me. I thought so sirrah, you gave him the slip. Faw, no forsooth; I'll tell you how it was: when we come from Bucklers-Burie into Cornwall, and I had taken the Cloak, than you should have turned down on your left hand and so have gone right forward, and so turned up again, and so have crossed the street; and you like an Ass. Why how now Rascal; is your manners such? You ass, you Dolt, why led you him through Cornhill, Your way had been to come through Canning street. Why so I did sir. Why thou seest ye were in Corn-Hill. Indeed sir there was three faults, the Night was dark, Master Mendall drunk, and I sleepy, that we could not tell very well, which way we went. Sirrah I owe for this a cudgeling: But Gentlemen, sith things have fallen out so, And for I see Vandal quakes for cold, This night accept your Lodgings in my house, And in the morning forward with your marriage, Come on my sons, sirrah fetch up more wood. Nay never weep Marina for the matter, Tears are but signs of sorrow, helping not. Would it not mad one to be crossed as I, Being in the very height of my desire? The strangers frustrate all: our true love's come, Nay more, even at the door, and Harvy's arms Spread as a Rainbow ready to receive me, And then my Father meet us: Oh God, oh God. Weep who that list for me, y faith not I, Though I am youngest yet my stomach's great: Nor 'tis not father, friends, nor any one, Shall make me wed the man I cannot love: I'll have my will ynfayth, i'faith I will. Let us determine Sisters what to do, My father means to wed us in the morning, And therefore something must be thought upon. we'll to our father and so know his mind, I and his reason too, we are no fools, Or Babes neither, to be fed with words. Agreed, agreed: but who shall speak for all? I will. No I. Thou wilt not speak for crying. Yes, yes I warrant you, that humours left, Be I but moved a little, I shall speak, And anger him I fear, ere I have done.
