I have prepared me A handsome female-shape, my man without Has them under his cloak; and I persuaded Sir Timothy, in hope that would court thee I his behalf, to have presented me Here for his Niece; you mark me. Very well; but now This the design is useless. By no means; It must be put in action; come go in, And help to dress me: Sir Timothy expects To meet me in that shape here: and besides In that disguise, secure I can at any time Steal out with you, and marry you. 'tis very right that sir, but yet methinks A wholesome song, sung to a fine new tune, Should not be much amiss: my boy here has one, And I'd be very loath, although I cannot Sing, as they say, myself, that she should hear What those, I can keep, can do; is not this right now?
