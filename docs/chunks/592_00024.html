Bounty, blessedness, and the spirit of wine attend my Mistress. Thanks good chub. God ye god morrow heartily mistress, and how do you since last I saw you. God's me you must not inquire how she does, that's privy counsel, fie, there's manners indeed. Pray you pardon my incivility, I was somewhat bold with you, but believe me I'll never be so saucy to ask you how you do again, as long as I live la. Square chub, what sullen black is that. A tassel that hangs at my purse strings, he dogs me and I give him scraps and pay for his ordinary, feed him, he liquours himself in the juice of my bounty, and when he hath sucked up strength of spirit he squeezeth it in my own face, when I have refined and sharpened his wits with good food, he cuts my fingers, and breaks jests upon me, I bear them, and beat him: but by this light the dull eyed thinks he does well, does very well, and but that he and I are of two faiths— I fill my belly, and feeds his brain, I could find in my heart to hug him, to hug him. Prithee persuade him to assume spirit and salute us. Lampatho, Lampatho, art out of countenance, for wits sake salute these beauties, how dost like them? Uds fut, I can liken them to nothing, but great men's great horse upon great days, whose tails are trust up in silk and silver. To them man, salute them. Bless you fair Ladies, God make you all his servants. God make you all his servants. He is holpen well had need of you, for be it spoken without profanism he hath more in this train, I fear me you ha' more servants than he, I am sure the Devil is an Angel of darkness. ay but those are Angels of light. Light Angels, prithee leave them, withdraw a little and hear a Sonnet prithee, hear a Sonnet. Made of Albanos widow that was, and monsieur Lauerdures wife that must be. Come leave his lips and command some liquour, if you have no Bottle-Ale, command some Claret-wine and borage, for that's my predominate humour sleek billed Bacchus, let's fill thy guts. Nay hear it, and relish it judiciously. I do relish it most judicially. Adored excellence, delicious sweet. Delicious sweet good, very good. If thou canst taste the purer juice of love. If thou canst taste the purer juice, good still, good still. I do relish it, it tastes sweet. Is not the metaphor good, is't not well followed? Passing good, very pleasing. Is't not sweet. Let me see't I'll make it sweet, I'll soak it in the juice of Helicon. by'r Lady, passing sweet, good, passing sweet. You wrong my Muse. The Irish flux upon thy Muse, thy whorish Muse, Here is no place for her loose brothelry, We will not deal with her, go, away, away, I'll be revenged.
