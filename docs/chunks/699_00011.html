Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

she present your Honour with this jewel? This jewel? how came she by such a jewel? She has had great Customers. she had need Madam, she sits at a great Rent. Alas for your great Rent: I'll keep her jewel, and keep you her out, ye were best: speak to me for a panderess? What shall we do? Go to; Let us alone. Arsace? I Lady. You must pardon us, we can not obtain your access. Mistress Sthenio, tell her Honour, if I get not access to her, and that instantly she's undone. This is something of importance. Madam, she swears your Honour is undone if she speak not with you instantly. Undone? Pray her for her honour's sake to give me instant access to her. she makes her business your Honour madam, and entreats for the good of that, her instant speech with you. How comes my Honour in question? Bring her to me. Our Cyprian Goddess save your good Honour. Stand you off I pray: How dare you Mistress importune access to me thus, considering the last warning I gave for your absence? Because, Madam, I have been moved by your honour's last most chaste admonition, to leave the offensive life. I led before. I? have you left it then? ay, I assure your Honour, unless it be for the pleasure of two or three poor Ladies, that have prodigal Knights to their husbands. Out on thee Impudent. Alas Madam, we would all be glad to live in our callings. Is this the reformed life thou talk'st on? I beseech your good Honour mistake me not, I boast of nothing but my charity, that's the worst. You get these jewels with charity, no doubt. But what's the point in which my Honour stands endangered I pray? In care of that Madam, I have presumed to offend your chaste eyes with my presence. Hearing it reported for truth and generally, that your Honour will take to husband a young Gentleman of this City called Tharsalio. I take him to husband? If your Honour does, you are utterly undone, for he's the most incontinent, and insatiate Man of Women that ever VENUS blessed with ability to please them. Let him be the Devil; I abhor his thought, and could I be informed particularly of any of these slanderers of mine Honour, he should as dearly dare it, as any thing wherein his life were endangered. Madam, the report of it is so strongly confident, that I fear the strong destiny of marriage is at work in it. But if it be Madam: Let your honour's known virtue resist and defy it for him: for not a hundred will serve his one turn. I protest to your Honour, When (VENUS pardon me) I winked at my unmaidenly exercise. I have known nine in a Night made mad with his love.

Shee present your Honour with this Iewell? This iewell? how came shee by such a iewell? Shee has had great Customers. Shee had neede Madam, shee sits at a great Rent. Alas for your great Rent: Ile keepe her iewell, and keepe you her out, yee were best: speake to me for a Pandresse? What shall we doe? Goe to; Let vs alone. Arsace? I Ladie. You must pardon vs, we can not obtaine your accesse. Mistris Sthenio, tell her Honour, if I get not accesse to her, and that instantly shee's vndone. This is some-thing of importance. Madam, shee sweares your Honour is vndone if she speake not with you instantly. Vndone? Pray her for her Honours sake to giue mee instant accesse to her. Shee makes her businesse your Honour Madame, and entreates for the good of that, her instant speech with you. How comes my Honour in question? Bring her to mee. OVr Cypriane Goddesse saue your good Honor. Stand you off I pray: How dare you Mistris importune accesse to me thus, considering the last warning I gaue for your absence? Because, Madam, I haue been mou'd by your Honours last most chast admonition, to leaue the offensiue life. I led before. I? haue you left it then? I, I assure your Honour, vnlesse it be for the pleasure of two or three poore Ladies, that haue prodigall Knights to their husbands. Out on thee Impudent. Alas Madam, wee would all bee glad to liue in our callings. Is this the reform'd life thou talk'st on? I beseech your good Honour mistake me not, I boast of nothing but my charitie, that's the worst. You get these iewels with charitie, no doubt. But whats the point in which my Honour stands endanger'd I pray? In care of that Madam, I haue presum'd to offend your chasteies with my presence. Hearing it reported for truth and generally, that your Honor will take to husband a yong Gentleman of this Citie called Tharsalio. I take him to husband? If your Honour does, you are vtterly vndone, for hee's the most incontinent, and insatiate Man of Women that euer VENVS blest with abilitie to please them. Let him be the Deuill; I abhorre his thought, and could I be inform'd particularly of any of these slanderers of mine Honour, he should as dearely dare it, as any thing wherein his life were endanger'd. Madam, the report of it is so strongly confident, that I feare the strong destinie of marriage is at worke in it. But if it bee Madam: Let your Honours knowne vertue resist and defie it for him: for not a hundred will serue his one turne. I protest to your Honour, When (VENVS pardon mee) I winckt at my vnmaidenly exercise. I haue knowne nine in a Night made mad with his loue.
