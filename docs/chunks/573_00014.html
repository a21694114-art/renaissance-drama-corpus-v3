Oh why do you so wilfully cherish your own poison? and breath against the best of life, chaste credit. Well may I call it chaste, for like a Maid Once falsely broke it ever lives decayed. O Captain, husband, you name that Dishonest, By whose good power all that are honest live, What madness is it to speak ill of that, Which makes all men speak well, take away credit By which men amongst men, are well reputed, That man may live, but still lives executed. Oh then show pity to that noble Title Which else you do Usurp: you're no true Captain, To let your enemies lead you, foul disdain, And everlasting Scandal, Oh believe it! The money you receive for my good name Will not be half enough to pay your shame. No, i'll sell thee then to the Smock: see here comes my honourable Chapman. Oh my poison! Him, whom mine honour and mine eye abhors. Lady, what so unjovially departed? Fine she policy? she makes my back her Bolster, but before my face, she not endures him, Tricks. Captain, how haps it she removed so strangely? Oh for modesty's cause awhile my Lord: she must restrain herself, she's not yours yet: Beside, it wear not wisdom to appear, Easy before my sight, fah, wherefore serves modesty but to pleasure a Lady now and then, and help her from suspect, that's the best use 'tis put too. Well observed of a Captain. No doubt you'll be soon friends my Lord. I think no less. And make what haste I can to my ship, I durst wager you'll be under sail before me. A pleasant voyage Captain. ay, a very pleasant voyage as can be: I see the hour is Ripe, here comes the Prisons Bawd the bond-maker, one that binds heirs before they are begot. And here are the crowns Captain, go? attend, Let our bay Courser wait. It shall be obeyed. A farmer's son, is't true? Has crowns to scatter? I give you your salute sir. I take it not unthankfully sir. I hear a good report of you sir— you'ave money. I have so, true. An excellent virtue. ay, to keep from you— hear you me Captain? I have a certain generous itch sir, to lose a few Angels in the way of profit: 'tis but a game at Tennis, Where if the ship keep above line, 'tis three to one: If not, there's but three hundred Angels gone. Is your venture three hundred? you're very preciously welcome: here's a voyage toward will make us all.— Beggarly fools, and swarming knaves. Captain, what's he? Fear him not my Lord, he's a Gull, he ventures with me, some filthy farmer's son, the father's a Iew, and the son a Gentleman: pha.— Yet he should be a Iew too, for he is new come from giving over swine.
