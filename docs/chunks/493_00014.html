Or shall I see her in another's power, And in his bosom laid, upbraid my loss, Whilst both with scornful smiles; then death more sour, To point me out for sport, report my cross? That sight which sometime did me sweetly charm, Should it become a cause of grief to me? No, none who live shall glory in my harm, Since she will not be mine, she shall not be.
