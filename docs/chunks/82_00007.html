Although by filthy force unjust Thy chastity he would annoy. Thee likewise whom thine own right hand, With sword did pierce, LUCRETIA true, Who tyrants rape could not withstand, Did bloody broils and wars ensue. And with her proud disdainful Make Lord Tarquin imp of cursed seed, Correction due doth TULLIA take For her unkindly shameless deed, Who on her Father's mangled corpse, To mischief bent, and wicked bane, The Carman she to drive did force, His cruel bruising wombling wane. And quite against all nature's law, Even from her own dismembered sire, The sacred rites she did withdraw, Denaying wonted burial fire This grief our woeful age doth feel, Through monstrous act against all kind, whenas in deadly crafty keel, To TYRRHEN seas, and wrestling wind, The proud presuming Prince did put, His mother trapped in subtle sort. The Mariners appointed cut, The swelling Seas from pleasant port. The clash resounds with stroke of oars, The Ship out launched apace doth spin, In surging froth aloof from shores, And ample course of seas doth win. Which gliding forth with loosened planks, In pressed streams with poised weight, The rifts do open closed cranks, That hidden were with secret sleight: And gulpeth up the leaking wave The woeful roaring noise and cry, With woman's shrieks themselves to save, Do reach and beat the starry sky. Then grisly present death doth dance Before their eyes with pining Cheeks: Whose deadly stroke and heavy chance For to avoid, than each man seeks: On rivened ribs some naked lie, And cut the beating waves in twain: And some their skilful swimming try, To get unto the shore again. The greatest part that sailed there, By destinies dire to men prefixed, In whirling swallows drowned were, The brinks of Seas and ground betwixt. Queen Agrippine her garments rends, She tears her ruffled locks of hair Abundant blubbering tears she spends, Through deep distress of fainting fear. Who when no hope of health she spies, Inflame with wrath, which woes appeased, O son, for so great gifts, she cries, Hast thou with such reward me pleased? This keel I have deserved sure, That bore and brought thee first to light: Who empire witless did procure, And Caesar's title for thy right. Show forth thy fearful spritish face, O Claudius now from Limbo lake, And of thy wife in wretched case, Revenge and due correction take. Thy death I causeless did conspire, Which now I rue with woeful heart: I dressed eke a funeral fire Unto thy son by deadly smart. Lo now as I deserved have, untombed go to thy guiltless Ghost, Enclosed in seas in stead of grave, And wrestling waves of Roman coast. The flashing flaws do flap her face, And on her speaking mouth do beat, Anon she sinks a certain space, Depressed down with surges great: Anon she fleets on weltering brim, And pats them of with tender hands Through fainting fear then taught to swim Approaching death, and fates withstands
