No with myself for loving such a swain, What fury made me dote upon these looks, Like winter's picture are his withered cheeks, His hair as ravens plumes, ah touch me not. His hands are like the sins of some foul fish, Look how he mows like to an aged ape, Over the chain Jack, or i'll make thee leap. What a sudden change is here? Now he swears by his ten bones, down I say. Did I not tell you I should have Larks, where is the larks, come we'll go catch some straight? No let us go a fishing with a net?
