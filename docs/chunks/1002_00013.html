Marry Sir they went to Sea, to aid the Cranes, and there have been mustering ever since, but for want of a Woodcock they ha' left behind 'em, they dare not venture upon the Pigmies, you may do well to overtake the Buzzard and relieve the Army Sir. I might have had more wit than to run myself into this calamity whom have we next. The Duke? what stranger's that? I must not seem dejected. Is this he your Highness discoursed of?
