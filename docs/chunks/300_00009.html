True, who bears hard His brother's death at Bristol the lord Scroop, I speak not this in estimation, As what I think might be, but what I know Is ruminated, plotted, and set down, And only stays but to behold the face Of that occasion that shall bring it on. I smell it. Upon my life it will do well. Before the game is afoot thou still lettest slip. Why, it cannot choose but be a noble plot, And then the power of Scotland, and of York, To join with Mortimer, ha. And so they shall. In faith it is exceedingly well aimed.
