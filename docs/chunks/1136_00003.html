I'll attend There will be gambols to please my Lady Salamandor. Zelmane you would say. I care not what you say, but if you mean to hear the Dances and see the fine songs you must make haste; Dorus you shall have leave to shake your heels, look you be mannerly, and show a clean calf, Mopsa what's that you ha' got there?
