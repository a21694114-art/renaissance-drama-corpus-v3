Tell him there's a Post come from my Master, with his horn full of good news, my Master will be here ere morning sweet soul. Let's in, and there expect their coming. And yet no matter: why should we go in. My friend Stephen, signify pray you within the house, your mistress is at hand, and bring your music forth into the air. How sweet the moonlight sleeps upon this bank, here will we sit, and let the sounds of music creep in our ears soft stillness, and the night become the tutches of sweet harmony: sit Iessica, look how the floor of heaven is thick inlaid with pattens of bright gold, there's not the smallest orb which thou beholdest but in his motion like an Angel sings, still quiring to the young eyed Cherubins; such harmony is in immortal souls, but whilst this muddy vesture of decay doth grossly close it in, we cannot hear it: Come ho, and wake Diana with a hymn, with sweetest tutches pierce your mistress ear, and draw her home with music. I am never merry when I hear sweet music.
