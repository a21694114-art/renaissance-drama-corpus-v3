Under a Mulberry tree I saw their conference, And under that tree she committed her offence, And meaning not for to conceal that fault, but it to tell, We mark the order and the tree, we also marked it well. Oh now thou liest thou wicked man, unto thy head I speak, And look that God his vengeance will with shame upon thee wreak. And lo thy sentence he hath heard, and thus he means to do, according to thy due desert, to cut thy life in two, And hearst thou bailie take thou aside, this vile and wicked wight, And so he cometh not to his mate, nor once within his sight. Now that this man is gone, I beseech you hear me speak, methinks he telleth still the tale, that he at first did break. Yea one man may still in one tale, in only order tell, But yet his Mate will not agree, do therefore mark him well. Hoe crier bring in voluptas there. Anon my Lord, see how he cometh here.
