Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Our worships shall attend their Ladyships thither. No Sir Giles by no means, they will go privately thither, but if you will meet them there. Meet them, we'll die for't, but we'll meet them. Let's go thither tonight knights, and you be true gallants. Content. How greedily they take it in Sirrah. No it is too far to go tonight, we'll be up betimes i'th' morning, and not go to bed at all. Why it's but ten miles, & a fine clear nightS. Gyles But ten miles? what do ye talk Captain? Why dost think its any more? ay, I'll ten pounds it's more than ten mile, or twelve either. What to Barnet? ay, to Barnet? 'Slid, I'll lay a hundred pound with thee, if thou wilt. I'll lay five hundred, to a hundred, Slight I will not be out borne with a wager, in that I know, I am sure it was four years agon ten miles thither, and I hope 'tis more now, 'slid do not miles grow think you, as well as other Animals. O wise Knight! I never Innd in the Town but once, and then they lodged me in a Chamber so full of these Ridiculus Fleas, that I was feign to lie standing all night, and yet I made my man rise, and put out the candle too, because they should not see to bire me. A pretty project. Intruth Captain if I might advise you, you should tarry, and take the morning afore you. How? O mon Diew, how the villain poltroon, dishonours his travail? you Buffonly mushroom, are you so mere rude, and English to advise your Captain? Nay I prithee Foulweather be not tempestuous with thy poor lackey. Tempestuous Sir Cutt, will your Frenchman think you, suffer his lackey to advise him? O God you must take heed Lackey how you advise your captain, your French lackey would not have done it. He would have been poxed first: alum le torch, sweet pages commend us to your Ladies, say we kiss their white hands, and will not fail to meet them: knights which of you leads? Not we Sir, you are a Captain, and a leader. Besides, thou art commended for the better man, for thou art very Commendations itself, and Captain Commendations. Why, what though I be Captain Commendations? Why and Captain commendations, is hearty? commendations, for Captains are hearty I am sure, or else hang them, Why, what if I be hearty Commendations, come, come, sweet knights lead the way. O Lord Sir, always after my hearty Commendations. Nay then you conquer me with precedent, by the Authentical form of all justice letters. Alloun. Here's a most sweet Gudgeon swallowed, is there not? ay but how will they digest it thinkest thou? when they shall find our Ladies not there? I have a vaunt-currying device shall make them digest it most healthfully.

Lets goe thether to night knights, and you bee true gailants. Content. How greedely they take it in Sirra. No it is too farre to goe to night, weele bee vp betimes ith morning, and not goe to bedd at all. Why its but ten miles, & a fine cleere nightS. Gyles But ten miles? what doe ye talke Captaine? VVhy doost thinke its any more? I, elaie ten pounds its more then ten mile, or twelue either. VVhat to Barnet? I, to Barnet? Slidd, Ile laie a hūdred poūd with thee, if thou wilt. Ile laie fiue hundred, to a hundred, Slight I will not be out borne with a wager, in that I know, I am sure it was foure yeares agon ten miles thether, and I hope tis more now, Slidd doe not miles growe thinke you, as well as other Animals.
