Our worships shall attend their Ladyships thither. No Sir Giles by no means, they will go privately thither, but if you will meet them there. Meet them, we'll die for't, but we'll meet them. Let's go thither tonight knights, and you be true gallants. Content. How greedily they take it in Sirrah. No it is too far to go tonight, we'll be up betimes i'th' morning, and not go to bed at all. Why it's but ten miles, & a fine clear nightS. Gyles But ten miles? what do ye talk Captain? Why dost think its any more? ay, I'll ten pounds it's more than ten mile, or twelve either. What to Barnet? ay, to Barnet? 'Slid, I'll lay a hundred pound with thee, if thou wilt. I'll lay five hundred, to a hundred, Slight I will not be out borne with a wager, in that I know, I am sure it was four years agon ten miles thither, and I hope 'tis more now, 'slid do not miles grow think you, as well as other Animals. O wise Knight!
