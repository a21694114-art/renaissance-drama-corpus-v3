Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

I would my master had left Some other to be his agent here: Faith I am weary of the office already, What signior Tremomundo, That rid a pilgrimage to beg cakebread. O take me not unprovided, let me fetch my weapons. Why I meant nothing but a Basolus Manus. No, didst thou not mean to give me the privy stab? No by my troth sir. Nay if thou hadst, I had not feared thee I, I tell thee my skin holds out Pistol proof. Pistol proof? i'll try if it will hold out pin prove, O shoot no more great God I yield to thee. I see his skin is but pistol proof from the girdle upward What sudden agony was that? Why sawst thou not, how Cupid God of love, Not daring look me in the martial face, Came like a coward stealing after me, And with his pointed dart pricked my posteriors. Then here my opinion concerning that point, The Ladies of Rhodes hearing that you have lost, A capitol part of your Lady ware, Have made their petition to Cupid, To plague you above all other, As one prejudicial to their muliebrity, Now sir, Cupid seeing you already hurt before, Thinks it a greater punishment to hurt you behind, Therefore I would wish you to have an eye to the back door Sooth thou sayest, I must be fenced behind, I'll hang my target there. Indeed that will serve to bear of some blows, When you run away in a fray. Sirrah, sirrah, what art thou? That thus encroachest upon my familiarity, Without special admittance. Why do you not know me? I am Erastus' man. What art thou that petty pigmy, That challenged me at Rhode;: Whom I refused to combat for his minority, Where is Erastus I owe him chastisement in Perseda's quarrel. Do not you know that they are all friends, And Erastus married to Perseda, And Erastus made governor of Rhodes, And I left here to be their agent? O coelum, O terra, O maria Neptune, Did I turn Turk to follow her so far, The more shame for you. And is she linked in liking with my foe? That's because you were out of the way. O wicked Turk for to steal her hence. O wicked turn coat that would have her stay. The truth is, i'll be a Turk no more. And I fear thou wilt never prove good christian. I will after to take revenge. And i'll stay here about my masters business. Farewell Constantinople, I will to Rhodes. Farewell counterfeit fool, God send him good shipping: 'tis noised about, that Brusor is sent, To fetch my master back again, I cannot be well till I hear the rest of the news, Therefore I'll about it straight. Now Fortune what hast thou done in this latter act? I brought Perseda to the presence, Of Soliman the Turkish Emperor, And gave Lucina into Brusor's hands.
