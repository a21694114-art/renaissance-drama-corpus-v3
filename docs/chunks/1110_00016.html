Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Tush, no matter, What's that to us? Come sweet, cut up the Pullet, And after we'll to Bed. Do you't, I am no Carver. Do you strain courtesies? had I it in fingering I'd make you both make but a Friday's feast; Oh how the steam perfumes my Nostrils. In faith it shall be thine. If I begin, may I ne'er eat more. Here's a third would do't, knew he but how to come by't. Well, since you'll force me to't, I will make bold this once. If that should be my Husband? Ha, what then? You were shamed, I quite undone; he knocks again; Upon my life 'tis he. What shall become of me? Dost thou not think he'll spare an Officer? But fall on the king's Image? So soon started? Why Nan, asleep or dead? My husband's voice, who's there? Where, which way? Creep beneath the Bed. Why dost not open door? Now to convey these victuals hence, and put out the fire, Lord Husband is it you? who looked for you so late? These shall into the Cupboard, if the smell Betray's not w'are safe. ay, if my swell betray me not. Why dost not rise? I have got such a cold, a cough withal, I thought I should have died; stay but a little Till I have cast my Petticoat about me, I'll quickly let you in; the fire's quite out, No sign of any Feast. I'm almost starved, prithee make haste. I am coming, good lie close, and if I can get him Once to bed, I have a trick to shift you. I do feel I had need of shift already. Here's brave juggling? For this night, Constable, I am your Watch; Oh but the smoking pullet. Lord Husband that you'll venture whom so late, So many Knaves abroad? I fear no robbing, and for my Carcase, I still bear About me to defend that, I've traveled hard today, And am very hungry prithee wife see what thou hast in the house? What, talk of Victuals now? is this a time of night? I by my faith, for one that could come by't. Talk not to me, something I must and will have. Had I known But of your coming home I had provided, But now I pray to bed, this cold so troubles me. Cold troubles me, lay me some Faggots on. You see the fire's quite out. I'll have't revived. You're such another man? See how she stirs? Would house and all were fired, So some of us were out on't. I for one. I would have had some company, I had not Such an appetite to be merry for an hour this seven year; That I could tell where to call up some good fellow That I knew, we would not part these two hours. That's my Qu. Now bless us wife, what noise is in the Garret? No hurt man; nay, pray will you to bed? Bed me no beds, I'll know the reason of't.
