And I'll pursue thee wheresoe'er thou fliest, Nor shalt thou rest in England till thou diest. Master, I would wish you to leave the Country, and see what good entertainment you will have in the City, I do not think but there you will be most kindly respected, I have been there in my youth, there's Hospitality, & you talk of Hospitality, and they talk of you abomination to see: for there Master come to them as often as you will, four times a day, and they'll make Nobody drink, they love to have Nobody trouble them, and without good security they will lend Nobody money. Come into Birchin Lane, they'll give Nobody a suit, choose where he list; go into Cheapside, and Nobody may take up as much plate as he can carry. Then I'll to London, for the Country tires me With exclamations, and with open wrongs, Sith in the City they affect me so. O Master, there I am sure Nobody may have any thing without money, Nobody may come out of the Tavern without paying his reckoning at his pleasure. That's better than the Country. Who comes here? Minion, where have you been all this night? Why do you ask husband? Because I would know wife. I have been with Nobody. 'tis a lie good man, believe her not, she was not with me. And who hath lain with you tonight? Lie with me, why Nobody. Oh monstrous, they would make me a whoremaster. Well, I do not think but Somebody hath been with you. Somebody was indeed. God's life husband, you do me wrong, I lay with Nobody. Well minion, though Nobody bear the blame, Use it no more, lest Somebody bide the shame. I will endure no longer in this Climate It is so full of slanders, I'll to the City, And there perform the deeds of charity. Now you rascal, who have you been withal at the alehouse? Sooth I was with Nobody. Not with me. And who was drunk there with you? Sooth Nobody was drunk with me. O intolerable! they would make me a drunkard to, I cannot endure any longer, I must hence, No patience with such scandals can dispense. Well sirrah, if I take you so again, I'll so belabour you: O neighbour good morrow. Good morrow, You are sad methinks, Faith sir I have cause, I have lent a friend of mine a hundred pound, and have nobody's word for the payment, bill, nor bond, nor any thing to show. Have you nobody's word, I'll assure you that Nobody is a good man, a good man I assure you neighbour, Nobody will keep his word, nobody's word is as good as his bond. ay, say you so, nay then let's drink down sorrow, If none would lend, than Nobody should borrow.
