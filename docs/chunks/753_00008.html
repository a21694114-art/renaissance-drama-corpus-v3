A little sleep will ease that complement. Once more good night. Once more dear Lady, and then all sweet nights. Dear Sir be short and sweet then. Not till the man be in his bed, his grave; his grave, his bed; the very same again Sir. Our Comic Poet gives the reason sweetly; Plenus rimarum est, he is full of loop-holes, and will discover to our Patroness. Your comment Sir has made me understand you. Sir be addressed, the graces do salute you with the full bowl of plenty. Is our old enemy entombed? He's fast? And does he snore out supinely with the Poet? No, he out-snores the Poet. Gentlewoman, this courtesy shall bind a stranger to you, ever your servant. Sir, my Sister's strictness makes not us forget you are a stranger and a Gentleman.
