If he scape, hang me. I shall wish thee hanged, if he do not: treason! I may thank my peevish humour for't. Madam the gondola is ready. Thou bringst physic when the patient's dead boy: our jest's turned earnest. Is a dead in earnest? As good, or rather worse, he's buried quick. O madam, many a good thing has been buried quick and survived again, I would be buried quick myself, and I might choose my grave. he's buried in close prison boy, he's known for the duke of Mantua's son, and by my father's letter attached for a traitor. Good gentleman, and I be not sorry for him: who is his keeper? The testy ass Hortensio. Uds foot let's enlarge him, Not possible boy. Not possible? 'tis: we'll cozen his keeper. We cannot. Cannot! we can: your father made a Lord of him, but be ruled by me, his daughter shall make a fool of him: you are not the first woman has made a fool of a wiser Lord than he is. Shall he be cozened? As palpably as at the Lottery, my brains are in labour of the stratagem already. Sons of Octavio if your princely thoughts Can stoop to such mean beauty, from this hand Receive your wives, but should the Duke your father— Fear not old man, he was the means that breathed this spirit into us. Wood us to this course. But should he prove Apostata, deny Love which he first enforced us to profess, So firm are our inseparate affections, To win our loves weed lose the names of sons. Your father thanks you, but hot-sprighted you that, Take counsel from experience, ere ye tie The gordian knot which none but heaven can lose, Crave his consent: when an imperial hand Shakes a weak shed, the building cannot stand. Not stand? it shall: not Jove himself can ruin the groundwork for our love. Not Ioue! Not Ioue, Should a speak Our love admits no separation. Then to 〈...〉 your love is chaste. As As And yours. And ours, And ours, Then lend me all your hands, Whilst thus a father's tongue forbids the bands, Forgetful boys, but most audacious traitor, That durst in thought consent to wrong thy Prince, Out of my fight; no Land that calls me Lord, Shall bear a weight so hateful as they self: Live ever banished, if (three days expire) Thou or these lustful strumpets. Father. boys; If you be mine show't in obedience: If (three days passed) you live within my dukedom, Thee as a slave i'll doom unto the galleys, And these thy brats as common prostitutes▪ Shall dry their lustful veins in the Burdello. Come boys, to Court; he that first gave you lives, Will to your births provide you equal wives. They have our loves. Our oaths. Our hearts and hands.
