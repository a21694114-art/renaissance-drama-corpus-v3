By my troth 'tis pretty. And by my conscience 'tis true, 'twere made i' Durham, on a Lass of my bigness. And in thy clothes I believe. But will you be 'gan now, than all my joy leaves me. Sweet soul, thou shalt have thy joy again. I will joy thee, enjoy thee, and over joy thee. Governor let us fly about this business. I will not sleep, before I have got a Licence, stolen her away, wedded her, bedded her, and put her in her wits again. Are you able to do that think you? I'll warrant thee: for all Maids are mad till they be married. What say you to that Lady. Pox on you, I runs sweet hazard to advance your fortune, do I not? Remember your Covenant with Master Tridewell Captain. And when the work is done, here's my hand, you shall partake of what I get by't. And hark you. She may perhaps, when she comes to herself, and finds me to be no sir Philip, be a little startled. But I mean the first night to put so much of my own love into her, as shall work out his I doubt not, or any his that came there before me. O Gentlemen! my Masters coming, all's spoilt if he take you. Part quickly. Is mine Uncle com'd? and must we part then? Kiss, and part; kiss and part. Sweet heart, not a word of me till I come to fetch you off with honour. All benisons be with you. Indeed you be the goodliest man, that ere made Maiden fain. Poor heart she dotes. I do not know how much I am in debt to my conscience, till I have made her amends.— This may breed good blood.
