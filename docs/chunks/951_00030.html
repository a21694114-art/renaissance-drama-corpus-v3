It is indeed an undelight full walk; But if I do not err in my belief, I think the ground, the trees, the rocks, the springs, Have since my Princely Master Charles his wrack Appeared more dismal, than they did before, I memory of his vntimelesse fall. For hereabouts, hereabouts the place, Where his fair body lay deformed by death Here Hoffman's son, and I embalmed him After we had concluded to deceive Your sacred person, and Duke Ferdinand By causing Hoffman to assume his name.
