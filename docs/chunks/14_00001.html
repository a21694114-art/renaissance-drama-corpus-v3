Why desire men than praise evermore Of the acts of their ancestors done before One cause there of is for lak of learning They ꝑseyve not the reason of the thing Another is be cause there be many That call themself gentlemen unworthy which life voluptuously & bestial And do no good in the world at all But lice in pride sloth & unthrystynes And be cause they have no manner goodness Nor property nor virtue in them where by Any man should think them any praise worthy Therefore they seek for commendation Of the actis that their ancestors have done Then Imeruell men desire to be called Of the blood
