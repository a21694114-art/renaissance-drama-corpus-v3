Berty farewell, to Santon bound we are, With these companions, and our conduct care, You people happy in a land of peace, That joy your consciences, with the world's increase, Look with indifference into my sad life, Here my poor husband, dares not know his wife, And I a prince's, to avoid like danger, Must use my own dear husband, as a stranger, Towards Santon we, through deserts, any way, Though all should leave me, I for grief must stay.
