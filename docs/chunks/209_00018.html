Why that's well said, and I commend thy honest mind, Would all men were of thy mind: I warrant thee, thou art an honest man, & one that loves the king But tell me, what wouldst thou have me do? Nothing but procure me the king's letter to convey corn beyond seas, for in England it is so good cheap, that a man can make no living by selling thereof: therefore if the King will grant me his letter, I will at any time, lend him five or six hundred and perhaps never ask it again, and I will not forget your pains. Sir, fear not, I will do it for you, I warrant you, For I tell you, I can do much with the King.
