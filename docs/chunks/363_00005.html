One of my colour, and a better too. One of your colour, I near remember him, one of that colour. Or of that complexion. What's that ye call complexion in a horse. The colour sir. Set me a colour on your jest, or I will: Nay good sir hold your hands. What, shall we have it? Why sir, I cannot paint. Well then, I can. and I shall find a pencil for ye sir. Then I must find the table if you do. A whoreson barren wicked urchin. Look how you chafe, you would be angry more, if I should tell it you. Go to, I'll anger ye and if you do not. Why sir, the horse that I do mean, Hath a leg both straight and clean. That hath nor spavin, splint nor flaw. But is the best that ever ye saw, A pretty rising knee, O knee!
