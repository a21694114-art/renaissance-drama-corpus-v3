Come to the Port. I'll tell thee Diomedes, This brave, shall oft make thee to hide thy head: Lady, give me your hand, and as we walk, To our own selves bend we our needful talk. Hark, Hector's Trumpet. How have we spent this morning The Prince must think me tardy and remiss, That swore to ride before him in the field. 'Tis Troilus fault: come, come, to field with him. Let us make ready strait. Yea, with a Bridegroom's fresh alacrity Let us address to tend on Hector's heels: The glory of our Troy doth this day lie On his fair worth, and single Chivalry.
