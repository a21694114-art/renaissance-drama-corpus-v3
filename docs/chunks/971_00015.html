Verily, for the disease of longing, it is a disease, a carnal disease, or appetite, incident to women: and as it is carnal, and incident, it is natural, very natural: Now Pig, it is a meat, and a meat that is nourishing, and may be longed for, and so consequently eaten; it may be eaten; very exceeding well eaten: but in the Fair, and as a Bartholmew-pig, it cannot be eaten, for the very calling it a Bartholmew-pig, and to eat it so, is a spice of Idolatry, and you make the Fair, no better than one of the high Places. This I take it, is the state of the question. A high place. I, but in state of necessity: Place should give place, Mr Busie, (I have a conceit left, yet.) Good Brother, Zeal-of-the-land, think to make it as lawful as you can. Yes Sir, and as soon as you can: for it must be Sir; you see the danger my little wife is in, Sir. Truly, I do love my child dearly, and I would not have her miscarry, or hazard her first fruits, if it might be otherwise. Surely, it may be otherwise, but it is subject, to construction, subject, and hath a face of offence, with the weak, a great face, a foul face, but that face may have a veil put over it, and be shadowed, as it were, it may be eaten, and in the Fair, I take it, in a Booth, the tents of the wicked: the place is not much, not very much, we may be religious in midst of the profane, so it be eaten with a reformed mouth, with sobriety, and humbleness; not gorged in with gluttony, or greediness; there's the fear: for, should she go there, as taking pride in the place, or delight in the unclean dressing, to feed the vanity of the eye, or the lust of the palat, it were not well, it were not fit, it were abominable, and not good. Nay, I knew that afore, and told her on't, but courage, Win, we'll be humble enough; we'll seek out the homeliest Booth i'the Fair, that's certain, rather than fail, we'll eat it o' the ground. I, and I'll go with you myself, Win-the-fight, and my brother, Zeal-of-the-land, shall go with us too, for our better consolation. Uh, vh. I, and Solomon too, Win, (the more the merrier) Win, we'll leave Rabbi Busy in a Booth. Solomon, my cloak. Here, Sir. In the way of comfort to the weak, I will go, and eat. I will eat exceedingly, and prophesy; there may be a good use made of it, too, now I think on't: by the public eating of Swine's flesh, to profess our hate, and loathing of Iudaisme, whereof the brethrens stand taxed. I will therefore eat, yea, I will eat exceedingly. Good, i'faith, I will eat hearty too, because I will be no Iew, I could never away with that stiffnecked generation: and truly, I hope my little one will be like me, that cries for Pig so, i'the mother's belly.
