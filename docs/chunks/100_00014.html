My friend, in this case I can do you no pleasure. Sir, you serve in the Court as Fame doth tell. I am of the Court in deed, but none of the Counsel. As I hear, none is in greater favour with the King than you at this day, The more in favour, the less I dare say. It is a Courtiers praise to help straingers in misery. To help an other and hurt myself, it is an evil point of courtesy. You shall not hurt yourself to speak for the innocent. He is not innocent, whom the king judgeth nocent. Why sir? do you think this matter past all remedy? So far past that Dionysius hath sworn Damon to morrow shall die. This word my trembling heart cutteh in two, Ah sir, in this woeful case, what witted I best to do. Best to content yourself, when there is no remedy, He is well relieved that for knoweth his misery, Yet if any comfort be, it resteth in Eubulus, The chiefest counsellor about king Dionysius: Which pitieth Damon's case in this great extremity, Persuading the king from all kind of cruelty. The mighty Gods preserve you for this word of comfort, Taking my leave of your goodness, I will now resort, To Eubulus that good Counsellor: But hark, me think I hear a Trumpet blow. The king is at hand, stand close in the press, beware: if he know You are friend to Damon, he will take you for a spy also: Farewell I dare not be seen with you. Gronoo, do my commandment, strike off Damon's Icons by & by, Then bring him forth, I myself will see him executed presently. O mighty King, your commandment will I do speedily. Eubulus: thou hast talked in vain, for sure he shall die. Shall I suffer my life to stand in peril of every Spy? That he conspired against your person, his Accuser can not say, He only viewed your City, and will you for that make him away. What he would have done, the guess is great, he minded me to hurt That came so slily to search out the secret estate of my Court: Shall I still live in fear? no, not: I will cut off such Imps betime, Lest that to my further danger, too hie they climb. Yet have the mighty Gods, immortal Fame assigned, To all worldly Princes, which in mercy be inclined. Let Fame talk what she list, so I may live in safety. The only mean to that, is to use mercy. A mild Prince the people despiseth. A cruel king the people hateth. Let them hate me, so they fear me. That is not the way to live in safety. My sword and power shall purchase my quietness. That is sooner procured by mercy and gentleness. Dionysius aught to be feared. Better for him to be well-beloved. Fortune maketh all things subject to my power.
