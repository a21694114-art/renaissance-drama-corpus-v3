Vallee loo lo lillee lilo, Vallee loo le lo lil'o. Ah sweet gentleman, he keeps it up stately. Well held i'faith sir— mass and now I remember too, I think you near saw my little banqueting, box above, since I altered it. Why have you altered that? Oh divinely sir: the pictures are all new run over again. Fie. For what had the painter done think you, drew me, Venus naked, which is the grace of a man's Room you know, & when he had done, drew a number of Oaken-leaves before her, had not lawn been a hundred times softer, made a better show, and been more gentlewoman like? More ladylike, a great dea'e. Come, you shall see how 'tis altered now? I do not think but you'll like her. Where be your liveries? They attend without. Go, call the Coach! Gentlemen, you have excelled in kindness as we in boldness. So you think amiss sir? Kind Ladies we commit you to sweet dreams, ourselves unto the fortune of the dice, dice ho? You rest firm mine. E'en all my soul to thee. You keep your vows. Why do I breath, or see? Is your love constant. I to none but these now gone, ay, now I love nor them nor thee, 'slife, I should be cloyed, should I love one in three. Oh here's master Bowser now. Save you sweet Gentlemen. Sweet master Bowser? welcome? When come these dice. Yet anon, anon sir. Hast thou shown art in 'em. You shall be judge sir, here be the Tavern Beakers, And here peep out the fine Alchemy knaves, looking like, well sir, most of our Gallants, that seem what they are not. Peace villain, am not I in presence. Why that puts me in mind of the jest sir. Again, you quarter her. Nay, compare 'em, and spare 'em not. The bigness of the boar, just the same size, The marks, no difference: away, put money in thy pocket, and offer to draw in upon the least occasion. I am no babe sir. Hist. What's the matter now? Give me a pair of false Dice, ere you go. Pax on't, you're so troublesome too, you cannot remember a thing before, if I stay a little longer, I shall be stayed anon. Here be Dice for your worships. Oh come, come. The Vintner himself, Ilo shift away these beakers by a slight. Master Goldstone? How now you conjuring rascal? Bless your good worship, you're in humours methinks. Humours: say that again. I said no such word sir,— would I had my beakers out on's fingers. What's thy name Vintner? Iack and please your worship. Turn Knight like thy companions, scoundrel, live upon usury, wear thy gilt spurs at thy girdle for fear of slubbering. O no, I hope I shall have more grace than so sir: Pray let me help your worship. Cannot I push 'em together without your help? O I beseech your worship, they're the two standards of my house. Standards, there lie your Standards.
