Not before the Gentlewomen; not I never. O while you live, men before women: Custom hath placed it so. Why then Custom is not so mannerly, as I would be. Farewell M. Very well sir. Come then go we together, let the Wenches alone. Do you see yonder fellow? Yes: prithee what is he? I'll give you him within, he must not now be thought on: but you shall know him. I have observed my sister, and her eye Is much inquisitive after yond fellow; She has examined him from head to foot: I'll stay and see the issue.
