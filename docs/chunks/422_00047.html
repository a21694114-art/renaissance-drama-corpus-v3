Rufus Laberius Crispinus, and Demetrius Fannius, hold up your hands. You have (according to the Roman Custom) put yourselves upon Trial to the Urn, for divers and sundry Calumnies, whereof, you have before this time been indicted, and are now presently arraigned: Prepare yourselves to hearken to the verdict of your triers. Caius Cilnius Maecenas pronounceth you, by this hand-writing, Guilty. Corneli. Gallus, Guilty. Pantilius Tucca— us Parcel Guilty; I. He means himself: for it was he indeed, Suborned us to the Calumny. I, you whoreson Cantharideses? was't I? I appeal to your conscience, Captain. Then, you confess it, now. I do, and crave the mercy of the Court. What saith Crispinus? O, the Captain, the Captain. My Physic begins to work with my Patient, I see. Captain; stand forth and answer. Hold thy peace, Poet Praetor: I appeal from thee, to Caesar, I. Do me right, Royal Caesar. Mary, and I will, Sir. Lictors, gag him: And put a case of vizards o'er his head, That he may look Bifronted, as he speaks. Gods, and Fiends. Caesar! thou wilt not Caesar? wilt thou? Away, you whoreson Vultures; away. You think I am a dead Corpse now; because Caesar is disposed to jest with a man of Mark, or so. Hold your hooked talons out of my flesh, you inhuman Gorboduckes. Go to, do't. What? will the Royal Augustus cast away a Gentleman of worship, a Captain, and a Commander; for a couple of condemned caitiff Calumnious Cargoes? Dispatch, Lictors. Caesar. Forward, Tibullus. Demand, what cause they had to malign Horace. In troth, no great cause, not I; I must confess: but that he kept better company (for the most part) than I: and that better Men loved him, than loved me: and that his writings thrived better than mine, and were better liked & graced: Nothing else. Thus, envious Souls repine at others good. If this be all; faith, I forgive thee freely. Envy me still; so long as Virgil loves me, Gallus, Tibullus, and the best-best Caesar, My dear Maecenas; while these, with many more (Whose names I wisely slip) shall think me worthy Their honoured and adored Society, And read, and love, prove, and applaud my Poems; I would not wish but such as you should spite them. O. How now, Crispinus? O, I am sick. A Basin, a Basin, quickly; our Physic works. Faint not, man. O— Retrograde— Reciprocal— What's that, Horace? Retrograde, Reciprocal, and Incubus are come up. Thanks be to Iupiter. O— Glibery— Lubrical— Well said: here's some store. What are they? Glibery, Lubrical, and Defunct. O, they came up easy. O— O— What's that? Nothing, yet. Magnificate. Magnificate? that came up somewhat hard. I. What cheer, Crispinus? O, I shall cast up my— Spurious— Good. Again. Chillblained— O— That Clumsy stuck terribly.
