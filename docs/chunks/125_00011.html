This savage life, were hard to brook, if hope no comfort gave: But I (whose life, from Tyrant's wrath, God's providence did save, Do take in worth this misery, as penance for my miss: Still fed with hope to change this state, when Gods good pleasure is. A hollow Cave for house, and bed, in worth Andrugio takes, Such sorry food, as fortune sends, he seldom now forsakes. I am myself forsooth, now Butcher, Cook, Cater and all: Yea, often times I fall to sleep, with none, or supper small.
