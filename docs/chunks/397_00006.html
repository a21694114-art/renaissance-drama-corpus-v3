Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Stand still a while, desire her to make means Unto Prince Richard for my liberty, At thy return (make speed) I will reward thee. I am g g gone si sir. Commend me to her gentle Huntingdon, Tell her in these affairs I'll stand her friend, Her brother shall not long be prisoner: Say I will visit her immediately. Be gone sweet boy to Marian Faukenbridge, Thou lookest like love persuade her to be loving. So far as honour will I will persuade, I'll lay loves battery to her modest ears, Second my mild assault, you may chance win, Far parley at the least, may hap pass in. Hear take your charge, let no man speak with him, Except ourself, our brethren, or Earl Leicester. Not I my Lord, may not I speak with him? Yes Faukenbridge thou shalt. And why? he is his wives brother. Earl Iohn, although I be, I am true unto the State, & so is he. What, shall I have no servant of my own? No but the household servants of the Fleet. I thank you kinsman King, your father knows, Gloster may boldly give a base slave blows. O but not here, it was not well done here. Farewell good Gloster, you shall hear from us. Even what your Sons will suffer you to send; Is't not a misery to see you stand, That some time was, the Monarch of this land, Entreating traitors for a subjects freedom? Let him not speak, away with him to prison. here's like to be a well stayed common wealth, Where in proud Leicester, and licentious Iohn, Are pillars for the King to lean upon. we'll hear your railing Lecture in the Fleet. On our displeasure see he speak no more. On thy displeasure, well ye have me here; O that I were within my Fort of Bungye Whose walls are washed with the clear streams of Aueney Then would not Gloster pass a halfpenny, For all these rebels, and their poor King too. Laughst thou King Henry? thou knows my words are true, God help thee good old man, adieu adieu. That Castle shall be mine, where stands it Faukenbridge Far from your reach sure, under Feckhill ridge, Five hundred men (England hath few such wight) Keeps it for Gloster's use both day and night: But you may easily win it, wantonness words Quickly can master men, tongues out brawl swords. Ye are an idiot. I prithee Iohn forbear. What shall old winter with his frosty jests, Cross flowery pleasure? I and nip you too, God mary mother I would tickle you Were there no more in place but I and you. Seize these contentions, forward to the Tower, Release Queen Elinor, and leave me there Your prisoner I am sure, if ye had power, there's nothing lets you but the Commons fear: Keep your State Lords, we will by water go, Making the fresh Thames, salt with tears of woe. And we'll by land through the City ride, Making the people tremble at our pride.
