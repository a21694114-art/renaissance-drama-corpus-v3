What pratest thou, have I not answered thee thy day is broken? Usurer take pity upon me. I have other matters to talk on, farewell fellows. Why but thou covetous churl, wilt thou not receive thy money and deliver me my recognizance? Well fellow, go with me, and i'll help thee to a Lawyer.
