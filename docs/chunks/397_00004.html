Be pleased King puppet? have I stood for thee, Even in the mouth of death? opened my arms To sercle in seditious ugly shape? shook hands with duty, bad adieu to virtue, Profaned all Majesty in heaven and earth; Writ in black Characters on my white brow, The name of rebel Iohn against his Father: For thee, for thee, thou Otimie of honour, Thou worm of Majesty thou froth, thou puble. And must I now be pleased in pease to stand, While statutes make thee owner of my land? Good pastime good, now will the thieves fall out? O if I do, let me be never held Royal King Henry's son pardon me father, Pull down this rebel that hath done thee wrong. Dick, come and leave his side, assail him Lords, Let's have no parley but with bills and swords. Peace Iohn, lay down thy arms, hear Henry speak, He minds thee no such wrong. He were not best. Why hayre-brained brother can ye brook no jest? I do confirm you Earl of Nottingham. And Morton too? I and Morton too. Why so, now once more I'll sit down by you. Blow wind, the youngest of King Henry's stock, Would fitly serve to make a weathercock. Gape earth, challenge thine own as Gloster lies, Pity such muck is covered with the skies. Be quiet good my Lords, the Kings command You should be quiet, and 'tis very mere, It's most convenient, how say you Prince Richard? It is indeed. Why that is wisely said, you are a very kind indifferent man, Mary a God and by my holidame, Were not I had a feeling in my head, Of some suspicion twixt my wife and him, I should affect him more than all the world. Take heed old Richard, keep thee there mad lad, My Sister's fair, and beauty may turn bad. Room there, make room for young Huntingdon. A gallant youth, a proper Gentleman. Richard I have had wrong about his wardship. You cannot right yourself. He can and shall. Not with your help but honourable youth Have ye performed the business I enjoined? I have, and Skinke is come, here is his bill, No matter for his bill let him come in. Let him not enter, his infectious breath Will poison the assembly. Never doubt, there's more infectious breaths about your Throne, Leyster is there, your envious Sons is there; If them you can endure, no poison fear. Content thee Gloster. I must be content, when you that should mend all are patiented, Welcome good Skinke thou justly dost complain, Thou stand'st in dread of death for Rosamond, Whom thou didst poison at our dread command, And the appointment of our gracious Mother; See here my Father's hand unto thy pardon. I receive it graciously, wishing his soul sweet peace, in heaven for so meritorious a work, for I fear me I have not his heart though his hand. Be sure thou hast not, murderous bloodsucker, To jealous envy executioner.
