Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Nay, nay, 'tis like, the boy had ever a captivating tongue to take a woman— o excellent money, excellent money, Mistress of my devotions, as if my Widow's estate is little less too, and then Sander, he has got a monied woman too, there will be a bulk of money, Sim is puling sir, I may tell thee, one that by nature's course, cannot live long: tother, a Midnight sunset cuts off, then have I a trick to cozen both their widows, and make all mine, o Iarvis, what a monied generation shall I then get upon thy Mistress? A very virtuous brood. Hast done? I have done Sir. I'll in and get some music for thy Mistress, to quicken her this morning, and then to Church in earnest, 'tis done, where is Sir Nicholas Nemo and his words that watch so for her? Ha, ha, ha, all's mixed with honey, I have mirth, a sweet young Widow and her money, o that sweet Saint, called money. joy I, and a 100 pound a year in a black box to the bargain given away i'th' dark last night, to we know not who, and to be heard of we know not when. 'sfoot and this be joy, wood we had a handful, some slice of sorrow to season it. By this light 'twas strange. Believe me Sir, I thought I had given it you, he that took it called me by my name. Did he speak Welsh or English? Alas, I know not, I enjoined him silence seeing the Watch coming, who parted us. If this were not Master Randalls of Randall Hall that I told ye of, I'll be flayed. Be masked, and withdraw a while, here comes our Dad. Why Master Busy, asleep as thou standst man? Some horse taught him that 'tis worth god a mercy. I watch all night, I protest Sir the Counters pray for me, I send all in Cut and long tail. What, what? I sent twelve Gentlewomen, our own neighbours last night, for being so late but at a woman's labour. Alas Sir, a woman in that kind you know must ha' help. What's that to me? I am to take no notice of that, they might have let her alone till morning, or she might have cried out some other time. Nay, nay, Master Busy knows his place I warrant ye. Son Alexander welcome, and Ancient Young to, I have heard all. You must pardon the meritable rudeness of the Gentlewomen Sir, in not unmasking, they entreated me to inform you: there are some i'th' house to whom they wood by no means be laid open. They are witty, they are witty. But for myself, I am now your most obedient virtuous Alexander. Obedience, hang her virtue, let her starve; has she money? hath she money? Two Chests of silver, and two Utropian Trunks, full of gold and jewels. They are all Alexander's women, do ye mark? Alexander was the Conqueror Sir.
