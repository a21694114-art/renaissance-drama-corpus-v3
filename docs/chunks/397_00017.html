This is your father's plot, revenge it son. Father by heaven if this were your advice, Your head or heart shall pay the bitter price, Come mother, Brother, Leyster, let's away, I, I'll be one, in hope to meet the bastard, And then no more myself will be his headsman. Richard and Faukenbridge follow the search, You may prevent mischance by meeting Gloster, If ye find Skinke see that you apprehend him, I hear there is a wizard at black heath, Let some inquire of him where Skinke remains, Although I trust not to those fallacies, Yet now and then such men prove Soothsayers. Will you be gone? Withal my heart, withal my heart my Lord, Come Princely Richard, we are ever yoked. Pray God there be no mystery in this, Be not suspicious where there is no cause, Nay nothing, nothing, I am but in jest. Call in a Pursuivant. hear's one my Liege, There is a Porter likely to be hanged, For letting Gloster scape, sirrah attend, You shall have a repreive to bring him us, These boys are to to stubborn Lancaster, But 'tis their mother's fault, if thus she move me, I'll have her head though all the world reprove me. Do not deny me gentle Huntingdon. My Lord will miss me. Tut let me excuse thee. Turn woman, O it is intolerable! Except you promise me to play the Page: Do that, try one night, and you'll laugh for ever, To hear the Orisons that lovers use; Their ceremonious sighs, their idle oaths, To hear how you are praised and prayed unto. For you are Richard's Saint, they talk of Mary The blessed Virgin, but upon his beads He only prays to Marian Faukenbridge. The more his error, but will you agree To be the Lady Faukenbridge one day? When is't? On Monday. Wherefore is't? Nay then you do me wrong with inquisition. And yet I care not greatly if I tell thee. Thou seest my husband full of jealousy; Prince Richard in his suit importunate, My brother Gloster threatened by young Henry; To clear these doubts, I will in some disguise, Go to black Heath unto the holy Hermit, Whose wisdom in foretelling things to come, Will let me see the issue of my cares. If destinies ordain me happiness, I'll chase these mists of sorrow from my heart, With the bright Sun of mirth: if fate agree, It, and my friends, must suffer misery, Yet I'll be merry too, till mischief come. only I long to know the worst of ill. I'll once put on a scarlet countenance. Be wary lest ye be discovered Robin. Best paint me then, be sure I shall not blush. Beat an Officer, Red cap I'll have ye talked withal, Beat Sir Richard's Porter? help Madam, help, Peace you damned rogue. Brother I pray you forbear. 'Zounds a hundredth at my heals almost, And yet the villain stands on complaiment. Abots one you, is't you?
