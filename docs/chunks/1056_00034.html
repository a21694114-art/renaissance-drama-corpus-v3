And I shall take it as a favour too, If for the same price you made him valiant, You will unblade him: here's the money sir, As weighty gold as tother; cause you should not Lay it on lightly; break no limb, and bruise him Three-quarters dead, I care not; he may live Many a fair day after it. You show An uncle's love in this, trust me to cure His valour. He is here; do but observe. And beat him, sir, accordingly. How now Uncle? Down presently, and before these gentlemen Desire his pardon. How, desire his pardon? Then let this go round. I will ask his pardon, I beseech you Uncle. And swear. And do swear. For more assurance do you kick me too. Am not I patient and obedient now? Will you have any more gentlemen, before I rise? If ever he prove rebellious in act, Or language, let me know it.
