Let me alone by god's bread This same sword shall strike of his head. I pray you hence that ye were rid Shortly have him out of my sight A little while give me respite And take heed what I do say Remember in what condition thou was when I first met the in this place Full simple in poor array Now by the grace of god and counsel of me Thou art come to great prosperity And so mayst continue until thou die if thou wisely take heed Let not sensuality lead the bridle Be occupied in virtue, and be not idle The better shalt thou proceed These wretches will thy goods spend and waste then shalt thou be taken for an out cast And mocked and scorned with most and leest Then will no man the help at need A sir evil might thou speed That so can read his destanye. Will ye suffer this knave in your company Then god be with you I will forsake you Go hence or in faith I shall make you. Then to almighty god I betake you Let me come to that bragger I shall thrust him thorough the arse with my dagger (And here the face Peace out of the place) How say ye, was not this a good face To drive a knave out of the place In faith thou made him run a pace Thou looked as thou had been mad Now by my troth my heart is glad Some minstrel now I would we had To revel and dance, for by saint ● had I am so light me think I flee. ye marry so should it be For now I hold you wise. Sir and ye will do by mine advise Let us go straight to the fleur-de-lys There shall ye find a man will play at dice with you for an hundredth pound. What man is he Colehassarde came late from beyond the see Ragged and torn in a guarded cote And in his purse never a groat And now he goeth like a lord I pray thee tell at one word Is he a gentleman bore. Tusshe take no thought therefore For be he gentleman knave or boy if he come here with a trifle, or a toy He can no money lack Now by the bread that god broke I think long till I him see Mirth go before and ordain a good dish One of flesh another of fish Nay let all be flesh A young pullet tender and nesshe That never came on brooch, have with the or thou go What shall I have? Four quarters of a knave Roasted upon a spit Now by my troth and Colhassard will sit I will play as longe as an hundredth pound will last And ye will play an hundredth pound at a cast He will keep you play. Then let us go our way I sit on thorns till I come there
