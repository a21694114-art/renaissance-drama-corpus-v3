For it doth me as much good when I sell it As all the buyers that taste it or smell it Now sins my medicines be so special And in operation so general And ready to work when so ever they shall So that in riches I am principal If any reward may entreat ye I beseech your mastership be good to me And ye shall have a box of marmelade So fine that ye may dig it with a spade. Sir I thank you but your reward Is not the thing that I regard I must and will be indifferent Wherefore proceed in your intent. Now if I wist this wish no sin I would to god I might begin. I am content that thou lie first Even so am I and say thy worst Now let us here of all thy lies The greatest lie thou mayst devise And in the fewyst words thou can Forsooth ye be an honest man. There said ye much but yet no lie.
