At the very push of invention, and went off clear untouched. And could you draw no blood of their wits? Not a drop. Nor Dametas neither, nor Manasses? Neither, to our disgrace be it spoken, the carriage of this stratagem deserves applause, and I hold it a credit to rest captive to such valiant conquerors. Why so be, I like a man that will confess his error. It merits commiseration Madam and my Liege, not to detract from our worth: your ear, we two are the parties you wot on. Were you the man? No he was the man, marry I was the woman in the Moon, that made you walk all this last night like the man in the mist, I could say somewhat to you Madam, as for Dametas and his man, let them stand like fools as they are. Can it be possible? No, no, we are Gulls, innocent sots, but lante tanta, the girls are ours we have won them away to Dargison. Come we have won the conquest, and that's sufficient. Your desert save me free, you have done a most (to use your own phrase) Metaphysical piece of service, but you had some help in't questionless. I do not think but the Ladies had some handined. A finger, I confess a finger by the hope of perseverance, a very little finger. I thought as much by making of the jest. I cannot detract from the Lady's worth, for I know them for excellent workwomen. Workwomen fit to make Tailors men. I by my faith do I, nay your best tailors are arrant butchers to them, you shall have a Lady make an end of a suit, a Court suit, especially when all the Tailors in a country know not how to set a stitch in't. Some ordinary suit perhaps. Your best Court suits that are, are finished by Ladies, I have known a suit myself lain a making and moring ●, ●, and five year together, and then a Lady hath dispatched it in a month with a wet finger, such a finger might the Ladies have in your plot.
