I'll tell you, gentlemen, it was the first, but the best leaguer, that ever I beheld, with these eyes, except the taking in of— what do you call it, last year, by the Genowayes, but that (of all other) was the most fatal, and dangerous exploit, that ever I was ranged in, since I first bore arms before the face of the enemy, as I am a gentleman, & soldier. so, I had as lief, as an angel, I could swear as well as that gentleman!
