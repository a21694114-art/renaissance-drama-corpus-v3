No, no; there is no hope, 'tis but an unmannerly trick of any Master, to leave his man of'is fashion; 'tis well there is no press abroad, no disguised Constables twelve pence, and the king's name would put me into a pitiful Fever, and I should curse Sir Gervace in another Country, as often as I heard the report of a Musket, for bringing me up no better, that might ha' hued quietly at home, and gone a feasting with the trained Band without any danger.
