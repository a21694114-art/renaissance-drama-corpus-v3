Excellent; as I would wish: she's plagued in Art. These presentations are but framed in wax. By the curious Master in that Quality, Vincentio Lauriola, and she takes them For true substantial Bodies. Indeed I thank him: nothing but noise, and folly Can keep me in my right wits, whereas reason And silence, make me stark mad: Sit down, Discourse to me some dismal Tragedy.
