Post officers, and fetch her to our presence. A weak delay to hinder my strong faith. I ever thought that Harlot would be one. See how that bold ●ace ieats it like a bride. Health to the king, good fortune to the peers. A bolder quean there lived not this many years. My piercing judgement, through the brittle glass, Of that frail beauty, doth deserve her looseness, Lady stand forth. I entertain that name most royal king, And boldly come here to clear myself, Of any thing that shall be laid against me. You are here acused as accessary, Unto Sentloes' death, speak are you guilty I or no. Doth this disgraced son of that proud man, Charge me with these surmises before the king. Thou hast not murdered him, but my renown, His death, this hand my death of fame doth wound. Thus lust makes young men's missery her law. Who then dares touch my reputation? Look there on thy accuser, that is he. This corish lump of flesh, this shaghaird slave, Aplie thy speech proud woman to the place, Speak reverently in presence of the King. Lady we stand not upon words, But one the proof, and estimate of right, Sirtha, stand forth: and freely speak the truth. The wretch my Lord that never did offend, Fears not to die, I charge thee Florence Of my masters death: Moreover, I charge thee That with this purse of massy gold Thou didst bribe me, To murder Sentloe, Sentlove that is dead. I bribe thee, with what falsehood dost thou urge me. Let Vallenger be asked what purse this is, Look on it Ladies, mark it, for you know it. This purse was mine. This purse I gave to Florence. Then with this purse, we boldly may conclude that she did bribe Blunt to murder Sentloe. Look on her face my Lord, her colour changeth. Am I descried, shall yonder corish slave, By blabing tongue bring me to infamy, No though I assure my death, i'll further his. Your answer Florence, what debate you there▪ My answer shall be resolute and true, Worthy the valour of a woman's mind, To bad for thee, thou lump of infamy. Nothing but badness is from bad derived. Most mighty king, I freely do confess, That cursed Blunt consented to murder Sentloe, This is the truth of all, live I or die. Lewd is her life my Lord, and lose her tongue. Well take them aside, In Florence I observe her impudence, In Vallenger despair, in Blunt remorse, In these three last front terror, Call them again, attend your sentence all, First Vallenger, thou soughtest to poison Anabell thy wise, Since by apparent proof Sentlove is slain, Our sentence is respective for thy good, A noble death: the loss of thy lewd head, Blunt, and this honest Florence, Both for committing and concealing murder, Must one the gallowes die, this is our sencence. No act is proved in me right royal king. Her words approve thy act, thy act her will.
