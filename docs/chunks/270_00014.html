This is the place now fellows, stand close a while, If any shall attempt to scale these walls, Assault him, and kill him if you can, For death is too good an end for him that favours dishonesty. I hear them coming: brother now stand close. Now sleeps the Sun in Thetis lickored lap: And watery eyes are pleased with pleasant rest: Now plays the silver Moon upon the Sea, And all the train of twinkling stars adorn: The hollow compass of our heavens sphere, This is the place where I must purchase life, or end my days. Marchetto boldly knock, to see if by permission We may enter in, lest rumour will bewray us this darksome night. How now what scabis at the door at this time of the night. Sirrah, tell thy mistress Fortunio is at hand to speak with her. Soft sir, keep out I say, lest I make garters of your guts, foot balls of your faces, ho let forth the dogs there. Sirrah dispatch, and call your Mistress forth, Or with my Sword I'll send thy soul to hell. Way way, you may carry the message thither yourself, for poor men's souls were made for heaven, and the rich for hell. What noise is this, what meaneth you thus to assault a hapless Lady's house? Annetta my fair love, my hearts sole Queen, Avaunt dishonest man, disturber of the poor: I know thy drift, I know Fortunio comes, To heap dishonour on my hapless house: But you may be gone and get you to your rest, For no man entereth these doors this night. Seize I this haggard I'll make her stoop. Heave me the doors from of the hinges strait. Who lifts his hands to force these barred doors. Shall buy his rashness with his dearest blood. What hath she champions to resist us then? I, such as scorn to be disgraced by thee: Down with the slaves, fellows beat them down. Give light. Fortunio is slain Soldiers, go raise the watch. The Prince is hurt, Zepheronus and Orphinio fly a pace. My senses fails, O help me to my bed. Lean on my shoulder and let us go. These are the Traitors Seruio, lay hands on them. Who's this, Orphinio and Zepheronus., The sons of Brishio, performers of this deed. Go Seruio keep them close, tell I inform the Duke. And visit young Fortunio in his bed. What ho Phillyda my girl come forth here. What would my father? Go take these prisoners, & see thou keep them close Lock them in the upper loft till I return. Use us like gentlemen we crave no more. Use you like knaves, for you deserve no less, go get you hence. So now shall I see the end of Brishioes race, Now shall Sempronioes' death be well revenged. First will I go to the Duke, and there procure their death, And hast again to see their execution done.
