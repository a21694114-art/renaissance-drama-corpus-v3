A mischief on that innocent face, away. Malicious furies hence, choke not the seeds Of holy penitence. This must be an Angel, How at his presence the fiends crawl away! Here is some light of mercy. Be thou wise, And entertain it, wretched, wretched man; What poor defence hath all thy wealth been to thee? What says thy conscience now?
