Date et dabitur vobis Good devout people/ this place of scripture Worshipful masters/ ye shall understand Is to you/ that have no literature That pope Leo the ten hath granted with his hand Is to say/ in our english tongue And by his bulls/ confirmed under lead As depart your goods/ the poor folk among To all manner people/ both quick and deed And god shall than/ give unto you again Ten thousand years/ & as many sents of pardon This in the gospel/ so is written plain When they are deed/ their souls for to guardon Therefore give your alms/ in the largest wise That will with their penny/ or alms deed Keep not your goods/ fie fie on covetise Put to their hands/ to the good speed That sin with god is most abominable Of the holy chapel of sweet saint Leonarde And is eke the sin/ that is most damnable Which late by fire/ was destroyed and marred In scripture eke/ but I say sirs how Ay by the mass/ one can not here What a babbling maketh yonder fellow For the babbling/ of yonder foolish frere In scripture eke/ is there many a place And also masters/ as I was about to tell Which showeth that many a man so farforth lacketh grace Pope Iuly the vi hath granted fair & well That when to them/ god hath abundance sent And doth .xii. thousand years of pardon to them send They would distribute none to the indygent That aught/ to this holy chapel lend Whereat god having/ great indignation Pope Bonyface/ the ix also punished these men/ after a divers fashion Pope Iuly/ pope Innocent/ with divers pope's more As the gospel/ full nobly doth declare Hath granted/ to the sustaining of the same How dives Epulus/ reigning in welfare v. thousand years of pardon/ to every of you by name And on his board/ dishes delicate And clean remission/ also of their sin Poor Lazarus/ came begging at his gate As often times/ as you put in desiring some food/ his hunger to relieve Any money/ into the pardoners coffer But the rycheman/ nothing would him give Or any money up unto it offer Not so much as a few crommies of bread Or he that offeryth/ penny or groat Wherefore poor lazarus/ of famine strayth was deed Or he that giveth/ the pardoner a new cote And angels his soul/ to heaven did carry Or take of me/ either image or letter But now the rich man/ of the contrary Whereby this poor chapel may/ fair the better When he was deed/ went to misery and pain And god wot/ it is a full gracious deed Where for evermore/ he shall remain For which god/ shall quite you well your meed In brenning fire/ which shall never cease Now help our poor chapel/ if it be your will But I say thou pardoner/ I bid the hold thy peace And I say thou frere/ hold thy tongue still What standest thou there/ all the day smattering Marry what standyst thou there/ all day clatterring
