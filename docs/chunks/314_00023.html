With speedy letters to your father send, Who will revenge you, and defend your right. As if they kill not me, who with him fight? As if his breast be touched, I am not wounded, As if he wailed, my joys were not confounded: We are one heart, though rent by hate in twain: One soul, one essence doth our weal contain: What then can conquer him that kills not me? Grieve not for those that perish if they stay. The time in words misspent, is little worth, Madam walk on, and let them bring us forth.
