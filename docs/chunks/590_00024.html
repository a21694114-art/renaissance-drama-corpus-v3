Could the little horse that ambled on the top of Paulest carry all the people; else how could they ride on the roofs! O sir, 'tis a figure in Poetry, mark how 'tis followed, Rod on their own roofs, Making all Neighbouring houses tilled with men; tiled with men ist not good. By Sesu, and it were tiled all with naked Imen 'twere better. You shall hear no more; pick your ears, they are foul sir, what are you sir pray? A Captain sir, and a follower of god Mars. Mars, Bacchus, and I love Apollo! a Captain! then I pardon your sir, and Captain what would you press me for? For a witty ditty, to a gentlewoman, that I am fallen in with all, over head and ears in affections, and natural desires. An Acrostic were good upon her name methinks. cross sticks: I would not be too cross Master Poet; yet if it be best to bring her name in question, her name is mistress Dorothy Hornet. The very consumption that wastes my Son, and the Aim that hung lately upon me: do you love this Mistress Dorothy? Love her! there is no captains wife in England, can have more love put upon her, and yet I'm sure Captains wives, have their pellies full of good men's loves. And does she love you? has there passed any great matter between you? As great a matter, as a whole coach, and a horse and his wife are gone to and fro between us. Is she? i'faith Captain, be valiant and tell truth, is she honest? Honest? god udge me, she's as honest, as a Punk, that cannot abide fornication, and lechery. Look you Captain, I'll show you why I ask, I hope you think my wenching days are past, yet Sir, here's a letter that her father, brought me from her and enforced me to take this very day. 'tis for some love— song to send to me, I hold my life. This falls out pat, my man tells me, the party is at my door, shall she come in Captain? O ay, ay, put her in, put her in I pray now. The letter says here, that she's exceeding sick, and entreats me to visit her: Captain, lie you in ambush behind the hangings, and perhaps you shall hear the piece of a Comedy: she comes, she comes, make yourself away. Does the Poet play Torkin and cast my Lucretia's water too in huggermuggers? if he do, Styanax Tragedy was never so horrible bloody-minded, as his Comedy shallbe,— Tawson's Captain Ienkins. Now Master Poet, I sent for you. And I came once at your ladyships call. My Ladyship and your Lordship lie both in one manner; you have conjured up a sweet spirit in me have you not rhymer? Why Medea! what spirit! would I were a young man for thy sake. So would I, for then thou couldst do me no hurt; now thou dost.
