Are these the shepherds that made love to me? Yea and the shepherds that yet love thee still. O that Pandora would regard my suit. They look like water Nymphs, but speak like men, Thou should be Nature in a man's attire, And thou young Ganymede Minion to Ioue. Then would I make a world and give it thee. Then would I leave great Ioue, to follow thee. Melos is loveliest, Melos is my love, Come hither Melos I must tell thee news, News tragical to thee and to thy flock. Melos I love thee, meet me in the vale, I saw him in the wolves mouth, Melos fly. O that so fair a Lamb should be devoured: I'll go and rescue him. Could Iphicles go from thee for a Lamb, The wolf take all my flock, so I have thee, Will me to dive for pearl into the sea, To fetch the feathers of the Arabian bird, The Golden Apples from the Hesperian wood, mermaid's glass, Flora's habiliment, So I may have Pandora for my love. He that would do all this, must love me well, And why should he love me and I not him? Wilt thou for my sake go into yond grove? And we will sing unto the wild birds notes. And be as pleasant as the Western wind, That kisses flowers and wantons with their leaves. Will I? O that Pandora would. I will and therefore follow Iphicles. Did base Learchus court my heavenly love? Pardon me Pan if to revenge this deed, I shed the blood of that dissembling swain, With jealous fire my heart begins to burn. Ah bring me where he is Gunophilus, lest he entice Pandora from my bower. I know not where he is, but here he'll be: I must provide the banquet, and be gone. What will the shepherd's banquet with my wife. O light Pandora canst thou be thus false? Tell me where is this wanton banquet kept? That I may hurl the dishes at their heads, Mingle the wine with blood and end the feast, With Tragic outcries like the Theban Lord, Where fair Hippodamia was espoused. Here in this place, for so she pointed me. Where might I hide me to behold the same. O in this cave for over this they'll sit. But then I shall not see them when they kiss, Yet you may here what they say, if they kiss i'll hollow, But do so then my sweet Gunophilus And as a strong wind bruising from the earth, So will I rise out of this hollow vault: making the woods shake with my furious words. But if they come not at all, or when they come do use themselves honestly, then come not out, lest you seeming jealous make her over hate you. Not for the world unless I hear thee call, Or else their wanton speech provoke me forth.
