I found them winding of Marcello's corpse; And there is such a solemn melody 'tween doleful songs, tears, and sad elegies: Such, as old grandames, watching by the dead, Were wont t'outwear the nights with; that believe me I had no eyes to guide me forth the room, They were so o'ercharged with water.
