Then there's Intemperance the bawd. A Tenant too. With the young Lady, Madam Incontinence. Search o'er my Doomsday book; is not she●… Plus One of my last compounders? I remember it. Then there is jumping Iude, Heroic Doll, With bouncing Nan, and Cis, your worship's sinner. All Subsidy women, go free'em all. Sir, here's a known offender: one that has Been stocked, and whipped innumerable times, Has suffered Bridewell often, not a jail But he's familiar with, burnt in the hand, Forehead, and shoulder, both his ears cut off, With his nose slit, what shall I do with him? So often punished? nay, if no correction Will serve his turn; even let him run his course. Here's Mistress Frailty too, the waiting woman. For what offence? A sin of weakness too. Let her be strongly whipped, An't please your worship She has a noble man's letter. Tell her, Plus, she must Have the King's Picture too. Sir here's Snip the Tailor Charged with a riot. Parum, let him go, He is our Neighbour. Then there is a stranger for quarrelling.
