Ye marry now I can ye thank In presents of this the rest be blank Would god this relic had come rather Kiss that relic well good father Such is the pain that ye palmers take To kiss the pardon bowl for the drink sake O holy yeste that looketh full sour and stale For god's body help me to a cup of ale The more I be hold thee, the more I thirst The oftener I kiss thee, more like to burst But sins I kiss the so devoutly Hire me and help me with drink till I die What so much praying and so little speed Ye for god knoweth when it is need To send folks drink but by saint Antony I ween he hath sent you to much all ready. If I have never the more for the Then be the relykes no riches to me Nor to thyself except they be More beneficial than I can see richer is one box of his treacle Then all thy relics that do no myrakell It thou hadst prayed but half so much to me As I have prayed to thy relics and the Nothing concerning mine occupation But straight should have wrought in operation And as in value I pas you an a Here lieth much richesse in little space I have a box of rhubarb here Which is as dainty as it is dear So help me god and hollydam Of this I would not give a deam To the best friend I have in England's ground Though he would give me twenty pound For though the stomach do it abhor It pourget you 〈◊〉 from the colour And maketh your stomach sore to walter That ye shall never come to the halter Then is that ●●●ycyn a sovereign thing To preserve a man from hanging.
