Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Pretty i'faith, have ye any more of these tricks? I may be outfaced of myself with a Card often, but i'faith Uncle, the best knave i'th' bunch, nor all the law in your Budget cannot do't, & as for you Sisley bumtrinkets, i'll have about with you at the single Stackado are you a woman? No. What then? A Maid. If his tongue falter, all our plots bewrayed. If a true maid, lend a true maid your hand. Both hand and heart The like of me command, Now I conjure thee by the love You bear Diane's silent Grove, By Cupid's bow, and go den arrow, Venus' Dove, and chirping Sparrow, By all those vows, the listening winds, Hear when Maids unclasp their minds. By those sweet oaths men use in bed, Besieging of a Maiden head. I do conjure you fore these Lords, To answer truly to my words. By these and more, by all the scapes Of amorous Ioue and Phoebus rapes, By lovers sweet, and secret meetings, Hand embracings and lip-greetings, I promise you before these Lords, To answer truly to your words. Where were you borne? In Genoa. Your name? Emilia. Lie not. 'twere open shame to lie before so many. In what Art were you instructed? That's not in my part. Say music. Quickly. I was taught to sing, Unto the Lute, and Court each amorous string With a soft finger. Good, how many springs Lived you in Monta Santa? Zounds this stings. How many? Nine. Zounds ten. Ten fops. Nay quick Twixt nine and ten. Right, answer. I'the nick. Your age? What? Any thing. Come, fie, you linger. I am just as old. As what? my little finger. You dally sixteen. eighteen. eighteen fools, Nay and you prompt we'll hiss you forth the schools. Come your reply, nay quick your certain age, I am just as old as— true Emila's page. A page? Disguised? Wrapped in a woman's smock? I'm in a wood. And I can spring a cock, Woodcock of our side: And you bill so fair, Make a clear glade, I'll single forth a pair. Fie her wit scalds. We shall have change of weather. Cocks of one hen, come prince, let's hold together. I thought we should find a Counterfeit of you Then nail me to your lips with a kiss, and make me currant payment. What, young Orlando? how does Charingcross? Is Westminster yet friends with Winchester? Zounds Iulio now. Alas my Lord, it was a mere device to— Make your Highness merry, when I heard You were returned without your wished Adventure. Oh Master Merchant, you adventured well, To cheat yourself thus of a thousand marks, How now my Lord? Nay, i'll but see your breast, mine eyes desire, No lower object. Go to, read your errors. I cannot now return, non est inventa, Brother embrace your child, your second heir, I found firm witness in her bosom bare. Art thou Emilia?
