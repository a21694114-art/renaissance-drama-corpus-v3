I thank ye good my Lord, And your Grace would but a little ply your learning, I warrant ye I'll keep my Knighthood from breeching. Faith Ned I will: how now what letter's that? From your grace's sister the Lady Mary. Come give it me, we guess at the contents. Cranmer, my sister oft hath writ to me, That you and Bishop Bonner might confer, About these points of new Religion, Tell me Tutor will ye dispute with him. With all my heart my Lord, and wish the king, Would deign to hear our disputation. What hast thou there? A Letter from your royal sister, young Elizabeth. Another Letter ere we open this, Well we will view them both immediately, I pray ye attend us in the next Chamber, And Tutors if I call ye not before, Give me some notice, if the king my Father Be walked abroad, I must go visit him. We will fair Prince. What writes Elizabeth, sweet sister thou hast my heart. And of Prince Edward's love hast greatest part. salute thee with a sister's love, Be in thy such, and let thy prayers Be dedicate to God only for 'tis he alone Can strengthen thee, and confound thine enemies, a settled assurance of thy hopes in heaven, God strengthen thee in all temptations, give thee grace to shun Idolatry, send thee life to inherit thy election, To God I commend thee, who still I pray preserve thee. Loving thou art, and of me best beloved.
