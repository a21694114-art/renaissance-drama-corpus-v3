Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

And thou call'st me knave thou art an other: My name is Job and Hob my next Neighbour. Hob and Job, a ye country Patches: A ye fools ye have made wrong matches. You have spoken treason against the king's grace: For it I will accuse ye before his face. Then for the same ye shallbe martyred: At the lest ye shall be hanged, drawn and quartered: O gentleman ye shall have two Pear pies and tell not of me. By God a vat Goose I'll give thee. I think no hurt by my Vathers soul I zweare: Chave lived well all my life time my naybors among: And now chould be loath to come to zuch wrong. To be hanged and quartered the grief would be great: A foul evil on thee Hob, who bid thee on it treat? Vor it was thou that first did him name. Thou liest like a varlet, and thou zaist the same, It was zuch a voolish Lob. thou: Speak many words and by cod's nails I vow: Upon thy pate my staff I will lay. By the Mass I will 'cause them to make a fray. Yea Job thou sayest true, all came through him. Bum vay thou Hob. a little would make me ye trim. Give thee a zawp on thy nose till thy heart ache: If thou darest do it, else man cry creak. I trust before thou hurt me: With my staff I'll make a Lob. thee. O the body of me husband Hob, what mean you to fight: For the passion of God, no more blows smite. Neighbours and friends so long, and now to fall out: What? in your age to seem so stout? If I had not parted ye, one had killed another: I had not cared I swear by God's mother. Shake hands again at the request of me: As ye have been friends, so friends still be. Bum troth cham content, and zaist word neighbour Job: I am content agreed neighbour Hob. So, get you to market, no longer stay. And with yonder knave let me make a fray. Content wife Marian, I'll do as thou dost say: But buss me ich pray thee at going away. Thou whoreson knave & prick-eared boy, why didst thou let them fight? If one had killed another here, couldst thou their deaths requited? It bears a sign by this thy deed, a cowardly knave thou art: Else wouldst thou draw the weapon thine, like a man them to part What Marian may be good, are you come prattling? You may hap get a box on the ear, with your talking. If they had killed one another, I had not cared a pease: A villain, myself on thee I must ease. Give me a box on the ear? that will I try: Who shallbe Master thou shalt see by and by. O not more, no more I beseech you heartily: Even now I yield, and give you the mastery.
