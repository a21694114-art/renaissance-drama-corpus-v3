Sir here is M. Page, and M. Shallow, Coming hither as fast as they can. Then it is very necessary I put up my sword, Pray give me my cowne too, mark you. God save you Sir Hugh. God save you M. parson. God plesse you all from his mercy's sake now. What the word and the sword, doth that agree well? There is reasons and causes in all things, I warrant you now. Well Sir Hugh, we are come to crave Your help and furtherance in a matter. What is I pray you? I faith 'tis this sir Hugh. There is an ancient friend of ours, a man of very good sort, so at odds with one patience, that I am sure you would heartily grieve to see him. Now Sir Hugh, you are a scholar well red, and very persuasive, we would entreat you to see if you could entreat him to patience. I pray you who is it? Let us know that. I am sure you know him, 'tis Doctor Cayus. I had as leeve you should tell me of a mess of porridge, He is an arant lousy beggarly knave: And he is a coward beside. Why I'll lay my life 'tis the man That he should fight withal. Keep them asunder, take away their weapons. Disarm, let them question. Let them keep their limbs hole, and hack our English. Hark van urd in your ear. You be un daga And de Iack, coward priest. Hark you, let us not be laughing stocks to other men's humours. By Ieshu I will knock your urinals about your knaves cockcomes, for missing your meetings and appointments. O Ieshu mine host of de garter, Iohn Rogoby, Have I not met him at de place he make appoint, Have I not? So kad judge me, this is the appointment place, Witness by my Host of the garter. Peace I say gall and gawlia, French and Wealch, Soul curer, and body curer. This is very brave, excellent. Peace I say, hear mine host of the garter, Am I wise? am I politic? am I Matchavil? Shall I lose my doctor? No, he gives me the motions And the potions. Shall I lose my parson, my sir Hu? No, he gives me the proverbs, and the noverbes: Give me thy hand terrestial, So give me thy hand celestial: So boys of art I have deceived you both, I have directed you to wrong places, Your hearts are mighty, you skins are whole, Bardolfe lay their swords to pawn. Fellow me lads Of peace, follow me. Ha, ra, la. Follow. Afore God a mad host, come let us go. I begar have you mocka may thus? I will be even met you my Iack Host. Give me your hand Doctor Cayus, We be all friends: But for mine hosts foolish knavery, let me alone. I dat be well begar I be friends.
