About you forsooth: I me drawing up a hole in your white silk stocking. Is my glass there? and my boxes of complexion? Yes forsooth: your boxes of complexion are here I think: yes 'tis here: her's your two complexions, and if I had all the four complexions. I should near set a good face upon't, some men I see are borne under hard-favoured planets as well as women: zounds I look worse now than I did before, & it makes her face glister most damnably, there's knavery in daubing I hold my life, or else this is only female Pomatum.
