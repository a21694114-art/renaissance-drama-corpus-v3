My husband's absence makes my heart to throb, Hieronimo. Here Isabella, help me to lament, For sighs are stopped, and all my tears are spent. What world of grief, my son Horatio? O where's the author of this endless woe. To know the author were some ease of grief, For in revenge my heart would find relief. Then is he gone? and is my son gone too? O gush out tears, fountains and floods of tears, Blow sighs and raise an everlasting storm. For outrage fits our cursed wretchedness.
