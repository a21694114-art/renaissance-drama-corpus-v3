Can this be real By my hopes of peace I'th' tother World you have no injury: My plot was only to betray you to Love and repentance. Be not troubled, sir, I am a witness of my cousin's truth, And hope you'll make all prosper, in renewing Your faith to her. Be wise and no more words Thou hast a treasure in thy wife, make much on her: For any act of mine, she is as chaste As when she was new borne. Love love her Jack. As when she was new borne. Love; love her Jack. I am ashamed, pray give me all forgiveness, I see my follies, heaven invites me gently To thy chaste bed, be thou again my dearest, Thy virtue shall instruct me: joy to all: Here be more.— Delamore is living, And Leonora marked to enjoy him, Violante is possessed of Beaumont too. These be love's miracles: a spring tide flow In every bosom. This day let me feast you Anon we'll visit Delamore. My soul Longs to salute him. Here all folly's die. May never Gamester have worse fate than I.
