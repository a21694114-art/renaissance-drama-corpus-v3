Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Tut, lovers oaths, like toys writ down in sands Are soon blown over, contracts are common wiles, Tintangle fools, Ioue himself sits and smiles At lovers perjuries, bawd, strumpets hence, My bosom's charged, give way to violence: Come do not mind them. How? not mind them father? When in your Court you courted us to love, You red another lecture, women then Were angels. True, but that was before Angels Had power to make them devils; they were then Fiends to themselves, and Angels unto men. When upon Po thou find'st a coal-black swan, thoust found a woman constant to a man. And not afore? Never afore. Your tongue unspeaks your former speech. It doth; new themes Must have new change of Rhetoric; all streams Flow not alike one way; when I spoke like a lover, it was to break you from your soldiers humour; Having made you lovers, ay, like Envy, speak To make you hate love; Art still strives to break Bad to make better: You have your wish. Then onward to the Court, Make us of love, as Schoolboys do of sport. Call out the jailor boy, yet do not; haste got a beard like Hortensio? Yes, madam, I have got his hair, if I could come as easily by his wit. Wouldst rob him of his wit? If I should, he could not hang me for't: 'tis not worth thirteen pence half penny, but what shall I do with it? Put it on boy. That shall I madam, O forward age, I am a man already: how do you like me Lady? Very ill, and my plot worse. Then leave't of, if you be grounded in the plot, You will but mar the Comedy. I purposed, thou in the habit of Hortensio, shouldst under pretence of removing Aspero to a new prison, have freed him out of the old one. Tut, I can tell you a trick, worth two of that: madam, your ear, take some care in the managing, and let me alone to prepare it. udsfoot break prison my Lord, 'tis but swimming the River. Break prison? 'twere both dishonour to my name, and treason to my love, what benefit were't for me to free my body, & leave my heart in bondage? i'll die, ere i'll harbour a disloyal thought. it bears no relish of disloyalty: being in prison you live as far from love as liberty: being abroad, you may by letters, or a thousand means purchase her company, and compass your content. Shalt be my Lawyer boy and counsel me. I'll look for my Fee then. If thy Counsel prospers. That's an exception Lawyers never respect, but come my lord, leap, as we have risen together, we'll fall together, Blame me not love, udsfoot your jailor my lord. Am I prevented? Yes faith, there had been a Counsellors fee cast away now.
