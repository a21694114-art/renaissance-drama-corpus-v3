Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Go too, put up vallenger, or i'll make you. Well sentlo another time shall serve for us. I prithee gentle love be patiented. Out ye Whore come not in my sight, For if thou dost by heaven i'll martyr thee. Caulest thou me whore, now by this light I'll have thee murdered, and if gold can do it. Gold can do much, but devil can do more, Here is a true pattern, of a common whore. Mistress what means my master to part in such a rage. Forsooth the Gentleman is jellious, But I would quickly rid him of that Fever, And if thou wouldst Blunt but content with me. What is if Mistress, it shall go hard Shall make me stack in what may profit you, Although you still thought that I loved you not. Now Blunt I see it, and will report thy love, And for asigne here take this purse of gold, And now but mark the issue of my purpose. Thou Seest that Sentloes' coin: begins to wear, And Vallenger is even now on the spur, And for my sake will empty all his treasure, And what I have I will impart to thee, But murder Sentlove, then is Florence free. Mistress if this should be done, a crash of your office were not cast away. Fear not Blunt we will not stick for that. Then here's my hand, before the sun go down, I'll do the deed Sentlove shall shortly die. The deed being done come presently to me, And we will frolic in his tragedy. O Sentlove, wert not for thy friend, How many dangers haddest thou fallen into, The mischief now abroach I did foretell, For by my means thy life in safety dwells. O my dear daughter how could he use thee thus? My son rob thee of thy fair Ornaments. And for a strumpet's love, O God, O God. Split soul a sunder, that thy sons so vild. Give me my child (Sir Eustice) as she is, A virtuous maid dishonoured by thy son. Give me my son, that I may punish him, For wronging this fair slower thy worthy child. Alack good knight, I make my moan to thee, And thou in true love canst but pity me. Alas good knight, my griefs so jumps with thine, That as I weep for thee, so pity mine. Where is my child, where is my Anabell? Here wife, let us hold hands, and in three parts, Let's sing around, and so weep out our hearts. How could the wretch (dear soul) abuse thee so. Call him not wretch, he is wretched but by me. In me consists the cause of all this woe, Fair Florence is the mistress of his heart, To her I am but as a Counterfeit, Rather I am an ethyop, foul, deformed And therefore hated of my Vallenger. O Beauteous maid, blemish not thy name, Thou art Heavenly bright, and she as black as hell.
