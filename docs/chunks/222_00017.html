Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Ay me, the King is dead: help, help, my lords Comfort my Lord, gracious Henry, comfort. What doth my Lord of Suffolk bid me comfort? Came he even now to sing a ravens note, And thinks he that the cherping of a Wren, By crying comfort through a hollow voice, Can satisfy my griefs, or ease my heart? Thou baleful messenger, out of my sight, For even in thy eie-balls murder sits, Yet do not go: come Basaliske And kill the silly gazer with thy looks. Why do you rate my lord of Suffolk thus, As if that he had caused Duke Humphreys death? The Duke and I too, you know were enemies, And you had best say that I did murder him. Ah woe is me, for wretched Gloucester's death. Be we for me, more wretched than he was, What dost thou turn away and hide thy face? I am no loathsome leper, look on me, Was I for this nigh wracked upon the sea? And thrice by awkward winds driven back from England's bounds, What might it bode but that well foretelling Winds said, seek not a scorpions nest. My lord, the Commons like an angry hive ofbees, Run up and down, caring not whom they sting, For good Humphreys death, whom they report To be murdered by Suffolk and the Cardinal here. That he is dead (good Warwick) is too true, But how he died, God knows, not Henry. Enter his privy chamber my lord, and view the body. Good father stay you with the rude multitude till I return. I will son. Ah uncle Gloster, heaven receive thy soul, Farewell poor Henry's joy, now thou art gone. Now by his soul, that took our shape upon him, To free us from his father's dreadful curse, I am resolved that violent hands were laid, Upon the life of this famous Duke. A dreadful oath sworn with a solemn tongue, What instance gives Lord Warwick for these words? Oft have I seen a timely parted ghost, Of ashy semblance, pale and bloodless: But lo, the blood is settled in the face, More better coloured, then when he lived, His well proportioned beard made rough and stern, His fingers spread abroad as one that grasped for life, Yet was by strength surprised, the least of these are probable, It cannot choose but he was murdered. Suffolk and the Cardinal had him in charge. And they I trust sir, are no murderers. Yea, but 'twas well known they were not his friends, And 'tis well seen he found some enemies. But have you no greater proofs than these? Who sees a heifer dead and bleeding fresh, And sees hard by a butcher with an axe, But will suspect 'twas he that made the slaughter? Who finds the partridge in the puttocks nest, But will imagine how the bird came there, Although the Kite soar with unbloody beak? Even so suspicious is this Tragedy.
