Camillo, Not for Bohemia, nor the pomp that may Be there at gleaned: for all the Sun sees, or The close earth wombs, or the profound seas, hides In unknown fathoms, will I break my oath To this my fair beloved: Therefore, I pray you, As you have ever been my Father's honoured friend, When he shall miss me, as (in faith I mean not To see him any more) cast your good counsels Upon his passion: Let myself, and Fortune Tug for the time to come. What course I mean to hold Shall nothing benefit your knowledge, nor Concern me the reporting. O my Lord, I would your spirit were easier for advice, Or stronger for your need. Now good Camillo, I am so fraught with curious business, that I leave out ceremony. Sir, I think You have heard of my poor services, i' th' love That I have borne your Father?
