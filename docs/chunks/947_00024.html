We have meats of all sorts of dressing; we have stewed meat for your Frenchmen, pretty light picking meat for your Italian, and that which is rotten roasted, for Don Spaniardo. A pox on't. We have Poulterers ware for your sweet bloods, as Dove, chicken, Duck, teal, Woodcock, and so forth: and butcher's meat for the Citizen: yet Muttons fall very bade this year. Stay, is not that my patient Linen Draper yonder, and my fine young smug Mistress, his wife? Sirrah Grannam, I'll give thee for thy fee twenty crowns, if thou canst but procure me the wearing of yond velvet cap. You'd wear another thing besides the cap. Y'are a Wag. Twenty crowns? we'll share, and I'll be your pulley to draw her on. Do't presently; we'll ha' some sport. Wheel you about, sweet men: do you see, I'll cheapen wares of the man, whilst Bots is doing with his wife. Too't: if we come into the shop to do you grace, we'll call you Madam. Pox a your old face, give it the badge of all scurvy faces, a Masque. What is't you lack, Gentlewoman? cambric or Lawns, or fine Hollands? Pray draw near, I can sell you a pennyworth. Some cambric for my old Lady. cambric? you shall, the purest thread in Milan. Save you, Signior Candido. How does my Noble Master? how my fair Mistress? My Worshipful good Servant, view it well, for 'tis both fine and even. Cry you mercy, Madam, though masked, I thought it should be you by your man. Pray Signior, show her the best, for she commonly deals for good ware. Then this shall fit her, this is for your Ladyship. A word, I pray, there is a waiting gentlewoman of my Ladies: her name is Ruyna, says she's your Kinswoman, and that you should be one of her Ants. One of her Ants? troth sir, I know her not. If it please you to bestow the poor labour of your legs at any time, I will be your convoy thither? I am a Snail, sir, seldom leave my house, if't please her to visit me, she shall be welcome. Do you hear? the naked troth is: my Lady hath a young Knight, her son, who loves you, y'are made, if you lay hold upon't: this jewel he sends you. Sir, I return his love and jewel with scorn; let go my hand, or I shall call my husband. You are an arrant Knave. What, will she do? Do? they shall all do if Bots sets upon them once, she was as if she had professed the trade, squeamish at first, at last I showed her this jewel, said, a Knight sent it her. Is't gold, and right stones? Copper, Copper, I go a fishing with these baits. She nibbled, but would not swallow the hook, because the Cunger-head her husband was by: but she bids the Gentleman name any afternoon, and she'll meet him at her Garden house, which I know.
