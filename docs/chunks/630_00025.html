Your own, my liege, we like inferior lights Take life from your reflection, for like stars Unto the sun, are Counsellors to Kings: He feeds their orbs with fire, and their shine Contend to make his glory more divine. And such are we to great Anthonio. The veins and arteries of Anthonio Through which the blood of greatness flows in us. Our life, and cause efficient of our state, And these our pretty partners in exile. We ha' yet performed but the least part of duty, Your reinstallment: it rests, that with our blood, We keep out innovative violence.
