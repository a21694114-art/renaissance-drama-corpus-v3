Unmannerly, untaught, unnurtured Girls, Do I bring Gentlemen, my very friends To feast with me, to revel at my House, That their good likings, may be set on you, And you like misbehaved and sullen Girls, Turn tail to such, as may advance your states: I shall remember't, when you think I do not. I am sorry Gentlemen, your cheer's no better; But what did want at Board, excuse me for, And you shall have amends be made in Bed. To them friends, to them; they are none but yours: For you I bred them, for you brought them up: For you I kept them, and you shall have them: I hate all others that resort to them: Then rouse your bloods, be bold with what's your own: For I and mine (my friends) be yours, or none. God-gee Good-morrow sir, I have brought you M. Mouse here to teach my young Mistresses: I assure you (forsooth) he is a brave Frenchman. Welcome friend, welcome: my man (I think) Hath at the full, resolved thee of my will. Monsieur Delion, I pray question him: I tell you sir, 'tis only for your sake, That I do mean to entertain this fellow, A bots of all ill luck, how came these here? Now am I posed except the Wenches help me: I have no French to flap them in the mouth, To see the luck of a good fellow, poor Anthony Could near have sorted out a worser time: Now will the pack of all our sly devices Be quite laid ope, as one undoes an Oyster: Francke, Heigham, and mad Ned, fall to your muses, To help poor Anthony now at a pinch, Or all our market will be spoiled and marred. Tut man, let us alone, I warrant you. Monsieur, Vous estes tresbien venu, de quell pais estes vous. Vous, that's you: sure he says, how do men call you Monsieur le Mouche? Sister, help sister; that's honest Anthonie, And he answers, your wooer cuius contrarium. Monsieur, Vous n'entens pas, Je ne demand puit, vostre nom? Monsieur Delion, he that made your Shoes, made them not in fashion: they should have been cut square at the toe. Madame, my Sho met de square toe, vat be dat? Why saucebox; how now you unreverent minx Why? in whose Stable haste thou been brought up, To interrupt a man in midst of speech? Monsieur Delion, disquiet not yourself, But as you have begun, I pray proceed To question with this Countryman of yours. Dat me sal do tres beien, but de bella Madonna de june Gentlewoman do monster some sing of amour to speak lot me, epurce monsieur, me sal say but two tree four five word to dir francois: or sus Monsieur Le mouche en quelle party de Fraunce esties vous ne? France. Ned. 'sblood, let me come. Master Pisaro, we have occasion of affairs, Which calls us hence with speed; wherefore I pray Defer this business till some fitter time, And to perform what at the Exchange we spoke of. A blessing on that tongue, saith Anthony.
