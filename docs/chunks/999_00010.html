Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

He makes at you forsooth. Let him come, I have ever an English virtue to put by a Welsh. O noble widows, her heart was full of woes. No noble Welshman, her heart was in her hose. ' Sounds, was that her manners to take away Randalls cups? No, it showed scurvy. take't you at worst then. Whelp of the Devil, thou shalt see thy Sire for't. Gentlemen what mean ye? Let her come, let her come, Randalls will redeem reputations her warrant her. Redeem your wit sir, first for you Sir, you are a stranger, and see you,— but you, fie Master Bloodhound. Ha, Bloodhound, good sir let me speak with you. Sounds, what do Randalls amongst bloodhounds, good widows lend her an ear. Ancient Young, how false our memories have played through long discontinuance, but why met here man, is Mars so bad a paymaster, that our Ancients fight under Cupid's Banner? Faith this was but a sudden start begotten from distraction of some fortunes, I pursue this Widow but for want of wiser work. The Welshman labours at it. A pair of a hundred of seepes, thirty brave Cows, and twelve dozen of Runts. Twelve dozen of Goose. Give her but another hark? He has the mortgage still, and I have a handsome sister, do but meet at the Fountain in Fleetstreet after dinner, o I will read thee a History of happiness, and thou shalt thank me. A, reade all's well, or weapons. A word Iarvis? Oh brave widows, her will meet her there, her know her times and her seasons, her warrant her, Randalls will make these brave Gallants hang her selfes in those garters of willow Garlands about her pates, mark her now, and remember. Adieu sweet widow, for my ordinary— 'twas not so much worth Sir. You mean, 'twere worth more then, and that's another handsomely begged. You conclude women cunning beggars then. Yes, and men good benefactors, my best wishes wait on so sweet a Mistress— will ye walk? I'll follow ye, wilt think on't soon at night, or not at all? I wood not have my wishes wronged, if I should bring it about handsomely, you can be honest? Can, dost conclude me a sattin cheat? No, a smooth Gallant sir, do not you fail to be here soon at nine, still provided, you will be honest, if I convey you not under her bed, throw me atop o'th' Tester, and lay me out o'th' way like a rusty bilbo. Enough, drink that, fare well, Widow, Fate, the Destinies, and the three ill favoured Sisters, have concluded the means, and when I am thy Husband? I shallbe your wife. Do but remember these cross Capers, than ye pitter sweet one? Till then adieu ye bitter sweet one. This dinner would have showed better in bed-lane, and she a other side, holdeth her whole nest of suitors play, what Art decks the dark Labyrinth of a woman's heart.
