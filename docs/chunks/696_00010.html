Save you; how does Mistress Tiltyard? Moll. Jack Dappper. How dost Moll. I'll tell thee by and by, I go but toth' next shop. Thou shalt find me here this hour about a feather. Nay and a feather hold you in play a whole hour, a goose will last you all the days of your life. Let me see a good shag ruff. Mistress Marry that shalt thou i'faith, and the best in the shop. How now, greetings, love terms with a pox between you, have I found out one of your haunts, I send you for hollands, and you're i'th' the low countries with a mischief, I'm served with good ware byth' shift, that makes it lie dead so long upon my hands, I were as good shut up shop, for when I open it I take nothing.
