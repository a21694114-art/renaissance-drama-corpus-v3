Our Admiral's father! call him back. I won't stay to hear 'em. Sir, be prudent, And do not for your son fright the King's health. What, ha' they left us? how does my Admiral? I am forbid to tell you sir. By whom. The Queen and my Lord Constable. Are there Remaining seeds of faction? have they souls Not yet convinced i'th' truth of Chabots' honour, Clear as the crystal heaven, and 'bove the reach Of imitation. 'tis their care of you, And no thought prejudicial to my son. Their care of me? How can the knowledge of my Admiral's state Concern their fears of me, I see their envy Of Chabots' happiness, whose joy to be Rendered so pure and genuine to the world Doth grate upon their conscience and affright 'em; But let 'em vex, and bid my Chabot still Exalt his heart, and triumph, he shall hauë The access of ours, the kingdom shall put on Such joys for him as she would boast to celebratë Her own escape from ruin. He is not in state to hear my sad news I perceive. That countenance is not right, it does not answë● What I expect, Say, how is my Admiral? The truth upon thy life. To secure his, I would you had. Ha'? Who durst oppose him? One that hath power enough hath practised on him And made his great heart stoop. I will revenge it With crushing, crushing that rebellious power to nothing. Name him. He was his friend. A friend to malice, his own black imposthume Burn his blood up, what mischief hath engendered New storms? 'tis the old tempest. Did not we Appease all horrors that looked wild upon him? You dressed his wounds I must confess, but made No cure, they bleed a fresh, pardon me sir, Although your conscience have closed too soon, He is in danger, and doth want new surgery Though he be right in fame, and your opinion, He thinks you were unkind. Alas poor Chabot, Doth that afflict him. So much, though he strive With most resolved and Adamantine nerves, As ever humane fire in flesh and blood, Forged for example, to bear all, so killing The arrows that you shot were (still your pardon) No Centaurs blood could rankle so. If this Be all, i'll cure him, Kings retain More Balsam in their soul them hurt in anger. far short sir, with one breath they uncreate, And Kings with only words more wounds can make Then all their kingdom made in balm can heal, 'tis dangerous to play to wild a descant On numerous virtue, though it become Princes To assure their adventures made in every thing, Goodness confined within poor flesh and blood, Hath but a queasy and still sickly state, A musical hand should only play on her Fluent as air, yet every touch command. No more, Commend us to the Admiral, and say, The King will visit him, and bring health.
