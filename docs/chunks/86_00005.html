What Inclination, me thought I did smell thee, give me thy hand or we further go. Now welcome in faith, and Greedy gut also, But sirs, are none of you both acquainted with Lust. Yes that they have been both of them I trust, welcome sirs in faith welcome unto me. By my troth I am glad your mastership to see In health and prosperity, as presently you be. Bom faith so am I would all my heart, This cow bellied knave doth come from the cart, Ise teach you to speak I hold you a pound, Curtsy Job curtsy, down to the ground. Che can make curtsy well enough. Lower old knave or Ile make ye to bow, The great bellied lout me think can not bend, yes so lo, he beginneth now to amend. Well sirs, now I remember Aesop's advise which he gave to the Samies against king Crassus, Therefore it is good to be witty and wise, And being in liberty to keep me still thus: I can not abide a life that is dolorous, And seeing that my name is properly Lust, I hate the conversation also of the just. Well master Lust, first join you to me Inclination Next here with Sturdines you must you acquaint Turn you about and embrace Elation, And that wealth may increase without any restraint join you with Greedy gut here in our presence, That all these in you may have prosperous influence. Out alas, what a sudden passion is this, I am so taken that I can not stand, The cramp, the cramp, hath touched me iwis, I shall die without remedy now out of hand. By my matins cheese, our master is sick. Stand back Nycoll noddy with the pudding prick, More brains in thy skin then wit in thy brain, Such Greedy guts in faith would be slain, This cramp doth signify nothing in effect But how he is bowed by me Inclination, None of all your counsels he will now reject, And therefore fear not to make full declaration. Then fear not the force of these that be just, But labour yourself to advance and augment, Be jocund and lively sith your name is lust, And then you shall easily obtain your intent. Esteem yourself always equal with the best, And seek for promotion, power, and dignity: It is good when men may live as they lust, And unto the just bear hate and malignity. O zur ye must be greedy to catch and to claw. Well said Greedy gut, as wise as a daw, Eat up at a mouthful, houses and lands. There's a vengeable mouth to, Never fear God nor the governors law, But gripe gripe greedily all that cometh in your hands. By the Mass but Hew Owlet is pestilens witty, What guttishe greediness the whoreson can teach That thou art not erected in faith it is pity, As high as three trees, and a halter will reach.
