Goes the case so well signior bottlenose? It may be we shall overreach your drift; This is the time the Wenches sent us word Our bombast Dutchman and his mates will come. Well neat Italian, you must don my shape: Play your part well, or I may haps pay you. What, speechless Ned? faith whereon musest thou? 'tis on your French corrival, for my life: He come eat vostre, and so forth, Till he hath foisted in a Brat or two? How then, how then? 'swounds I'll geld him first, Ere that incestuous lozel revel there. Well Matt. I think thou know'st what Ned can do; Shouldst thou change Ned for Noddy, me for him, Thou didst not know thy loss, i'fath thou didst not. Come leave this idle chat, and let's provide Which of us shall be scarecrow to these Fools, And set them out the way? Why that will I. Then put a Sword into a madman's hand: Thou art so hasty, that but cross thy humour, And thou'lt be ready cross them o'er the pates: Therefore for this time, I'll supply the room. And so we shall be sure of chat enough; You'll hold them with your flouts and gulls so long, That all the night will scarcely be enough To put in practice, what we have devised: Come, come, I'll be the man shall do the deed. Well, I am content to save your longing. But soft, where are we? Ha, here's the house, Come let us take our stands: France stand you there, And Ned and I will cross tother side. Do so: But hush, I hear one passing hither. Oh de favourable aspect of de heaven, 'tis so obscure, so dark, so black dat no mortal creature can know de me: I pray a Dio I sal have de reight Wench: Ah si I be recht, here be de huis of signior Pisaro, I sall have de madonna Marina, and darvor I sall knock to de door. What a pox are you mad or drunk; What, do you mean to break my Glasses? Wat be dat Glass? Wat drunk, what mad? What Glasses sir; why my Glasses: and if you be so crank, I'll call the Constable; you will not enter into a man's house (I hope) in spite of him? Nor durst you be so bold as to stand there, If once the Master of the House did know it. Is dit your House? be you de signior of dis Cassa? signior me no signors, nor cassa me no cassas: but get you hence, or you are like to taste of the Bastinado. Do, do, good Ferdinand, pummel the loggerhead. Is this neit the House of mester Pisaro? Yes marry when? can you tell: how do you? I thank you heartily, my finger in your mouth. Wat be dat? Marry that you are an Ass and a loggerhead, To seek master Pisaro's house here. I pray de gratia, what be dis plash? Wat do ye call dit street?
