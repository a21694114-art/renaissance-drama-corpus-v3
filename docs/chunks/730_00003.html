Garlic stinks to this, prove that you have not more whores to see this, then ere Garlic had. say I am a boaster of mine own works, disgrace me on the open stage, and bob me off with near a penny. O lord sir, far be it from us, to debar any worthy writer of his merit; but I pray you sir, what is the title you bestow upon it? Marry that which is full as forcible as Garlic, the name of it is who buys my four ropes of hard Onions, by which four ropes is meant four several kind of livers, by the onions hangers on, as at some convenient time I will more particularly inform you in so rare a hidden and obscure a mystery. I pray let me see the beginning of it, I hope you have made no dark sentence in't, for I'll assure our audience commonly very simple idle-headed people, and if they should hear what they understand not, they would quite forsake our house. O near fear it, for what I have writ is both witty to the wise, and pleasing to the ignorant; for you shall have these laugh at it far more heartily that understand it not, than those that do. methink the end of this staff is a foot too long. O no, sing it but in tune, and I dare warrant you. Why hear ye, And you that delight in trulls and minions, Come buy my four ropes of hard S. Thomas onions: Look you there S. Thomas might very well have been left out, beside, hard should have come next the onions, Fie no, the dismembering of a rhyme to bring in reason shows the more efficacy in the writer. Well as you please, I pray you sir what will the gratuity be, I would content you as near hand as I could. So I believe, Why M. Change-coat, I do not suppose we shall differ many pounds, pray make your offer, if you give me too much, I will most Doctor of physic like restore. You say well, look you sir, there's a brace of angels, besides much drink of free cost if it be liked. How M. Change-coat a brace of angels besides much drink of free cost if it be liked, I fear you have learned it by heart, if you have powdered up my plot in your sconce, you may home sir and instruct your Poet over a pot of ale, the whole method on't, but if you do so juggle, look to't Shrovetuesday is at hand, and I have some acquaintance with Bricklayers and plasterers. Nay, I pray sir be not angry, for as I am a true stagetrotter, I mean honestly and look ye, more for your love then otherwise, I give you a brace more.
