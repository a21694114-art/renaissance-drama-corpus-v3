Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Hark. O wretched villain. Two or three groans, it is a heavy night, These may be counterfeits, let's think't unsafe To come into the cry without more help. No body come, then shall I bleed to death. Hark. Here's one comes in his shirt, with lights and weapons, Who's there? whose noise is this, that cries on murder. I do not know. Did not you hear a cry? Here, here, for heaven's sake help me. What's the matter. This is Othello's Ancient, as I take it. The same indeed, a very valiant fellow. What are you here, that cry so grievously? Iago, O I am spoiled, undone by villains, Give me some help. O my Lieutenant: what villains have done this? I think the one of them is here about, And cannot make away. O treacherous villains: What are you there? come in and give some help. O help me here. That's one of 'em. O murderous slave, O villain. O damned Iago, O in humane dog,— o, o, Kill him i'the dark? where be those bloody thieves? How silent is this Town? Ho, murder, murder: What may you be, are you of good or evil? As you shall prove us, praise us. Signior Lodovico. He sir. I cry you mercy: here's Cassio hurt by villains. Cassio. How is it brother? My leg is cut in two. Marry heaven forbidden: Light Gentlemen, I'll bind it with my shirt. What is the matter ho, who is't that cried? Who is't that cried. O my dear Cassio, O my sweet Cassio, Cassio, Cassio. O notable strumpet: Cassio may you suspect Who they should be, that thus have mangled you? No. I am sorry to find you thus, I have been to seek you. Alas he faints, O Cassio, Cassio, Cassio. Gentlemen all, I do suspect this trash To bear a part in this: patience a while good Cassio: Lend me a light; know we this face, or no? Alas my friend, and my dear country man: Roderigo? no, yes sure: O heaven Roderigo. What of Venice? Even he sir, did you know him? Know him? I. Signior Gratiano, I cry you gentle pardon: These bloody accidents must excuse my manners, That so neglected you. I am glad to see you. How do you Cassio? O a chair, a chair. Roderigo. He, 'tis he: O that's well said, a chair: Some good man bear him carefully from hence, I'll fetch the General's Surgeon: for you mistress, Save you your labour, he that lies slain here Cassio, Was my dear friend, what malice was betwixt you? None in the world, nor do I know the man. What, look you pale? O bear him out o'th' air. Stay you good Gentlewoman, look you pale mistress? Do you perceive the ieastures of her eye, Nay, an you stir, we shall have more anon: Behold her well I pray you, look upon her, Do you see Gentlemen? Nay guiltiness Will speak, though tongues were out of use.
