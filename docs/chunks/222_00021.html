I am a Gentleman look on my Ring, Ransom me at what thou wilt, it shall be paid. I lost mine eye in boarding of the ship, And therefore ere I marchantlike sell blood for gold, Then cast me headlong down into the sea. But what shall our ransoms be? A hundredth pounds a piece, either pay that, or die. Then save our lives, it shall be paid. Come sirrah, thy life shall be the ransom I will have. Stay villain, thy prisoner is a prince, The Duke of Suffolk, William de la Poole. The Duke of Suffolk folded up in rags? Yea sir, but these rags are no part of the duke, Ioue sometime went disguised, and why not I? Yea but Ioue was never slain as thou shalt be. Base jady groom, King Henry's blood, The honourable blood of Lancaster, Cannot be shed by such a lowly swain, I am sent Ambassador for the Queen to France, I charge thee waff me cross the channel safe. I'll waff thee to thy death, go Walter take him hence And on our long boats side, chop off his head. Thou dar'st not for thine own. Yes Poole. Poole? Yea, Poole, puddle, kennel, sink and dirt, I'll stop that yawning mouth of thine, Those lips of thine that so oft have kissed the Queen, Shall sweep the ground, and thou that Smildst at good duke Humphreys death, Shalt live no longer to infect the earth. This villain being but Captain of a Pinnais, Threatens more plagues then mighty Abradas, The great Macedonian Pirate, Thy words adds fury and not remorse in me. Yea but my deeds shall stay thy fury soon. Hast not thou waited at my trencher, When we have feasted with Queen Margaret? Hast not thou kissed thine hand and held my stirrup? And barehead plodded by my foot-cloth Mule, And thought thee happy when I smiled on thee? This hand hath writ in thy defence, Then shall I charm thee, hold thy lavish tongue. Away with him Walter, I say, and off with his head. Good my lord, entreat him mildly for your life. First let this neck stoop to the axe's edge, Before this knee do bow to any, Save to the God of heaven, and to my King, Suffolk's imperial tongue cannot plead To such aiady groom. Come, come, why do we let him speak? I long to haue his head, for ransome of mine cie. A sworder and band to slave, Murdered sweet Tully: Brutus bastard hand stabbed Iulius Caesar, And Suffolk dies by Pirates on the sees. Off with his head, and send it to the Queen, And ransomless this prisoner shall go free, To see it safe delivered unto her: Come let's go. Come away Nick, and put a long staff in thy pike, and provide thyself, for I can tell thee, they have been up this two days.
