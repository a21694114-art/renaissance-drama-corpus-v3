This Bawd belike has her house pulled down. So: come hither, nearer, where shines this star? I'th' City, brightly, sprightly, bravely, oh 'tis a Creature— Young! Delicate, piercing eye, enchanting voice, lip red and moist, skin soft and white; she's amorous, delicious, inciferous tender, neat. Thou mad'st me, newly married! New married, that's all the hole you can find in her conte, but so newly, the poesy of her wedding Ring is scarce warm with the heat of her finger; therefore my Lord, fasten this wagtail, as soon as you can lime your bush, for women are Venice-glasses, one knock spoils 'em. Cracked things pox on 'em. And then they'll hold no more than a lawyer's Conscience. How shall I get a sight of this rich Diamond. I would have you first disguised go along with me, and buy some toy in her shop, and then if you like Danae fall into her lap like Iove, a net of goldsmith's work will pluck up more women at one draught, than a Fisherman does Salmons at fifteen. What's her Husband? A flatcap, pish; if he storm, give him a court-loaf stop's mouth with a Monopoly. thouhast fired me. You know where to quench you. I'll steal from Court in some disguise presently. Stand on no ground good your Highness. Away, I'll follow thee, speak not of haste, Thou tiest but wings to a swift grey Hound's heel, And add'st to a running Chariot a fift wheel. Thou now dost hinder me, away, away. Lazarillo art bound yet? No, but my Indentures are made. Make as much haste to seal, as younger Brothers do at taking up of Commodities: for Lazarillo, there's not any Deigo that treads upon Spanish leather, goes more upright upon the soles of his Conscience, than our Master does. Troth so I think, now I like my little smirking Mistress as well. Like her, did not I like her simply, to run away from her father (where I had both men Servants and maid Servants under me) to wear a flat cap here and cry what do you lack. What is't you lack Gentlemen, rich garters, spangled roses, silk stockings, embroidered gloves or girdles.
