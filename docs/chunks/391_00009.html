Lord how art thou changed: how dost thou and thy Master agree, I have brought him a present; how 'gree you now? Well, well, but for mine own part, as I have set up my rest to run away, so I will not rest till I have run some ground; my master's a very Iewe, give him a present, give him a halter, I am famished in his service.
