Why that at four women were fools, at fourteen drabs, at forty bawds, at fourscore witches, and a hundred cats. O unlimitable impudency! But as for poor Ferneze's fixed heart, Was never shadeless meadow drier parched, Under the scorching heat of heavens dog, Then is my heart with your enforcing eyes. A hot simile. That I could rail now! A mischief fill thy throat, thou fowl jawed slave: Say thy prayers. I ha' forgot 'em. Thou shalt die. So shalt thou; I am heart mad. I am horn mad. Extreme mad. Monstrously mad. Why? Why? thou, thou hast dishonoured my bed.
