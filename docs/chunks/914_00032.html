O, by your leave, Sir. I must be bold to raise ye, else your Physic Will turn to further sickness. Physic, Bear-leech? Yes physic, you are mad. Trollio, Cleophila. Sir, I am here. I know thee, Rhetias, prithee rid the room Of this tormenting noise. He tells me, sirrah. I have took physic, Rhetias, physic, physic. Sir true, you have; and this most learned Scholar Applied t'ee. O you were in dangerous plight Before he took ye hand. These things are drunk, Directly drunk. Where did you get your liquour? I never saw a body in the wane Of age, so over spread with several sorts Of such diseases as the strength of Youth Would groan under and sink. The more your glory in the miraculous cure. Bring me the Cordial Prepared for him to take after his sleep, 'twill do him good at heart. I hope it will, Sir. What dost think I am, that thou shouldst fiddle So much upon my patience? Fool, the weight Of my disease sits on my heart so heavy, That all the hands of Art cannot remove One grain to ease my grief. If thou couldst poison My memory, or wrap my senses up Into a dulness, hard and cold as Flints? If thou couldst make me walk, speak, eat and laugh Without a sense or knowledge of my faculties, Why then perhaps at Marts thou might'st make benefit Of such an Antic motion, and get credit From credulous gazers, but not profit me. Study to gull the wise; I am too simple To be wrought on. I'll burn my books (old man) But I will do thee good, and quickly too. Most honoured Lord Meleander, our great Master, Prince Palador of Cyprus, hath by me Sent you this Patent, in which is contained Not only confirmation of the Honours You formerly enjoyed, but the addition Of the Marshalship of Cyprus, and ere long He means to visit you. Excuse my haste, I must attend the Prince.— There's one Pill works. Dost know that spirit? 'tis a grave familiar, And talked I know not what. he's like, methinks, the Prince his Tutor, Aretus. Yes, yes; it may be I have seen such a formality; No matter where, or when. The Prince hath sent ye (My Lord) this Staff of Office, and withal Salutes you Grand Commander of the Ports Throughout his Principalities. He shortly Will visit you himself: I must attend him.— D''ee feel your physic stirring yet? A Devil is a rare juggler, and can cheat the eye, But not corrupt the reason in the Throne Of a pure soul.— Another? I will stand thee, Be what thou canst, I care not. From the Prince, dear Brother, I present you this rich Relic, A jewel he hath long worn in his bosom: Henceforth he bade me say, he does beseech you To call him son, for he will call you Father. It is an honour (brother) that a subject Cannot but entertain with thankful prayers. Be moderate in your loys, he will in person Confirm my errand, but commands my service.
