I warrant you Sir. Well Vallenger if all things fall out right: You shall have little cause to thank The Doctor, but here he comes. How now Doctor, what will Florence come, She will be here Sir, presently, and see You can no sooner speak but she is come. Go Sirrah, do you tend at door, Let none come in unless I call to you. I will, strait fetch Sentloe, to this match, Are you there Doctor, y saith i'll be even with you. Sweet mistress welcome. vallenger now by this light Thou art the welcomest man in Christendom. Thanks gentle mistress, but how if Sentlove come. Hang him I never loved him in my life, Only I gull the Rascal for his money. The more villain vallenger To leave his true wife for a common stall. Now by this hand, I wonder vallenger, What delight thou takest in such a wife, But that she is somewhat wise, and modest, But to content a gallant spirit indeed, By this light she is a very block to me. hang her, I care not for her, our fathers made the match Now with a devil what whirl wind blew you hither?
