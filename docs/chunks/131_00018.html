Bay may troth, in reading any other, ne taym do I spend Far that ay ken, bay general caunsell, is canonised And bay the hely Pope himself is authorized: That Book farther, is wholly permitted, whereas, the Bayble in part is prohibited. And therefore, give it be lawful to utter my conscience, Before the new Testament ays give it credence. I allow his judgement before Ambrose & Austin, And for Hypocrisy, a more convenient Chapleyn, It grieveth me much that no fault we can spy, For now of some bribe disappointed am I, Yet happily he may tell us of some Heretics. Is there M. Parson in your parish no schismatics? Yai mara is ther a vara busy body, When will jest with me and call me fool and noddy, And sets his Lads te spout latin against me, But ay suppose then with Deparfundis Clam avi, And oftentimes he will reason with me of the sacrament, And say he can prove bay the new testament, That Christ's body is in Heaven placed, But ays not believe him, ay will not be awt faced, He says besaid that the Pope is Antichrist, Figured of Iohn bay the seven headed beast, And all awre religion is but mons invention, And with God's ward is at utter dissension, And a plaguy deal mare of sayk layk talk, That ay dare not far may nars bay his gate walk, But ay wawd he were brunt that ay mawght be whaiet, He must. have a cooler his tongue runs at riot. What is his name sir Iohn, canst thou tell us? Yai sir that ay ken he is cleped Phailelegoos. Wilt thou go show his house where he dwell? Yai or else ay wawd may sawl war in Hell, Te de him a pleasure ay wawd gang a whole year, Give it war but te make him a fadock te bear. Go with us Avarice and bear us company. Nay, if you go hence I will not here tarry. Away sirs in your business in a corner do not lurk, That my Lord Legate when he comes may have work. Come on let us go together sir Iohn. Ay sall follow after, God boy you good Gentleman. Farewell, three false knaves, as between this and London. What sayst thou? Hip. As honest men as the three Kings of Colon. This gear goes round if that we had a fiddle: Nay, I must sing too, heigh dery dery dery, I can do but laugh my heart is so merry, I willbe minstrel myself heigh diddle diddle diddle, But lay there a straw I began to be weary: But hark I here a trampling of feet, It is my Lord Legate I will him go meet. GO to Master Zeal, bring forth that Heretic, Which doth thus disturb our religion Catholic. Room for my Lords grate: what? no manner reverence, But Cap on head Hodge, and that in a lord's presence.
