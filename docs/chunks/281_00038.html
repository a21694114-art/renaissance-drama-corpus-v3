My lord, your promise for the Earldom. Richmond, when last I was at Exeter, The Mayor in courtesy showed me the Castle, And called it Ruge-mount, at which name I started, Because a Bard of Ireland told me once I should not live long after I saw Richmond. My lord. I, what's a clock? I am thus bold to put your grace in mind Of what you promised me. Well, but what's a clock? Upon the stroke often. Well, let it strike. Why let it strike? Tut, tut, thou troublest me, I am not in the vain. Is it even so, rewardst he my true service With such deep contempt, made I him king for this? O let me think on Hastings and be gone To Brecnock while my fearful head is on.
