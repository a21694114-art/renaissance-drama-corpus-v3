If I can close with him, I care not for his thrust. No, nor I neither: I'll be at your elbow. If I but fist him once: if he come but within my Vice. Snare let him not scape, he comes continuantly to Py-Corner (saving your manhoods) to buy a saddle, and he is indicted to dinner to the Lubbars' head in Lombardstreet, to M. Smooths the Silkman. I pra'ye, since my Exion is entered, and my Case so openly known to the world, let him be brought in to his answer: A 100. Mark is a long one, for a poor lone woman to bear: & I have borne, and borne, and borne, and have been fubbed off, and fub'd-off, from this day to that day, that it is a shame to be thought on. There is no honesty in such dealing, unless a woman should be made an Ass and a Beast, to bear every Knaves wrong. Yonder he comes, and that arrant Malmesey-Nose Bardolfe with him.
