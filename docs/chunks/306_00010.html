Farewell good Brother, we shall thrive, I trust. Uncle, adieu: O let the hours be short, Till fields, and blows, and groans, applaud our sport. Heigh-ho, an't be not four by the day, I'll be hanged. Charles wain is over the new Chimney, and yet our horse not packed. What Ostler? Anon, anon. I prithee Tom, beat Cuts Saddle, put a few Flocks in the point: the poor jade is wrung in the withers, out of all cease. Pease and Beanes are as dank here as a Dog, and this is the next way to give poor jades the Bottes: This house is turned upside down since Robin the Ostler died. Poor fellow never joyed since the price of oats rose, it was the death of him. I think this is the most villainous house in all London road for Fleas: I am stung like a Tench. Like a Tench? There is ne'er a King in Christendom, could be better bit, than I have been since the first Cock. Good morrow Master Gadshill, it holds currant that I told you yesternight. There's a Franklin in the wild of Kent, hath brought three hundred Marks with him in Gold: I heard him tell it to one of his company last night at Supper; a kind of Auditor, one that hath abundance of charge too (God knows what) they are up already, and call for Eggs and Butter. They will away presently. Sirrah, if they meet not with S. Nicholas Clarks, I'll give thee this neck. No, I'll none of it: I prithee keep that for the Hangman, for I know thou worship'st S.
