This was a day of peace, The day wherein the holy priest hath tied Our hearts together, Hymen's Tapers yet Are burning, and it cannot be a sin Less than a sacrilege, to extinguish them With blood, and in contempt of heavens proceeding Thus to conspire our separation No Christian would profane the marriage day, And when all other wish us joys, could you Intrude yourself to poison all our mirth, Blast in the very bud all our happiness Our hopes had laid up for us. I was a stranger, That makes ye more uncivil, we were merry Which could not offend you. I had no thought To violate your mirth. What came you for?
