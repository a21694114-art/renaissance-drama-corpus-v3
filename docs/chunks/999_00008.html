Why then I pray keep your quantity of your wit, from the quality of my maidenhead, and ye shall find my maidenhead more than your wit. A witty maidenhead by this hand. Is my Mistress ready for dinner? Yes, if dinner be ready for my Mistress. Half an hour ago man. But prithee sir, is't for certain, for yet it cannot sink into my head, that she is to be married tomorrow. Troth, she makes little preparation, but it may be she wood be wedded, as she wood be bedded, privately.
