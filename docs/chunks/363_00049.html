Yes if my Mall were here, than all were well. Yonder they be Mall, stay, stand close and stir not Until I call: God save ye Gentlemen. Nay mistress Barnes, I wish her better, that those joyful days may be turned to joyful nights. Faith 'tis a pretty wench, and 'tis pity but she should have him. And mistress Mary, when ye go to bed, God send you good rest, and a peck of Fleas in your nest, every one as big as Francis.
