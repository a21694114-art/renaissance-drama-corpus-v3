I would your tongue Was tied as fast; then there was hope I might Escape with life. What are you fellow, speak? You may go look, go meddle with your own. So angry 'pray thee? how came thy hands in mortgage? Shall I redeem them? Redeem your own lands I pray you, Let me alone or else I'll spurn you— yet He knows me not. Sure I have seen that face. O, O, O— Is 't he or not, ha? Anteros. No more. Death not a word. But heaven and earth man! how Comes this to pass? What has begot this change? Wilt thou untie me? I will tell thee all. But pray thee Anteros.— But pray thee Iack Thou wilt undo me quite by thy delays, Wilt thou undo me? 'Tis not a friendly part. Pox o' that jest, as common as a woman, Or her Synonomy; wilt thou untie me? 'Tis done. Thou art my Patron Love-all, So. But stay a while, I must desire your aid A little further. What has he now in hand? Can you become a peaceable man? How now? A Snake, a Snake; he's young again, ha, ha, he. What? Pinks and Roses too? Why so, he plucks Iune out of 's pocket. Can you be quiet yet? And Garters too? That slippery tongue of yours I doubt will spoil all. What? and a band? so, so; The veil of Tempe's not so fresh, the picture, The very picture of the Spring, when th' earth Lays by her freeze-coat, and turns Forester. Thus far it prospers, once more your help sweet Iack, Nay come, and take me that same rope again, And bind me as I was before, directly In the same garb you found me— Do not stand Gazing, but do 't. Thou art not mad I hope? If I be mad, I will not trouble you For counsel, nor for Physic; nay wilt thou come? But hold a little, I must first borrow of you Your Hat, and Sword. Which way this plot will look I know not— there— come let me see your hand's Since you will needs. Why now thou'rt right, thou'rt right, What will you have me do beside? come on, Your legs too if you will. No more, St. hark. The Shepherds door. Trouble us not good Love-all. Only stand close and hear. What should this mean? Fie Ieoffry, are you not ashamed, to touch one by the skin? My daughter denies all this most stiffly but I will Ferret-claw my Lobcock i'faith. So, now I am armed. Go to, go to, come you knave, where are you? Ha, ha, he. Ha? ha? ha? How now by'r Lady? How now? ay, I by'r Lady? what's this? What's this? gaudy? gaudy? Fine clothes? fine clothes? Ha? has nobody stole my eyes? let me be sure of that in the first place. Am I Stipes or not? ha? ha? ha? Is this our Ieoffry or not?
