Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Your Mistress. How! my Mistress! I begin to have a Christians heart, made of sweet butter, I melt, I cannot strike a woman. Not I, unless she scratch, bum my mistress! You're Coxcombs, silly Animals, What's that? Drones, Asses, blinded Moles, that dare not thrust Your arms out to catch Fortune, say you fall off, It must be done you are converted Rascals, And that once spread abroad, why every slave Will kick you, call you motley Christians, And half faced Christians. The guts of my conscience begin to be of whitleather, I doubt me I shall have no sweet butter in me. Deny this, and each Pagan whom you meet Shall forked fingers thrust into your eyes. If we be Cuckolds. Do this, and every god the Gentiles bow to, Shall add a fathom to your line of years. A hundred fathom, I desire no more. I desire but one inch longer, The Senators will as you pass along Clap you upon your shoulders with this hand, And with this hand glue you gold when you are dead, Happy that man shall be can get a nail, The paring—, they the dirt under the nail Of any of you both, to say this dirt Belonged to Spungius or Hercius. They shall not want dirt under my nails, I'll keep'em long of purpose, for now my singer's itch to be at her. The first thing I do I'll take her o'er the lips. And I the hips, we may strike anywhere. Yes, anywhere. Then I know where i'll hit her. Prosper and be mine own; stand by, I must not To see this done, great business calls me hence, he's made can make her curse his violence. Fear it not sir, her ribs shall be basted. He come upon her with rounce, robble-hobble, and thwick-thwack thirlety bouncing. According to our Roman customs, bind That Christian to a Pillar. Infernall Furies, Could they into my hand thrust all their whips To tear thy flesh thy soul, 'tis not a torture Fit to the Vengeance, I should heap on thee, For wrongs done me: me! for flagitious facts By thee done to our gods, yet (so it stand To great Caesarea's Governors high pleasure) Bow but thy Knee to Iupiter and offer Any slight sacrifice, or do but swear By Caesar's fortune, and be free. Thou shalt. Not for all Caesar fortune, were it chained To more worlds, then are kingdoms in the world, And all those worlds drawn after him: I defy Your hangmen; you now show me whither to fly. Are her tormentors ready? Shrink not dear mistress. My Lord, we are ready for the business. You two! whom I like fostered children fed, And lengthened out your starved life with bread: You be my hangman! whom when up the ladder Death hauled you to be strangled, I fetched down Clothed you, and warmed you, you two my tormentors.
