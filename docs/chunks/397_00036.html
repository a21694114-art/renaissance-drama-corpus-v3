Ye are too cursed (me thinks sir) to your Lady; Ah wench content thee, I must bear her hard, Else she'll be prining into my dalliances: I am an old man sweet girl I must be merry, All steel, all sprite, keep in health by change, Men may be wanton, wowen must not range. You have given good counsel sir, i'll repent me, here's your ring, i'll only love my husband. I mean not so, I think to day thou toldes me Thy husband was an unthrift, and a bankrupt, And he be so, tut thou hast favour store, Let the knave beg, beauty cannot be poor. Indeed my husband is a bankrupt, Of faith, of love, of shame, of chastity, Dotes upon other women more than me. Ha do he so? then give him tit for tat, Have one so young and fair, and loves another, He's worthy to be coockolded by the mass. What is he old or young? About your age. An old knave and cannot be content with such a peat, Come to my closet girl, make much of me, We'll appoint a meeting place some twice a weak, And i'll maintain thee like a Lady, ha? O but you'll forget me presently, When you look well upon your Lady's beauty. Who upon her? why she is a very dowdy, A dishclout, a foul Iipsie unto thee, Come to my closet lass, there take thy earnest Of love, of pleasure and good maintenance. I am very fearful. Come fool never fear I am Lord heat, who shall disturb as then? Nay come, or by the rood I'll make you come, Help Madam Faukenbridge for god's sake. How now, what meanest? Help Gentle Madam help, How now what ail'st thou? Nay and't be a woman, near fear my master Madam Why speak'st thou not, what ail'st thou? Why nothing, by the rood nothing she ails. O Madam this vile man would have abused me, And forced me to his closet, Ah old coal, now look about, you are catched, Call in your fellows block, Do not thou knave, Do or I'll crack your crown, Nay I'll do't, I know she means to shame you. Why Mall wilt thou believe this paltry woman? housewife I'll have you whipped for slandering me. What Lecher, no she is an honest woman, Her husband's well known, all the household knows. here's some now, to tell all the town your mind, Before ye all I must sure complain, You see this wicked man, and ye all know How oft he hath been jealous of my life, Suspecting falsehood being false himself; O master, O master, She slanders me, she is a cozening quean, Fetch me the Constable, I'll have her punished, The Constable for me fie, fie upon ye. Madam do you know this ring? It is sir Richards. O I, that's my masters too sure.
