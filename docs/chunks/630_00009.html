And see your discourse has conjured up beauty in the likeness of two country-maids, but you shall not come in the circles of their arms if I can keep you out. These are too mean for love, brother let's leave them. What? speechless? will you make dumb virgins of them? Oh we are sons of a great father. So is the sun of heaven, yet he smiles on the bramble as well as the Lily, kisses the check of a beggar as lovingly as a gentlewoman, and 'tis good to imitate, him 'tis good. Say sister, had we not fine sport today? We had, if death may be accounted play. Why 'tis accounted pleasure to kill fish. A pleasure nothing pleasant to the fish. Yet fishes were created to be killed. Cruel creation then to have lives spilled. Their bodies being food, maintain our breath. What bodies then have we to live by death? Come, come, you vainly argue, it is good. What is it good to kill? oh god oh god? If it be sin, than you yourself's a sinner. I thank proud fortune for't, my woes beginner. Foot are ye not ashamed to stand by like idle ciphers, & such places of account void? and they had been rich offices and you poor Courtiers, you would have been in them in half the time. Though against stomach.— Nothing against stomach and you love me. Fair maids if so you be you are well met. shepherds or be what else you are well met. 'tis well, if that well met we be to you. If not to us you are unto yourselves. We did not meet, you saw us come together. whate'er we saw, you yet ere you came hither. We did, we met in kindred, we are brothers. So shepherds we did meet for we are sisters. Then sisters, let vs brothers husbands e. So brothers without our leaves you well may be. Say we desire to husband it with you. Know we desire no husbands such as you. A shepherd is an honest trade of life. Yet honest shepherd has with honest trade some strife. He seldom swears but by his honesty. So honest men do too as well as he. But will you trust a shepherd when he vows, No never, if his oath be that he loves. Yet if I swear, that needs must be mine oath, Swear not, for we are misbelievers both. Let us persuade you to believe we love you. First we entreat you give us time to prove you. Take time, mean time we'll praise ye to our powers. Oh time, sometime shepherds have idle hours. I'll say thy cheek no natural beauty lacks, Good, if it had been spoke behind our backs. I'll say this is the heaven of heavenly graces. O heaven, how they can flatters to our faces. Exeunt. Brother the last is fairest in my eye.
