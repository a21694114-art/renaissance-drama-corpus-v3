He has given it you, CENTAURE, i'faith. But, do you hear, M. MOROSE, a jest will not absolve you in this manner. You that have sucked the milk of the court, and from thence have been brought up to the very strong meats, and wine, of it; been a courtier from the biggen, to the nightcap: (as we may say) and you, to offend in such a high point of ceremony, as this! and let your nuptials want all marks of solemnity!
