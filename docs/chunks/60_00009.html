Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

A trembling cold doth run throughout my bloodless limbs each one. What heinous thing comes to mine ears? I feared not then at all, When (all peace broke) the noise of war did by the city wall Resound about, I bore all that unfearfully to see, I fear the wedding chambers: now I captive seem to me. Let heavy chains my body grieve, and eke with hunger long Let lingering death be slowly brought, yet shall no force full strong My truth subdue: for even thine own Alcides will I die. Doth then thy husband drowned in hell give thee this stomach hic? The hells allow he touched, that he the height again might get. The heavy poise oppresseth him of all the earth full great. He with no burden shall be priest, that heaven itself sustained. Thou shalt be forced. He wots not how to die, that is constrained. Speak, what may rather I prepare then wedding new for thee. More royal gift? Thine own death else, or else the death of me. Thou shalt mad woman die. I shall then to my husband go. More than my sceptres is to thee a servant loved so? How many hath this servant slain of kings with handy stroke? Why doth he yet a king then serve, and still sustain his yoke? Take once away the hard behests, what's virtue then at last? Dost thou it virtue count, to be to beasts, and monsters cast? 'tis virtues part, to tame the things, that all men quake to know. Him great things bragging, darkness deep of Tartar press full low. There never may from ground to stars an easy passage be. Of whom begot, the housen then of Gods through pierceth he? O wretched wife of Hercles' great, thy words a while now spare. My part it is, the father of Alcides to declare. And his true stock, yet after all of man so stout as this So famous deeds, and after all appeased with hand of his whatever Titan risen up, doth see, or else at fall, And after all these monsters tamed, and Phlegrey sprinkled all With wicked blood, and after Gods defended all on high, Is not his father yet well known? or Ioue do we belie? Believe it yet by Juno's hate. Why dost thou slander Ioue? No mortal kindred ever may be mixed with heaven above. To many of the Gods in skies is this a common trade. But were they ever servants yet, before they Gods were made? Of Delos I'll the shepherd lo the flocks of Pherey fed. But through all coasts he wandered not abroad as banished. Whom straying mother first brought forth wandering loud to sight. Yet Phoebus did no monsters fear, or beasts of cruel might.
