My rapier? no sir: my rapier is my guard, my defence, my revenue, my honour: (if you cannot impart, be secret, I beseech you) and I will maintain it, where there is a grain of dust, or a drop of water. (Hard is the choice when the valiant must eat their arms, or clem:) Sell my rapier? no, my dear, I will not be divorced from thee, yet, I have ever found thee true as steel— and (you cannot impart sir?) Save you gentlemen: (nevertheless if you have a fancy to it, sir.) Pray thee away: is signor DELIRO departed? Ha'you seen a pimp outface his own wants better? I commend him, that can dissemble 'em so well. True, and having no better a cloak for it, than he has neither. God's precious, what mischievous luck is this! adieu gentlemen. Whither? in such haste, Monsieur FASTIDIUS? After my merchant, signor DELIRO, sir. O hinder him not, he may hap lose his tide, a good flounder i'faith. Hark you, signor WHIFF, a word with you. How? signor WHIFF? What was the difference between that gallant that's gone, and you, sir. No difference: he would ha'giu'n me five pound for my rapier, and I refused it; that's all. O, was't no otherwise? we thought you had been upon some terms. No other than you saw, sir. Adieu, good Master APPLE-JOHN. How? WHIFF, and APPLE-JOHN too? Heart, what'll you say if this be the appendix, or label to both yond indentures? It may be. Resolve us of it, JANUS, thou that look'st every way: or thou Hercules, that hast travailed all countries. Nay, CARLO, spend not time in invocations now, 'tis late. signor, here's a gentleman desirous of your name, sir. Sir, my name is CAVALIER SHIFT: I am known sufficiently in this walk, sir. SHIFT? I heard your name varied e'en now, as I take it. True, sir, it pleases the world (as I am her excellent Tabbacconist) to give me the stile of signor WHIFF: as I am a poor esquire about the town here, they call me Master APPLE-JOHN. Varierie of good names does well, sir. I, and good parts, to make those good names: out of which I imagine yond bills to be yours. Sir, if I should deny the manuscripts, I were worthy to be banished the middle I'll, for ever. I take your word, sir: this gentleman has subscribed to 'em, and is most desirous to become your pupil. Marry you must use expedition. signor Insulso Sogliardo, this is the professor. In good time, sir, nay, good sir, house your head, do you profess those sleights in tobacco? I, do more than profess, sir, and (if you please to be a practitioner) I will undertake in one fortnight to bring you, that you shall take it plausibly in any ordinary, theatre, or the tiltyard, if need be, i'the most popular assembly that is.
