Send him a letter that I come to kill him. 'twere great valour but little policy my Lord. How long have you been a Matchevillian boy? Ever since I practised to play the knave my Lord, Then policy and knavery are somewhat a kin. As near, as penury and gentry, a degree and half removed no more. How came in the kindred twixt gentry and penury? Shall I tell you sir. First tell me what thou art? Lime and hair: plaster of Paris, kneaded together with rye dow and goats milk, I am of a hot constitution, wonot freeze. Thy profession. A fool or a knave choose you which. Then thou art fit for any gentleman's company. True boy, for your sweet fool and your fine knave are like a pair of upright shoes, that gentlemen wear so long now of one foot, then of another, till they leave them never a good sole. That makes your fool & your knave have such bad soles; but what dost thou seek? Mine own undoing sir, service. Indeed service is like the common law, it undoes any one that follows it long, canst describe service? Yes, 'tis a vacant place, filled up with a complete knave, a miserable pandar, or an absolute beggar. your opinion boy. I say a servingman is an Antecedent. Because he sits before a cloak-bag. He is likewise a nominative Case, and goes before his mistress. That's when the verb he goes before his mistress and he can agree together. If not, he turns Accusative and follows his master. wilt follow me fellow. To a tavern, and thou wilt pay for my ordinary. My business is more serious, thou dost not know me. Nor myself neither so long as I have maintenance. Didst never hear of the wars betwixt Venice and Mantua. I cut some few of the Mantuans throats. And wert not a knave for't. No, I was a Venetian commander, a great man: the reason of this question. Dost know the Duke of Venice? I am his right hand. wilt do me a message to him. What is't? Tell him I hate him, my name's Aspero, has banished my father, usurps his dukedom, and I come to be revenged. Anthonio's son? vesfoot haste any gold. Thy reason. Shalt be revenged, give me money, i'll be thy snail & score out a silver path to his confusion. No, my revenge shall be like my father's wrongs in Aperto lend me any honest aid. Pax of honesty, it goes a-begging upon crutches, and can get relief out of few but scholars, I shall not kill him? Jde be thy death first. Yet you say you hate him. Equal with my shame. Make him chew a bullet then. No, though my state with poverty be tainted, Mine acts and honour shall live still acquainted.
