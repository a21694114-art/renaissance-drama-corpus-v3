Or, else as if your Spouse Sir, had rebuked you How is it, Sir? you feel have brought along My Fiddlers with me; my Wife and Mrs. Holland Are good wind Instruments. I have matched a Stews; The notedst woman o'th' Town. Indeed I heard She was a Chambermaid. And they by their place, Do wait upon the Lady, but belong Unto the Lord. But is this true?
