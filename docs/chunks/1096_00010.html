You will have justice, is your will so strong Now against mine? your power being so weak Before my favour gave them both their forces Of all that ever shared in my free graces, You Philip Chabot a mean Gentleman Have not I-raised you to a supremest Lord, And given you greater dignities than any? You have so. Well said, and to spur your dulinesse With the particulars to which I raised you, Have not I made you first a Knight of the Orders Then Admiral of France, than Count Byzanges, Lord, and Lieutenant general of all My country, and command of Burgady; Lieutenant general likewise of my son Dauphin, and heir, and of all Normandy, And of my chiefly honoured privy Counsel, And cannot all these powers weigh down your will? No Sir, they were not given me to that end, But to uphold my will, my will being just. And who shall judge that Justice, you or I? I Sir, in this case your royal thoughts are fitly Exempt from every curious search of one, You have the general charge with care of all. And do not generals include particulars? May not I judge of any thing comprised In your particular as well as you? far be the misery from you, that you may, My cares, pains, broken sleep therein made more Than yours should make me see more, and my forces Render of better judgement. Well Sir, grant Your force in this my odds in benefits Paid for your pains, put in the other scale, And any equal holder of the balance Will show my merits hoist up yours to air In rule of any doubt or deed betwixt us. You merit not of me for benefits More than myself of you for services. Is't possible. 'tis true. Stand you on that? I to the death and will approve to all men. I am deceived, but I shall find good Judges That will find difference. Find them being good. Still so? what if conferring My bounties, and your services to sound them, We fall foul on some licences of yours, Nay, give me therein some advantage of you. They cannot. Not in sisting their severe discharges Of all your offices? The more you sift The more you shall refine me. What if I Grant out against you a commission Joined with an extraordinary process To arrest, and put you in laws hands for trial. Not with laws uttermost. I'll throw the dice. And I'll endure the chance, The dice being square. Reposed in dreadless confidence, and conscience, That all your most extremes shall never reach, Or to my life, my goods or honours breach. Was ever heard so fine a confidence? Must it not prove presumption, and can that Escape bracks and errors in your search of law, I prithee weigh yet, with more soul than danger, And some less passion.
