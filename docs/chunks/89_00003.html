Like will to like I see very well, God father wilt thou dance a little before thou go home to hell I am content so that tom collier do agree, I will never refuse Devil to dance with thee. Then godfather name what the dance shall be. Tom Colier of Croidon hath sold his coal, Why then have at it by my father's soul. Tom Colier of Croidon hath sold his Coals, and made his market today: And now he danceth with the Devil, for like will to like always. Wherefore let us rejoice and sing, let us be merry and glad, Sith that the Colier and the Devil, this match and baunce hath made. Now of this dance we make an and, with mirth and eke with joy: The Colier and the Devil will be, much like to like always. Aha, marry this is trini singing, I had not thought the Devil to be so cunning. And by the mass Tom Colier is as good as he, I see that like with like will ever agree.
