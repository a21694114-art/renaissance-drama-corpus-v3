This is for your sake Mary: take heed cousin, A man is not so soon made. O my fortune, But it is just, I be despised and hated. Despair not, 'tis not manly: one hours' goodness Strikes off an infinite of ills. Weep truly And with compassion Cousin. How exactly This cunning young thief plays his part. Well Tom My Tom again, if this be truth. She weeps boy. O I shall die. Now heaven defend. Thou hast her. Come lead me to my Friend to take his farewell, And then what fortune shall befall me, welcome. How does it show? O rarely well. Say you so Sir. O ye grand Ass. And are ye there my juggler Away we are abused Alice. Fool be with thee. Where is she. Gone; she found you out, and finely, In your own noose she haltered ye: you must be whispering To know how things showed: not content to far well But you must roar out roast meat; till that suspicion You carried it most neatly, she believed too And wept most tenderly; had you continued, Without doubt you had brought her off. This was thy Rouging. For thou wert ever whispering: fie upon thee Now could I breeches thy head. You spoke to me first. Do not anger me, For by this hand i'll beat the buzzard blind than She shall not scape me thus: farewell for this time, Good night, 'tis almost bed time: yet no sleep Must enter these eyes, till I work a wonder. Thou shalt along too, for I mean to plague thee For this night's sins, I will never leave walking of thee Till I have worn thee out. Your will be done Sir. You will not leave me Sam. Not I Away then: i'll be your guide now, if my man be trusty My spiteful Dame, i'll pipe ye such a huntsup Shall make ye dance a tipvaes: keep close to me. NEver persuade me, I will marry again What should I leave my state to, pins & poking sticks To Farthingals, and frownces, to fore-horses And a old leather bawdy house behind'en To thee? You have a son Sir. Where, what is he? Who is he like? Yourself. Thou liest, thou hast marred him, Thou, and thy prayer books: I do disclaim him: Did not I take him singing yesternight A godly Ballad, to a godly tune too, And had a catechizme in's pocket Damsel, One of your dear disciples, I perceive it? When did he ride abroad since he came over? What Tavern has he used to? what hangs done That shows a man, and mettle? when was my house At such a shame before, to creep to bed At ten a clock, and twelve, for want of company? No singing, nor no dancing, nor no drinking? Thou thinkest not of these scandals; when, and where Has he but showed his sword of late.
