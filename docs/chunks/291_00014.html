here comes Romeo. Without his Roe, like a dried Hearing. O flesh flesh how art thou fishified. Sirrah now is he for the numbers that Petrarch flowed in: Laura to his Lady was but a kitchen drudge, yet she had a better love to berhyme her: Dido a dowdy Cleopatra a Gipsy, Hero and Helen hildings and harletries: Thisby a gray eye or so, but not to the purpose. signor Romeo bon iour, there is a French courtesy to your French slop: ye gave us the counterfeit fairly yesternight. What counterfeit I pray you? The slip the slip, can you not conceive? I cry you mercy my business was great, and in such a case as mine, a man may strain courtesy. Oh that's as much to say as such a case as yours will constrain a man to bow in the hams. A most courteous exposition. Why I am the very pink of courtesy. Pink for flower? Right. Then is my Pump well flowered: Well said, follow me now that jest till thou hast worn out thy Pump, that when the single sole of it is worn the jest may remain after the wearing solely singular. O single soald jest solely singular for the singleness. Come between us good Benuolio, for my wits fail. Swits and spurs, swits & spurs, or I'll cry a match. Nay if thy wits run the wild-goose chase, I have done: for I am sure thou hast more of the goose in one of thy wits than I have in all my five: Was I with you there for the goose? Thou wert never with me for any thing, when thou wert not with me for the goose. I'll bite thee by the ear for that jest. Nay good goose bite not. Why thy wit is a bitter sweeting, a most sharp sauce And was it not well served in to a sweet goose? Oh here is a wit of Chevril that stretcheth from an inch narrow to an ell broad. I stretched it out for the word broad, which added to the goose, proves thee fair and wide a broad goose. Why is not this better now than groaning for love? why now art thou sociable, now art thou thyself, now art thou what thou art, as well by art as nature. This drivelling love is like a great natural, that runs up and down to hide his babble in a hole. Stop there. Why thou wouldst have me stop my tale against the hair. Thou wouldst have made thy tale too long? Tut man thou art deceived, I meant to make it short, for I was come to the whole depth of my tale? and meant indeed to occupy the argument no longer. Heers goodly gear. A sail, a sail, a sail. Two, two, a shirt and a smock. Peter, pray thee give me my fan. Pray thee do good Peter, to hide her face: for her fan is the fairer of the two.
