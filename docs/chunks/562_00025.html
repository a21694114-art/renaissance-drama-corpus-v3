Herald, for you have spent all my devices already, but since you are here, let me ask you a question, in your own profession, how comes it to pass that the victorious Arms of England, quartered with the conquered Coat of France are not placed on the dexter side, but give the flower deluce the better hand? Because that the three Lions are one coat made of two French Dukedoms, Normandy and Aquitaine: but I pray you Visus, what jay is that, that follows him?
