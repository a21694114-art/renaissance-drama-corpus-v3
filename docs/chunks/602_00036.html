O 'twill be excellent. But how shall we shift 'em off? Not as ill debtor do their Creditors (with good words) but as Lawyers do their Clients when their overthrown, by some new knavish trick: and thus it shall be: one of us must dissemble to be suddenly very sick. I'll be she. Nay, though we can all dissemble well, yet I'll be she: for men are so jealous, or rather envious of one another's happiness (Especially in this out of town gossipings) that he who shall miss his hen, if he be a right Cock indeed, will watch the other from treading. Puh, puh, say not so. Pray let my clothes be utterly undone, and then lay me in my bed. Walk up and down a little. O master Lynstock, 'tis no walking will serve my turn: have me to bed good sweet Mistress Honisuckle, I doubt that old Hag Gillian of Brainford has bewitched me.
