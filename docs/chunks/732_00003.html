Why but whither do you mean to go Master? Why to Sea. To sea! Lord bless us, methinks I hear of a tempest already, but what will you do at Sea? Why as other Gallants do that are spent, turn pirate. O Master! have the grace of Wapping before your eyes, remember a high tide, give not your friend's cause to wet their handkerchers: nay Master, I'll tell you a better course than so, you and I will go and rob mine uncle; if we scape, we'll domineer together, if we be taken, we'll be hanged together at Tyburn, that's the warmer gallows of the two.
