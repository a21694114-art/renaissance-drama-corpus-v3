An uncle so insatiate? ay, Is't not strange too, That all should be by Nature vicious, and he bade against Nature? Then you have heard the sum of all my wrongs. Lady, we have, and desire rather now To heal 'em, than to hear 'em: For by a letter from Fidelio Direct to us, we are entreated jointly, To hasten your remove from this foul den, Of theft and purposed incest: I rejoice in his chaste care of me, I'll soon be furnished. He writes that his return cannot be long. I'm chiefly glad,— but whither is the place? To the safe seat of his late wronged mother. I desire it,— Her conference will fit mine, well you prevail, At next grove we'll expect you: I'll not fail. It stands upon the frame of my reputation I protest Lady. Lady, that word is worth an hundred Angels at all times, for it cost more: if I live till tomorrow night, my sweet pleasure thou shalt have them. Could you not make 'em a hundred and fifty think you? I'll do my best endeavour to multiply, I assure you. Could you not make 'em two hundred? No by my faith.— Peace, i'll rather be confined in the hundred & fifty. Come e'en much about this time, when Taverns give up their Ghosts, and Gentlemen are in their first cast.— I'll observe the season. And do but whirl the Ring a'th' door once about, my maidservant shall be taught to understand the language. Enough my sweet Revenue. Good rest my effectual pleasure. Come hither Phoenix. What makes your honour break so early? A toy, I have a toy. A toy my Lord. Before thou layest thy wrath upon the duke,— be advised. ay, ay, I warrant you my Lord. Nay, give my words honour, hear me, I'll strive to bring this Act into such form, And credit amongst men, they shall suppose. Nay verily believe the Prince his son, To be the plotter of his Father's murder. Oh that were infinitely admirable! Wert not, it pleaseth me beyond my bliss: Then if his son meet death as he returns, Or by my hired Instruments turn up, The general voice will cry— O happy vengeance! O blessed vengeance! ay, i'll turn my brain Into a thousand uses, Tire my inventions, Make my blood sick with study, and mine eye More hollow than my heart, but I will fashion, Nay I will fashion it,— Canst counterfeit? The PRINCE's hand, more truly, most direct, You shall admire it. Necessary mischief. Next to a woman, but more close in secrets Thou'rt all the kindred that my breast vouchsafes, Look into me anon,— I must frame, and muse, and fashion— 'twas time to look into thee, in whose heart Treason grows ripe, and therefore fit to fall, That slave first sinks, whose envy threatens all, Now is his Venom at full height.
