The device for him; a Cuckoo sitting on a tree? The word, En avis ex auibus; one bird made of many; for you know as the Sparrow hatches the Cuckoo, so the Gentleman feathers the Broker? Let me admire thee, master Fitsgrave? They will scorn gentlemen: and to assist them the better, Pursnets boy that little precious pickpocket, has a compendious speech in lattin, and like a Mercury presents their their dispositions more liberally: Never were poor gallants so abused? Hang 'em, their counterfeits, no honest spirit will pity 'em▪ This is my Crown, So good men smile I dread no rascals frown. Away, bestow yourselves secretly, o'erhead, this is the place appointed for the rehearsal, to practise their behaviours. We are vanished. Master Bowcer, well-said i'faith, off with your cloaks gallants, let's fall roundly to our business: Is the boy perfect? That's my credit sir, I warrant you? What's that prithee? Your bounty powers itself forth to all men. And so it does i'faith? that's all my fault, bountiful? For the device A Candle in a corner; the word, Consumptio, Victus: The meaning of that sir? My light is yet in darkness, till I enjoy her, right sir? Now mine sir? The Device an unvalued pearl hid in a Cave. Ah ha sirs? The word, Occultes vendit honores; Very-good I warrant, A black man's a pearl in a fair ladies eye, I said 'twas some such thing; My turn must needs come now; am I fitted Master Bowcer? Trust to me, your device here is a Cuckoo sitting on a tree, The Welsh leaguer, good. The word, En Anis ex Auibus. I marry sir. Why do you know what 'tis sir. No by my troth not yet sir. Oh, I keep one tune, I recant not. I me like the Cuckoo in that indeed, where I love I hold. Did I not promise you I would fit you. They re all very well done i'faith, and very scholarlike, though I say't before thy face little Bowcer, but I would not have thee proud on't now: come if this be performed well. Who the Boy, he has performed deeper matters then this.
