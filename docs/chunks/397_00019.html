You sp sp speak like an honest ge ge Gentleman, re re rest you me me merry, co co come my f f friends, I be believe h h he r ran by the g g garden w wall toward the wa water side. This fellow is of the humour I would choose my wife, Few words and many paces, a word and a way, and so Must I: Sister adieu, pray you for me, I'll do the like for you. Robin farewell, commend me to the Prince. Can ye not stay here safe? No, I'll not trust the changing humours of old Faukenbridge, Adieu young Earl, Sister let's kiss and part; Tush, near mourn, I have a merry heart. Farewell all comfort. What weeping Lady? Then I perceive you have forgot Blackheath. No, there I'll learn both of his life and death. Till Monday Madam I must take my leave. You will not miss then: Nay, if Robin fail ye, let him have never favour of fair Lady. Mean while I'll spend my time in prayers & tears, That Gloster may escape these threatened fears. Thus jets my noble Skinke along the streets, To whom each bonnet veils, and all knees bend; And yet my noble humour is too light, By the six shillings▪ here are two cracked groats To helter skelter, at some vawting house. But who comes yonder? ha, old Faukenbridge? Hath a brave-chaine, were Iohn and he good friends, That chain were mine, and should unto Blackheath I'll venture, it's but trial, luck may fall. Good morrow good sir Richard Faukenbridge. Good morrow my sweet Prince, hearty good morrow, This greeting well becomes us, marry does it; Better iwis then strife and jangling. Now can I love ye, will ye to the sheriffs? Your brother Richard hath been there this hour. Yes I am plodding forward as you do; What cost your chain? it's passing strongly wrought, I would my Goldesmith had a pattern of it. 'tis at your grace's service, show it him. Then dare ye trust me? Who the Princely Iohn? My Sovereign's son, why what a question's that? I'll leave you, ye may know I dare trust you. I'll bringed ye to the sheriffs, excuse my absence. I will my noble Lord, adieu sweet Prince. Why so, this breakfast was well fed upon, When Skinkes devices on Black heath do fail, This and such cheats, would set me under sail. I'll to the water side, would it were later, For still I am afraid to meet Prince Iohn. But what a mischief meant Faukenbridge To come again so soon? that way he went, And now comes peaking; upon my life The buzzard hath me in suspitition, But whatsoever chance, I'll filch a share. Yonder's Prince Iohn I hope he cannot know me, there's nought but Gloster Gloster in their mouths; I am half strangled with the Garlic breath, Of rascals that exclaims as I pass by, Gloster is fled, once taken he must die. But I'll to Iohn, how does my gracious Lord? What tattles rumour now? what news of Gloster?
