I tell ye true Gallants, I have seen neither of them today. Shall I give him the lie? They are both locked up i'faith, trimming of one another. Tim is a puling Sirrah, I may tell it thee, a midnight surfeit too may cut off Sander, I'll cozen their Wives, make all mine own, and then, Oh Iaruis what a moneyed generation shall I get, upon this widow Coote that hath two teeth. Did we bring you to Music with a mischief Earlack? thou art a Goat, thou hast abused the best bed in my house, I'll set a Sumner upon thee. Bloodhound, thou art a Usurer, and takest forty in the hundred, I'll inform against thee. Are you a Bawd housewife, ha? Alas Sir, I was merely envied, betrayed by Iaruis, but as I have been Bawd to the flesh, you have been Bawd to your money; so set the Hare Pie against the Goose giblets, and you and I are as daintily matched as can be sir. Sim, run to the widow wags, tell her we are both abused, this Iaruis is a Juggler, say.
