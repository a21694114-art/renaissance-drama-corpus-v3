Yes, sir. The great God, JUPITER, Of his licentious goodness, Willing to make this feast, no fast From any manner of pleasure; Nor to bind any God or Goddess, To be any thing the more god or goddess, for their names: He gives them all free licence, To speak no wiser, than persons of baser titles; And to be nothing better, than common men, or women. And therefore no God Shall need to keep himself more strictly to his Goddess, Than any man does to his wife. Nor any Goddess Shall need to keep herself more strictly to her God, Than any woman does to her husband.
