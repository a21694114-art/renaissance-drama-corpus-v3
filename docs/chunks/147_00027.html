Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

I am so fast wrapped in the upper sheet. That I can not get out, I pray you make not such haste, Till you think that the hottest of the broil be past. Stay Captain, lay no hands on me, a Gentleman I am, And will not flit, Woe worth the time that to my house he came. Ottaviano, what's the cause of your lamenting cry? Let's know, hath Sir Fortunio done you any injury? Come quickly man, let's see this Pageant ere it take an end, He that breaks me of my sleep, is none of my friend, Ah wretch that am I alas, and half undone, What strange kind of broil is this that is begun? Is it Fortunio in deed? This is thy treachery, Medusa. Mine, alas good Sir, you do me injury, I grant that after I had brought my young mistress to bed, Feeling the sleep shut up mine eyes, and drooping with my head, I laid me down to take my rest, and so with haste forgot, To lock the doors about the house, and how it comes God wot, I can not tell, but when I fet a nap and woke again, I heard a bustling in the dark, and then did I complain. And cried aloud to you for help, whereat immediately, This Gentleman withdrew himself, and forth began to fly. Master. What sayst thou? Your cake is dough, It kills me to think on it: the greater my woe. This is luck nidget with all my heart, I am glad, that I have somebody to take my part. But oh that my hands were at liberality now to strike, I would set my Gramariner a lesson to pike. Ah Sir Fortunio, use you thus the man that loved you best, Take him, this villainy shall not be turned to a jest. Quiet yourself Ottaviano, sith it is so past, The brute will not be called back so long as life doth last. His punishment makes not your daughter as she was before, But give her unto him to wife, and talk of it no more. His living is as good as yours, make up the match with speed, Need hath no law, I am content, if they be both agreed. Alas I never knew the man, he never touched me yet, I love Fedele, and he alone is for Virginia fit? I'll take no wife at second hand, thanks for your courtesy, Let him that hath possessed your honour, wear the same for me. In every Tennis Court in the world, false play it is found, To take up the Ball at the second rebound. Virginia, if that you can be content, To like of him that loves you in his heart: Give me your hand, and if your mind be bent, To marry me, I never mean to part. My life, and living, more you can not crave, Remaineth yours, do now but ask and have.

And cryed aloude to you for helpe, whereat immediatly, This Gentleman withdrewe him selfe, and foorth began to flye. ¶Master. ¶What sayst thou? ¶Your cake is dowe, ¶It killes me to thinke on it: the greater my woe. ¶This is lucke nidget with all my heart, ¶I am glad, that I haue some body to take my part.
