Your worship begins to talk idly, your bed shall be made presently; if we should not lodge knaves, I wonder how we should be able to live honestly, are there honest men enough think you in a Term time to fill all the Inns in the town, and as far as I can see, a knaves Gelding eats no more hay than an honest man's: nay thieves Gelding eats less, i'll stand to't, his Master allows him a better ordinary: yet I have my eightpence day & night, 'twere more for our profit I was, you were all thieves, if you were so contented: I shall be called for: give your worship's good morrow. A royal knave i'faith: we have happened into a Godly Inn. Assure you my L. they belong all to one church. This should be some old busy turbulent fellow: villainous Law-worm, that eats holes into poor men's causes. May it please your worship to give me leave? I give you leave sir, you have your Veniam, now fill me a brown toast sirrah. Will you have no drink to't sir? Is that a question in law? Yes, in the lowest Court: i'th' cellar sir. Let me ha't removed presently sir. It shall be done sir. Now as you were saying Sir, i'll come to you immediately too. Oh very well sir: I'm a little busy sir, But as how sir? I pray sir? Has brought me into the Court, marry my adversary has not declared yet. Non declaravit Aduersarius sayst thou: what a Villain's that, I have a trick to do thee good: I will get thee out a Prox, & make him declare with a Pox to him. That will make him declare to his fore grief, I thank your good worship: but put case he do declare? Si declarasset, If he should declare there, I would be loath to stand out to the judgement of that Court Non ad judicium, do you fear corruption? then i'll relieve you again: you shall get a Suersedias, Non molest andam; and remove it higher her. Very good. Now if it should ever come to a Testificandum, what be his witnesses? I little fear his witnesses. Non metuis testes? more valiant man then Orestes Please you sir to dissolve this into Wine, Ale, or Beer, I come a hundred mile to you I protest, and leave all other counsel behind me. Nay you shall always find me a sound Card, I stood not ath Pillory for nothing in eighty eight, all the world knows that: now let me dispatch you sir; I come to you Presenter. Faith the party hath removed both body and cause with a habeas corpus. Has he that knavery? But has he put in bail above canst tell? That I can assure your worship, he has not. Why then thy best course shall be, to lay out more money, take out a Procedendo, and bring down the cause and him with a Vengeance.
