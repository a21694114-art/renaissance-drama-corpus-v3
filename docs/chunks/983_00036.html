Then take it in a word. Supposing it the only way to win One of you to her, I counselled her to feign A love unto some other Gentleman. Whilst we were busy in these Consultations, As fortune would, your Page Endymion Came hither (Lucius) to seek his Master. We lay the train for him, she courts the Boy, And he (poor Lad) thinking her serious Was caught immediately. But is this true? Would I could call it false— But otherwise than was expected hath it prospered. Placenta, ah Placenta. Who's that calls me? Shall I disclose myself? I am ashamed. If it be so, Pandora, we crave pardon. And do restore him life; but now If thou dost aim to reach a life so happy So full of all content, that thou may'st fit Within thy Sphere (like Venus) and look down On all thy Sex, and pity them; love this man. Love this man. For as for myself I am Already furnished with a Mistress, see My wife here— Sweetest wife. Is this your wife? I judge her happy who so e'er she is, Believe him not, this is Boy, a villain (Whom I, but that—) Lucius forbear. Dressed up In woman's Clothes by that same dotard Lively. Sweetest Neander leave. It is a woman. By all the gods, it is a boy, 'tis false. But for to rob you of all hope of me Give me but care, I am an Eunuch, if You can endure to have a frozen statue, Sleep by your side, whilst you awake, recount The tedious minutes of your widowed nights And sigh, and think, and think, and sigh again, Behold an husband for you, I am he. O me! an Eunuch? Hold the Gentlewoman Ay me! she swoons, sweetest Pandora, ah. What is the matter? Ah good Lucius help, she's gone— alas good heart. What shall I do? But see she breathes again. Ah honey sweet Pandora speak. Ah! Hands off thou outside of a man; and thou Uxorious creature, I do crave no aid From you, forbear. How does my sweetest honey? I am not well Placenta, let us go Into your house a while. Please you fair Lady To use my service? How? Your service sir? You can do nothing, nor do I expect it. But if your love towards me be worthy, lend me Your Page, but for an hour. He is yours. Then sir adieu. Shall I be vanquished thus In friendship? But I will once more to Lively. And see what further counsel he will give me, Fair wife let's go— Rise up you villain boy; Lucius' farewell. What is he gone? so soon? To's Engineer I know, to his contriver; But I will follow them so fast, that not A syllable shall pass without my Knowledge. How now you Rascal? where are your eyes I wonder?
