Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Come douse. Now we have time and place as They say, I prithee use me with no delay, But still say, do not say you will not have me, Now because I am none of your burghers, But Douce as I am hasty yet I am not the hastiest, And though I am resty, Yet I am not lousy, And of one that cannot talk much, So I love to speak little, for as that Worthy Philosopher Hector says, the words Of the wise do offend the foolish, so Douce in few words and in tedious talk, Tell me when is this day. What day Frog. What day Frog? dost thou ask what day, why Douce this day of wedlock Douce, This day of going together Douce, This day of wearing out sheeets and Throwing down blanckets Douce. I saith Frog you know I have little, And for your own part your as poor as Iob, But not so scabed I thank God Douse, Well, I see you regard not the wisdom But the wealth, not the man: but the money, O Dowse, Dowse, much hast thou to answer for. Besides I think you do not love me. Not love thee, why I cannot dress my masters Horses for thinking of thee: I cannot dream ●or Sleeping of thee: but for a certainty, I love thee indeed, when 〈◊〉 go to bed And pluck of my shoes, there you may smell Love out of me: and then 〈◊〉 sigh and then I pause, And say that Dowce is the only cause. Well Frog, I have but jested all this while Y faith Frog haddest thou been ruled by me, Thou haddest not been Froging out of the well So long: but Frog 'twas thy fault. The more is to come Dowce, than you will Have me, we shall to this gear? I sweet heart, name you the time, The sooner the better. So say I dowse, for as the old saying is, He that hath a good dinner, knows better the way To supper: but ●ows●, we will be married a sunday, And that we will be spoken to be liberal, we'll give ten groats to the poor: with this Provisso, that if we need it, we'll have our ten groats again. but afterwards will you not prove unkind? How dowse unkind? When tinkers leave to drink good ale, And Soldiers of their weapons fail, When peddlers go without there pack, And water is more dear than sack, When Shoemakers drinks that is small, And Lawyers have no tongues at all, When Fencers leave of giving knocks, And young men hate fair Maidens smocks, When drunkards scorn a copar nose, And Butchers near mend lousy hose, Or when the cat shall hate a mouse. then Frog shall prove unkind to Dowse, and so sweet heart let's go and wed, and after to dinner and then to bed. Go Sirrah at the back door, Bring mistress Anabel, make haste away.
