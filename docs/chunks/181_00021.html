He hath laid the plot to be prudent, why 'tis pasty crust, eat enough and it will make you wise, an old proverb. ay, and eloquent, for you must tipple wine freely, & foecundi calices quem non fecere disertum. Fecere dizardum. Leave off these toys, and let us seek out Midas, whom we lost in the chase. I'll warrant he hath by this started a covey of Bucks, or roused a skull of Pheasants.
