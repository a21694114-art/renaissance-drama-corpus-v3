But it may well be called poor mortals Plague; For like a pestilence it doth infect The houses of the brain: first it begins Solely to work upon the fantasy, Filling her seat with such pestiferous air, As soon corrupts the judgement, and from thence, Sends like contagion to the memory, Still each of other catching the infection, Which as a searching vapour spreads itself Confusedly through every sensive part, Till not a thought or motion in the mind Be free from the black poison of suspect.
