Go in, man, never stand upon those niceties; for we are all of a house. Now, just now, they are covering the Table. O my young amorous youths, my Pearls of gold! O! you must give her leave, it is her living. But I wonder what devil taught her all her knacks, and her knaveries. What? marry, I will tell you. Necessity, Poverty, and Hunger; then which there are no better. tutors in the world: No better quickeners, and revivers of the wit. O! it is a fine time of day! is this a fit hour, when you have invited your friends, to a feast?
