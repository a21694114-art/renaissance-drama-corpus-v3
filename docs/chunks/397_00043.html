He's ready too, Block Bl. my man, must be her waiting man, Nay will ye go? for god's sake let us go. Is the jest so? nay then let us away. O 'twill allay his heat, make dead his fire. Ye bobbed me first, ye first gave me my hire, But come a gods name, Prince Iohn stays for us. This is the word, ever at spendthriftes feasts, They are gulled themselves, and scoffed at by their guests. Buffild and scoffed, Skinke, Gloster, women, fools, and boys abuse me? I'll be revenged, Revenged, and why good child? Old Faukenbridge hath had a worse basting.
