Bid 'em wait on us. On to Spinthers' house. Bring Lentulus forth. Here, you, the sad revengers Of capital crimes, against the Public, take This man unto your justice: strangle him. Thou dost well, Consul. 'twas a cast at dice In Fortune's hand, not long since, that thyself Shouldst have heard these, or other words as fatal.
