Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

No, 'tis no matter. Tomaso you shall do't, Go see nothing want; you are all my guests, you dine with Me that's certain: Nay, I will not be denied: Most welcome Sir to you: will you walk? Ever, ever welcome. Will you walk sir? will you munch? Sirrah Fub, thou wouldst not think how sore my head is, ever since I had the knock with the Ladle? I believe you: will you feed sir? No more i'th' Ladle. methinks I am pocky melancholy here of late. So I guessed, ever since you knew the Gentlewoman that beat you. Thou sayest true, ever since i'faith. Why she is able to make anybody pocky melancholy. But would you would snap a bit sir. I love her I cannot tell how: i'faith and I were well searched, I think I am little kin to a spaniel, the more I am beaten, the better I affect. Would I were sure of that. Well, she shall hear from me in some Sonnet or Ditty; some rare thing of mine own invention, and that speedily: Let me see to what tune shall I have it? And if you please, let it go to the Punks delight; 'tis your only sweet tune: for women do love the Punks delight. By this hand gramercy; they do indeed, thou canst sing if need be. I can make a scurvy shift: But to say truth I am no good chorister. But canst thou do well and scurvily? After my manner. Would I might be hanged presently, but methinks I am a piece of a Poet already, there's such a whistling in my pate. That's nothing but your conceit sir. Conceit merrily: O that my love were any thing but woman. O that your love were any thing but Common: then might she be.— What might she be. Nay what you will i'faith. I'll to't while 'tis hot, I know I'm in an excellent vein. Prick it quickly then: But Hark you Signior, shall you not need my help? I defy't, It shall be all mine own, I cannot abide, 'tis the scurviest thing to rob others of their wit, good or bad, it shall flow from mine own sweet brain. I believe you'll find the tide turned, 'tis ebbing water there, would I might be begged, as he had like to have been, if his foolery do not vex my discretion, but he gives me means, and I could do little if I could not smile. Well Vallentius, and you be caught i'th' purlieus: and you be not stung for't I'll forswear privacy, and all that belongs too't, I have a Girl, the very spirit of what she was made for, and she were honest, she might crave supremacy of Helen, and make her ride behind. And I love one were she not honest, that's her only fault, she were a Paragon unparalleled, mingle all beauty that our Venice yields, and set herself aside, she would stand peerless, overshine them all, and dim the Artists cunning.
