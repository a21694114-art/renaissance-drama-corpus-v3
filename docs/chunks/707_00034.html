My soul was sunk too low, to look more high, Forgiveness heaven.— alarums. The whips of furies lash me: the foe comes on. And we will meet him, dare confusion, And the worlds mixed poisons, there is a hand That fights for Kings, and under that we'll stand. Whither runs this Friar? To save my wretched life, From th'insolent soldier, threatening the City's spoil. Of what house art thou? Of father Clement's Order, The Capuchins' Subprior: a quick messenger fetched me to be rich Bartervile's confessor, who lies a dying. A ! He does, but I Have come thus far, with so much jeopardy. That could I safely get the keys shore, Nor the priory would I see more. For charity's sake, direct me, and defend me.
