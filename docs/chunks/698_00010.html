Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

We took him for a gull, but now I see he hath had command, he can cheat his Soldiers. I hope sir you will make better respect of your credit, you know your oaths and promise. My promise, if a Citizen had bought a company, he could but keep day with 'em: you must pardon him gentlemen, a fresh Soldier wants seasoning. Salt us, look to't, we shall hardly relish you sir. How? threatens and braves? Forbear, give the poor fellow leave to prate, he pays for't. Good: you are now upon your guard, we shall meet you upon discharge of the watch, and knock you down with a bill of accounts, we shall skeld. Out gull, talk to a commander, a man of war of equal sharing, we have other use for our money then to pay followers. Shall we accost these Ladies, gallants? 'tis the custom of the whole world, the greater thief preys upon the less still: how's this? The happiness of the day befall you I adyes. The night equal the days-happiness, say I. All content both night and day stand to your desires Our desires equal your wishes sir. Your desires are above my performance then. I am drawing on, if my life lie upon her honesty, I am upon the point of giving up already. That Gentleman is very moving. Could you not entreat him stay his pace. And trot in your ring, Lady, if you please. I purpose not to take a courser of your choosing, lest I be jaded sir. You presume the more of your own horsemanship. Heyday, they are riding already, 'sfoot I am like to go post to the Devil for this. Next night, time of my project, if I prove not as hot a shot as came in your quarters since the loss of your virginity, let me suffer the pains of S. Anthony's purgatory. He must necessarily be a man of deeds, he is of so few words. You shall do well to put him to the trial. Without immodesty, may I question the reason you're so heavy sir? Nay then it rings out for me, should the Iew see this, I were as good as speechless: there were but a little gasping between me and the grave. The reason of my heaviness is, that you and I might agree the better: for women love contraries, and you are light I see. How's this? you see me light, true, to be in love With one so far disdainful. What success woman? the Dutchman & I have bartered wares I have made exchange too, sold my liberty, To purchase base ingratitude, I am rejected Voads. My Mistress, Gentlemen, did you see my Mistress? Your business sirrah? You must make provision to entertain two of the richest Pirates ever landed here, one Captain Ward and Franciscus, they have brought a prize in worth three thousand ducats: beside, they sell their prisoners slaves, my master hath engrossed them all.
