Of us? ay, of you; but especially of Cheiromantes. Of me? what? Nothing, but that you are a Cutpurse. I defy mine accusers, and I call honesty itself to witness, that I get my living by my finger's ends. Come, come, leave these protestations: a bad cause is better defended by silence, than argument. Faith 'tis true; let us be friends: and since thy Master Medicus has taught thee to Kill, we'll teach thee to Steal: but honestly, Sanguis, honestly. We three will set upon the next man we meet. I would 'twere Choler that broke my head tother day: o' that condition, I'd stay; but my Master has sent me to Magus. I must begone.
