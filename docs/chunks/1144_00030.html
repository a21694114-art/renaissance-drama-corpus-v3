Yes 'tis he, fate would not suffer two such Noble souls to be so disunited, gentle boy, Thy duty to thy Master will continue, Thy name in story, as the great example Of loyalty in servants. 'Twas the zeal I ought in duty to my Mrs. life, Hath put me on the attempt, which if he pardon, I'm fully satisfied. My joys does with a sudden ecstasy oppress My frail mortality, and I should sink, Wert not for my supporters, my Sabelli, Thou hast restored two lovers to their bliss, Whose gratitude shall pay to thy desert The tribute of their hearts: Dear Madam, now I hope your scrupulous doubts will remain free From any new suspicion. Since I have scaped the danger past, believe i'll avoid The like hereafter; my Lord please you confirm My choice; and let my sister be disposed To good Vitelli, he deserves her. Your wishes are fulfilled, Cosen Bonivet welcome to life Again; you and the General must be friends. Your goodness will pardon my misfortune? And desire to be esteemed your servant. With your leave gentlemen: Madam I have such news to tell you, as will tickle your understanding, to believe the General is married; and more, Signior Doria, Lord Bonivet lives; That's lucky news for you. He's here, good Signior Frangipan. My news has ever the worst luck; I must resolve to leave it off. But sir I have some sudden news to tell you: The thousand Ducats you contracted to pay me, When you could understand the French as perfectly As myself; by all these Lords indifferent judgement is Due on this very minute. This is news indeed; you do not mean to make a gull of me, a figo for a thousand Ducats: as I am a gentleman I know not French for any thing, not for an Ass: good your grace let me not be abused. 'Twas I my Lord who made the bargain with him, The money is not due until my cousin Have French as perfect as himself. He has, i'll bear him witness; for Adorni Speaks not one true French word. How not one true French Word? No not a word, you must disburse. Tutor, i'll tell you news, You made a fool of me, I could abuse him horribly, If I durst for fear of beating. My Lord If he will undertake wars, I'll quit my bargain. I'll pay it triple first, the name of war Has brought an age on me. You two agree that: cousins I rejoice To see this happy period of your loves. Let's back unto the Temple, that the Priest May by his sacred power unite your hearts. Lead to the Temple. GEntlemen, I'll tell you News, the Play is done, And he that writ it betwixt hope and Fear Stands pensive in the Tiring-house to hear Your Censures of his Play: Good Gentlemen Let it be kind, or otherwise his Pen Will write but dully, for he needs must lack If you dispraised the quickening Spirit of Sack To inflame his Genius, which you'll ever find Devoted to you, if your Votes be kind.
