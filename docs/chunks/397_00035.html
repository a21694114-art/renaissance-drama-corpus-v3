Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

My Ladyship hath done my part, my task, line all alone for lack of company, I might have had Prince Richard, Was he here? He went away but now; I have been loved & wood too simply, God rid me of the woman once again, I'll not be tempted so for all the world, Come, will you to your chamber and uncase? Nay keep my habit yet a little while, Old Faukenbridge is almost at the gate, I met him at Black heath just at the Hermits, And taking me to be a Merchant's wife, Fell mightily in love, gave me his ring, Made me protest that I would meet him here. I told him of his Lady, O tut quoth he, I'll shake her up, i'll pack her out of sight, He comes kind Robin Hood, hold up the jest. God's mary knave, how long hath she been here? Sir she came but even in afore you. A cunning quean, a very cunning quean, Go to your business Block, i'll meet with her. Ah old Muttonmounger I believe here's work towards. Do not believe her Mall, do not believe her: I only spoke a word or two in jest, But would not for the world have been so mad, Do not believe her Mall, do not believe her: What should I not believe? what do you mean? Why good Sir Richard, let me speak with you, Alas will you undo me? will you shame me? Is this your promise? came I here for this? To be a laughing stock unto your Lady How now Sir Richard, what's the matter there? I'll talk with you anon, come hither woman? Didst not tell my wife what match we made:? I tell your wife? think ye I am such a beast? Now God forgive ye, I am quite undone. Peace duck, peace duck, I warrant all is well. What's the matter? I pray ye sir Richard tell me? Mary Mall thus, about some twelve months since, Your brother Gloster, that mad prodigal, Caused me to pass my word unto her husband, For some two thousand pound: or more perchance, No matter what it is, you shall not know, Nay ye shall never ask to know. And what of this? Mary the man's decayed, And I believe a little thing would please her; A very little thing, a thing of nothing. Go in good Mall, and leave us two alone, I'll deal with ye as simply as I can. Fox look about ye, ye are caught i'faith. Deal with her simply, o ho; what kind of dealing? Can ye not deal with her and I be by? Mary a God, what are ye jealous? Ye teach me what to do: in, get you in. O I have heard Prince Richard was your guest, How dealt you than? In get you in I say, Must I take care about your brother's debts, And you stand crossing me, in, or i'll send you in. Ha sirrah, you'll be master, you'll wear the yellow, You'll be an overseer: mary shall ye.
