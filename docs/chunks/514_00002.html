What would ye ha' me do? Why do nothing, be like a gentleman, be idle the curse of man is labour. Wipe thy bum with testons, & make Ducks and Drakes with shillings: What Eastward hoe. Wilt thou cry, what is't ye lack? stand with a bare pate, and a dropping nose, under a wooden penthouse, and art a gentleman? wilt thou bear Tankards, and mayst bear Aims? be ruled, turn gallant, Eastward hoe, ta lyre, lyre, ro., Who calls Ieronimo? speak here I am: gods so, how like a sheep thou look'st, a my conscience some cowherd begot thee, thou Goulding of Goulding-hall, ha boy? Go, ye are a prodigal coxcomb, I a cowherd's son, because I turn not a drunken whorehunting rakehell like thyself? Rakehell? rakehell? Pish, in soft terms ye are a cowardly bragging boy. I'll ha' you whipped. Whipped, that's good i'faith, untruss me? No, thou wilt undo thyself. Alas I behold thee with pity, not with anger; thou common shot-clog, gull of all companies: methinks I see thee already walking in Moore fields without a Cloak, with half a Hat, without a band, a Doublet with three Buttons, without a girdle, a hose with one point and no Garter, with a cudgel under thine arm borrowing and begging three pence. Nay 'slife, take this and take all: as I am a Gentleman borne, I'll be drunk, grow valiant, and beat thee. Go thou most madly vain, whom nothing can recover but that which reclaims Atheists, and makes great persons sometimes religious: Calamity. As for my place and life thus I have read: whate'er some vainer youth may term disgrace, The gain of honest pains is never base: From trades from arts, from valour honour springs, These three are founts of gentry, yea of Kings. For the passion of patience, look if sir Petronel approach; that sweet, that fine, that delicate, that— for loves sake tell me if he come. O sister Mill, though my father be a low capped tradesman, yet I must be a Lady: and I praise God my mother must call me Medam, (does he come?) off with this gown for shames sakes, off with this gown: let not my Knight take me in the City cut in any hand: tear't, pax on't (does he come) tear't of. Thus whilst she sleeps I sorrow, for her sake, etc. Lord sister, with what an immodest impatiency and disgraceful scorn, do you put off your City tier: I am sorry to think you imagine to right yourself, in wronging that which hath made both you and us.
