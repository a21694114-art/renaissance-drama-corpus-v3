Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Room here, we must come into the court within. What damned fiends are those dare make this noise? A jury of Brokers impaneled, and deeply sworn, To pass on all the villains in hell. evil-conscience be their keeper. Look to the jury: evil-conscience look to the Brokers. Now proceed. Stay, let the King of Ghosts have first a view Of those who are doomed to pains horrid, but new. Then produce those who came to your prison untried. Peace there. Here's one, hell's tortures does deride.— Ravillac. Arraign me, rend me piece-meal, i'll confess nothing. Peace, thou shalt ball thy throat out. Merciless hangmen! to tyrannize over so brave a Roman spirit. Ho, ho, what country devil is this? Thine own. A french. The eagerest blood hound that ere came from thence; Is there a King to be murdered, whilst he does stand Colossus-like, supporting a whole land, And when by his fall that Land most fears a wrack, Send forth this devil; his name is Ravillac. Ravillac: I am Ravillac, that laughs at tortures, spurns at death, defies all mercy: Gibbets, racks, fires, pincers, scalding oil, wild-horses, I spit in the face of all. Peace. No: were my tongue torn out with burning flesh-hooks, Fame's 1●00. tongues shall thunder out Rauilla's name, extol it, eternize it, 〈◊〉 it! canonize it: oh! Down with this devil toth' dung on, there let him howl. World's shall applaud my Act, and crown my soul. Another. Come, you lean dog.— One drop, a bit. What's he? what starvelings this? One that lacks a medicine for hunger: I am fallen away. From heaven. Toth' common jail with him. He must feed on beggaries basket: leave bawling serra. Shall I be undone for a little drink. No, thart undone for drinking. Starve him away— What was he when he lived. A prodigal: Who (in one year,) spent on whores, fools and slaves, An Army's maintenance, now begs for crumbs, and raves To see his sumptuous buildings, pastures, woods, That stood in uplands, drowned in Rhenish floods. Is here all? All! no, the arch-hellhound's here. What Peter Goner's this? Speak softly, within an inch of giving fire, within an inch. Had all thy grey devils in their highest lust sat, T' have littered furies, they could not have begot. One to match this: i'th' dark he groped damnation, Now, now. digged cellars to find where hell stood and has found it There was but one villainy unborn, and he crowned it. So: all the billets lie close; glorious bonfire? pontifical bonfire; brave heads to contrive this, gallant souls to conspire in't, resolute hand to seal this with my blood, through fire, through flint; ha, ha, ha, whither fly myself to heaven, friends to honour, none to the halter, enemies to massacre, ha ha, dismal tragical Comedy now? What does he? As he thinks, giving fire to powder; near in any land could devils have found, such walks, As he was beating out.
