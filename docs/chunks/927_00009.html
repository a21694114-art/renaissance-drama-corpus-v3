Think on the frailty of The flesh; and be not long unmerciful— Sister! Madam! Away; I come, I come. The Milan Count (my Suitor) is arrived. Signior Dandolo! Why 'tis yet a month Ere he should meet your expectation here. 'tis he Madam, and such a he as must Be only likened to himself. More rich, And gaudy than the East. So liberal too, That you would think he Natures Steward were. He gives amongst my slaves, as if unto Ambassadors he did cast Donatives. Milk him (my Charintha) oft I have preached Unto thine Ear a sovereignty o'er man. Take all he gives, it's princely fashion now. Could but thy acceptation beggar him, Thy little wit were ever magnified. You know I am o'th' wits, I have been sworn To beggar Suitors, and jeer my husband. Which is the Lady that I come to woo? My name's Charintha— Present me thy Lips! I say advance— You are not understood— My Lord, I scarcely own desert enough T'entice this jewel from your Cabinet. A Stone, a Stone! the thing grows, I've enough— So free? I am Charintha's Sister Sir— It may be so. A fine house, fine Town too!
