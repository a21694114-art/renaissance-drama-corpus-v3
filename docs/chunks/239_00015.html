The dame of the house? She might have said the beldame, for her face and years, and attire. Good mother tell us, if by your cunning you can, what shall become of my brother and me. Let me see your hands, and look on me steadfastly with your eyes. You shall be married to morrow hand in hand, by the laws of good nature & the land, your parents shall be glad, & give you their land, you shall each of you displace a fool, & both together must relieve a fool. If this be not true call me old fool. This is my sister, marry we cannot: our parents are poor and have no land to give us, each of us is a fool to come for counsel to such an old fool. These doggerel rhymes and obscure words, coming out of the mouth of such a weather-beaten witch, are thought divinations of some holy spirit, being but dreams of decayed brains; for mine own part, I would thou mightest sit on that stool till he & I marry by law. I say Mother Bombie never speaks but once, and yet never spoke untruth once. Come brother, let us to our poor home, this is our comfort, to bewray our passions, since we cannot enjoy our love. Content sweet sister, and learn of me hereafter, that these old saws of such old hags, are but false fires to lead one out of a plain path into a deep pit. Ingenium quondam fuerat pretiotius auro, the time was wherein wit would work like wax, and crock up gold like honey. At nunc barbary est grandis habere nihil, but now wit and honesty buy nothing in the market. What Risio, how spedst thou after thy potting? Nay, my master rung all in the tavern, & thrust all out in the house. But how spedst thou? ay, it were adays dayes work to discourse it, he spoke nothing but sentences, but they were vengible long ones, for when one word was out, he made pause of a quarter long till he spoke another. Why what did he in all that time? Break interjections like wind, as eho, ho, to. And what thou? Answer him in his own language, as evax, vah, hui. These were conjunctions rather than interjections. But what of the plot? As we concluded I told him that I understood that Silena was very wise, and could sing exceedingly, that my device was, seeing Accius his son a proper youth, & could also sing sweetly, that he should come in the nick when she was singing and answer her. Excellent. Then he asked how it should be devised that she might come abroad, I told that was cast already by my means, than the song being ended, and they seeing one another, noting the apparel, and marking the personages, he should call in his son for fear he should overreach his speech.
