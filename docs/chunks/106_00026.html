WHen she that rules the rolling wheel of chance, Doth turn aside her angry frowning face, On him, whom erst she deigned to advance, She never leaves to galled him with disgrace, T tosse and turne his state in euery place, Till at the last she hurl him from on high And yield him subject unto misery: And as the branch that from the root is reft, He never wins like life to that he left: Yea though he do, yet can no taste of joy Compare with pangs that passed in his annoy.
