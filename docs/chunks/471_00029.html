Here's a person would speak with you Sir. Hah! A parson sir would speak with you. Vicar? Vicar? no sir, has too good a face to be a Vicar yet, a youth, a very youth. What youth? of man or woman? lock the doors. If it be a woman, marrow-bones and Potato pies keep me for meddling with her, for the thing has got the breeches, 'tis a male-varlet sure my Lord, for a woman's tailor ne'er measured him.
