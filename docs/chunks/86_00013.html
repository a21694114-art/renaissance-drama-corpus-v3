Ah Amorous Lady of beautiful face, Thou art heartily welcome into this place, My heart is inclined to thee lady Treasure, My love is insatiate, it keepeth no measure. It is I master Lust that will you advance, Treasure, it is that things doth enhance Upon me set your whole affection and lust, And pass not a point for the ways of the just, Treasure is a pleasure, bear that in mind, Both trusty and true ye shall me always find. As trusty as is a quick eel by the tail, What Lady Treasure welcome without fail, To be better acquainted with you once I trust, But I dare not in the presence of my master Lust, ye are welcome sir heartily, what be of good courage: Drawer, let us have a pint of white wine & borage. Wherefore I pray thee tell, Marry me think you are not well.
