But shoals of Sharks eat up the Fish at Sea. Brave pitchy villains there. Yet you playing here. No, No; most awful Pluto. Were you good Hellhounds, every day should be A Symon-and-Iude, to crown our board with Feasts A black-eyed-soul each minute: were you honest devil▪ Each officer in hell should have at least, A brace of whores to his breakfast: above us dwell, Devils braver and more subtle than in Hell. we'll fill thy palace with them. I'll try that: go: Rufman, take instantly a courtier's shape O any country: choose thine owne disguize And return swiftly. Yes. Shackle-soul wear thou A friars grave habit. Well. Grumshall walk thou In treble-ruffs like a Merchant. So: 'tis done. The bars of our litigious Courts had wont To crack with thronging pleaders, whose loud din Shook the infernal hell, as if 't had been An earthquake bursting from the deep Abyss, Or else Ioues thunder, thrown at the head of Dis (The God of gold,) for hiding it below, Thereby to tempt churls hither. Nor did we know What a Vacation meant: continual term fattened hell's Lawyers, and shall so again. Here. Here. Command us. Fly into the world: As you're in shapes transformed be so in name, For men are our sides only: be you the same; Have thee to Naples, (Rufman), thou shalt find A Prince there (newly crowned,) apply inclined de To any bendings▪ least his youthful brows Reach at Stars only, weigh down his loftiest boughs With leaden plomers poison his best thoughts with taste things most sensual; if the heart once waste The body feels consumption; good or bad kings Breed Subjects like them: clear streams flow from clear springs. Turn therefore Naples to a puddle: with a civil Much promising face, and well oiled play the court devil. I'll do't in bravery: if as deep as hell, Thy large ears hear a Land curse me, my part's played well. Fly Shackle-soul. Whither? To the friary, Best-famed in Naples for strict orders: throw What nets thou seest can catch them: Amongst 'em sow Seeds of contention, or whatever sin They most abhor, sweat thou to bring that in. A wolf in lamb skin leaps into the rout, Bell, book, or candle cannot curse me out: I'll curse faster than they. Do: Here. Be thou a city-devil, make thy hands O Harpyes clawes, which being on courtiers lāds Once fastened, ne'er let loose, the Merchant play, And on the Burse, see thou thy flag display. Of politic bankruptness: train up as many To fright under it, as thou canst, for now's not any That break, (they'll break their necks first) if, beside Thou canst not through the whole city meet with pride, Riot, lechery, envy, Avarice, and such stuff, Bring 'em all-in coached, the gares are wide enough. The spirit of gold instruct thee: hence all. Fly.
