whose's that with a pox? Who art thou with a pestilence. Why I am Dick Coomes? What have I found thee Dick? nay then I am for ye Dick, Where are ye Dick? What can I tell where I am? Can ye not tell, come, come ye weight on your mistress well, come on your ways, I have sought you till I am weary, and called ye till I am hoarse, good Lord what a jaunt I have had this night, heigh ho? Is't you mistress that came over me, 'sblood 'twere a good deed to come over you for this night's work, I cannot afford all this pains for an angel I tell ye true a kiss were not cast away upon a good fellow, that hath deserved more that way then a kiss, if your kindness would afford it him, What shall I have it mistress? Fie, fie, I must not kiss my man. Nay, nay, near stand, shall I, shall I, nobody sees, say but I shall, and i'll smack ye soundly i'faith. Away bawdy man, in truth I'll tell your master. My master, go to, near tell me of my master, he may pray for them that may, he is past it, and for mine own part, I can do somewhat that way I thank God, I am not now to learn, and 'tis your part to have your whole desire. Fie, fie, I am ashamed of you, would you tempt your mistress to lewdness. To lewdness, no by my troth there's no such matter in't, it is for kindness, & by my troth if you like my gentle offer, you shall have what courteously I can afford ye. Shall I indeed Dick? i'faith, if I thought nobody would see. Tush, fear not that, 'swounds they must have Cats eyes then, Then kiss me Dick. A kind wench i'faith, where are ye mistress?
