How! Is the Princess so in love with him? Indeed they say she is. Come, and eat your meat, you shall Go to bed, I know you ate weary. Sir, I cannot eat, I had rather sleep, Come then, I'll show thee to a Bed. No Sir, I'll lie upon the Rushes, I never use To lie with anybody, and I am sure Here in this house there are not many beds. Come, thou shalt lie alone; There are two beds, we two will lie together. Please Sir to leave me here, I'll go to bed. No child, I'll help thee. If he should see my breasts, I am undone; I will keep on my doublet. Go to bed sweet child, we'll leave thee. What news at Court? Sad news believe me. Why, must brave Lysander suffer today? The King hath sworn to have his head off ere Sunset. The Kingdom will be poor in such a loss, For he leaves none behind him worth his equal. ay, but is't not strange the King should grace That villain Iacomo that did betray him? His extreme love unto the Duke makes him Love Iacomo, who doth profess that he did not Discover Lysander in hope of gain; but only Out of love to the Duke's memory. At one o'th' clock he is to suffer, let us be there betimes and get a place near the Scaffold to hear his last words. How black and sorrowful this day looks! This day, in which Lysander is to suffer: Noble Lysander, to whom my Child and I Are so much bound; and yet he is the cause Of both our ruins; or rather I am cause: It was my ambition to have a Duke My Son in Law: no, it was my Clarinda's Beauty bred all this mischief, and it was The Heavens that gave Beauty to her: Why did they then not bless that gift in her, But turn it to her curse? Peace wretched man And argue not with those high powers, But wait their pleasure, and pray for their assistance, Who can yet change this scene of blood into— A scene of joy, and back return thee thy Clarinda. If't please your Lordship, my young Lady Is returned and gone again. How! She hath been in the house this hour as the maids tell me, hath changed her clothes, and's newly stolen out at the back-gate, and gone toward Lysander's prison; two of my fellows are gone after her, and I came back to tell your Lordship. And does the king's cruel resolution hold still? O Madam yes, my poor Brother must die today. And wilt not thou die with him: speak Mariana. madam, I could wish that I might not outlive him. Why sayst thou thou couldst wish, hast thou not hands? Or dost thou want a knife? if so, yet there's many ways to die. madam, how strangely do you talk.
