What dare they do, their counsellors who are? Advance their Empress old, subvert the new: And grant her, brothers beds, as is her due. Which Poppy now, with hole consent doth hold? Yea that unbridled rage in breast uprold, Sets them agog, and makes them wondrous wood. Some parts with ropes sure tide, they trail them forth, Which spurned with dirty feet, as though nought worth With filthy stinking mire, they it all bewray. And with their deeds their talk doth jump agree, Which mine amazed mind, thinks true to be.
