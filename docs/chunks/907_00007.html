That trumpet could speak Persian well, I can hardly hit upon them in the original. Varletto, polmone, manigoldo. You mask unknown, unseen. Descend I say to the apprehension of the base vulgar. Give us them in translation. Indoctrinate of young Nobility. Accomplisher of king's Courts, chief engineer of cap and knee. clock-keeper of nod and shrugge, and finally, engrosser of all unsailable, available addresses, garbs, faces, graces in all places. Yet gooder and gooder. O admirable! This man is for us to the heavens. It is well, somewhat toward the superlative of my worth and profession But Imp. I like not that word finally. When you rehearse me before the Lady Muses, they will think we are at the wall, that thou hast told all my virtues, and offices. I tell thee, not thou, nor the loud long tongue blab fame, can give the inventory of my perfections. Thinkest thou with the small wherry boat of thy short memory, to arrive at the Terra incognita of the virtues and endowments of mighty Captain Compliment? O eloquence. I see now that Tully and Ovid are but coarse I tell thee, and thou shalt find, that tittle tattle Tully is but brown bread, if he be laid near our Marchpane. Ergo therefore my nimble Imp. take away that padlocke finally, and in stead of the aforesaid, adde to the agge end of my Catalogue, that endlesse chaine which the vulgar Gramatians call e cra. Indeed Master I have heard you say, that you would have your title like the great Western monarch's title, with a long tail behind it. True: And therefore you know, the motto which I subscribe in banner is plus vltra. My sweet friend, Master Implement, you told me that you would speak— I marry, and so I do. For me to the mighty man. All you have yet said to him, is but in the vocative case. And that case makes no sentence, as I have learned when I was in the second form. You know I gave you. What did you give me? Nothing but a dozen of rotten silk points. You must tag them better ere I the up your request. I le steal my mother's Peacock, and bring it you. A Peacock? That's well. You may make us latter by it; but you cannot make us prouder. What talk you of a Peacock? Sir, this young gentleman presents you with a Peacock. It has a tail. he has a small request to your worship. I dare as well he hanged as steal the Peacock. My mother loves it so well, and feeds it every day, and pricks the feathers of it in her sanne. Where is this Peacock? What shall I dee? I have none. I mistook a Peacock for a Woodcock. I have never a Woodcock neither. Sir he would fain present you with a Woodcock every day. His suit is to be your scholar for an hour in a day. He would dedicate himself to fine fashions and quaint quallicoms.
