Nay then my admirations at an end. I remember nobody would know me last time. But these are a couple of honest fellows, and yet they serve a Lord, if anybody have use of their eyes these will. By your leave Gentlemen, did you see the young Lord Frederick. where's he that asked that question? 'Sfoot I cannot see him, yet heard him plain, The invisible voice is come again, I believe so, People are blind, that's certain—— look how they stare— I do not think there was ever any such thing in the world as an eye, a seeing eye. I know tailor's needles have eyes, and Cheeses, but a discerning eye, that's the eye I would encounter with. It did me more good than my dinner, I protest, to see her transported to prison again. And so it did me I protest, for her brother's sake my Lord Frederick Fadoudle. I wonder he is not taken yet, he'll Fadoudle at the Gallows, I believe whensoe'er he is, for I told the Duke what a saucy companion he was. I will requite your kindness instantly, How now, what's that? Will you too Coxcombs never be uncoupled, Who was that my Lord? Nay, I know not: Who was that then? Who was that said, who was that? Marry it was I Sir, You, who are you? One that'll'le bestow a little pains with you, Pray knock there no more my friend, that's the back gate, your best go about. I will do so, and wipe your Noses for you. I'd rather you would let it alone, unless you had a finer handkerchief; 'tis the spirit of some Porter, and wipes her with his Frock. 'Zounds I'll not endure this: I'll draw first. And so will I. Why do you not draw? Hark, the voice asked why we do not draw. ay, I heard it, but I'll be hanged before I'll draw for that trick. And so will I to— See yonder's a company of Gentlemen, let's fly to them for succour: Are you walking. Yes Sir. we'd be glad of your company. here's an invisible voice follows us in the likeness of I know not what- And plays upon us like a Flute backward, and forward. We heard a voice indeed but felt no touch of any thing. No, It may be you are valiant and would strike again, but we are tender hearted, and ten to one, but it knows as much. Why if you'll walk our way, we will guard you. Yes sir, your way is ours now however. Well I am lost I see, there's no hope that ever I shall be seen again of mortals; I walk i'the clouds; But that the other two before these, and before them others, could not perceive me, I should think I had with beating made the last pair cast a mist before their own eyes, Iubia and my Lord of mischief with his two Faces Winter and Summer. Sweet Iulia kiss me.
