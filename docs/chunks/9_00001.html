Lord I will in the world go renover all And truly out search both great and small Everyman I will be set that liveth beastly Out of god's laws/ and dreadeth not folly He that loveth riches I will strike with my dart His sight to blind/ and from heaven depart Except that alms deeds be his good friend In hell for to dwell/ world without end Loo/ yonder I see everyman walking Full little he thinketh on my coming His mind is on fleshly lusts/ and his treasure And great pain/ it shall cause him to endure Before the lord heaven king Everyman/ stand still/ whether art that going Thus gaily Hast thou thy maker forget Why askest thou wouldest thou were ye sir I will show you In great hast I am send to the From god out of his majesty What/ send to me ye certainly Though thou have forget him here He thinketh on the in the heavenly spear As or we depart thou shall know What desireth god of me That shall I show the A reckoning he will needs have Without longer respite To give a reckoning longer leisure I crave This blind matter trubleth my wit On the thou must take a long journey Therefore thy book of count with the thou bring For turn again thou can not by no way And look thou be sure of thy reckoning For before god shalt thou answer and show Thy many bad deeds and good but a few How thou hast speed thy life and in what wise Before the chief lord of paradise Have a do that we were in that way For were thou well thou shalt make none attournay Full uneedy I am such reckoning to give I know the not what messenger art thou I am death that no man dreadeth For every man I rest and none spareth For it is god's commandment That all to me should be obedient O death thou cummest what I had the leeft in mind In thy power it lieth me to save yet of my good will I give the if ye will be kind ye a thousand pound shalt thou have And defer this matter till another day Every man it may not be by no way I set not by gold silver nor richesse Ne by pope/ emperor/ king/ duke/ ne princes For and I would receive geftes great All the world I might get All my custom is clean contrary I give the no respite come hens and not tarry Alas shall I have no longer respite I may say death giveth no warning To think on the it maketh my heart seek For all unready is my book of reckoning But .xii. year and I might have a biding My counting book I would make so clear That my reckoning I should not need to fere Wherefore death I pray the for god's mercy Spare me till I be provided of remedy
