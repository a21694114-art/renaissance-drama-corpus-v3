Why then comes she not now to Apollo's Court at this solemn assembly, to lay in her claim for it? Siren tells me, she had done so, but that she knew not of the set day, and now resideth in one of the fortunate Islands in the Atlantic sea far oft from Thessaly. And besides all this, she is invited to a feast of four or five gods by Mercury into Ethiopia. Such draff of silens by thy feast.
