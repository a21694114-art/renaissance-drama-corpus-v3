Here they are, I'll not trouble you. I will leave this Gentleman; He is my best friend Ladies. You oblige us more to respect him. I hope you will be just, At my return, when I declare myself. Sir, you sha'not need to urge it. I am your honourer. Now Thump, I am resolved to speak to her, whatsoever Come on't; hum, hum. What another suitor? I were best lock up my daughters, they'll be smothered with Gentlemen: Sir Gervace you are a welcome man. You shall see, if I do not speak to her, now Now, and to the purpose, in spite of the devil. Stroke me o'th' back. There's something in my head, and 'twere out. I hope you do not mean your brains. Would somebody loved me, but as well, as I loved Somebody. Now 'tis out. La you there, I think I talked to her. But in my opinion you were quickly daunted. Was I so? well mark me now, now, I will wink As hard as I can, and then I'm sure she cannot put me out. Beauty— How now? what means this? Let me but kiss thy hand, and tell How much I love thee; I know very well Thou art a Phoenix, beauteous and bright, And dost burn every man for thy delight Thy eyes are Sun and Moon, not to be matched. This was intended for you sister. Thy hair is fine as gold, thy chin is hatched With silver, needs must brazen be my face, That cannot come into thee with a grace. Before or after meat. Sweet, hear me out. But this I know, I am thy faithful Lover, Oh quench my fire. Or else the pot runs over. Y'are in the right: Accept me for thy servant, not thy foe, For I do love thee better than any man in the whole world can do. Come kiss me then, and open thy fair eyen. With all my heart. Ha, ha. Gentlemen, rest you merry; Thump, By this hand I'll be drunk presently. And you had taken my counsel, you had done so Before you came hither: Wine is armour of proof. Ladies I am sorry, this rude fellow has Abused your ear, with harsh, and untuned numbers. Your verses have a tune belike I came a purpose, to present a copy Of verses that should make your genius stand O'th' tiptoe, list to me, and grow immortal. We shall be troubled now. If please you Lady we'll walk aside, I have something of more weight I would impart. I'll leave him too.
