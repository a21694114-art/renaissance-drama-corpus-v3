Then say when thou art dead, thou wert offered conditions for thy life: Cam, thou shalt feed, and feed high Chameleon,— let me see;— come 'tis my foolish nature to ha' compassion o'thee, I know thouart sorry, shalt only confess thyself a rascal under thy hand then, and stay my in ended revenge which else would ha' been immortal. Let me consider. Oho Cam.— Both cowards, we shall have no skirmish. Now I think on't, what if my man Iasper, should be valiant and kill Lodam— umh? what pickle were I in: worse-worse, he'll run away, I shall be taken and hanged for the Conspiracy. Ah— Iasper, rogue that I was, where were my brains to challenge him— he won't hear— a stubborn knave, he looks as if he meant to kill: ah Iasper. I ha' seen a dog look like him, that has drawn a Wicker bottle, rattling about the streets, and leering on both sides, where to get a quiet corner to bite his tail off. I do imagine myself apprehended already: now the Constable is carrying me to Newgate— now, now.
