Say on my sweet bully, mine Host will attend thee, speak roundly to the purpose and welcome my bully. Marry thus: there are are great revels & shows prepared to beautify the nuptials of Lentulus and Tully, in which the Citizens have the least share, now would but you and some others that I shall collect, join hands with me in some quaint jest, Our show shall deserve grace, and brave the rest. I have thee brave spirit, thou'rt of the true seed of Troy, let's be merry and wise, merry hearts live long mine Host, my brave Host with his neighbour Cornutus shall be two of the Maskers, and the Morris shall be danced, Not so mine Host, I dare not do so, 'twill destemper my wife, my house will be unquiet, mum, mum, I know the world well enough. Thou shalt go says mine Host, merry hearts live long, welcome bully, mine Host shall make one, so shall my Cornutus, for if I say the word the mouse shall be dun. Save ye mine Host, here's a parcel of Corn was directed to be delivered at your house. What ware my little Atlas, what ware is it? I know not, but i'm sure 'tis as heavy as a horse and— I think 'tis a barrel of oil, for it spurged at my back. It was oil, for I drew the Tap. What Bos, what mak'st thou here? Oh charadeum soboles magnum bovis increm entum! Bos art there there? As sure as you are there signor. Bos, will ye not forsake your cabin? Oh sir, he that has not a tiled house must be glad of a thatched house: may I crave a suit of you signor? What suit Bos? What you please, beggars must not choose. Bos is grown mystical, he's too dark. I speak hebrew indeed like Adam and Eve, before they fell to spinning: not a rag. What, naked Bos? As ye see, will ye hear my suit signor? Drunk & his clothes stolen, what thief would do it? Any thief sir, but no true man. Well Bos, to obtain a suit at my hands, and to do some penance for your fault, you shall here maintain an argument in the defence of drunkenness: mine Host shall hear it i'll be your oppoment, Acutus moderator: wilt thou do it. A mad merry prig, all good spirits, wilt thou do it Bos? I'll do't. Seat ye, here's my place, now Bos propound. Drunkenness is a virtue. Your proof. Good drink is full of virtue, Now full of good drink is drunk, Erge, to be drunk is to be virtuous. I deny it, good drink is full of vice, Drink takes away the senses, Man that is senseless is vicious, Ergo, good drink is full of vice. I deny it still, good drink makes good blood, Good blood needs no Barber, Ergo, 'tis good to drink good drink.
