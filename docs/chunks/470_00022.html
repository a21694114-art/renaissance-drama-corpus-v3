Why me thought I pleased you with singing, and then I dreamt you gave me that short sword. Prettily begged: hold thee, i'll prove thy dream true, take't. My duty: but still I dreamt on my Lord, and me thought, and 't shall please your excellency, you would needs out of your royal bounty give me that jewel in your hat. O thou didst but dream boy, do not believe it, dreams prove not always true, they may hold in a short sword, but not in a jewel. But now sir, you dreamt you had pleased me with singing, make that true as I have made the other. Faith my Lord, I did but dream, and dreams you say prove not always true: they may hold in a good sword, but not in a good song: the truth is, I ha' lost my voice. Lost thy voice, how? With dreaming faith, but here's a couple of Sirenical rascals shall enchant ye: what shall they sing my good Lord?
