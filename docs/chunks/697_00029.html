So that doors fast, and they are busied. About their charge: on with this rob of mine, This Vizard and this Cap; help me a little. At first Change I must tell her who I am? Right, 'tis agreed, I (leading of the Mask,) Should dance with Bellafront. And at the second, I come away with her, & leave them dancing, and shall find you at the back door. The rest That follows, is digested in my breast. What would you do? Stand back, Unless you can eat Torches. Come, come, away for shame. 'tis such a tedious rascal. So ha wi'ye. Thou hast well fitted 'em, though thou mad'st 'em stay. I forbid any man to mend 'em Sir, good night unto your worship. Alas Sir, I have another to set forth This very night: By your leave my Masters. By your leave, by your leave, you'll let a man go out? Now go with me, and let all in that will. I am your Scudmore. Ha? By heaven I am, Be ruled by me in all things. even to death. 'sfoot did you not know me by my purse? I should ne'er have known you by that, for you wear it on your head, and other folks in their pockets. Which is my Lord I pray? The second man Young Nevill leads. And where's Sir. Abraham? He with the terrible visage. Now out upon him to disfigure himself so, And 'twere not for my bottle, I should swoon. Good very good. But where's the Bride and Nevill? Ha. Ware tricks. Oh, there they come, it was their parts to do so This Nevill, this is Scudmore. How? But here's my Lady, No my Gentlewoman. zounds Treason, I smell powder. In short know, that I am married to this Gent. To whom I was contracted long ago: This Priest the inviolable knot hath tied, What ease I find being un-ladified. What Riddle's this? Ware the last Statute of two Husbands. Bellafront, pish. This is the very Priest that married me, Is it not Sister? No. Lord bless us here is conjuring, Lend me your Aquavitae bottle good Mother. Heyday, the world's turned up side down, I have heard and seen two or three Benefices to one Priest, or more, But two Priests to one Benefice, ne'er before. Married not you the Earl? Bonafide, no. You did then? Yes. I have the Privilege then. Right, you were married first. Sir Iohn you dote, This is a Devil in a parson's coat. A pretty Emblem. Who married her, or would have caused her marry To any man but this, no better was, Let circumstances be examined, Yet here's one more, and now I hope you all, Perceive my marrying not Cannon call. Nevill, whoop. Heart, what a deal a Knavery a priest's cloak can hide, if it be not one of the honestest friendliest cozenages that ere I saw, I am no Lord. Life, I am not married then in earnest. So Mistress Kate, I kept you for myself.
