A very handsome woman, I could love her, Did I but know her portion. Mistress welcome. What's in that house is yours? Sir Timothy, You have much graced me by the sweet acquaintance Of this good gentlewoman. Pray Cosen know her; She's worthy your endearment. I shall be proud To do you service. I most fortunate To be esteemed your creature. Very right she's a poor niece of mine, yet she can speak you May perceive or see. Life Thorowgood with young Holdfast, pray heaven my folly Has not undone me. You'll please to pardon Our rude intention sir, we have some business. Please you declare't. This gentleman and myself, Come to inform you that this spark my Cosen, Is son and heir to sir Geoffrey Holdfast, And since I hear you have disposed your daughter To that good knight, I in his father's name, Desire your niece should be his wife. Pray Sir speak In your own cause he needs no advocate. I've been abused, In this Sir Geoffrey's son the scholar? The very same sir. I am the spark sir. Valentine, i'th' name Of madness: man why in this shape? Valentine, Ha, ha, ha. Very right, my niece is Valentine. And how is't bully, hast not found these girls Of a hot appetite, how often ha? Has my Landlady Provided me a cullis, life my back Does needs a swathband. What means this gentleman? Nothing sir, But to inform you what strange things your niece, And daughter and, nay never blush he has Performed it better than your Uncle's foreman. I know he has. Timothy this abuse must not be thus put up, Did not you say I was your Niece. Very right, but it was Valentine. He has been here all night too Cosen we are basely betrayed. Take courage. Do you think sir, my Cosen shall mix with such Stale ware that keep their gamesters in their chambers. Or this knight have Valentine's reversions? Very right, I scorn it. Keep them. they I serve to set up some twice Broken Merchant, or undone Linen-draper, come away Valentine, thou hast made a brave discovery. Farewell, My witty virgins, you are paid now. I'll be revenged for this, and if it cost me Half my estate Formal send post, for sir Geoffrey, The whole town shall know of this abuse. I'll make you fast enough. THey are both sparks, that's certain, if e'er I take them in my watch, I'll make them stoop Under my staff of office, Mistress Clare, Though I'm a Citizen, and by my charter, Am not allowed much wit, as being free Oath Linendrapers, and a man in office, Yet if my counsel, if you please to follow it, Do not revenge you on these saucy mad caps, May taking up of Holland at dear rates, Be quite abjured by courtiers: and I canvased Out of authority, how's that now?
