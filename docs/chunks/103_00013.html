Yea, but amongst honest men it is more worth than gold. Yea marry sir, but where be they? but will you needs have me to tell it unto you? Yea I pray thee if it any thing appertain to me. Yes it is of you, and I would gladly tell it you, because I would not have such a man of worship so scorned by a villain ribald. I pray thee tell me then. I will tell you so that you will swear never to tell it to Pasiphilo, to my master, nor to any other body. Surely it is some toy devised to get some money of him. I think I have a book here. If he knew him as well as I, he would never go about it, for he may as soon get one of his teeth from his jaws with a pair of pinchers, as a penny out of his purse with such a conceit. Here is a letter will serve the turn: I swear to thee by the contents hereof never to disclose it to any man. I will tell you, I am sorry to see how Pasiphilo doth abuse you, persuading you that always he laboureth for you, where in deed, he lieth on my master continually, as it were with tooth and nail for a stranger, a scholar, borne in Sicilia they call him Roscus or arse-kiss, he hath a mad name I can never hit upon it. And thou reckonest it as madly: is it not Erostrato? That same, I should never have remembered it: & the villain speaketh all the evil of you that can be devised. To whom? To my master, yea and to Polynesta herself sometimes. Is it possible, Ah slave, and what saith he? More evil than I can imagine: that you are the miserablest and most niggardly man that ever was. Sayeth Pasiphilo so by me? And that as often as he cometh to your house, he is like to die for hunger, you fare so well. That the devil take him else. And that you are the testiest man, and most divers to please in the whole world, so that he cannot please you unless he should even kill himself with continual pain. O devilish tongue. Furthermore, that you cough continually and spit, so that a dog cannot abide it. I never spit nor cough more than thus, who, who, and that but since I caught this murr, but who is free from it? You say true sir, yet further he sayth, your arm holes stink, your feet worse than they, and your breath worst of all. If I quite him not for this gear. And that you are bursen in the cods. O villain, he lieth, and if I were not in the street thou shouldest see them.
