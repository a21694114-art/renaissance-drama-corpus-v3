And all that spot which stains your same, by Fortune came to pass. This heinous Act, this frantic coil you can no wise excuse▪ But wittingly and willingly sound counsel ye refuse. It resteth free within your choice: of these take which ye list: If peace delight for mother's sake this brabbling broil untwist.
