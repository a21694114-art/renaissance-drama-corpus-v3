Do/ wretched caitiff whether shall I flee That I might scape this endless sorrow Now gentle death spare me till to morrow That I may amend me With good advisement. Nay thereto I will not consent Nor no man will I respite But to the heart suddenly I shall smite Without any advisement And now out of sight I will me high See thou make the ready shortly For thou mayst say this is the day That no man living may scape away Alas I may well weep with sighs deep Now have I no manner of company To help me in my journey/ & me to keep And also my writing is full unready Now shall I do now/ for to excuse me I would to god I had never be get To my soul a great profit it had be For now I fear pains huge and great The time passeth/ lord help that all wrought For though I mourn it availeth nought The day passeth/ and is almost ago I wots not well what to do To whom were I best my complante to make What and I to fellowship thereof spoke And showed him of this sudden chance For in him is all mine affiance We have in the world so many a day Be good friends in sport and play I see him yonder cartaynely I trust that he will bear me company Therefore to him will I speak to ease my sorrow Well met god fellowship and good morrow.
