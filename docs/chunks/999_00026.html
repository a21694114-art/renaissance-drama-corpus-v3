Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Yes, you may put her in so, but be sure you cohere in every particle, with the precedent Fallacy, as that you have loved her long, though till this day, and so as I did demonstrate. But how and she wooed say she is not Widow Come, and that she knows no such woman, and so spoil all? Trust that with her wit, and my instructions we suspected a spy, and therefore she will change her voice. Thou hast a delicate Mistress of her. One thing more, and ye meet presently, mine Aunt has had mine husbands, tell her you'll hazard a limb and make the tenth. Prithee let me alone, and Sir Nicholas were here himself, he should swear 'twere thine Aunt. Go forwards towards him, be not too full of prattle, but make use of your instructions. Who's there, Widow Coote? Master Bloodbound as I take it. she changes her voice bravely, I must tell thee true Widow, I have loved thee long time, look how the Rogue looks, but had never the wit to let thee know it till today. So I was given to understand Sir. Is't not a fool finely? Handsome by this hand. I like thy dwelling well upon the Fleet ditch. A pretty wholesome Air Sir, in the Summer time. Who would think 'twere she Iarvis? I told ye she was tutored. I'll home with her presently, some stays up i'th' dark. Fool and he have any private discourse with her, they discover themselves one to another, and so spoil the plot, no trick, no by no means sir, to hazard your person with her, the bold rogue may come up close to discover her to be my Mistress, and recover her with much danger to you. He has got a dagger. And a sword six foot in length, I'll carry her home for you, therefore not a light be stirring; for I know your Rivals will watch your house, Sim shall show us the chamber, we'll conduct her up i'th' dark, shut the door to her above, and presently come down and let you in below. There was never such a Iaruis heard off, bid Sim to be careful, by the same token, I told him he should feed tomorrow for all the week after: good night Widow, Coote my man stayeth up, we will bob Sir Nicholas bravely. Good night sweet widow Coote. I do but seem to part, we'll meet at home wench. Adieu my sweet dear heart. Go you with me, so, so, I will Cage this cuckoo, and then for my young Madcap, if all hit right, this morning's mirth shall crown the Craft o'th' night, follow me warily. I warrant thee Iaruis, let me alone to right myself into the garb of a Lady: Oh strange to see how dreams fall by contraries, I shall be coached tomorrow, and yet last night dreamed I was carted: prithee keep a little state, go Iaruis.

Who's there, Widdovv Coote? Master Bloodbound as I take it. Shee changes her voice bravely, I must tell thee true Widdow, I have loved thee long time, looke how the Rogue lookes, but had never the wit to let thee know it till to day.
