Mother and Leicester add not oil to fire. Wrath's kindled with a word, and cannot hear The number less persuasions you infort, O but my son thy father favours him. Richard that vile abortive changeling brat, And Faukenbridge, are fallen at Henry's feet. They woo for him, but entreat my son Gloster may die for this that he hath done, If Gloster live thou wilt be overthrown, If Gloster live thy mother dies in moan, If Gloster live Leyster will fly the realm, If Gloster live thy kingdom's but a dream, Have I not sworn by that eternal arm That puts just vengeance sword in monarch's hands, Gloster shall die for his presumption? What needs more conjuration gracious Mother? And honourable Leyster mark my words. I have a Beadrole of some threescore Lords, Of Gloster's faction, Nay of Henry's faction. Of thy false father's faction, speak the truth, He is the head of factions; were he down: Peace, plenty, glory will empale thy crown. I there's the But; whose hart-white if we hit, The game is ours. Well we may rage's and rove, At Gloster, Lancaster, Chester, Faukenbridge, But he is the upshot. Yet begin with Gloster. The destinies run to the book of Fates, And read in never-changing Characters Robert of Gloster's end he dies to day, So fate, so heaven, so doth King Henry say. Emperially resolved. The old King comes, Then comes Luxurious lust, The King of Concubines, the King that scorns The undefiled, chaste and numptiall bed, The King that hath his Queen Imprisoned. For my sake scorn him, son call him not father, Give him the style of a competitor, Pride seize upon my heart, wrath fill mine eyes, Sat lawful majesty upon my front Duty fly from me, pity be exiled, Senses forget that I am Henry's child, I kiss thee, and I bless thee, for this thought. O Lancaster bid Henry yield some reason Why he desires so much the death of Gloster, I hear thee Henry, and I thus reply. I do desire the death of Bastard Gloster. For that he spends the Treasure of the Crown. I do desire the death of bastard Gloster, For that he doth desire to pull me down. Or were this false (I purpose to be plain) He loves thee, and for that I him disdain. Therein thou showest a hate corrupted mind, To him the more unjust, to me unkind, He loves you as his father loved his mother. Fie, fie upon thee hateful Elinor. I thought thou hadst been long since scarlet dyde, She is and therefore cannot change her colour. You are to strict, Earl Gloster's fault Merits not death, By th'rood the Prince says true. Hear is a statute from the Confessor, The Confessor was but a simple fool. Away with books my word shall be a law, England her breath shall from this bosom draw, Gloster shall die, Let Gloster die the death. Leyster he shall not, he shall have law, despite of him and thee.
