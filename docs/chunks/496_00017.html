Such a Fellow would I fain meet withal: Well overtaken sir. 'sblood come before me sir: What a Devil art thou? A man at least. And art thou valiant? I carry a sword and a bucklerye see. A sword and a buckler, and know not me, Not Black Will? No trust me. Slave, than thou art neither traveler, nor Purse-taker: for I tell thee, Black will is known and feared through the seventeen provinces: there's not a sword and Buckler man in England nor Europe, but has had a taste of my manhood. I am tole-free in all Cities, & the Suburbs about them: this is my Sconce, my Castle, my Citadel, and but King Harry, God bless his Majesty, I fear not the proudest. O ves, some of his guard. Let his guard eats beef and be thankful, give me a man will cover himself with his buckler, and not budge and the devil come. methinks thou wert better live at Court as I do, King Harry loves a man, I can tell ye. Would thou and all the men he keeps were hanged, and ye love not him then: but I will not change my revenues for all his guards wages. Hast thou such store of ruing? Art thou a good fellow? May I speak freely, and wilt not tell the king on't? Keep thine own counsel, and fear not, For of my faith the King shall know no more for me, than thou tell'st him. And I tell him any thing let him hang me: but for thyself, I think if a fat purse come i'th' way, thou wouldest not refuse it. Therefore leave the Court and shark with me, I tell thee, I am chief commander of all the Stews, there's not a whore shifts a smock but by my privilege, nor opens her shop before I have my weekly tribute: And to assure thee my valour carries credit with it, do but walk with me through the streets of London, and let me see the proudest watch disturb us. I shall be glad of your conduct syr. Follow me then, and i'll tell thee more. Stand, who goes there? A good fellow: come close, regard them not. How shall we know thee to be a good fellow? My names Black Will. Oh, God give ye good night, good Master Black William. God boy sir, God boy, I am glad we are so well rid on him. la sir, you see here's egress enough, Now follow me, & you shall see we'll have regress back again, Hoe comes there? Come afore the Constable. Will What have ye forgot me so soon? 'tis I. O, 'tis M. Black William, God bless ye sir, God bless ye. How lik'st thou now? Faith excellent: but prithee tell me, dost thou face the world with thy manhood, that thus they fear thee, or art thou truly valiant?
