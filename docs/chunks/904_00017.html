I'll take no knowledge on't what makes she here? How dare you pawn unpennanced with a cheek Fresh as her falsehood yet, where castigation Has left no pale print of her visiting anguish, Appear in this assembly, let me alone, Sin must be bold, that's all the grace 'tis bound too. What's this? I'm wonder struck. Assist me goodness, I shall to prison again. At least I've mazed 'em, Scattered by admiration of her innocence, (As the fired ships put in, severed the Fleet In eighty eight) I'll on with't, impudence Is mischief's patrimony, is his justice injured reverence no sharplier righted, I ever held that Majesty impartial, That like unequal, Heaven looks on the manners, Not on the shapes they shroud in. This black Knight Will never take any answer, 'tis a victory To make him understand, he does amiss, When he knows in his own clear understanding, That he does nothing else, show him the testimony Confirmed by good men, how that foul attempter, Got but this morning to the place from whence He dated his forged lines six days past, Why may not the corruption sleep in this, By some connivance, as you have walked in ours, By too rash confidence. I'll undertake That Knight shall teach the devil how to lie. If sin were half so wise as impudent, She'd ne'er seek father for an advocate. Now to act treachery with an angel's tongue, Since all ours come out, i'll bring him in strongly again, Where is his injured chastity? this goodness Whose worth no transitory prize can equal, This rock of constant, and invincible virtue That made sin's tempest weary of his fury. What is my pawn distracted? I think rather there's some notable masterprize of roguery, This drum strikes up for. Let me fall down with reverence Before this blessed Altar. This is madness. Mark the end, I stand for roguery still, I will not change my side, I shall be taxed I know I care not what the black house thinks of me. What say you now? I will not be unlaid yet. however censure flies, I honour sanctity, That is my object I intend no other, I saw this glorious and most valiant virtue, Fight the most noble combat with the devil. If both the Bishops had been there for seconds T'had been a complete Devil. Then thou hast heard the violence intended? 'tis a truth, I joy to justify I was an agent On virtue's part, and raised that confused noise, That started his attempt and gave her liberty. Oh 'tis a righteous story she has told (Sir) My life and fame stands mutually engaged, Both to the truth and goodness of this pawn. Does it appear to you, yet clear as the Sun. Lass I believed it long before 'twas done. Degenerate Base, Perfidious. Traitorous pawn What are you all besides yourselves? But I remember that pawn.
