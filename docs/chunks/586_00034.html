Not till you tell me Sir, I ha' got thee with Child in my Conscience, and like a kind Husband, methinks I breed it for thee. For I am already sick at my stomach and long extremely. Now must thou be my helpful Physician, and provide for me. even to my blood, What's mine is yours, to gain your peace or good. What a kind soul is this, could a man have found a greater content in a wife, if he should ha' sought through the world for her: Prithee heart as I said, I long, and in good troth I do, and methinks thy first child will be borne without a nose, if I lose my longing, 'tis but for a trifle too, yet methinks it will do me no good unless thou effect it for me. I could take thy keys myself, go into thy Closet, and read over the deeds and evidences of thy Land, & in reading over them, rejoice I had such blessed fortune to have so fair a wife with so much endowment, and then open thy Chests, and survey thy Plate, jewels, Treasure. But a pox on't, all will do me no good, unless thou effect it for me. Sir I will show you all the wealth I have, Of Coin, of jewels, or Possessions,
