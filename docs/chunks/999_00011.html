Marry old Earelack, is my Father mad? They are both a concluding on't yonder, to morrow's the day, one wedding dinner must serve both marriages. Oh Sim, the Ancient, the delicate Ancient there's a man, and thou talk'st of a man, a good face & sparkling eye, a strength body, a delicate hand, a clean leg and foot, ah sweet Sim, there's a man worth, Maidenhead. But I say, Master Earelack the old man, a foot like a Bear, a leg like a bedstaff, a hand like a hatchet, an eye like a pig, and a face like a winter pigmy, there's a man for a Maidenhead. Oh look, look, oh alas what shall I do with him? What, why what shall fifteen do with sixty and twelve, make a screen of him, stand next the fire, whilst you sit behind him, and keep a friend's lips warm, many a wench would be glad of such a fortune. Your oath struck it dead then? o my sides. Five hundred deep of your side i'faith father. Moll, come hither Moll, I hope Sim has discovered the project? And tomorrow must be the day Moll, both of a day, one dinner shall serve, we may have store of little ones, we must save for our family. Good Sir, what rashness was parent to this madness, marry an old man— Earelack the Informer? Madness— y'are a whore. Is she a whore Sim? She must be your wife, I tell— An arrant whore, to refuse Master Innocent Earelack of Rogue-land, that for his dwelling, next that he doth Inform now and then against enormities, and hath been blanketed, it may be pumped in's time: yet the world knows, he dost not out of need, he's of mighty means, but takes delight now and then to trot up and down, to avoid Idleness, you whore. Good Sir. Pray Father. This wound wants oil, good sir in all my paths I will make you my guide, I was only startled, And with the suddenness of the word marriage In that I knew, that this deserving Gentleman And I, had never so much conference, Whereby this coal of Paphos, by the rhetoric Of his love-stealing heart, Captivating Language, Might be blown into a flame. Does she take Tobacco Father? No, no, man, here are out of ballads, She has all the Garland of good will by heart. Snails she may sing me asleep a nights than Sim Why, right Sir, and then 'tis but tickling you o'th' forehead with her heels, you are awake again, and ne'er the worse man. Is he but five years older than yourself sir? Nay, I want a week and three days of that too. I'll tell thee an old saw for't girl, old, say he be, old blades are best, young hearts are never old. Ah Hay. Gold is great Glee, gold begets rest, what fault is found in Gold? I will answer presently Sir, with another Saw.
