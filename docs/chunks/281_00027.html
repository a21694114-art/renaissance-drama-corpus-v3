I know they do, and I have well deserved it. What my Lo: where is your boar-spear man? Fear you the boar and go so unprovided? My Lo: good morrow▪ good morrow Catesby: You may jest on: but by the holy rood. I do not like these several counsels I. My Lo: I hold my life as dear as you do yours, And never in my life I do protest, Was it more precious to me than it is now: Think you, but that I know our state secure▪ I would be so triumphant as I am? The Lords at Pomfret when they road from London, Were jocund, and supposed their states was sure, And they indeed had no cause to mistrust: But yet you see how soon the day overcast, This sudden scab of rancour I misdoubt, Pray God, I say, I prove a needless coward: But come my Lo: shall we to the tower? I go: but stay, hear you not the news, This day those men you talked of, are beheaded. They for their truth might better wear their heads, Then some that have accused them wear their hats: But come my Lo: let us away. Go you before, I'll follow presently. Well met Hastings, how goes the world with thee? The better that it please your Lo: to ask. I tell thee fellow 'tis better with me now. Then when I met thee last where now we meet: Then was I going prisoner to the tower, By the suggestion of the Queen's allies: But now I tell thee (keep it to thyself.) This day those enemies are put to death, And I in better state than ever I was. God hold it to your honours good content. Gramercy Hastings hold spend thou that▪ God save your Lordship. God keep the Prince from all the pack of you: A knot you are of damned bloodsuckers. O Pomfret Pomfret▪ Oh thou bloody prison▪ Fatal and ominous to noble peers.
