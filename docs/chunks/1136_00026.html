You are transported Philonax But that I have compassion for the death Of that good King I could laugh at thee. Meant you me Philonax, unhand those prisoners Away with'em I command Yet stay and hear me As you did love Basilius hear his daughter, This insolence doth interrupt the tears Due to my dear dead father, and enforces Me, since he thus forgets, to declare to you With confidence who I am, I am Pamela The eldest daughter of Basilius Your Queen if I mistake not, since my father Is dead, to whose memory these pious drops Fall as the tribute of my grief, who then Shall be obeyed, he that was trusted with My father's power, which in his death is canceled Or I your natural princess?
