Which if you do, My wife shall thank you for it; but your better Course is to seek one fitter for your turn, You'll lose your aim in me; and I befriend you To tell you so. Gip gaffer Shotten, fagh, Take that for your coy Counsel. Help, oh help. What mean you gentlewoman? That to you sir. O murder, murder. Peace good Master, And come away. Some Cowardly Jade I warrant, That durst not strike a woman. What's the matter? But and we were your match.— What would you do? Come, come afore the Constable: now if You were her match, what would you do sir?
