And hark you, having done, ascend the Turret And see if you can discover his Majesty Coming to the Castle: this house he appointed For his recreation, if you do, descend, And give me warning. I will. So I have lulled my Lord asleep, I see he takes my music heavily, Therefore I'll sing no more: now to my Turret To see if the king come, now he may take him napping. There is no resting place within a prison To make my sorrows less by recounting. And best for my escape: fare ill, not well, Thou and thy lustful Master: from all but one, This key now frees me, O! that I bear about, Which none but mercy's key can deliver out.
