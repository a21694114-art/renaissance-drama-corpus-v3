And I to Ford will likewise tell How Falstaff varlet wild, Would have her love, his dove would prove, And eke his bed defile. Let us about it then. I'll second thee: sir Corporal Nym troup on. M. Slender is your Master's name say you? I indeed that is his name. How say you? I take it he is somewhat a weakly man: And he has as it were a whey coloured beard. Indeed my masters beard is kane coloured. Kane colour, you say well. And is this Letter from sir Yond, about Mistress An, Is it not? I indeed is it. So: and your Master would have me as it 'ttwere to speak to mistresses Anne concerning him: I promise you my M. hath a great affectioned mind to mistress Anne himself. And if he should know that I should as they say, give my verdict for any one but himself, I should hear of it thoroughly: For I tell you friend, he puts all his privities in me. I by my faith you are a good stay to him. Am I? I and you knew all yowd say so: Washing, brewing, baking, all goes through my hands, Or else it would be but a woe house. I be shrew me, one woman to do all this, Is very painful. Are you avised of that? I, I warrant you, Take all, and pay all, all go through my hands, And he is such a honest man, and he should chance To come home and find a man here, we should Have no who with him. He is a parlowes man. Is he indeed? Is he quoth you? God keep him abroad: Lord bless me, who knocks there? For God's sake step into the Counting house, While I go see whose at door. What Iohn Rugby, Iohn, Are you come home sir already? I begar I be forget my ointment, Where be Iohn Rugby? Here sir, do you call? I you be Iohn Rugbie, and you be lack Rugby Go run up met your heels, and bring away De ointment in de vindoe present: Make haste Iohn Rugbie. O I am almost forget My simples in a box in de Countinghouse: O Ieshu vat be here, a devella, a devella? My Rapier Iohn Rugby, Vat be you, vat make You in my Countinghouse? I tinck you be a teefe. Ieshu bless me, we are all undone. O Lord sir no: I am no thief, I am a Servingman: My name is Iohn Simple, I brought a Letter sir From my M. Slender, about mistresses Arm Page Sir: Indeed that is my coming. I begar is dat all? Iohn Rugby give a ma pen An Ink: tarche un pettit tarche a little. O God what a furious man is this? Nay it is well he is no worse: I am glad he is so quiet.
