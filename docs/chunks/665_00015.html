I pity the fool i'faith, but the other Horseleech, I wish his blows trebled: I conversed with him, but a Rogue so stuffed with a library of new minited words, so tearing the sense, I never met with. But now we have spoiled our determinate dinner at my hostess of the Hobbye, we shall now be known.
