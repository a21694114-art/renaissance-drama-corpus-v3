Dear husband. Oh! most punishment of all I have a wife, I do entreat you as you love your soul, Tell me the cause of this your discontent. A vengeance strip thee naked, thou art cause, Effect, quality, property, thou, thou, thou. Bad, turned to worse? both beggary of the soul, as of the body. And so much unlike himself at first, As if some vexed spirit Had got his form upon him. He comes again: He says I am the cause, I never yet Spoke less than words of duty, and of love.
