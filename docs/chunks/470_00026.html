You both go jointly to the Citadel, There sup there poision him: and Maria, Because she is our opposite, shall bear The sad suspect, on which she dies, or loves us. I run. We that are great, our sole self good still moves us. They shall die both, for their deserts craves more Than we can recompense, their presence still Imbraids our fortunes with beholdingness, Which weabhorre like deed, not doer: then conclude, They live not to cry out ingratitude.
