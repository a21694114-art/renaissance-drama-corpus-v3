If ye two bid me, we will with him pitch a field, I and my maids together. Let us see, be bold. Ye shall see women's war. That fight will I behold If occasion serve, taking his part full brim, I will strike at you, but the rap shall light on him. When we first appear. Then will I run away As though I were afeard. Do you that part well play And I will sue for peace. And I will set him on. Then will he look as fierce as a Cotssold lion. But when ghost thou for him? That do I very now. Ye shall find us here. Well god have mercy on you. There is no cause of fear, the least boy in the street: Nay, the least girl I have, will make him take his feet. But hark, methink they make preparation. No force, it will be a good recreation. I will stand within, and step forth speedily, And so make as though I ran away dreadfully. Now sirs, keep your ray, and see your hearts be stout, But where be these caitiffs, methink they dare not rout, How sayst thou Merrygreek? What doth Kit Custance say? I am loath to tell you. Tush speak man▪ yea or nay? Forsooth sir, I have spoken for you all that I can. But if ye win her, ye must e'en play the man, e'en to fight it out, ye must a man's heart take. Yes, they shall know, and thou knowest I have a stomach. A stomach (quoth you) yea, as good as ere man had. I trow they shall find and feel that I am a lad. By this cross I have seen you eat your meat as well, As any that ere I have seen of or heard tell, A stomach quoth you? he that will that deny I know was never at dinner in your company. Nay, the stomach of a man it is that I mean. Nay the stomach of an horse or a dog I ween. Nay a man's stomach with a weapon mean I. Ten men can scarce match you with a spoon in a pie. Nay the stomach of a man to try in strife. I never saw your stomach cloyed yet in my life. Tush I mean in strife or fighting to try. We shall see how ye will strike now being angry. Have at thy pate then, and save thy head if thou may. Nay then have at your pate again by this day, Nay thou mayst not strike at me again in no wise. I can not in fight make to you such warrantise: But as for your foes here let them the bargain buy. Nay as for they, shall every mother's child die. And in this my fume a little thing might make me, To beat down house and all, and else the devil take me.
