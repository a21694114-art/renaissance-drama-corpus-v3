Of neither Iohn I muse at my disgrace, That I am thus kept prisoner in this place. O sir, a number are here prisoners, My Cousin Morton whom I came to visit, But he good man is at his morrow mass, But I that neither care to say nor sing, Come to seek that preaching hate and prayer, And while they mumble up their Orisons, We'll play a game at bowls, what sayst thou Gloster? I care not if I do, You do not care, Let old men care for graves, we for our sports, Off with your gown, there hes my hat and Cloak, The bowls there quickly, he? No my gown stirs not, it keeps sorrow warm, And she, and I am not to be divorced, Yes there's an axe must part your head and you, And with your head, sorrow will leave your heart. But come shall I begin? a pound a game, More pounds and we thus heavy? well begin. Rub rub rub rub. Amen God send it short enough, and me A safe running with them clothes from thee. Play Robin, run run run. Far enough and well, fly one foot more, Would I were half so far without the door. Now Porter what's the news? Your Cousin Morton humbly craves, Leaving your game, you would come visit him, Bowl Gloster I'll come presently. So near mad Robin? then have after you, Would I were gone, make after as you may, Well sir 'tis yours, one all, throw but the Iacke While I go talk with Morton: I'll not stay, Keep Cloak and hat in pawn I'll hold out play, I would be sorry Iohn but you should stay, Until my bias run another way, Now pass, and hay pass, Skink unto your tricks, 'tis but a chance at hazard: there lies Gloster, and hear stands Skinke, now Iohn play thou thy part, And if I scape I'll love thee with my heart. So porter let me forth. God bless your grace, ye spoke with the L. Morton. I have and must about his business to the Court.
