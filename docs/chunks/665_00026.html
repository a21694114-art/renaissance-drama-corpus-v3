And then to Apollo, hollo trees, hollo, Tapster a few more clothes to my feet. Oh heavens! Gentles, keep your places, fear nothing: in the name of God what art thou? My Hearse and winding sheet: what means this? why Gentles I am a living man. Spirit thou liest, thou deludest us. Conjure him Friar. In nomino Domini, I thee charge, Respond mihi here at large. Cuium pecus whence thou art: Et quam obrem, thou makest us start, In spiritus of the gloomy night? Qui Venis huc us to affright Per trinitatem I there charge thee, Quid tu vis hic to tell to me. Why gentles, I am a living man Philantus, what instance shall I give ye? hear me, I have sight, understanding, I know mine hosts, I see that Gentlewoman, I can feel. Feel this Gentlewoman! s'ild if ye were ten Ghosts, i'll not endure it. Spirit thou deludest us. Why, what should I say? will ye hsare my voice, here's none but— Nay, that's a lie, then 'tis a living spirit, i'll have about with him. Oh sir, meddle not with shadows, spirit thou liest, I saw thee dead, so did many more: We know ye wandering dwellers in the dark, Have power to shape you like mortality, To beguile the simple, & deceive their souls, Thou art a Devil. Sweet Gent, behold I am flesh and blood, here's my flesh feel it. By my troth methinks he should be alive, I could find in my heart to feel his flesh. Try with your Rapier Accutus, if he bleed he lives. If I bleed I die, sweet Gentlemen draw no blood. How shall we know thou art flesh and blood then? Take heed Accutus he'll blast thee. What instance shall I give ye? I am Phylantus, he that must needs confess he was drunk in your companies last day, sweet Gentlemen conceive me aright. Why true, true, that we know, and those swilling bowels, Death did arrest thee, many saw thee dead, Else needles were these rites of funerals, And since that time till now, no breath was known. Fly from you: and twenty times the hourglass, Hath turned his upside down: and twenty times The nimble current sand hath left his upper room, To lie beneath, since spark of life appeared, In all which time, my care employed itself, To give the rights of burial: now if you live, Who so glad as I? Sir, your love hath shown itself abundant, but the cold air is a means to divorce me from your companies: mine host let me crave passage to my chamber. Out of my doors knave, thou interest not my doors, I have no chalk in my house, my posts shall not be guarded with a little sing song, si nihil attuleris ibis Homere foras.
