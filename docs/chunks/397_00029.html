By this broad beaten path, it should appear, The holy Hermit's Cave cannot be far, And if I err not, this is he himself. What honoured tongue enquereth for the Hermit? What honoured tongue? I Lady Faukenbridge, I know ye, and I know for what you come. For Gloster and your husband's jealousy. O thou, whose eye of contemplation, Looks through the windows of the highest heavens, Resolve thy Handmaid, where Earl Gloster lives: And whether he shall live, and scape the hate, Of proud young Henry and his brother Iohn? I'll have you first in, I'll tell you more anon. Madam, they say bushes have ears and eyes, And these are matters of great secrecy: And you'll vouchsafe enter my holy Cell, There what you long to know, i'll quickly tell. Stay here are strangers. A plague upon them, come they in the nick, To hinder Raynald of his Fox's trick? Good day old Hermit. So to you fair Dame. By Elinor's grey eye she's fair indeed; Sweet heart come ye for holy benizons? Hermit hast thou good custom with such Cliants? I cannot blame your feats, your juggling tricks, Plague juggle you. Why curse ye sacred worth? Ill done in sooth my Lord, very ill done, Wrong holiness: a very pretty woman. Mock gravity; by the mass a cherry lip, A it's not well done, deride a holy Hermit? I have it in my purse shall make amends. His purse and yours, shall make me some amends, For hindering me this morning from the Lady; For scaring me at Tavern yesternight, For having back your chain, I'll fit you both. Hermit, a word. A word with you fair mistress. Where lie your devils that tell all your news? Would you would trouble them for half an hour, To know what's become of traitor Gloster, That in my clothes broke prison in the Fleet? No, it was Skinke. Come old fool ye dote. But hear me. Hear him Prince. 'Swounds who hears you? I'll make your Lady graft ye for this work: but to your tale sir. Know thrice honoured Prince, that Skinke did cousin Redcap of his clothes. Gloster did cozen Skinke, and so escaped. Well done Faukenbridge? My Lord, he tells you true. You find it on her lips: but forward sir. 'twas Skinke in Gloster's gown, whom you did visit, That played at bowls and after stole your cloths, While you went into the Lord Moortons' chamber. This savours of some truth, 'tis very like, Well Faukenbridge by heaven I'll tell your wife, She'll much believe you: you will come? Tell me of my wife: this evening fail me not. My wife quoth you: I'll send my wife from home, Do, tell my wife prince Iohn, by my dear mother, I love her too too well to like another.
