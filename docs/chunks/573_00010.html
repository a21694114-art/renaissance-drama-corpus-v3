Is my sweet Knight coming, are you certain he's coming? Certain for sooth, I am sure I saw him out of the barber's shop, ere I would come away. A barber's shop, O he's a trim Knight, would he venture his body into a barber's shop, when he knows 'tis as dangerous as a piece of Ireland: O yonder, yonder, he comes, get you back again, and look you say as I advized you. You know me mistress? My mask, my mask. My sweet revenue! My pleasure welcome: I have got single, none but you shall accompany me to the justice of peace my Fathers. Why, is thy Father justice of Peace, and I not know it? My father! i'faith sir I, simply though I stand here a citizens wife: I am a justice of pieces Daughter. I love thee the better for thy birth. he's at thy service my sweet revenue, for thy money paid for 'em. Why then let him run a little before I beseech thee, for a my troth he will discover us else. He shall obey thee, before sirrah, trudge: but do you mean to lie at your Fathers all night? Why should I desire your company else? 'Sfoot where shall I lie then. What an idle question's that? why do you hinke I cannot make room for you in my Father's house, as well as in my husbands, theyare both good for nothing else? A man so resolute in valour as a Woman in desire, were an absolute Leader. May it please your good worship master justice. Please me and please are yourself, That's my word. The party your worship sent for, will by no means be brought to appear. He will not, then what would you advise me to do therein? Only to grant your worships warrant, which is of sufficient force to compel him. No by my faith: you shall not have me in that Trap: am I sworn justice of Peace, and shall I give my warrant to fetch a man against his will? why there the peace is Broken, we must do all quietly, if he come he's welcome, and as far as I can see yet, he's a fool to be absent, I by this gold is he (which he gave me this morning) Why, but may it please your good worship.— I say again please me and please yourself, that's my word still. Sir, the world esteems it a Common favour, upon the Contempt of the party, the justice to grant his warrant. ay, 'tis so common, 'tis the worse again, 'twere the better for me 'twere otherwise. I protest sir, and this Gentleman can say as much, it lies upon my half undoing. I cannot see yet, that it should be so— I see not a cross yet. I beseech your worship show me your immediate favour, and accept this small trifle but as a remembrance to my succeeding Thankfulness.
