Now say this again: he hath somewhat to doing Which followeth the trace of one that is wooing, Specially that hath no more wit in his head, Than my cousin Roister Doister with all is led. I am sent in all haste to espy and to mark How our letters and tokens are likely to work. Master Roister Doister must have answer in haste For he loveth not to spend much labour in waste. Now as for Christian Custance by this light, Though she had not her troth to Gavin Goodluck plight, Yet rather than with such a loutish dolt to marie, I dare say would live a poor life solitary, But fain would I speak with Custance if I wist how To laugh at the matter, yond cometh one forth now. AH that I might but once in my life have a sight Of him that made us all so ill shent by this light, He should never escape if I had him by the ear, But even from his head, I would it bite or tear. Yea and if one of them were not enough, I would bite them both off, I make God avow. What is he, whom this little mouse doth so threaten? I would teach him I trow, to make girls shent or beaten.
