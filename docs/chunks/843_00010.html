For blood of grapes you sold the widow's food. And starving them, 'tis murder, what's this but hell. Hircius your name, and Goatish is your nature: You snatch the meat out of the prisoner's mouth, To fatten harlots, is not this hell to, No Angel, but the devil waits on you. Shall I cut his throat. No, better burn him, for I think he is a witch, but soothe, soothe him. Fellow Angelo, true it is, that falling into the company of wicked he-Christians for my part. And she-ones for mine, we have 'em swim in shoals hard by. We must confess, I took too much of the pot, and he of tother hollow commodity. Yes indeed, we laid lil on both of us, we cozened the poor, but 'tis a common thing, many a one that counts himself a better Christian then we two, has done it, by this light. But pray sweet Angelo, play not the tell-tale to my Lady, and if you take us creeping into any of these mouseholes of sin any more, let Cats flay off our skins. And put nothing but the poisoned tails of Rats into those skins. Will you dishonour her sweet charity. Who saved you from the tree of death and shame. Would I were hanged rather than thus be told of my faults. She took us, 'tis true, from the gallows, yet I hope she will not bar yeomen sprats to have their swinge. She comes, beware and mend. Let's break his neck, and bid him mend. Have you my messages (sent to the poor) Delivered with good hands, not robbing them Of any jot was theirs. Rob 'em Lady, I hope, neither my fellow nor I am thieves Delivered with good hands madam, else let me never lick my fingers more when I eat buttered fish. Who cheat the poor, and from them pluck their alms. Pilfer from Heaven, and there are thunderbolts From thence to beat them ever, do not lie, Were you both faithful true distributers? Lie madam, what grief is it to see you turn Swaggerer, and give your poor minded rascally servants the lie. I'm glad you do not, if those wretched people Tell you they pine for want of any thing. Whisper but to mine ear, and you shall furnish them. Whisper, nay Lady, for my part I'll cry whoop. Play no more Villains with so good a Lady, For if you do———. Are we Christians? The foul Fiend snap all Pagans for me. Away, and once more mend. Takes us for Butchers. A patch, a patch. My book and Taper. Here most holy Mistress. Thy voice sends forth such music, that I never Was ravished with a more celestial sound, Were every servant in the world like thee, So full of goodness, Angels would come down To dwell with us, thy name is Angelo, And like that name thou art, get thee to rest, Thy youth with too much watching is oppressed.
