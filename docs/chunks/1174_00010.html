Why, Mother, I have heard ye bid to give; And often, as the Cause calls. I know well, It is a witty part, sumtimes, to give. But what? to whame? no monsters! nor to maidens! He suld present them with mare pleasand things, Things natural, and what all women covet To see: the common Parent of us all! Which Maids will twire at, 'tween their fingers, thus! With which his Sire got him! he's get another! And so beget posterity upon her! This he should do!
