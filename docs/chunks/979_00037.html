Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

No, you may keep her still. My thinks you are much dejected with your fall, I find an alteration in your face. You look like an Almanac of last years date. Or like your livery cloak, of two years wearing. Worse than the smoky wall of a bawdy house. Villain, dost thou insult on me? No faith sir, Alas, 'tis not within the reach of man, To countermine your plots. Well, slave, because I'll rid my hands of thee, I'll give thee a share. You must have none, without lawful proceeding. And that I know, you dare not. But I dare. Have you been partners all this while in mischief, And now fall out, who shall be the most knave? What do you mean? I mean to search your house For ammunition, no otherwise, Which I suspect you send unto the leaguer. Sir I have nothing there, but one cracked piece Belongs to this Gentleman, can do no service. She is spoilt in the boar. we'll have her new cast. Come, bring them away. Nay good sir, you know, That I was lately quit before a justice. And if I fall in a relapse. all's one To me, but you must satisfy the Law. Well then, I know the worst of it. Master Trimalchio, 'tis an age since I saw you. I was ne'er out of town. Not out of town? We sought you about all the Ordinaries, Taverns and bawdy-houses, we could imagine You ever haunted. You might have found us then. Nay more, we inquired at the playhouses. 'Twas once in my mind, to have had you cried. We gave you lost. Well, shall I tell you, Captain? I; do, what is't? This Gentleman and I Have passed through purgatory, since I saw you. If I should tell you all the passages At the leaguer. Thither we came to meet you, And you were gone. And then at the justices. Were you before the justice? 'Tis such a story Would fill a Chronicle. We met with a party of the enemies, Took all we had from us, and then it cost us Forty shillings in fees at the justices. That was hard dealing. The old boy and I Grew to be kin at last. He made me sure To my mistress, before we parted. How? By what strange accident? Honest Ardelio, And Misellanio, we were all together In rebellion, and quit by a Letter, That came from my mistress. Is't possible? And Miscellanio turn traitor? What. Would have your mistress from you, thinks the Letter Was sent for his sake. That I am sure he does not. Threatens, and swears that he will fight for her. If he be weary of his life, he may. Why what can he pretend to her? I know not, What has passed between them, but I am sure, He has been practising at the Fencing school, To get a trick to kill you. He kill me! I'll kill him first. I fight by Geometry.
