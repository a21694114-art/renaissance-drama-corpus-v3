Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Dead? I'll go take possession presently. Do not with too strict rigour exercise your power on his distressed family. My time is come, I will not lose an hour. It's just, that every man should take his own. Sir, you speak law, not charity. He that will Be nothing more than just, is unjust still. Woe to that quited soul, to whom from heaven All justice, and no mercy shall be given. Your mercy to the widow, to the Orphans. As much as a Puritan has upon a good feast. Well— let me tell you this— Sager is dead. So flies report, borne on presumptions wings. But how he died, that aery bird not sings. Killed— but by whom— weight deeply— I must hence. The mutter's strong— look to your conscience. How's this? killed?— muttering? and conscience? Look, his ghastly melancholy points him out for the murderer. As sure, as a hat-brinks pulled down declares a cuckold, this darkness discovers him. I am a villain. Tell him, that knows it not. My narrow heart cannot be capable Of this huge bulk of sorrow. It must out. Now, to whose bosom better than my friends? This hand killed Sager. How? Nay, do your worst. 'twas but chance medley, accidental slaughter. Intending with my Piece to strike a fowl, Against my will the cock went down, and he Stood in death's way. It was his destiny. But Griffin, hark you— let not your tongue stir. Do not I know you for a forger? And more— you wot— let not your tongue be loose. Thus are two Foxes catched in one poor noose. Our guilt shall bind our secrecy; who lives An unsuspected villain, winks at others Unlawful deeds, to teach their eyelids how To wink at his— Shall we go to our new Hostess? Where? who? For your where, at the Maidenhead, a good likely place. For your who: the widow that old Gripe suspects for the robbery; but young Gripe hath tenanted to his Inn. Mass, she prevents us. Widow, we were coming. Pray Gentlemen walk in; you shall have attendance. Your company, sweet widow. I'll not be long from you, Sir. Oh, some retiring from this house of sin. Fate! I was never bred to keep an Inn. More customers? that which all Inns would see; Great store of guests: this is a plague to me. Yonder's mine Hostess. Now the water's up, that we cannot get over to the Abbey, it is our securest course to commit the money to her custody. If any search should be made, and these tokens found about us, we are all dead men: there's not so much mercy in Gripe, as in the Plague. Agreed. Widow, we have some money to pay to a Londoner in Bedford here; and he's not yet come to receive it. Will you lock it up safe for us? But hear you? Deliver it not to any one of us. Except all three demand it together, keep it still.
