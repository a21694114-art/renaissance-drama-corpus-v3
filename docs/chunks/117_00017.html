When ich saw this, ich was worth see now and start between them twain, see now Else ich durst take a book oath, see now my Gammer had been slain, see now. This is even the whole matter, as Hodge has plainly told And chould fain be quiet for my part that chould But help us good master, beseech ye that ye do Else shall we both be beaten and lose our needle too. What would ye have me to do tell me that I were gone I will do the best that I can, to set you both at one But be ye sure dame Chat hath this your needle found: Here comes the man that see her take it up on the ground, Ask him yourself master Rat if ye believe not me. And help me to my needle, for God's sake and saint charity. Come near diccon and let us hear, what thou can express. Wilt y• be sworne y• haue▪ ¶ Nay by S. Benit wil I not, then might ra. ¶ Why did deny it I marry gammer: but I said I would not abide by it. Will you say a thing, and not stick do it to try it. Stick to it quoth you master rat, marry sir I defy it. Nay there is many an honest man, when he such blasts hath blown In his friends ears, he would be loath the same by him were known If such a toy be used oft among the honesty It may beseem a simple man, if your and my degree. Then we be never the nearer, for all that you can tell. Yes marry sir, if ye will do by mine advise and counsel, If mother chat see all us here, she knoweth how the matter goes Therefore I read you three go hence, and within keep close, And I will into dame chats house, and so the matter use, That or you could go twice to church, I warrant you hot now, She shall look well about her, but I durst say a pledge, Ye shall of gammers needle, have shortly better knowledge.
