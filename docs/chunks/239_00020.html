Ten to one thy dream is true, thou wilt be swinged. Nay gammer, I pray you tell me who stole my spoon out of the buttery. Thy spoon is not stolen but mislaid, thou art an ill housewife though a good maid, look for thy spoon where thou hadst like to be no maid. Body of me let me fetch the spoon, I remember the place. Soft swift, the place if it be there now, it will be there to morrow. ay but perchance the spoon will not. Wert thou once put to it? No sir boy, it was put to me. How was it must? I'll warrant for want of a mist. But what's my fortune▪ mother? Thy father doth live because he doth die, thou hast spent all thy thrist with a die, and so like a beggar thou shalt die. I would have liked well if all the gerunds had been there, di, do, and dumb, but all in die, that's too deadly. My father in deed is a dyer, but I have been a dicer, but to die a beggar, give me leave not to believe Mother Bombie, and yet it may be, I have nothing to live by but knavery, and if the world grow honest welcome beggary. But what hast thou to say Risio? Nothing till I see whether all this be true that she hath said. I Risio would fain see thee beg. Nay mother tell us this, What is all our fortunes, we are about a matter of legerdemain, how will it fadge? You shall all thrive like cozeners, that is, to be cozened by cozeners: all shall end well, and you be found cozeners. Gramercy Mother Bombie, we are all pleased, if you were for your pains. I take no money, but good words, rail not if I tell true, if I do not revenge. Farewell. Now have we nothing to do but to go about this business, Accius' apparel let Candius put on, and I will array Accius with Candius clothes. Here is Silena's attire, Linceo put it upon Livia, and give me Livia's for Silena, this done, let Candius & Livia come forth, and let Dromio and me alone for the rest. What shall become of Accius and Silena? Tush, their turn shall be next, all must be done orderly, let's to it, for now it works. This attire is very fit. But how if this make me a fool and Silena wise, you will then woo me and wed her. Thou knowest that Accius is also a fool, and his raiment fits me, so that if apparel be infectious, I am also like to be a fool, and he wist what would be the conclusion, I marvel Here comes our counsellors. Well said, I perceive turtles fly in couples. Else how should they couple? So do knaves go double; else how should they be so cunning in doubling.
