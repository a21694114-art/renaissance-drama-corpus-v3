They seek to me both far and near Me think it is a good deed To help a man at his need Yet I have other means Whereby to get great gains I think ye know not that I no God wot No ye are but an ydyote I sold a man as much ware, as came to .xl. pondue And in an obligation, I had him bound To pay me at a certain day And when the bargain was made plain Mine own servant, bought the same ware again For the third penny it cost, ye wot what I mean But was not this a wise way? Thou shalt repent it another day I charge the as far as I may Such false ways never begin Wherefore this is no sin it is plain buying and selling Lawful it is for a man to win Else rich shall he never be. winning to be had, with due sufficience In true buying and selling, is not to discommend But for thy false usuri, thou art cursed in the sentence I pray God give thee grace for to amend. Is every man accursed, that doth buy and sell Then shall no man with merchandise mell How shall the world then be uphold Nay sir, amiss ye do understand me All those that occupy false usury And transgresseth the laws of God by iniquity All such are accursed I you told As for buying and selling, needs must be And God commanded to lend to them that are needy So it be not to their injury For lucre to them sold. How should I sell, show me your ways ye may not sell the dearer for days if ye do, it is contrary to God's laws It is used in our Country it is the more pity One such is able to destroy a city And God show not his great mercy All such are damned by his equity God forfend that should be How shall men do that be of great reputation Which keep their goods on this same fashion By usury, deceit and by extortion I do so myself, wherefore should I lie Thou mayst be the more sorry It is so now, what remedy Do make restitution What call ye restitution? Restore such goods as ye have gotten wrongfully by oppression. Then shall I have little in my possession I will make God amends, another way I will fast and I will pray And I will give alms every day That I have done amiss, I am sorry therefore This is not sufficient, thou must restore Quia non dimittitur peccatum Nisi restituatur ablatum ye must restore to them, ye have offended unto Then I shall show you what I shall do I will put it in my Testament That mine executors shall pay and content For wile I live I will not have my good spent For if I do I am but spilt Make amends man for thy guilt Rather spill thy body then thy soul Men of substance are a shamed to fall
