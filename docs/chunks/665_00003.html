Away, keep no distance, even both together, For wit, ye may be Coached together. What sleek browde Saint can see this Idiotism, The shape and workmanship of omnipotency, To be so blind with drugs of beastliness, And will not bend the brow, and bite the lip, Trouble his quiet soul, with venom spleen, And fear least the all overseeer, Can without vengeance, see these ignomies. Why therefore are they beloved like sergeants, and entertained like Beggars, thinkest thou but any honourable Gate but will be shut against these Butterflies? Oh Graecus! thou beguil'st opinion, The Gates of great men stand more wide To entertain a fool, than Croesus' arms, To hug his Golden God: and faster bar Against necessity, then Dives entrance At Olympus' gate. Fa, la, sol, lasol: Boy a Glass; 'tis but one and all sir. Angels protect us, what have we hear? Ye have a good memory Sir, for they are five minutes ere windefall of your Glass. Sir, be credible, 'tis balanced to be a superlative politic custom in these hours to dwell in shallow accoutrements, as a defence for the ability of his purse, from the infringed Oath of some impudent face, that will borrow a gentleman's revenues, if he be vestally adorned: I'll tell you sir, by this bright Horizon— A word I pray ye sir ere ye go any further: Boy my Tables? Your Tables are ready Sir, and all the men ye keep which is indeed half a Boy, Scillicet, Videlicet. I pray ye let me request that oath of you. A graceful inquiry, and well observed: Sir my company shall make ye copious of novelties, let your Tables befriend your memory: writ, by this bright Horizon. Here's none but only I, sing: Boy, how lik'st thou my head of hair? Your Glass may flatter ye, but truly I will not, your head is not a hair better than it should be. Is there any scarcity of hair Boy? Somewhat thin, and yet there is more hair then wit. How Boy? Then wit of man can number sir, take it i'th' right sense I pray ye. Most ingenious! O muffle, muffle good Graccûs, do not taint thy sense, With sight of these infectious animals, Lest reason in thee have the upper hand To govern sense, to see and shun the sight: Here's new discovered sins, past all the rest, Men strive by practice how to swear the best. I have quoted it sir, by this bright Hore, whore son, pronounce ye sir. Horizon Horizon the widows mite Sir? Not for the Sultan's crown sir. Indeed ye shall, by this bright horizon ye shall, believe me if I swear, I think myself beholding, for I know it to be no common oath. Were it common, it passed not these doors: Sir, I shift my oaths as I wash my hands, twice in the artificial day, for in dialoguising, 'tis to be observed, your sentences must ironically, metaphorically, and altogether figuratively mixed with your morning oaths.
