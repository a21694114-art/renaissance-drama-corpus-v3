Well, I were best be going, now I am so fairly offered. Mistress, your reward hath stopped my tears, and enticed my legs to be walking. Farewell, I will go, God knows whither, to seek and to find both, and neither. Farewell, sweet Mistress. O Pembroke, let me kneel unto thy blood: And yet I know not whether't be thy blood, Save that my soul by a divine instinct, Tells me it is the treasure of thy veins. If thou be'st dead, thou mirror of all men, I vow to die with thee: this field, this grove Shall be my receptacle till my last: Content with that that feeds the airy souls, My pillow shall be made a bank of moss, And what I drink, the silver brook shall yield. No other camp nor Court will Katharine have, Till fates do limit her a common grave. Our daughter fled! when? whither? which way? how? I know not. Bellamira, my lives joy, Upon those pinionw that support her flight, Hovers my heart, you bear away my soul. Turn, turn again, and give this earthly frame Essential power, which for thine absence dies: Thou art the sweet of sweets, the joy of joys: For thee was Philip borne. O turn again, And Philip is the blessedest of men. We are glad she's gone, though we dissemble it. Son, bridle this affection, cease these laments, She did not value them. Lewes, she did, Till savage hate that shape disfigured. O, she was worthy to be Queen of heaven. Then say not but her worth surmounts these woes. She grieved to tie you to a hated bed, And therefore followed Bourbon for revenge. Bourbon? who names him? that same verbal sound Is like a thunder clap to Philip's ears, Frighting my very soul. Sure, you said, Bourbon, And to that prodigy you joined revenge: Revenge, that like a shadow follows him. 'Twas he that made me bankrupt of all bliss, Sued the divorce of that pure white and red, Which decked my Bellamira's lovely cheeks: And shall he scape unpunished? join your hands, And all with us swear vengeance on the Duke. Not for the world: who prosecutes his hare On Bourbon, injures me: I am his foe, And none but I will work his overthrow. What means our son?
