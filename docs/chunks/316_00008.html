Another of these Students at that time, Was there with him, as I have heard a truth. Berowne they call him, but a merrier man, Within the limit of becoming mirth, I never spent an hours talk withal. His eye begets occasion for his wit, For every object that the one doth catch, The other turns to a mirth-moving jest. Which his fair tongue (conceits expositor) Delivers in such apt and gracious words, That aged ears play trevant at his tales, And younger hear are quite ravished.
