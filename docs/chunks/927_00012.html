Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Come swear, swear now (on this victorious sword) Thou wilt obey th'injunctions of my wrath, And yet in personal act, not move, but when My will appoints a mixture of thy strength. My choice Religion, & the honours of my blood, I here do pawn to ratify the vow— Now work my injured spirits, till you make Dull sorrow rise in nimble flame. Anger, Is blood, powered, and perplexed into a froth, But Malice is, the wisdom of our wrath. Your Lordship's letters were of a prompt style! This orient Rope is yours, and you must wear't; I sicken else, and grow a villager In Elysium! Your bounty chokes my thanks. Take it. Afflict me Fame, if ere I knew His parallel; he woes at Nero's rate. Madam, there's a new Dandolo, a new Count, lighted at the Gate, and craves entrance. That's rare i'faith; have I a shadow walks Without leave o'the Sun? A conspiracy. Did not Mervolle say, 'twould be a month, Ere he arrived? Be bold and meet your chance. Stand firm, and stiffly on your sinews then, Avouch me still, for the true Dandolo. Charintha, 'tis some stratagem; but if He jewels bring, and in a hand as large, And open as yond easy snipes, thou hast Ill luck if thou constrain him not to loss. I'll smile no longer than these rich sparkles Shine in my Eyes. The Lady that is named Charintha here, must be revealed; for I'd Exalt her Lips, and make'em known to mine. 'Slight, this is the great Cham. Hark ye, Signior— My Lord, I pray afford him leave to speak. What, is that saucy Groom? Does he sell Eggs? I'll show ye my Trade; A poor swordman, I— Sir you destroy the quiet of my House: He shall have safety here, and leave to speak. I'm called Charintha Sir; proceed to speech. I'll first do special grace unto your Lip— My Pen hath wooed you oft; but now by stealth Thus single I'm arrived, a Moon before The time I did prefix. ay sir, the cause. 'twas Winter when I specified the date Of my approach, and then my blood was cold; But now the spring is come, Things would couple. These (Lady) are very secular Pearl— ay sir, and in such Toys, she much delights: Although her modesty be loath, to make Her fancies known. Would you had brought some few To mach'em. Do you affect'em Lady? I rejoice in all the works of Nature! I am instructed then. As how, dear Sir? That I did ill, to leave my Pearls behind. Sister this Hulk, is neither rigged, nor fraught, I'll have no more of him, he is too costive. Sir a word. methinks you look, like raw Pork. Has not this house the privilege of peace? ladies, this Impostor steals my Titles, And my name, I ask leave, to punish him. we'll not protect his crimes, use your justice.
