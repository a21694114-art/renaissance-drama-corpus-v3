Sir, As I have hope that there is any thing To reward doing well, my usages Which have been (but 'tis no matter what) Have put me so far from the thought of Greatness, That I should welcome it like a disease That grew upon me, and I could not cure. They are my enemies that gave you this, And yet they call me friend, and are themselves I fear abused. I am weary of my life, For God's sake take it from me: it creates More mischief in the State than it is worth. The usage I have had, I know would make Wisdom herself run frantic through the streets.
