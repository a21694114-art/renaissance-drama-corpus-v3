No Philip no. What, wilt thou were a point but with one tag? Well Francis well, I see you are a wag. 'swounds where he these timber turners, these troll the bowls, these green men, these. What, what sir? These bowlers sir. Well sir, what say you to Bowlers? Why I say they cannot be saved. Your reason sir? Because they throw away their souls at every mark Their souls, how mean ye? Sirrah he means the soul of our bowl. Lord how his wit holds bias like a bowl. Well, which is the Bias? This next to you. Nay turn it this way, than the bowl goes true. Rub, rub. Why rub? Why you overcast the mark and miss the way. Nay boy juse to take the fairest of my play. Dick Coomes methinks thou art very pleasant. When got'st thou this merry humour? In your father's Seller, the merriest place in th'house. Then you have been carousing hard, Yes faith, 'tis our custom when your father's men & we meet. Thou art very welcome thither Dick. By God I thank ye sir, I thank ye sir, by God I have a quart of Wine for ye sir in any place of the world, there shall not a servingman in Berkshire fight better for ye than I will do, if you have any quarrel in hand, you shall have the maidenhead of my new sword: I paid a quarter's wages for't by Iesus. Oh this meat failer Dick, How well t'as made apparel of his wit, And brought it into fashion of an honour, prithee Dick Coomes but tell me how thou dost? Faith sir like a poor man at service. Or serving man. Indeed so called by the vulgar. Why where the devil hadst thou that word? O sir, you have the most eloquenst ale in all the world, our blunt soil affords none such, Philip leave talking with this drunken fool, Say sirrah where's my father? Marry I thank ye for my very good cheer, O Lord it is not so much worth, you see I am bold with ye, Indeed you are not so bold as welcome, I pray ye come oftener, Truly I shall trouble ye, all these ceremonies are dispatched between them, and they are gone. Are they so? I before God are they. And wherefore came not you to call me then? Because I was loath to change my game, What game? You were at one sort of bowls, as I was at another, Sirrah, he means the buttery bowls of beer. By God sirrah we tickled it.
