Who should buy't? Why he that asks the question, ay, my Lord; If you will walk by Land I'm your first man, I'll bid as fair as any. He's mad still, leave him. A Bankrupt idle fellow brave your Lordship? Beat him to's wits, or let me kick him for you. Ha, what's that? who, Geoffrey Codpiece, Iohn Boe Peep, Is't you that prate so freely? my young Iudas, Have you conspired against me? Endure this? Stay, first we will debate in friendly terms, Before you grow to outrage. Done most Lordly; your tongue will grace you Better than your Sword, 'tis the most Noble fashion. I'll be brief, I understood your state was much Decayed, which in my honour I commiserating, My purpose was in some sort to relieve you; And would you better understand yourself, It might appear a noble courtesy. Because it came so free and uncompelled Such as one Neighbour to another doth To get his wife with Child, a courtesy, and so it fitly may Be called because it brings him to his Knees. Still, still misprision; might I advise you, proffer What you purpose, and if refused, so leave him. Counselled well: there's twenty pieces for you. For a wife? Her Ladyship is yours for nought already, And should I sell her twice? This saucy fellow usurps upon our patience, Which we'll punish be our strong hand. A punisher, my Lord? what beg the Beadle's Office? Dash out's brains. I doubt his Lord ships warrant is not currant, therefore I'll not obey. Sir, all's dispatched, but by my faith I lie, Here's something here to do. Why God a mercy Master, I perceive Your spirits not all spent. Thanks to thy Sword, or I had been oppressed else. But how differed you? to be assailed by such a shameful odds. They scoffed at my supposed poverty, And my great Lord forsooth, as I had begged, Would have bestowed his guerdon on my want, Which taking in foul scorn, the valiant sir Assailed me at these base advantages: But hast thou done as I enjoined? Most carefully; all's to the full dispatched. If with the Devil I could dispense as well, I should have ease within. Ha, sirrah Geoffrey, I Think you played at leap Frog? You are sad sir? Do not demand thy debt before the day; Thy forfeit is not due yet. No such thing; the business that I come to treat with thee Is of another nature. Sir, what's he? too hot for thy acquaintance, do not ask, Attend me to my Chamber, whether anon, I will not fail to come. Too hot, and why? he may be honest, but he hath the Countenance of an old subtle knave; well, I'll attend you. Now what to me? Have I not kept my word? Thou hast. Stored thee with all the supplements Man's use can ask. It cannot be denied. Nay, was not I assistant in this quarrel, prompting thy Man to come to thy release, just at the instant danger:
