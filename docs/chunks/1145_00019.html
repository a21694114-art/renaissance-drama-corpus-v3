Yours shall be The choice noble Sir Timothy. Yours indeed, Magnanimous Mr. Holdfast. On my gentility yours. Yours on my knighthood. Good sir Timothy, No striving, they are free for you, and for The stain those idle gallants put upon them, 'twas on my credit gentlemen to keep All other suitors off, in hope by that means To obtain them for themselves. 'tis very likely That Valentine's a wag.
