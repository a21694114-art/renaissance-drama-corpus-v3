Go, get you gone. What lamb, what Lady bird? fast I warrant. What Iuliet? well▪ let the County take you in your bed▪ ye sleep for a week now, but the next night, the County Paris hath set up his rest that you shall rest but little. What lamb I say, fast still: what Lady, Love, what bride, what Iuliet? God's me how sound she sleeps? Nay then I see I must wake you indeed. What's here, laid on your bed, dressed in your clothes and down, ah me, alack the day, some Aqua vitae ho. How now what's the matter? Alack the day, she's dead, she's dead, she's dead. O heavens, O nature, wherefore did you make me, To live so vile, so wretched as I shall. O here she lies that was our hope, our joy, And being dead, dead sorrow nips us all.
