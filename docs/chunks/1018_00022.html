Text (EarlyPrint-regularized spelling)

Well Sir, I'll pledge you. Drink a good hearty draught, it breeds good blood man. Do not you feel it thaw you? Stay, I tell you after a draught or two more. Spare it not, the Duke has more couz: Eat now. Yes. I am glad you have so good a stomach. I am gladder I have so good meat to't. Is't not mad lodging, here in the wild woods Cosen Yes, for then that have wild Consciences. How tastes your victuals? your hunger needs no sauce I see, Not much. But if it did, yours is too tart: sweet Cosen: what is this? Venison. 'tis a lusty meat: Give me more wine; here Arcite to the wenches We have known in our days. The Lord Steward's daughter. Do you remember her? After you couz. She loved a black-haird man. She did so; well Sir. And I have heard some call him Arcite. and Out with't faith. She met him in an Arbour: What did she there couz? play o'th' virginals? Something she did Sir. Made her groan a month for't; or 2. or 3. or 10. The Marshal's Sister, Had her share too, as I remember Cosen, Else there be tales abroad, you'll pledge her? Yes. A pretty brown wench t'is-There was a time When young men went a hunting, and a wood, And a broad Beech: and thereby hangs a tale: heigh ho. For Emily, upon my life; Fool Away with this strained mirth; I say again That sigh was breathed for Emily; base Cosen, Dar'st thou break first? you are wide. By heaven and earth, there's nothing in thee honest. Then I'll leave you: you are a Beast now: As thou mak'st me, Traitor. there's all things needful, files and shirts, and, perfumes: I'll come again some two hours hence, and bring That that shall quiet all, A Sword and Armour. Fear me not; you are now too foul; farewell. Cet off your Trinkets, you shall want nought; Sir ha: I'll hear no more. If he keep touch, he dies for't. I am very cold, and all the Stars are out too, The little Stars, and all, that look like aglets: The Sun has seen my Folly: Palamon; Alas no; he's in heaven; where am I now? Yonder's the sea, and there's a Ship; how't tumbles And there's a Rock lies watching under water; Now, now, it beats upon it; now, now, now, there's a leak sprung, a sound one, how they cry? Upon her before the wind, you'll lose all else: Up with a course or two, and take about Boys. Good night, good night, y'are gone; I am very hungry, Would I could find a fine Frog; would tell me news from all parts o'th' world, then would I make A Carecke of a Cockle shell, and sail By east and North East to the King of Pigmies, For he tells fortunes rarely. Now my Father Twenty to one is trust up in a trice To morrow morning, I'll say never a word.
