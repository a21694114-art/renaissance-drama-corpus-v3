Thou canst not fly me, There is no Cavern in the Earth's, vast entrails But I can through as pearcant as the light, And find thee, though thou were't entombed in stone, Thou canst not catch my unsubstantial part, For I am air, and am not to be touched. From flaming fires of burning Phlegeton, I have a time limited to walk, Until the morning Cock shall summon me For to retire to misty Erebus. My pilgrimage has no cessation, Until I bring thee with me to the place Where Rhadamant, and sable Aeacus dwell Alive or dead, 'tis I that must enjoy thee, To tell the story where we spirits live Would pluck Vermilion from thy Rosy cheeks, And make them pale, as Snowy Apennines, And from thine eyes draw liquid streams of tears More full of issue then a steepy Fountain, Alive or dead I must, and will enjoy thee, Think on thy promise. Distraction like an Ague seizes me, I know not whether I see here, or speak, My intellectual parts are frozen up At sight of thee, thou fiery Effigies Of my wronged Bateman. What weeping again? Do you not see it? See! what? I see nothing but a Bird fly o'er the house. Nor I, but a blind Buzzard looks as like her husband as may be. Are you blind, or will you make yourselves so? See! how like a dreadful magistrate it stands, Still pointing at me the black offender; And like a cunning poisoner, will not kill me, But lets me linger on, for days, and years. It stares, beckons, points, to the piece of Gold We broke between us; look, look there, here there! I see nothing, perceive nothing, feel nothing! Nor I, no quick thing, neither clothed nor naked. No, no, no! you drank Balm, Burrage or bugloss last night to bedward, that makes you think on your dreams this morning. But I will to't, hug, and embrace it. Thy time is not yet come; i'm now exiled I may not touch thee while thou art with child. you do not hear it neither? Whom should we hear? Young Batemans visage. In every limb as perfect as he lived. If it be so, 'tis done by sorcery. The father has combined, with some witch, To vex thy quiet patience, and gain credit, That he would haunt thee dead, as oft he said, Hell can put life into a senseless body, And raise it from the grave, and make it speak; Use all the faculties alive it did, To work the devil's hellish stratagems! If I but find he deals in exorcisms I'll make him burn to pacify the Witch, But do not believe it girl. 'Tis vanished in an instant! I will not be too confident in my eyes, Will you grant me leave to visit Bateman? Visit mine enemy?
