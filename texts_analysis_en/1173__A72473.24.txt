THis Tale of mee, the Tub of Totten-Court,
A Poet, firſt invented for your ſport.
Wherein the fortune of moſt empty Tubs
Rowling in love, are ſhewne; and with what rubs,
W'are commonly encountred: When the wit
Of the whole Hundred ſo oppoſeth it.
Our petty Chanon's forked plot in chiefe,
Slie Iuſtice arts, with the High Conſtables Briefe,
And brag Commands; my Lady Mothers care;
And her Pol-martens fortune; with the rare
Fate of poore Iohn, thus tumbled in the Caske;
Got In-and-In, to gi't you in a Maſque:
That you be pleas'd, who come to ſee a Play,
With thoſe that heare, and marke not what wee ſay.
Wherein the Poets fortune is, I feare,
Still to be early up, but nere the neare.
