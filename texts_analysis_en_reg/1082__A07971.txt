You are a Country Gentleman; a Gallant out of fashion all the year; but especially at Sessions, and upon high Holidays, when your satin doublet draws away the eyes of the simple, and distracts their devotion almost into Idolatry: giving it more worship than the Heralds ever gave your Ancestors. You intend as I understand to come forth in a new Edition: and when the Mercers and Tailors have new printed you; and that some gentile wit may be read in your Character to marry a Wife in the City.
